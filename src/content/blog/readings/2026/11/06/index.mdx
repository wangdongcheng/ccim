常年期第三十一周（星期五） | Friday of the Thirty-first Week in Ordinary Time

2026年11月6日 · 星期五 | November 6, 2026 · Friday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**斐理伯人书 3:17—4:1 | Philippians 3:17—4:1**

弟兄们，你们要一同效法我，并留意那些按照我们所给的榜样生活的人。我曾多次告诉你们，如今又流着泪重说：有许多人生活的方式，竟与基督的十字架为敌。他们走向灭亡，把口腹的满足当作神明，以可耻的事自夸，心思只停留在地上的事物。

我们的公民身分却属于天上，我们从那里热切盼望救主、主耶稣基督。他要以那能使万有归服于他的大能，改变我们卑微的身体，使之肖似他光荣的身体。

所以，我深爱而思念的弟兄们，你们是我的喜乐和冠冕；亲爱的，你们应当这样在主内坚定站立。

Brothers and sisters, follow my example together, and pay attention to those whose lives reflect the pattern we have given you. Many people live in opposition to Christ's cross. I have warned you of them repeatedly, and I repeat it now with tears. Their course ends in ruin; appetite is their idol, shame becomes their boast, and their attention is confined to earthly things.

Our citizenship, however, belongs to heaven. From there we eagerly await our Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ. With the power by which he can subject everything to himself, he will transform our humble bodies to share the likeness of his glorious body.

Therefore, my beloved brothers and sisters, whom I long to see—you who are my joy and my crown—remain steadfast in the Lord in this way, dear friends.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 122:1-5 | Psalm 122:1-5**

**答：我们要高兴地进入上主的圣殿！ | R. Let us go joyfully to the house of the Lord!**

有人邀请我说：“一起往上主的殿去！”我心中多么欢喜。耶路撒冷，如今我们的双脚已站在你的城门之内。

**答：我们要高兴地进入上主的圣殿！**

耶路撒冷是一座建造齐整、紧密相连的城。众支派都向那里上行，他们是属于上主的支派。

**答：我们要高兴地进入上主的圣殿！**

他们遵照以色列的法令，在那里感谢上主的圣名。那里安设了施行审判的王座，就是达味王家的宝座。

**答：我们要高兴地进入上主的圣殿！**

My heart was glad when I heard the invitation to visit the Lord's house. Jerusalem, our feet now stand inside your gates.

**R. Let us go joyfully to the house of the Lord!**

Jerusalem is built as a city joined firmly together. The tribes make their pilgrimage there, the tribes belonging to the Lord.

**R. Let us go joyfully to the house of the Lord!**

According to Israel's ordinance, they gather to thank the Lord by name. There the thrones of justice are established, the thrones of David's royal house.

**R. Let us go joyfully to the house of the Lord!**

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣路加福音 16:1-8 | Luke 16:1-8**

耶稣又对门徒讲道：“有一个富人，雇了一名管家。有人向主人控告，说管家在挥霍他的财产。主人便召他来，问：‘我听见你这些事，是怎么回事？把你经管财物的账交出来，你不能再作管家了。’

“管家心里想：‘主人要撤掉我的职务，我以后怎么办？挖地没有力气，讨饭又觉得羞耻。我知道该怎么办了，好叫我失去管家职务以后，有人愿意接我到他们家里。’

“于是，他逐一叫来欠主人债的人，问第一个：‘你欠我主人多少？’那人回答：‘一百桶油。’管家说：‘拿好你的债单，赶快坐下，改成五十。’他又问另一个：‘你呢，欠多少？’那人答：‘一百石麦子。’管家就说：‘拿你的债单，改成八十。’

“主人称许这不义的管家办事精明，因为今世之子处理自己这一代的事务，比光明之子更为机敏。”

Jesus also spoke to his disciples: “A wealthy man had a steward who was accused of dissipating his property. The owner summoned him and demanded an explanation. ‘Submit your management accounts,’ he said, ‘because your stewardship is coming to an end.’

“The steward thought: ‘What can I do now that my master is removing me? I lack the strength for digging, and begging would shame me. I know how to ensure that people will welcome me into their homes after I lose my position.’

“He therefore called in the people who owed his master money, one at a time. He asked the first how much he owed. ‘A hundred measures of oil,’ the man answered. The steward told him to take his debt document, sit down at once, and change the amount to fifty. He asked another the same question. This man owed a hundred measures of wheat, so the steward instructed him to change his document to eighty.

“The master acknowledged the dishonest steward's shrewdness. For in dealing with their own generation, the people of this world show greater practical foresight than the children of light.”

## 反省 | Reflection

据[李建平神父当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-11-06)摘要（乐仁出版社供稿）：耶稣借管家的危机应变，指出果断和远见的价值，并非赞成失职与不义。基督徒既称为光明之子，也该认真善用时间、权力和钱财。我们要检视自己怎样管理上主交托的一切，使今世的资源服务于永生。

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau: Jesus draws attention to the steward's decisive response and foresight, without approving his misconduct. Christians, as children of light, should also use time, authority, and money thoughtfully. We must examine our care for everything entrusted by the Lord and direct earthly resources towards eternal life.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

据[教宗良十四世2025年9月21日三钟经讲话](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/06.html)摘要：生命和财物都是主交托的礼物，我们负有自由而真实的责任，终须交账，也须对社会与后代负责。福音邀请我们走出自私的孤立，运用今世的资源建立友爱；真正的财富，是与主、与弟兄姊妹的共融。

Summary of Pope Leo XIV's Angelus address of 21 September 2025: Our lives and possessions are gifts entrusted by the Lord. We must account for their use before God, society, and future generations. The Gospel calls us beyond selfish isolation, using earthly resources to build friendship. Lasting wealth lies in communion with the Lord and with our brothers and sisters.
