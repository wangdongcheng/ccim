## 读经一 | First Reading

**圣保禄宗徒致斐理伯人书 2:5-11 | Philippians 2:5-11**

弟兄们：你们应怀有基督耶稣所怀有的心情。他虽具有天主的形体，却没有把与天主同等视为必须把持的特权，反而虚己，取了奴仆的形体，成为与人相似的；他以人的姿态出现，贬抑自己，服从至死，且死在十字架上。

为此，天主极其举扬他，赐给他一个超越一切名字的名字，使天上、地上和地下的一切，一听见耶稣的名字都屈膝叩拜；众口都承认耶稣基督是主，以光荣天主圣父。

Brothers and sisters: Let the mind of Christ Jesus be in you. Though he shared the form of God, he did not cling to equality with God as something to exploit. Instead, he emptied himself, took the form of a servant, and became like us. Appearing in human likeness, he humbled himself and obeyed even to death—death on a cross.

Therefore God raised him above all and bestowed on him the name beyond every name. At the name of Jesus every knee in heaven, on earth, and under the earth must bend, and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 22:26-27,28-30,31-32 | Psalm 22:26b-27,28-30ab,30e,31-32**

**答：上主，我要在盛大的集会中赞美你。 | R. I will praise you, Lord, in the assembly of your people.**

我在敬畏上主的人面前履行我的誓愿；贫苦的人必得饱餐，寻求上主的人必要赞美他。愿你们的心永远生活！

**答：上主，我要在盛大的集会中赞美你。**

大地四极都要想起上主，回心转向他；普世万族都要在他面前朝拜，因为王权属于上主，他统治万邦。

**答：上主，我要在盛大的集会中赞美你。**

凡安眠于尘土的人都要向他俯伏。我的灵魂要为他生活，我的后裔要事奉他；世世代代都要传述上主，向未来的民族宣告他的正义：“这是上主所行的。”

**答：上主，我要在盛大的集会中赞美你。**

Before those who revere the Lord I will fulfil my vows. The poor shall eat and be satisfied; those who seek the Lord shall praise him. May their hearts live for ever!

**R. I will praise you, Lord, in the assembly of your people.**

All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn to the Lord; every family of nations shall bow before him. Kingship belongs to the Lord, who rules over the nations.

**R. I will praise you, Lord, in the assembly of your people.**

All who sleep in the earth shall bow before him. My soul shall live for him, and my descendants shall serve him. Coming generations will be told of the Lord and proclaim his justice to a people yet unborn: “This is what the Lord has done.”

**R. I will praise you, Lord, in the assembly of your people.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣路加福音 14:15-24 | Luke 14:15-24**

那时候，与耶稣同席的一人说：“在天主的国里吃饭的人是有福的！”耶稣便对他说：有一个人设了盛宴，邀请许多人。到了宴会时刻，他派仆人告诉受邀者：“请来吧，一切都准备好了。”

众人却不约而同地推辞。第一个说自己买了田地，必须去查看；另一个说买了五对牛，要去试一试；还有一个说刚结了婚，所以不能前来。仆人回来报告，家主便动怒，吩咐他赶快到城里的大街小巷，把贫穷的、残疾的、失明的和瘸腿的领进来。

仆人照办后说：“主人，你吩咐的已经完成了，可是还有空位。”主人又命他到路旁和篱笆边，劝人进来，好使屋子坐满。主人最后说，先前受邀而拒绝的人，没有一个会尝到他的宴席。

At that time, one of those dining with Jesus said, “Blessed is the one who will eat in the kingdom of God!” Jesus replied with a story. A man prepared a great supper and invited many guests. When everything was ready, he sent his servant to tell them, “Come, for everything is now ready.”

But they all began to make excuses. One had bought a field and felt obliged to inspect it; another had purchased five yoke of oxen and was going to test them; a third had just married and said he could not come. When the servant reported this, the householder became angry and ordered him into the city streets and lanes to bring in the poor, the disabled, the blind, and the lame.

After doing so, the servant said there was still room. The master sent him out again, along the roads and hedges, to urge people to enter so that his house might be filled. He declared that none of those who had first been invited and refused would taste his supper.

## 反省 | Reflection

天主为人预备的救恩宴席已经齐备，他的邀请直接而迫切。福音中的人并非公开反对主人，却让田产、生意和个人安排占据首位，以一个个看来合理的借口错过了恩宠。真正的贫穷，是自以为什么都不缺；真正有福的，是承认自己的需要，并欣然回应天主召叫的人。今天，让我们放下拖延与借口，也以同样敞开的心接纳那些常被社会忽略的人。

God's saving banquet stands ready, and his invitation is direct and urgent. The people in the Gospel do not openly oppose the host; they simply allow property, business, and personal plans to take first place, missing grace through a series of reasonable-sounding excuses. True poverty is imagining that we need nothing. Those who recognize their need and gladly answer God's call are blessed. Today we are asked to set aside delay and excuses, and to welcome with the same open heart those whom society often overlooks.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各指出，天主的邀请不收费，也没有任何人的功劳可以购买这场盛宴。面对无偿的恩典，人有时宁愿抓住自己的利益和日常事务，因而拒绝主。教会的使命是走出去，把邀请带给所有人，尤其是贫弱者和被排斥的人；天主的家仍有空位，他愿意人人都来分享他的喜乐。

Pope Francis taught that God's invitation is free; no human merit can purchase a place at this banquet. Faced with a gift, people sometimes prefer to hold on to their interests and daily concerns and so refuse the Lord. The Church is called to go out and carry the invitation to everyone, especially the poor and excluded. There is still room in God's house, and he desires all people to share his joy.
