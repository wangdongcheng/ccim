## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄则克耳先知书 34:11-12,15-17 | Ezekiel 34:11-12,15-17**

上主天主宣告：我要亲自寻找并照顾自己的羊。牧人怎样寻找失散的羊，我也要怎样寻回羊群，把它们从阴暗多云的日子所流散的地方救出。

我要亲自作它们的牧人，让它们安然躺卧。我会寻觅失落的，带回走迷的，为受伤的包扎，使软弱的恢复力量，也看顾肥壮的；我将依照正义牧养它们。

至于我的羊群，上主天主说：我要在羊与羊之间，在公绵羊和公山羊之间，作出公正的裁断。

The Lord God declares that he himself will find and care for his sheep. Like a shepherd searching for a scattered flock, he will recover them from every place to which they wandered during the dark and cloudy day.

He will personally shepherd them and give them a place to rest. The missing he will seek, the straying he will return, the injured he will bandage, and the weak he will strengthen. He will also watch over the well-fed and strong, tending the flock with justice.

Addressing his flock, the Lord God announces that he will decide between one sheep and another, between rams and male goats.

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**格林多人前书 15:20-26a,28 | 1 Corinthians 15:20-26,28**

基督确实已由死者中复活，成为所有安眠者复活的初果。死亡既由一个人而来，死者的复活也由一个人而来。众人在亚当内走向死亡，也将在基督内重新获得生命。

但各有其次序：先是基督这初果，随后，在他来临时，是所有属于他的人。末期到来，他将摧毁一切统治、权势和能力，把王国交还天主父。他要继续作王，直到所有仇敌都被置于脚下；最后遭消灭的仇敌，就是死亡。

当万物都服从圣子以后，圣子自己也将服从那使万物归服于他的父，使天主成为万有中的一切。

Christ truly has risen from death, the first harvest of those who sleep in the grave. As death entered through one human being, resurrection also comes through one. All share death in Adam; in Christ all will receive life.

There is an appointed order: Christ first, then those who belong to him when he returns. Finally comes the consummation. Having abolished every dominion, authority and power, he hands the kingdom to God the Father. His reign continues until every enemy lies defeated beneath him; death is the final enemy to be abolished.

Once everything is brought under the Son, he himself will submit to the Father who placed everything under him, so that God fills all things completely.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 25:31-46 | Matthew 25:31-46**

耶稣向门徒讲述：人子将带着光荣，与天使一同来临，坐上荣耀的宝座。万民聚在他面前，他要如同牧人区分绵羊和山羊，把他们分别安置在右边与左边。

君王邀请右边的人承受天父自创世以来为他们预备的国度，因为在他饥饿时，他们送上食物；口渴时，他们给他饮水；作异乡人时，他们接待他；没有衣服时，他们给他穿着；患病时，他们照料他；在监狱中时，他们前去探望。

义人询问，他们何时曾看见主有这些需要，而给予帮助。君王说明，他们为他最微小的一个弟兄所做的一切，都是向他本人所做的。

他又命左边的人离开，进入为魔鬼和其使者预备的永火，因为面对他的饥渴、流落、赤身、疾病和囚禁，他们没有给予帮助。他们也询问，何时曾看见主处于这些困境，却没有服事他。君王回答，凡他们拒绝为最微小者做的，就是拒绝为他做。

这些人将面临永远的刑罚，义人则进入永远的生命。

Jesus described the Son of man's glorious arrival with angels. Enthroned before all nations, he will divide them like a shepherd sorting sheep from goats, placing them on opposite sides.

The King will welcome those on his right into the kingdom his Father prepared at creation. They fed him when hungry, gave him drink when thirsty, welcomed him as a stranger, clothed him, cared for him during illness and visited him in prison.

The righteous will ask when they ever saw him in those circumstances and helped. He will explain that every kindness shown to even his least brother was shown to him.

He will dismiss those on his left into everlasting fire prepared for the devil and his angels. They left his hunger, thirst, homelessness, nakedness, illness and imprisonment unattended. They too will ask when they encountered him in need without serving him. He will explain that withholding help from even the least person meant withholding it from him.

They will face unending punishment, while the righteous enter unending life.

## 反省 | Reflection

最后审判使我们正视自己怎样对待弱小者。基督与那些遭到忽略和排斥的人相连；对他们的关怀，就是对他的回应。无论祈祷和教理知识如何，我们都能从付出一点时间、资源和实际帮助开始，把爱化为行动。

The final judgment asks us to examine our treatment of vulnerable people. Christ is bound to those whom others overlook or reject; caring for them is a response to him. Whatever our ability in prayer or knowledge of doctrine, we can begin by giving time, resources and practical help, making love visible through action.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

基督审判的准则，是对近人的实际爱德，尤其是对饥渴、流落、缺衣、患病和被囚者的关怀。他认同这些最微小的弟兄，使我们今生的选择具有永恒的分量。我们以慷慨行动回应他，便参与建设他那爱、正义与和平的王国。

Christ judges by practical love for our neighbours, especially people suffering hunger, thirst, displacement, nakedness, illness or imprisonment. By identifying himself with the least, he gives our earthly choices lasting significance. Generous deeds answer his invitation to share in building his kingdom of love, justice and peace.
