圣嘉禄·鲍荣茂主教纪念日 | Memorial of Saint Charles Borromeo, Bishop

2026年11月4日 · 星期三 | November 4, 2026 · Wednesday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**斐理伯人书 2:12-18 | Philippians 2:12-18**

亲爱的弟兄们，你们向来服从我的教导；如今我不在你们身旁，更应如此。你们要怀着敬畏，认真致力于自己的得救，因为使你们愿意行善、并使你们能够实行，以承行他美意的，正是在你们内工作的天主。

凡事不要发怨言，也不要争执，好使你们成为纯洁而无可指责的人，在弯曲败坏的世代中，作天主无瑕的子女。你们在世人中应如星辰发光，持守生命之言；这样，到了基督的日子，我便能因没有徒然奔跑、没有白费劳苦而喜乐。

即使我的生命如奠酒一般，倾注在你们信德的祭献和服务上，我仍愿意欢喜，并与你们众人分享喜乐。你们也应同样欢喜，与我一同欢乐。

My dear friends, you have always responded obediently to my teaching. Continue to do so even more earnestly now that I am away. Attend to your salvation with reverent seriousness: God himself is working within you, awakening your willingness and enabling your action according to his gracious purpose.

Let your conduct be free from complaints and disputes, so that you may be innocent and beyond reproach, God's unblemished children amid a twisted and corrupt generation. Shine among its people like stars, keeping hold of the life-giving word. Then, when Christ's day comes, I can rejoice that my race and my labour were fruitful.

Even if my life is poured out like a drink offering over the sacrifice and service arising from your faith, I welcome it with joy and share that joy with you all. You, in turn, should rejoice and share your gladness with me.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 27:1,4,13-14 | Psalm 27:1,4,13-14**

**答：上主是我的光明，我的救援。 | R. The Lord is my light and my salvation.**

上主照亮我，拯救我，我还怕谁？上主护卫我的生命，我何必惊惶？

**答：上主是我的光明，我的救援。**

我向上主所求、所追寻的，只有一件事：终身居住在他的殿宇，瞻仰他的美善，在他的圣所寻求他。

**答：上主是我的光明，我的救援。**

我深信，在生者的土地上，必能看见上主的恩惠。要等候上主，鼓起勇气，坚定你的心；要等候上主。

**答：上主是我的光明，我的救援。**

The Lord gives me light and delivers me; who can make me afraid? He is the strong defence of my life; why should I tremble?

**R. The Lord is my light and my salvation.**

One desire shapes my prayer and my search: to dwell in the Lord's house throughout my life, to gaze upon his beauty and seek him in his sanctuary.

**R. The Lord is my light and my salvation.**

I remain confident that I shall witness the Lord's goodness here among the living. Await the Lord with courage; let your heart grow strong as you wait for him.

**R. The Lord is my light and my salvation.**

阿肋路亚。你们若因基督之名而遭辱骂，是有福的，因为光荣的神、天主的神停留在你们身上。阿肋路亚。

Alleluia. Blessed are you when people insult you because you bear Christ's name: the Spirit of glory, God's own Spirit, rests upon you. Alleluia.

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣路加福音 14:25-33 | Luke 14:25-33**

大批群众与耶稣同行，他转过身来告诉他们：“谁来到我这里，若不将父母、妻子、儿女、兄弟姊妹，甚至自己的生命，都放在我之后，就不能作我的门徒。谁不背起自己的十字架，跟着我走，也不能作我的门徒。

“你们中间若有人想建一座塔，难道不先坐下核算费用，看看有没有足够的钱把它完成吗？否则，地基打好了，工程却无法完成，看见的人都会嘲笑：‘这人开了工，却盖不成。’

“又有哪一个国王出兵迎战另一位国王，不先坐下衡量：自己的一万兵士，能不能抵挡迎面而来的两万人？若不能，他便会在对方还远的时候，派使者去求和。

“同样，你们无论是谁，若不舍下自己的一切所有，就不能作我的门徒。”

Large crowds were accompanying Jesus. Turning towards them, he told them: “Anyone who comes to me must relinquish every rival claim—even father and mother, wife and children, brothers and sisters, and one's own life. Otherwise that person cannot be my disciple. Nor can anyone be my disciple without taking up the cross and following behind me.

“Suppose one of you wishes to build a tower. Would you not first sit down and reckon the expense, to discover whether you can afford to finish? Otherwise you might lay the foundation and be unable to complete it. Everyone who saw it would ridicule you for starting a building you could not finish.

“Or suppose a king is setting out to fight another king. Would he not first consider whether his ten thousand soldiers can withstand an opponent approaching with twenty thousand? If they cannot, he sends envoys to seek terms of peace while the other king is still far away.

“So it is with you: unless you surrender all that you possess, you cannot be my disciple.”

## 反省 | Reflection

据[李建平神父当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-11-04)摘要（乐仁出版社供稿）：建筑与战争需要筹划，作门徒也须认真面对代价。然而，人的本领和安排不能独自完成天主的工程。基督徒的生命有赖恩宠，应谦逊地把自己交给主，求他使召叫结出果实。

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau: Building and warfare require preparation, and discipleship also demands an honest reckoning of its cost. Yet skill and planning alone cannot accomplish God's work. Christian life depends on grace; we humbly entrust ourselves to the Lord and ask him to bring his calling to fruition.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

据[教宗良十四世2025年9月7日讲道](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/04.html)摘要：耶稣召叫我们全心投入跟随他的道路，背起十字架，放下占有。圣神赐予我们回应的力量，但我们须腾出内心，聆听主的话。方济各和奥思定等圣人的生命，都因向天主交出自己而获得新的方向。

Summary of Pope Leo XIV's homily of 7 September 2025: Jesus calls for wholehearted commitment, carrying the cross and releasing our possessions. His Spirit supplies strength for the journey as we make room to hear his word. Saints such as Francis and Augustine found a new direction by giving themselves to God.
