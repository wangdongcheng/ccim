## 读经一 | First Reading

**斐理伯人书 3:3-8a | Philippians 3:3-8a**

弟兄们，真正受割礼的乃是我们：我们藉天主的圣神敬礼他，以基督耶稣为夸耀，并不倚靠血肉的条件。其实，若论这些条件，我也有理由倚靠；别人若以为自己有可凭恃的，我更有。

我出生第八天便受了割礼，属于以色列民族、本雅明支派，是希伯来人所生的希伯来人。论法律，我是法利塞人；论热忱，我曾迫害教会；论法律所规定的义，我也无可指责。

然而，那些过去被我当作收益的事，如今为了基督，我都视为亏损。不止如此，为了认识我的主基督耶稣这无上的宝藏，我把一切都看作损失。

Brothers and sisters, we are the people of the true circumcision: our worship is offered through God's Spirit, our pride is in Christ Jesus, and our confidence does not rest on outward human advantages. If such advantages could be a basis for confidence, I would have ample grounds—more than anyone who claims them.

I was circumcised when eight days old, born into Israel and the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew with Hebrew parents. In relation to the law I belonged to the Pharisees; my zeal led me to persecute the Church; measured by legal righteousness, my record was beyond reproach.

Yet everything I once counted as an advantage I now reckon a loss for Christ. Indeed, knowing Christ Jesus my Lord is worth so much more that, beside it, I count everything else as loss.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 105:2-7 | Psalm 105:2-7**

**答：愿寻求上主的人满心喜悦。 | R. Let the hearts of those who seek the Lord rejoice.**

要向他歌唱，为他奏乐，述说他所行的一切奇事。要因他的圣名而欢欣；寻求上主的人，应从心底喜乐。

**答：愿寻求上主的人满心喜悦。**

要寻求上主和他的力量，不断追寻他的慈颜。要记念他所完成的奇功、他的异迹，以及他口中宣告的裁判。

**答：愿寻求上主的人满心喜悦。**

你们是他仆人亚巴郎的后裔，是他所拣选的雅各伯的子孙。他是上主，我们的天主；他的裁判遍及普世。

**答：愿寻求上主的人满心喜悦。**

Sing to him and make music in his honour; recount all his wonders. Take delight in his holy name, and let everyone searching for the Lord be glad at heart.

**R. Let the hearts of those who seek the Lord rejoice.**

Seek the Lord and the strength he gives; continually turn towards his presence. Keep his astonishing deeds in memory, together with his signs and the judgments he has spoken.

**R. Let the hearts of those who seek the Lord rejoice.**

You are descendants of Abraham his servant, children of Jacob whom he chose. He is the Lord, our God; his judgments reach throughout the earth.

**R. Let the hearts of those who seek the Lord rejoice.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣路加福音 15:1-10 | Luke 15:1-10**

税吏和罪人纷纷来到耶稣身边，要听他说话。法利塞人和经师却抱怨：“这人接纳罪人，还同他们吃饭！”耶稣便给他们讲了一个比喻：

“你们中间若有人有一百只羊，丢了一只，难道不把其余九十九只留在旷野，去追寻那只失落的羊，直到找到吗？找到了，他便满心欢喜，把羊放在肩头带回家，再召集朋友和邻居，说：‘来分享我的喜乐吧，我找回了那只走失的羊！’我告诉你们，一个罪人回头悔改，天上也会如此欢欣，比为九十九个不需要悔改的义人更为喜乐。

“又或者，一个妇人有十枚银币，丢了一枚，难道不点灯、扫屋，仔细地寻找，直到找到吗？找到以后，她便把朋友和邻居请来，说：‘来分享我的喜乐吧，我找到了丢失的银币！’我告诉你们，一个罪人悔改，天主的天使面前也会有这样的喜乐。”

Tax collectors and sinners were coming close to hear Jesus. The Pharisees and scribes objected that he welcomed sinners and shared meals with them. In answer, Jesus told them this parable:

“Suppose one of you owns a hundred sheep and one goes missing. Would you not leave the other ninety-nine in the wilderness and pursue the missing sheep until you find it? Once you find it, you joyfully lift it onto your shoulders. Back home, you gather your friends and neighbours to celebrate with you because the sheep that wandered away has been recovered. I tell you, heaven likewise rejoices over a single sinner who repents, even more than over ninety-nine righteous people who need no repentance.

“Or consider a woman with ten silver coins who loses one. Would she not light a lamp, sweep her house, and search attentively until she finds it? Once it is found, she gathers her friends and neighbours to celebrate the recovery of her missing coin. I tell you, the angels of God likewise witness joy over a single sinner who repents.”

## 反省 | Reflection

据[李建平神父当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-11-05)摘要（乐仁出版社供稿）：自认正义的人容易把罪人排除在天主的爱之外；耶稣却看见每个人都是天主所爱的子女。天主憎恶罪，却主动寻回陷于罪中的人。我们应以基督的目光看待迷失者，也别让自以为已经够好的心态阻挡继续悔改。

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau: Self-righteousness can exclude sinners from our understanding of God's love. Jesus sees all as God's beloved children. God rejects sin while seeking those caught in it. We should regard the lost with Christ's compassion and never let satisfaction with our present goodness prevent further conversion.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

据[教宗方济各2019年9月15日三钟经讲话](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/05.html)摘要：耶稣接纳罪人、与他们同席，正是每次弥撒中发生的恩典。天主不按损益计算而放弃迷失的人；即使你尚未认识他的爱，或仍受罪恶与创伤困扰，他仍不懈寻找。你在他心中独一无二，无人可以取代。

Summary of Pope Francis's Angelus address of 15 September 2019: Jesus' welcome to sinners is renewed at every Mass. God does not calculate losses and abandon the missing person. Even when you have not discovered his love, or remain troubled by sin and suffering, he continues searching. Your place in his heart is unique and irreplaceable.
