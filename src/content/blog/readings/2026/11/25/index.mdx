常年期第三十四周（星期三） | Wednesday of the Thirty-fourth Week in Ordinary Time

2026年11月25日 · 星期三 | November 25, 2026 · Wednesday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 15:1-4 | Revelation 15:1-4**

我若望又看见天上有一个伟大而奇妙的征兆：七位天使带着最后的七种灾祸，天主的震怒将在这些灾祸中达到终结。

我又看见一片仿佛搀着火的玻璃海。那些战胜了兽、它的像和它名字所代表的数字的人，站在玻璃海旁，拿着天主的琴。他们唱着天主仆人梅瑟的歌和羔羊的歌：

上主，全能的天主，你的功行伟大奇妙。万民的君王，你的道路公正而真实。上主，谁会不敬畏你、不光荣你的名呢？唯有你是圣的。万民必来朝拜你，因为你正义的作为已经显明。

I, John, saw another great and astonishing sign in heaven: seven angels bearing the final seven plagues, in which God's wrath would reach its completion.

I saw what looked like a sea of glass mixed with fire. Those who had overcome the beast, its image, and the number of its name stood beside the glassy sea with harps from God. They sang the song of Moses, God's servant, and the song of the Lamb:

Lord God Almighty, your deeds are great and wondrous. King of the nations, your ways are just and true. Who would fail to fear you, Lord, and give glory to your name? You alone are holy. All nations will come to worship before you, for your righteous deeds have come to light.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 98:1-3,7-9 | Psalm 98:1-3b,7-9**

**答：上主，全能的天主，你的功行伟大奇妙。 | R. Lord God Almighty, your deeds are great and wondrous.**

向上主唱一首新歌，因为他行了奇事。他以右手和神圣的臂膀取得胜利。

**答：上主，全能的天主，你的功行伟大奇妙。**

上主使人认识他的救恩，在万国眼前显出他的正义。他记得向以色列家所施的仁爱和信实。

**答：上主，全能的天主，你的功行伟大奇妙。**

愿海洋和其中的一切欢呼，愿世界和居民一同应和！愿江河鼓掌，山岳齐声欢唱。

**答：上主，全能的天主，你的功行伟大奇妙。**

要在上主面前欢跃，因为他来审判大地。他将以正义审判世界，秉公判断万民。

**答：上主，全能的天主，你的功行伟大奇妙。**

**R. Lord God Almighty, your deeds are great and wondrous.**

Sing a new song to the Lord, for he has done marvelous things. His right hand and holy arm have won the victory.

**R. Lord God Almighty, your deeds are great and wondrous.**

The Lord has made known his salvation and shown his righteousness before the nations. He has remembered his steadfast love and faithfulness toward the house of Israel.

**R. Lord God Almighty, your deeds are great and wondrous.**

Let the sea and all it holds roar, and let the world and its inhabitants join in. Let the rivers clap their hands and the hills sing together for joy.

**R. Lord God Almighty, your deeds are great and wondrous.**

Rejoice before the Lord, who comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness and deal equitably with all peoples.

**R. Lord God Almighty, your deeds are great and wondrous.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 21:12-19 | Luke 21:12-19**

耶稣说，在那些事发生以前，人们会拘捕并迫害你们，把你们交给会堂，关进监狱。你们将因我的名被押到君王和总督面前，这便是你们作见证的机会。你们要拿定主意，毋须预先筹划如何申辩。我会赐给你们言语和智慧，让所有敌对的人都无法抵挡，也无法驳倒。

连父母、兄弟、亲族和朋友也会出卖你们，你们当中有些人会被杀害。众人将因我的名而憎恨你们。然而，你们头上的一根发丝也不会失落。你们要凭着坚忍，保全你们的生命。

Jesus said that before these things happened, people would seize and persecute his followers, handing them over to synagogues and prisons. Because of his name they would be brought before kings and governors, and this would become their opportunity to bear witness. They should resolve in their hearts to leave their defense unplanned. He himself would give them words and wisdom that none of their opponents could withstand or refute.

Even parents, brothers, relatives, and friends would betray them, and some would be put to death. Everyone would hate them on account of his name, yet not a hair of their heads would perish. Through endurance you will gain your lives.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父指出，忠于信仰有时会招来社会甚至家人的排斥。人也可能借神圣之名散播暴力。让圣神居住在心中的信友仍能放下恐惧，因为圣神会藉他们作证，那位数过他们每一根头发的主始终与他们同在。

Fr. Li Jianping observes that fidelity can bring rejection even from one's own family. People can invoke what is sacred to spread violence. Believers who welcome the Holy Spirit can face this without fear, trusting the Spirit to bear witness through them and the Lord who has counted every hair to stay with them.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

方济各说，坚忍是在祈祷、公义和爱德上持续行善，即使心情低落、处境艰难或无人感谢，也仍然忠于主。这样的恒心使人在逆境中扎根于良善，并让人看见天主始终如一的爱。

For Pope Francis, perseverance means continuing in prayer, justice, and charity even when enthusiasm fades, circumstances grow difficult, or gratitude is absent. This constancy keeps us rooted in goodness through adversity and makes God's faithful love visible.
