常年期第三十二主日 | Thirty-second Sunday in Ordinary Time

2026年11月8日 · 星期日 | November 8, 2026 · Sunday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**智慧篇 6:12-16 | Wisdom 6:12-16**

智慧光辉照耀，永不衰残；爱她的人容易认出她，寻找她的人能够找到她。她抢先迎接渴望她的人，让他们认识自己。清晨起来寻求她的人，不必疲于奔走，因为会发现她就在门旁等候。

用心思量智慧，便趋于圆满的明智；为了她而醒寤的人，很快就能摆脱忧虑。智慧也亲自四处寻访配得上她的人，在道路上亲切地向他们显现，在每个思念中与他们相遇。

Wisdom shines with a brightness that never fades. Those who love her can readily discern her, and those who search for her can find her. She goes ahead of those who desire her, making herself known. Whoever rises early to seek her need not exhaust himself, for she will be waiting at his doorway.

To ponder wisdom is to attain complete understanding; whoever remains awake for her will soon be released from anxiety. She herself goes out in search of those worthy of her, appearing graciously along their paths and meeting them in every thought.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 63:2-8 | Psalm 63:1-7**

**答：上主，我的天主，我的灵魂渴慕你。 | R. O God, my God, my soul thirsts for you.**

天主，你是我的天主，我殷切寻求你。我的心灵渴望你，我的身体思慕你，仿佛干旱、疲乏而无水的土地。

**答：上主，我的天主，我的灵魂渴慕你。**

我曾在圣所仰望你，瞻仰你的大能与光荣。你的慈爱胜过生命，因此我要用双唇赞美你。

**答：上主，我的天主，我的灵魂渴慕你。**

我愿终身称颂你，呼求你的名，高举双手。我的心灵如享丰盛的宴席，我要欢欣歌唱，口出赞美。

**答：上主，我的天主，我的灵魂渴慕你。**

躺在床上，我仍记念你；夜间醒着的时候，我默想你。你一直是我的援助，在你翅翼的荫庇下，我欢唱喜乐。

**答：上主，我的天主，我的灵魂渴慕你。**

God, you are my God, and I seek you eagerly. My soul longs for you; my body yearns for you like parched, weary ground without water.

**R. O God, my God, my soul thirsts for you.**

I have gazed upon you in the sanctuary, beholding your might and splendour. Your steadfast love surpasses life itself, and so my lips will praise you.

**R. O God, my God, my soul thirsts for you.**

Throughout my life I will bless you and lift my hands in your name. My soul is satisfied as at a rich banquet; with joyful lips my mouth gives praise.

**R. O God, my God, my soul thirsts for you.**

On my bed I remember you, pondering you through the watches of the night. You have been my help, and beneath the shelter of your wings I sing for joy.

**R. O God, my God, my soul thirsts for you.**

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**得撒洛尼人前书 4:13-18 | 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18**

弟兄们，关于已经安眠的人，我们愿你们有所认识，免得你们像没有希望的人那样忧伤。既然我们相信耶稣曾经死亡、又已复活，也就相信天主要借着耶稣，把那些安眠的人同他一起带来。

我们根据主的话告诉你们：我们这些存留到主来临时还活着的人，决不会在那些安眠的人以前到达。主将亲自从天而降，随着号令、总领天使的呼声和天主号角的响声，死于基督内的人要先复活。随后，仍然活着的我们，要与他们一同被接到云中，在空中迎接主。于是，我们将永远与主同在。你们应以这些话互相安慰。

Brothers and sisters, we want you to understand the situation of those who have fallen asleep, so that your grief may differ from that of people without hope. Since we believe that Jesus underwent death and rose again, we also believe that God, through Jesus, will bring the sleeping faithful with him.

On the Lord's authority we tell you this: those of us still living when he comes will have no precedence over those who have fallen asleep. The Lord himself will descend from heaven amid a commanding cry, an archangel's call, and the sound of God's trumpet. Those who died in Christ will rise first. Afterwards, we who remain alive will be taken up with them among the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. From then on we shall be with him for ever. Let these words bring comfort to one another.

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣玛窦福音 25:1-13 | Matthew 25:1-13**

那时候，耶稣向门徒讲了这个比喻：“天国可比作十位童女，提着灯出去迎接新郎。其中五位糊涂，五位明智。糊涂的带着灯，却没带备用的油；明智的除了灯，也带了盛油的器皿。新郎迟迟未到，大家都困倦睡着了。

“到了半夜，忽然有人呼喊：‘新郎到了，快出来迎接！’童女们都起来整理灯芯。糊涂的向明智的说：‘分点油给我们吧，我们的灯快熄灭了。’明智的回答：‘恐怕不够你们和我们一起用；你们还是去卖油的人那里买吧。’

“她们离开去买油的时候，新郎来了。预备妥当的就与他一同进入婚宴，门随即关上。后来，其余的童女赶到，呼喊：‘主啊，主啊，请为我们开门！’他却回答：‘我实在告诉你们，我不认识你们。’所以，你们要保持警醒，因为那一天、那一刻，你们都不知道。”

Jesus told his disciples this parable: “The kingdom of heaven may be compared to ten maidens who took their lamps and set out to welcome a bridegroom. Five were foolish and five sensible. The foolish brought their lamps without a supply of oil; the sensible carried containers of oil as well. As the bridegroom took a long time to arrive, all of them grew sleepy and dozed off.

“At midnight a shout rang out: ‘The bridegroom is here! Come and welcome him!’ The maidens all rose and put their lamps in order. Those who were foolish asked the sensible ones for some oil, because their lamps were dying. But the others replied: ‘There may not be enough for all of us. Go to the sellers and buy your own.’

“While they were away buying oil, the bridegroom arrived. Those who were ready accompanied him into the wedding celebration, and the door was closed. Later the remaining maidens came and called, ‘Lord, lord, please open the door!’ He answered solemnly that he did not know them. Therefore remain watchful, since neither the day nor the hour is known to you.”

## 反省 | Reflection

据[李建平神父当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-11-08)摘要（乐仁出版社供稿）：初期信徒热切期待基督再来，但等待的延长带来了考验。我们不知道主何时来，所能做的就是常作准备，按福音忠实地过好现在。猜测日期不如认真生活；在未知中持守信赖、希望与爱，才能成为真正警醒的天主子民。

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau: Early believers eagerly awaited Christ's return, yet its apparent delay tested them. Since its timing remains unknown, we should stay prepared by living the Gospel faithfully today. Speculating about dates matters less than a life of fidelity. Trust, hope, and love amid uncertainty form a people who are truly watchful for God.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

据[教宗本笃十六世2011年11月6日三钟经讲话](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/08.html)摘要：十童女的婚宴比喻指向天国与永生。圣奥思定等教父把灯油解释为爱：它不是可以买来的东西，而是领受、珍惜并付诸行动的恩赐。真正的智慧，是趁今生仍有机会时实行慈悲。末日审判将察看我们在世所活出的爱；基督借圣神赐下的爱，使希望如灯，引领我们穿过死亡的黑夜，赴生命的盛宴。

Summary of Pope Benedict XVI's Angelus address of 6 November 2011: The wedding feast in the parable points towards God's kingdom and eternal life. Augustine and other early Christian writers understood the oil as love: a gift to receive, preserve, and express through action, rather than something to buy. Wisdom means practising mercy during our earthly life. Judgement will concern the love we have lived. Christ's gift of love, poured into us through the Holy Spirit, sustains hope like a lamp guiding us beyond death towards life's feast.
