## 读经一 | First Reading

**费肋孟书 7-20 | Philemon 7-20**

弟兄，你的爱德使圣徒的心得到舒畅，也使我满怀喜乐与安慰。我在基督内虽然有权命你做合宜的事，却宁愿凭爱恳求你。我是年老的保禄，如今又为基督被囚；我为在锁链中所生的儿子敖乃息摩求你。他以前对你无益，如今对你我都有用。我送他回去，仿佛把自己的心送去。我本想留他在身边，代你服侍为福音被囚的我；但未经你同意，不愿擅自决定，好让你的善举出于自愿。或许他暂时离开，是为使你永远得到他，得到的不再只是奴隶，更是一位亲爱的弟兄。他为我如此，为你在肉身和主内更当如此。若你视我为伙伴，就像接纳我一样接纳他。他若亏欠你什么，算在我账上；我保禄亲笔保证偿还。何况你连自己也欠于我。弟兄，请让我在主内得到你的帮助，使我的心在基督内获得安慰。

Brother, your love has refreshed the saints and brought me joy and comfort. Though I could command what is fitting in Christ, I prefer an appeal through love. I, Paul, an old man and now Christ's prisoner, plead for Onesimus, who became my child during my imprisonment. Formerly unhelpful to you, he is now useful to us both. Sending him back is like sending my own heart. I wished to keep him to serve me on your behalf in my captivity for the gospel, but would do nothing without your consent: your goodness should be voluntary. Perhaps his brief separation enables you to receive him forever, beyond a slave, as a beloved brother, especially dear to me and still more to you, both in ordinary life and in the Lord. If you regard me as your partner, welcome him as you would me. Charge any wrong or debt to my account. I write personally that I will repay, without mentioning that you owe me your very self. Brother, let me have this benefit in the Lord; refresh my heart in Christ.

他扶助孤儿寡妇，却使恶人的图谋受挫。熙雍，你的天主世世代代掌权。

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 17:20-25 | Luke 17:20-25**

法利塞人问天主的国何时来到，耶稣回答：天主的国不以可供观察的征象来到；人不能指着说在这里或在那里，因为天主的国已在你们中间。他又告诉门徒：日子将到，你们盼望看见人子的一个日子，却不能看见。有人指称他在这里或在那里，你们不可追随。闪电从天的一端照亮另一端，人子在他的日子也要如此。但他先必须经历许多苦楚，并遭这一世代拒绝。

Asked by the Pharisees when God's kingdom would come, Jesus answered that its arrival could not be tracked by outward signs. People could not locate it by pointing here or there: God's kingdom was already in their midst. He told the disciples that days would come when they would long to see one of the Son of man's days and would not see it. They must not run after people claiming he was here or there. His day would be like lightning illuminating the sky from end to end. First, however, he must suffer greatly and be rejected by this generation.

## 反省 | Reflection

天国不能被限定在某处，也不等于奇观。人向慈爱开放、团体实践宽恕、门徒为别人承担苦难时，基督的生命就在他们中间显露。

God's kingdom cannot be confined to a location or identified with wonders. When hearts receive mercy, communities forgive, and disciples suffer for others, the life of Christ becomes visible among them.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

天国临近我们，隐藏于普通家庭的坚忍和日常的圣德中。它像谦卑的种子，因圣神的力量生长；我们应让圣神在安静、和平及关怀近人中改变自己。

The kingdom is near, hidden in ordinary families' perseverance and everyday holiness. Like a humble seed it grows through the Holy Spirit. We should allow the Spirit to change us through quietness, peace, worship, and closeness to others.

*方济各，2014年11月13日圣玛尔大弥撒讲道 | Francis, Santa Marta, November 13, 2014；撮要与中译 | Summary and Chinese translation. [Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/12.html)*
