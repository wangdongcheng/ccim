2026年11月26日 · 星期四 | November 26, 2026 · Thursday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 18:1-2,21-23; 19:1-3,9 | Revelation 18:1-2,21-23; 19:1-3,9**

我若望看见另一位天使从天降下，拥有极大的权柄。他的光荣照亮大地。他高声宣告：伟大的巴比伦陷落了！她成了邪魔的住处、不洁之神的牢笼，以及不洁而可憎的飞禽的巢穴。

一位强有力的天使举起一块像大磨盘的石头，抛入海里，说：巴比伦大城也将这样被猛力抛下，从此再也找不到。你城中再听不见弹琴、歌唱、吹笛或吹号，再找不到任何工匠，也听不见磨石转动。灯火不再照耀，新郎新娘的声音也不再响起。你的商人曾是地上的权贵，万民都被你的邪术迷惑。

此后，我听见天上仿佛有大群人高声呼喊：阿肋路亚！救恩、光荣和威能归于我们的天主。他的裁决真实而公正。他判罚了那以淫乱败坏大地的大淫妇，为他仆人流出的血伸了冤。他们再度欢呼：阿肋路亚！她的烟永远上升。

天使对我说，要写下这句话：蒙召来赴羔羊婚宴的人，真是有福。随后他告诉我，这些是天主真实的话。

I, John, saw another angel descend from heaven with great authority. His glory lit up the earth. He proclaimed loudly that mighty Babylon had fallen and become a dwelling for demons, a prison for unclean spirits, and a haunt of foul and hateful birds.

A mighty angel lifted a stone like a great millstone and cast it into the sea. Babylon, he said, would be thrown down with such force and would never be found again. Harps, singing, flutes, and trumpets would fall silent. No craftsman would remain, and no millstone would be heard. No lamp would shine, and the voices of bridegroom and bride would fall silent. Her merchants had been powerful on earth, and her sorcery had deceived the nations.

Then I heard what seemed to be a great crowd in heaven acclaiming God: Alleluia! Salvation, glory, and power belong to our God. His judgments are true and just. He has judged the great harlot who corrupted the earth and has avenged the blood of his servants. The crowd cried out again: Alleluia! Her smoke rises forever.

The angel told me to write: Blessed are those invited to the wedding feast of the Lamb. He added that these were God's true words.

怀着感恩进入他的门，唱着赞歌来到他的庭院。感谢他，赞颂他的名。

上主是良善的。他的慈爱永无止息，他的信实代代长存。

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 21:20-28 | Luke 21:20-28**

耶稣对门徒说：看见耶路撒冷被军队包围时，你们就知道它荒芜的日子近了。那时，在犹太的要逃往山中，城里的人要出去，乡间的人不要入城。这是报应的日子，经上所写的一切都要应验。那时怀孕和哺乳的妇女有祸了，因为大灾难将降临这地方，义怒将临到这百姓。他们会倒在刀剑下，或被掳往各国。耶路撒冷将被外邦人践踏，直到外邦人的时期满了。

日月星辰将出现征兆。地上的邦国因海浪的咆哮而困惑惊惶。人们想到将临于世界的事，便吓得昏厥，因为天上的威能将被震动。那时，人们会看见人子带着威能和大光荣乘云而来。这些事开始发生时，你们要挺身抬头，因为你们的救赎近了。

Jesus told his disciples that when armies surrounded Jerusalem, its devastation would be near. Those in Judea should flee to the mountains, those inside the city should leave, and those in the country should stay out of it. These would be days of vengeance, fulfilling all that Scripture had foretold. Alas for pregnant women and nursing mothers in those days, for great distress would fall on the land and wrath upon its people. Some would fall by the sword and others would be led captive among the nations. Gentiles would trample Jerusalem until their appointed times were fulfilled.

Signs would appear in the sun, moon, and stars. On earth, nations would be bewildered and distressed by the roar of the sea and its waves. People would faint in fear of what was coming on the world, for the powers of heaven would be shaken. Then they would see the Son of man coming in a cloud with power and great glory. As these things begin, stand up and raise your heads. Your redemption is drawing near.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父从耶路撒冷陷落谈起，提醒信友仰望天上的耶路撒冷，等候人子再来。无论身处何地，信友都可以挺身抬头，因为救主临近。这份盼望使人能在动荡中守护信德、望德和爱德。

Fr. Li Jianping reflects on Jerusalem's fall and invites believers to look toward the heavenly Jerusalem and await the Son of man's return. Wherever they find themselves, they can raise their heads because the Savior is near. That hope sustains faith, hope, and charity through upheaval.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

方济各指出，灾难和忧虑会让人灰心，自我封闭。耶稣邀请门徒抬起头，留心天主如何在动荡中施行救恩。即使在艰难时刻，基督的爱也会走近我们。给他留出空间，就能重新找到希望。

Pope Francis observes that disasters and worries can discourage us and turn us inward. Jesus invites his disciples to look up and stay attentive to God's saving work amid upheaval. Christ's love draws near even in difficult times. Making room for him lets us rediscover hope.
