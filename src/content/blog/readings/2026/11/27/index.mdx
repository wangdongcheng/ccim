2026年11月27日 · 星期五 | November 27, 2026 · Friday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 20:1-4,11–21:2 | Revelation 20:1-4,11–21:2**

我若望看见一位天使从天上降下，手持深渊的钥匙和一条大锁链。他抓住那条龙，就是那古蛇、魔鬼撒殚，把它捆绑了一千年。他把它抛进深渊，关上入口，加以封印，使它在这一千年内不能再迷惑万民。此后，它必须被释放片刻。

我又看见一些宝座，坐在上面的人得了审判的权柄。我还看见那些为了给耶稣作证、为了天主的圣言而被斩首者的灵魂。他们没有朝拜那兽和它的像，也没有在额上或手上接受它的记号。他们复活了，与基督一同为王一千年。

随后，我看见一座白色的大宝座和坐在上面的那位。天地从他面前退去，再无踪迹。死者无论大小，都站在宝座前。案卷被展开，另有一本生命册也被打开。死者按案卷所记录的行为接受审判。海洋交出其中的死者，死亡和阴府也交出其中的死者，每个人都按自己的行为受审。死亡和阴府被抛入火湖，这火湖就是第二次死亡。凡名字没有记在生命册上的，也被投入火湖。

我又看见新天新地，先前的天地已经消逝，海也没有了。我看见圣城新耶路撒冷从天上、从天主那里降下，仿佛一位为丈夫装扮妥当的新娘。

I, John, saw an angel descend from heaven carrying the key to the abyss and a great chain. He seized the dragon, that ancient serpent who is the Devil and Satan, and bound him for a thousand years. He cast him into the abyss, shut it, and sealed it over him, so he could no longer deceive the nations until the thousand years were over. After that he must be let loose for a short time.

I also saw thrones, and those seated on them were given authority to judge. I saw the souls of those beheaded for bearing witness to Jesus and for God's word. They had refused to worship the beast or its image and had received no mark on their foreheads or hands. They came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years.

Then I saw a great white throne and the one seated on it. Earth and sky fled from his presence and vanished. The dead, great and small, stood before the throne. Books were opened, including another book, the book of life. The dead were judged by their deeds as recorded in the books. The sea yielded its dead, and Death and Hades yielded theirs. Each was judged according to what they had done. Death and Hades were cast into the lake of fire, which is the second death. Anyone whose name was absent from the book of life was also thrown into the lake.

I saw a new heaven and a new earth. The former heaven and earth had passed away, and the sea was gone. I saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven, ready like a bride adorned for her husband.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 21:29-33 | Luke 21:29-33**

耶稣给门徒讲了一个比喻：看看无花果树和其他树木。它们一发芽，你们一看就知道夏天近了。同样，当你们看见这些事发生，就知道天主的国临近了。我实在告诉你们，这一切发生以前，这一世代不会过去。天地都要过去，我的话绝不会过去。

Jesus told his disciples a parable. Look at the fig tree and all the other trees. As soon as they put out leaves, you can see for yourselves that summer is near. In the same way, when you see these things happening, know that the kingdom of God is near. Truly, this generation will not pass away before all these things take place. Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父从树木发芽、结果到凋零的过程，看见人生与世事的变迁。留心日常的变化，也能帮助我们辨认天国临近的迹象。繁华和强盛的国度都会消逝，基督的十字架、圣言与天国却长存。

Fr. Li Jianping sees the changes of human life and history in the way trees bud, bear fruit, and wither. Paying attention to everyday changes can also help us recognize signs that God's kingdom is near. Splendor and mighty kingdoms fade, while Christ's cross, word, and kingdom endure.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

方济各指出，危机和失败让人重新衡量自己所依恋的事物。耶稣的话永存，让我们在面对丧失和死亡时信靠福音的许诺。复活的希望使我们相信，在基督内，所爱的人和以爱建立的一切都有未来，天主正为我们预备生命与喜乐。

Pope Francis explains that crises and failures can help us put our attachments in perspective. Jesus' words endure, giving us reason to trust the Gospel's promise in the face of loss and death. The hope of resurrection tells us that in Christ the people we love and what we have built in love have a future. God is preparing life and joy for us.
