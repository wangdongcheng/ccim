## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 14:1-3,4-5 | Revelation 14:1-3,4b-5**

若望看见羔羊站在熙雍山上，身旁有十四万四千人，额上写着羔羊及他父的名字。我听见天上发出的声音，如奔腾的大水，又如震耳的雷鸣；那声音也像许多琴师弹奏竖琴。

他们在宝座、四个活物与长老们面前唱起新歌。除了那从地上赎回来的十四万四千人，没有谁能学会这歌。羔羊无论到哪里去，他们都跟随他。他们从人类中被赎回，作为初熟的果实献于天主和羔羊。他们口中没有虚假，都是无瑕的人。

John saw the Lamb standing on Mount Zion, accompanied by a hundred and forty-four thousand people bearing the Lamb's name and his Father's name upon their foreheads. A sound reached me from heaven, like rushing waters and mighty thunder; it also resembled harpists playing their instruments.

Before the throne, the four living creatures, and the elders, they sang a new song. Only those hundred and forty-four thousand, redeemed from the earth, could learn it. They follow the Lamb wherever he goes. Ransomed from humanity, they are offered as first fruits to God and the Lamb. Falsehood has no place on their lips; they are without blemish.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 24:1-6 | Psalm 24:1-6**

**答：上主，这是寻求你圣容的世代。 | R. Lord, this is the generation that seeks your face.**

大地和其中的一切属于上主，世界及其居民也归于他。他使大地稳立海上，在江河之上奠定它的根基。

**答：上主，这是寻求你圣容的世代。**

谁能登上上主的山？谁能站在他的圣所？是双手清洁、内心纯正的人，不向虚妄交付自己的灵魂，也不以谎言起誓。

**答：上主，这是寻求你圣容的世代。**

这样的人将领受上主的祝福，得到拯救他的天主所赐的正义。这就是寻求他的世代，是寻求雅各伯天主圣容的人。

**答：上主，这是寻求你圣容的世代。**

**R. Lord, this is the generation that seeks your face.**

The earth and all it contains belong to the Lord, as do the world and everyone dwelling in it. He established it upon the seas and made it firm above the rivers.

**R. Lord, this is the generation that seeks your face.**

Who may climb the Lord's mountain or stand within his holy place? Those whose hands are clean and hearts are pure, who do not give their souls to falsehood or swear deceitfully.

**R. Lord, this is the generation that seeks your face.**

They will receive the Lord's blessing and righteousness from God their Savior. Such are the people who seek him, who seek the face of the God of Jacob.

**R. Lord, this is the generation that seeks your face.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 21:1-4 | Luke 21:1-4**

耶稣抬眼，看见富有的人将献仪放进圣殿的奉献箱，又看见一位贫穷的寡妇投入两个小铜钱。

他告诉众人：我确实对你们说，这贫穷寡妇所献的，比其余所有人更多。他们从富余中拿出一些作为奉献；她却在匮乏中，把赖以生活的一切全都献上了。

Looking up, Jesus watched wealthy people place their gifts in the temple treasury. He also noticed a poor widow contributing two small copper coins.

He declared that this poor widow had truly given more than all the others. Their offerings came from what they could spare; hers came from poverty, and she had offered everything she depended upon for her living.

## 反省 | Reflection

耶稣在寡妇微薄的奉献中，看见一颗完全倚靠天主的心。她以信德教导人什么是真正的敬拜。石造的圣殿会消逝，信赖上主的心却成为他居住之所。

In the widow's small offering, Jesus recognizes a heart depending entirely on God. Her faith teaches the meaning of true worship. A temple built of stone can pass away, while a trusting heart becomes God's dwelling.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

耶稣提醒我们，不可让金钱奴役神圣的事，也不可剥削贫困者。同时，他称赞寡妇毫无炫耀地奉献自己，完全信靠天主。她教我们以真诚而谦卑的爱，活出不依赖外表的信德。

Jesus warns against allowing money to dominate sacred life or exploit the poor. He also honors the widow's unassuming gift of herself and her complete trust in God. She teaches us sincere faith expressed through humble love rather than outward display.
