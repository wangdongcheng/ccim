## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 3:1-6,14-22 | Revelation 3:1-6,14-22**

主命若望写给撒尔德教会的使者：那拥有天主七神与七星的说，我知道你的行为；你有活着的名声，其实已经死了。要醒来，坚固尚存却将衰亡的一切，因为你的工作在天主面前并不完整。记起你领受并听见的，持守它，悔改吧。若不醒寤，我便像贼一样，在你不知道的时刻来到。撒尔德仍有少数人未玷污衣服，他们配穿白衣与我同行。得胜者也将穿白衣，我不会从生命册抹去他的名字，反要在父和天使面前承认他。有耳的，应听圣神向教会所说的话。

又写给劳狄刻雅教会的使者：那作为阿们、忠信真实的见证、天主创造的根源者说，我知道你既不冷也不热；宁愿你冷或热！因为你只是温吞，我要把你吐出。你自称富足、毫无缺乏，却不知道自己困苦、可怜、贫穷、瞎眼、赤裸。向我买火炼的金子，使你真正富有；买白衣遮羞，买眼药恢复视力。我责备并管教所爱的人，所以要热切悔改。我在门外叩门；听见我声音而开门的人，我要进到他那里，与他共席。得胜者将与我同坐宝座，正如我得胜，与父同坐。有耳的，应听圣神向众教会所说的话。

John was instructed to write to the angel at Sardis. The one possessing God's seven spirits and seven stars knows their deeds: their reputation is alive, but they are dead. They must wake, strengthen what remains, and remember, keep, and repent according to what they received and heard; their works are incomplete before God. Otherwise he will arrive unexpectedly like a thief. A few at Sardis have kept their garments unstained and are worthy to walk with him in white. The victor will wear white, retain a place in the book of life, and be acknowledged before the Father and angels. Whoever can hear should heed the Spirit's message to the churches.

To Laodicea, the Amen, the faithful and true witness and beginning of God's creation, says that he knows their lukewarm deeds. He wishes they were cold or hot; their tepid state will cause him to spit them out. They claim wealth and independence, unaware of their misery, poverty, blindness, and nakedness. He counsels them to obtain from him refined gold, white clothing, and eye ointment, becoming rich, clothed, and able to see. He reproves and disciplines those he loves: they must be earnest and repent. He stands outside knocking; if anyone hears and opens, he will enter and share a meal. The victor will share his throne, as he conquered and shared his Father's. Let everyone hear what the Spirit tells the churches.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 15:2-5 | Psalm 15:2-5**

**答：我们尊重敬畏天主的人。 | R. We honor those who fear God.**

谁能安居上主的帐幕？是行为正直、秉公行事、心中诚实的人。

**答：我们尊重敬畏天主的人。**

他不用舌头诽谤，不伤害近人，不羞辱邻舍；他厌弃卑劣，却尊敬畏主的人，并持守誓言，即使因此受损。

**答：我们尊重敬畏天主的人。**

他不以贷款牟取高利，也不受贿陷害无辜。这样生活的人必能站立稳固。

**答：我们尊重敬畏天主的人。**

**R. We honor those who fear God.**

Who may dwell in the Lord's tent? Those who walk blamelessly, act justly, and speak truth from the heart.

**R. We honor those who fear God.**

He neither slanders nor harms a neighbor, nor heaps disgrace on others. He rejects what is vile, honors those who fear the Lord, and keeps his oath even at a cost.

**R. We honor those who fear God.**

He does not lend for exploitative gain or accept bribes against the innocent. Whoever lives this way stands secure.

**R. We honor those who fear God.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 19:1-10 | Luke 19:1-10**

耶稣经过耶里哥。当地有位富裕的税务长匝凯，想看看耶稣，却因身矮人多而受阻，便跑到前头爬上桑树。耶稣来到树下，抬头叫他赶快下来，因为今天要住在他家。匝凯急忙下来，喜乐地接待他。众人却抱怨耶稣竟投宿罪人家中。匝凯向主表示：我要把一半财物送给穷人，若曾勒索任何人，就四倍赔偿。耶稣说：今天救恩来到这家，因为他也是亚巴郎的子孙；人子来到，为寻找并拯救迷失的人。

Passing through Jericho, Jesus encountered Zacchaeus, a wealthy chief tax collector. Short in stature, he could not see over the crowd, so ran ahead and climbed a sycamore. Jesus looked up and called him down quickly: he intended to stay at his house that day. Zacchaeus hurried down and welcomed him gladly, while the crowd complained that Jesus had lodged with a sinner. Zacchaeus promised the Lord half his possessions for the poor and fourfold repayment to anyone he had defrauded. Jesus declared that salvation had reached this household, for this man too was Abraham's son. The Son of man had come to seek and save the lost.

## 反省 | Reflection

耶稣主动接近匝凯，使他经验被接纳的喜乐。这样的爱引发慷慨的悔改：他愿意施舍、赔偿，不再倚靠财富，而在善牧的寻找中重获尊严。

Jesus takes the initiative toward Zacchaeus and gives him the joy of acceptance. This love brings generous repentance: he gives and restores, ceases to depend on wealth, and recovers dignity through being found by the shepherd.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

匝凯惯于利用权位牟利，耶稣却以慷慨的接纳触动他。他因与基督相遇而认错，主动周济穷人、加倍赔偿。慷慨能唤醒人心，使生命改变。

Zacchaeus had used authority for personal gain, but Jesus' generous welcome unsettled him. Encountering Christ led him to admit wrongdoing, assist the poor, and make abundant restitution. Generosity awakens our humanity and changes our lives.
