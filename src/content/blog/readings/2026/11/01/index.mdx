2026年11月1日 · 星期日 | November 1, 2026 · Sunday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 7:2-4,9-14 | Revelation 7:2-4,9-14**

若望看见另一位天使从东方升起，手中持有永生天主的印。他高声命令那四位获准损害陆地和海洋的天使：先不要伤害陆地、海洋或树木，要等天主的仆人在额上领受印记。若望听见领印者的数目：从以色列各支派选出的，共十四万四千人。

随后，他看见数不清的群众，来自所有邦国、支派、民族和语言；他们穿着白袍，拿着棕榈枝，侍立于宝座和羔羊面前。他们高声宣认：拯救我们的，是坐在宝座上的天主和羔羊。

众天使环绕宝座、长老和四个活物，在宝座前俯伏朝拜，把赞美、荣耀、智慧、感恩、尊崇、大能和力量，永远归于天主，并以阿们回应。

一位长老问若望，这些白衣人是谁，又从何处而来。若望回答说，长老自会知道。长老便解释：他们经历了大患难，藉着羔羊的血洗净衣袍，使衣袍洁白。

John saw an angel rising in the east, carrying the living God's seal. He called to the four angels permitted to harm earth and sea: they must spare the land, waters and trees until God's servants received the mark upon their foreheads. John heard their number: 144,000, drawn from Israel's tribes.

Next appeared an uncountable assembly from every nation, tribe, people and language. Clothed in white and carrying palms, they faced the throne and the Lamb, proclaiming that deliverance belongs to the enthroned God and to the Lamb.

The angels encircled the throne, the elders and the four living creatures. Falling down in worship, they acclaimed God with praise, glory, wisdom, gratitude, honour, might and strength for ever, affirming their worship with Amen.

An elder asked John to identify the white-robed people and their origin. John acknowledged that the elder knew. The elder explained that they had passed through the great ordeal; through the Lamb's blood they had cleansed their garments and made them white.

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**圣若望一书 3:1-3 | 1 John 3:1-3**

请体会天父赐给我们的爱是多么深厚：他让我们被称为自己的儿女，而我们确实就是他的儿女。世人不能认出我们，因为他们没有认识天主。

亲爱的诸位，我们现在已是天主的儿女，未来的光景却尚未显明。但我们确信，当主显现时，我们将与他相似，因为我们会直接看见他的真貌。凡把这样的希望寄托于他的人，都会洁净自己，以他的纯洁为准则。

Consider the extraordinary love the Father has shown: we bear the name of his children, and that is truly our condition. The world fails to recognise us because it failed to recognise him.

Dear friends, we already belong to God as his children, though our future condition remains undisclosed. We know that at his appearing we shall resemble him, because we shall behold him as he really is. All who place this hope in him cleanse themselves, taking his purity as their pattern.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 5:1-12 | Matthew 5:1-12a**

耶稣望见群众，便登上山，坐下来。门徒聚到他身旁，他开始教导他们：

“神贫的人是有福的，因为天国是他们的。”悲伤哀恸的人蒙受祝福，因为天主要安慰他们。温和谦让的人蒙受祝福，因为他们将承受大地。对正义怀有饥渴般渴望的人蒙受祝福，因为天主要使他们满足。

向别人施予怜悯的人蒙受祝福，因为他们也要领受怜悯。内心纯净的人蒙受祝福，因为他们将得见天主。致力促成和平的人蒙受祝福，因为他们将被称为天主的儿女。因坚持正义而遭迫害的人蒙受祝福，因为天国属于他们。

你们若因跟随我而受到侮辱、迫害，以及种种捏造的恶言攻击，便是蒙受祝福的。要满怀喜乐，因为在天上为你们存留着丰厚的赏报。

Seeing the crowds, Jesus climbed the hillside and sat down. His disciples gathered beside him, and he began their instruction:

“Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.” Those grieving are blessed: God will bring them consolation. The gentle are blessed: the earth will become their inheritance. Those longing for righteousness as for food and drink are blessed: their hunger will be satisfied.

Those who practise mercy are blessed: mercy will be granted them. Those inwardly pure are blessed: they will behold God. Those who work to establish peace are blessed: God will acknowledge them as his children. Those persecuted because they uphold righteousness are blessed: heaven's kingdom belongs to them.

You are blessed when your allegiance to me brings insults, persecution and every kind of slander falsely spoken against you. Be filled with gladness, because a generous reward awaits you in heaven.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父反省摘要（乐仁出版社供稿，[万有真原，2026年11月1日](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-11-01)）：圣人的生命也有软弱、过失和挣扎。他们之所以走向圣德，是因为诚实面对自己的不足，让天主改变和引导自己，把基督放在心中首位。诸圣的榜样邀请我们承认自身的有限，依靠天主，并认真回应成圣的召叫。

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau ([WanYou ZhenYuan, November 1, 2026](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-11-01)): Saints also experienced weakness, wrongdoing and struggle. Their growth in holiness came through honestly acknowledging their failings, allowing God to transform and guide them, and giving Christ first place in their hearts. Their example invites us to recognise our limitations, depend on God and respond earnestly to the call to holiness.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世讲道摘要，2025年11月1日（载于[梵蒂冈新闻网，2026年11月1日](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/01.html)）：真福八端教导我们以天国的眼光理解现实，也显明耶稣亲自走过的道路。世俗眼中难以理解的祝福，在天国中显出意义；圣人的生活让这个国度在我们中间变得可见。耶稣是卓越的导师和教育者，门徒在他的学校里学习，让他的生命照亮对一切知识的理解。

Summary of Pope Leo XIV's homily of November 1, 2025, featured by [Vatican News on November 1, 2026](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/01.html): The Beatitudes teach us to understand reality through God's kingdom and reveal the road Jesus himself followed. Blessings that seem puzzling by worldly standards become intelligible within that kingdom, which the saints make visible among us. Jesus is the supreme teacher and educator; as his pupils, we learn to let his life illuminate our understanding of every field of knowledge.
