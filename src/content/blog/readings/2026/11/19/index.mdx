## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 5:1-10 | Revelation 5:1-10**

若望看见坐在宝座上的那一位，右手持着一卷书，内外都有文字，用七个封印封住。一位强有力的天使高声询问：谁堪当揭开封印，展开书卷？然而，无论天上、地上或地下，都没有谁能展开或观看这书卷。我因找不到堪当展开、观看它的，便痛哭起来。一位长老安慰我：不要哭！看，那出自犹大支派的狮子、达味的根苗已经获胜，能够展开书卷，揭开七印。

在宝座、四个活物和长老们之间，我又看见一只站立的羔羊，带着被宰杀的痕迹。他有七角七眼；这些眼睛就是奉派往普世的天主七神。羔羊上前，从坐在宝座者的右手接过书卷。

他接过书卷时，四个活物与二十四位长老都俯伏在他面前，各持竖琴和盛香的金盂；这香象征圣徒的祈祷。他们唱起新歌，颂扬他堪当接受书卷、开启封印，因为他曾被杀，以自己的血，从各支派、语言、民族和邦国中赎回人民，归于天主；又使他们成为事奉我们天主的国度和司祭，他们将在地上为王。

John saw a scroll in the right hand of the one seated on the throne. It bore writing inside and on the back, and seven seals closed it. A powerful angel called out, asking who was worthy to break the seals and unfold it. Yet no one in heaven, on earth, or beneath the earth could open the scroll or look into it. I wept bitterly because no worthy person could be found. Then an elder reassured me: do not weep. The Lion from Judah's tribe, the Root of David, has conquered and can open the scroll and its seven seals.

Amid the throne, the four living creatures, and the elders, I saw a Lamb standing, bearing the marks of slaughter. He had seven horns and seven eyes, which are God's seven spirits dispatched throughout the earth. He approached and received the scroll from the right hand of the one enthroned.

As he received it, the four living creatures and twenty-four elders prostrated themselves before him. Each held a harp and golden bowls of incense, representing the prayers of the saints. Their new song acclaimed him as worthy to receive the scroll and undo its seals: he had been slain, and his blood had ransomed people for God from every tribe, language, people, and nation. He had formed them into a kingdom and priests serving our God, and they would reign upon the earth.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 149:1-6,9 | Psalm 149:1-6a,9b**

**答：你使我们成为国度和司祭，以事奉我们的天主。 | R. You have made us a kingdom and priests to serve our God.**

为上主唱一首新歌，在忠信子民的聚会中赞美他。愿以色列因创造自己的主而欢喜，愿熙雍的子女为他们的君王踊跃。

**答：你使我们成为国度和司祭，以事奉我们的天主。**

以舞蹈颂扬他的名，以手鼓和竖琴向他奏唱。上主喜爱他的人民，以救恩装饰谦卑的人。

**答：你使我们成为国度和司祭，以事奉我们的天主。**

愿忠信者因所得的光荣而喜乐，在床榻上也欢呼歌唱；愿他们口中高扬天主的赞颂。这是他所有忠信者的荣耀。

**答：你使我们成为国度和司祭，以事奉我们的天主。**

**R. You have made us a kingdom and priests to serve our God.**

Offer the Lord a fresh song and praise him in the assembly of his faithful. Let Israel rejoice in its Maker, and Zion's children celebrate their King.

**R. You have made us a kingdom and priests to serve our God.**

Honor his name with dancing, and make music for him with tambourine and harp. The Lord takes delight in his people, clothing the humble with salvation.

**R. You have made us a kingdom and priests to serve our God.**

Let the faithful rejoice in their glory and sing gladly even upon their beds. May exalted praise of God fill their mouths. This honor belongs to all his faithful ones.

**R. You have made us a kingdom and priests to serve our God.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 19:41-44 | Luke 19:41-44**

耶稣接近耶路撒冷，望见城邑，便为它流泪。他说：但愿你在今天也能明白什么带给你平安！如今，这一切却向你的眼睛隐藏了。

日子将到，敌人会筑起围垒，环绕你，从四面把你困住。他们要把你和你城中的子女夷平，不留一块石头叠在另一块上，因为你未能认出天主来眷顾你的时刻。

Drawing near Jerusalem, Jesus looked upon the city and wept for it. He wished that on this very day it might recognize what would bring peace; instead, those things were concealed from its sight.

Days would come when enemies would build a siege wall, encircle the city, and press it on every side. They would level it and its children within, leaving no stone resting on another, because it had failed to recognize the moment of God's visitation.

## 反省 | Reflection

耶稣深爱耶路撒冷，也看见它将遭遇的灾难，因此为它痛哭。这座以圣殿和特殊地位自豪的城，却对近在眼前的救恩无动于衷。他的眼泪揭示：外在的宗教荣耀不能代替信德与心灵的转变。

Jesus weeps because he loves Jerusalem and foresees its suffering. Proud of its temple and privileged status, the city remains unresponsive to salvation close at hand. His tears reveal that outward religious prestige cannot replace faith and a change of heart.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

耶稣曾为拉匝禄和耶路撒冷流泪。我们也该祈求发自内心流泪的恩宠，让悔改与怜悯唤醒心灵。当战争、贫穷、无家可归和种种排斥使人受苦时，不要麻木；让别人的痛苦真正触动我们。

Jesus wept for Lazarus and Jerusalem. We should ask for heartfelt tears that express repentance and compassion. War, poverty, homelessness, and the rejection of vulnerable people must not leave us indifferent. Their suffering should reach our hearts and move us to mourn.
