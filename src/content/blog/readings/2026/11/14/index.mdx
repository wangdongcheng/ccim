## 读经一 | First Reading

**若望三书 5-8 | 3 John 5-8**

亲爱的，你为弟兄，尤其素不相识的旅客所做的，都表现了忠信。他们在教会面前见证你的爱德。请以相称于天主的方式援助他们继续旅程；他们为主的名出发，没有向外教人索取供给。我们理应扶持这样的人，好与他们共同为真理效劳。

Beloved, your care for fellow believers, even those unknown to you, shows fidelity. They have testified to your love before the church. Help them onward in a manner worthy of God: they set out for his name and accept no support from unbelievers. We ought to sustain such people and thus become fellow workers for the truth.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 18:1-8 | Luke 18:1-8**

耶稣讲了一个比喻，教门徒常常祈祷，别失去勇气。某城有一位判官，不敬畏天主，也不尊重人。一位寡妇反复求他，替她向对头讨回公道。他一直拒绝，后来却想：我虽不敬天主，也不顾人，但这寡妇实在烦扰我；我得替她伸冤，免得她不停前来纠缠。主说：听听这不义判官的话！天主岂会不为日夜向他呼求的选民伸冤？难道他要迟延吗？我告诉你们，他会迅速施行正义。然而人子来到时，还能在地上找到信德吗？

Jesus gave his disciples a parable about continual prayer and courage. In a city lived a judge who respected neither God nor people. A widow kept asking him for justice against her opponent. He refused for a time, but finally reasoned that, though he feared neither God nor people, he would grant her justice because her persistence troubled him. The Lord told them to consider the unjust judge's words. Would God fail to vindicate his chosen ones who call to him day and night? Would he delay over them? He would act swiftly for them. Yet when the Son of man came, would he find faith on earth?

## 反省 | Reflection

恒心祈祷不是提醒一个冷漠的天主，而是培育信德，使人内心坚强。信靠公正的天主，能帮助我们面对不义，为自己和受压迫的人发声。

Persistent prayer does not remind an indifferent God of his duty. It forms faith and strengthens the heart. Trusting God's justice enables us to confront injustice and speak for ourselves and for the oppressed.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

祈祷维持灵魂的生命，如同呼吸维持肉身。基督在十字架上与受苦者同在，显露天主以宽恕救赎邪恶的正义。我们的呼求和眼泪，都没有被他遗忘。

Prayer sustains the soul as breathing sustains the body. On the cross Christ stands with the suffering and reveals God's justice, which redeems evil through forgiveness. No cry or tear lies beyond his compassionate attention.
