追思已亡诸信者 | The Commemoration of All the Faithful Departed (All Souls)

2026年11月2日 · 星期一 | November 2, 2026 · Monday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**圣保禄宗徒致罗马人书 6:3-9 | Romans 6:3-9**

弟兄们：领洗归于基督耶稣，就是进入他的死亡。我们借洗礼与他同葬，为能如同他藉父的光荣复活那样，开始新的生活。既与他的死亡结合，也必分享他的复活。

我们的旧人与基督同钉十字架，使受罪支配的生命失去权势，我们便不再受罪奴役；死去的人已脱离罪的辖制。既与基督同死，就信赖将与他一起生活。他已复活，永不再死，死亡再不能掌管他。

Brothers and sisters, baptism into Jesus Christ joins us to his death. In baptism we share his burial, so that the Father's glory, which raised him, may also lead us into a new way of living. Union with his death promises union with his resurrection.

Our former self was nailed to the cross with him, breaking the power of our sinful existence and releasing us from slavery to sin. Death brings freedom from that bondage. Having shared Christ's death, we trust that we shall share his life. The risen Christ can never die again: death has lost its authority over him.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 25（所选经句：6-7,17-18,20-21） | Psalm 25:6-7,17-18,20-21**

**答：上主，凡期望你的人决不致失望。 | R. Lord, those who hope in you will not be put to shame.**

上主，求你顾念自古以来的怜悯与慈爱；不要记住我年少的罪过，求按你的仁爱，因你的美善而眷顾我。

**答：上主，凡期望你的人决不致失望。**

求你解开我内心的忧结，救我脱离困境；垂顾我的劳苦与悲伤，宽赦我的一切罪过。

**答：上主，凡期望你的人决不致失望。**

求你护佑我的生命，拯救我；我投奔你，求别使我受辱。愿纯洁与正直保护我，因为我仰望你。

**答：上主，凡期望你的人决不致失望。**

Lord, remember your enduring compassion and love. Set aside the offences of my youth; in your goodness, look upon me with mercy.

**R. Lord, those who hope in you will not be put to shame.**

Ease my heart's distress and lead me out of suffering. See my hardship and pain, and pardon all my sins.

**R. Lord, those who hope in you will not be put to shame.**

Protect and rescue me. I seek shelter in you; do not let me suffer disgrace. Let honesty and uprightness keep me safe as I wait for you.

**R. Lord, those who hope in you will not be put to shame.**

阿肋路亚。主说：派遣我来的父愿意，我不失去他托付给我的任何一个，并在末日使他们复活。阿肋路亚。

Alleluia. The Lord says: My Father's purpose is that none of those entrusted to me be lost, but that I raise them on the last day. Alleluia.

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣玛窦福音 25:31-46 | Matthew 25:31-46**

耶稣告诉门徒：人子将带着众天使，在光荣中来临，登上王位。万民聚在他面前，他要如牧人分开羊群一般，区分众人：绵羊在右，山羊在左。

君王邀请右边的人承受创世之初已为他们预备的国度，因为他们在他饥饿、口渴、流落、缺衣、生病和坐监时，曾供应、接待、照顾和探望他。义人惊讶地问：什么时候曾见到主这样困乏，并服侍过他？君王解释，他们为他最卑微的弟兄所做的，就是做在他身上。

他命令左边的人离开，进入为魔鬼及其使者预备的永火，因为他遭遇同样的困苦时，他们没有援手。他们也问：什么时候曾见主困乏而没有服侍？君王指出，对最微小者拒绝施援，就是拒绝他。这些人走向永罚，义人却进入永生。

Jesus told his disciples that the Son of man would arrive in glory with his angels and take his royal seat. Gathering every nation, he would divide the people as a shepherd sorts sheep from goats, placing them on opposite sides.

The King invites those on his right into the kingdom prepared since creation. They fed him, quenched his thirst, welcomed him, clothed him, and visited him in sickness and imprisonment. Astonished, the righteous ask when they ever saw him in such need. He explains that their care for his humblest brothers was care for him.

Those on his left must depart into the everlasting fire intended for the devil and his angels. They failed to provide food, water, welcome, clothing, or visits when he needed them. They too ask when they neglected him. He answers that withholding help from the least was withholding it from him. Their destination is eternal punishment; the righteous enter everlasting life.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父反省摘要：天上的诸圣、净化中的亡者和仍在世上旅途中的信友，在基督内彼此共融。炼灵渴望摆脱生命留下的亏欠与污秽，接受圣神的净化，得以清晰瞻仰天主。追思亡者时，我们怀着希望，期待基督唤起他们进入新生。

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection: The saints in heaven, the departed undergoing purification, and believers still on their earthly pilgrimage share communion in Christ. Those being purified long to leave behind their failings and receive the Spirit's cleansing, so that they may behold God clearly. Remembering them, we hope for Christ's call to new life.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世反省摘要（2025年11月2日三钟经）：耶稣的复活照亮我们的归宿；天主愿每个人都得以保存，并显出独有的美善。教会在感恩祭中纪念亡者，怀着复活的盼望走进墓园。基督的声音引领我们朝向永生，使追思超越对往昔的眷恋。

Summary of Pope Leo XIV's Angelus of November 2, 2025: Jesus' resurrection illuminates our destiny. God desires each person to be preserved and to flourish in their own beauty. Remembering the dead at the Eucharist and visiting their graves express hope in resurrection. Christ calls us towards eternal life, carrying remembrance beyond attachment to the past.
