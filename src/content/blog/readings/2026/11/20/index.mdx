## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 10:8-11 | Revelation 10:8-11**

若望又听见先前从天上发出的声音，叫他前去，取过那位一脚踏海、一脚踏地的天使手中展开的书卷。我走到天使那里，请他把小书卷给我。他吩咐我拿去吃下，并告诉我：它在口中将甘甜如蜜，却会使腹中苦涩。

我便从天使手中接过小书卷，吃了下去。口中的滋味确实像蜜一样甜，吞下之后，腹中却感到苦涩。随后，他们告诉我：你还必须就许多民族、邦国、语言和君王，再度宣讲预言。

John again heard the heavenly voice that had spoken earlier. It directed him to take the opened scroll from the angel whose feet stood upon sea and land. I approached the angel and asked for the little scroll. He told me to take and eat it: it would taste like honey in my mouth, yet bring bitterness to my stomach.

I received the little scroll from his hand and ate it. Its taste was indeed honey-sweet, but after I swallowed it my stomach grew bitter. Then I was told that I must prophesy once more concerning many peoples, nations, languages, and kings.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 119:14,24,72,103,111,131 | Psalm 119:14,24,72,103,111,131**

**答：上主，你的教言是多么甜蜜。 | R. Lord, how sweet are your words.**

遵循你的训诲使我欢欣，如同拥有丰厚财富。你的典章带给我喜乐，也成为引导我的谋士。

**答：上主，你的教言是多么甜蜜。**

你口中所出的法律，为我远胜万千金银。你的教言在我口中比蜜更甘甜。

**答：上主，你的教言是多么甜蜜。**

你的训诲是我永远承受的产业，是我心里的欢悦。我张口渴求，如同喘息，因为切慕你的诫命。

**答：上主，你的教言是多么甜蜜。**

**R. Lord, how sweet are your words.**

Following your testimonies gives me joy like the possession of great riches. Your decrees delight me and guide me as counselors.

**R. Lord, how sweet are your words.**

The law spoken by you is worth more to me than thousands of gold and silver pieces. Your words are sweeter than honey to my mouth.

**R. Lord, how sweet are your words.**

Your testimonies are my enduring inheritance and my heart's delight. I open my mouth and pant with longing for your commandments.

**R. Lord, how sweet are your words.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 19:45-48 | Luke 19:45-48**

耶稣进入圣殿，开始把售卖物品的人赶出去。他指出，经上说这殿应是祈祷的居所，他们却使它成了盗贼藏身的地方。

他每天留在圣殿教导。司祭长、经师和民间首领设法要除掉他，却找不到下手的机会，因为全体民众都专心聆听他的话，不愿错过。

Jesus entered the temple and began expelling the sellers. He reminded them that Scripture designated his house for prayer, whereas they had turned it into a refuge for robbers.

He taught in the temple each day. The chief priests, scribes, and leading figures among the people sought to destroy him, but could find no means of doing so: the entire crowd listened intently, hanging on his teaching.

## 反省 | Reflection

圣殿原为祈祷与祭献之地，却被牟利的心态侵占。耶稣为光荣父而行动，即使因此面对死亡也不退缩。我们的心同样是圣神居住的殿宇；需要省察其中是否仍有真正的祈祷，并勇于清除隐藏的污垢。

The temple was intended for prayer and sacrifice, but profit had displaced its purpose. Jesus acted for his Father's glory despite the danger to his life. Our hearts too are temples of the Spirit. We should examine whether prayer truly dwells there and have the courage to clear away concealed wrongdoing.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

圣殿的遭遇也警醒教会：依恋金钱和权势，会使人背离对耶稣的忠信，并陷入腐败。耶稣的力量来自他的话、见证与爱。让他居于中心，就必须舍弃世俗化，专心事奉天主。

The temple warns the Church about attachment to money and power, which undermines fidelity to Jesus and breeds corruption. His strength comes through his word, witness, and love. Keeping him at the center requires renouncing worldliness and choosing to serve God wholeheartedly.
