## 读经一 | First Reading

**弟铎书 2:1-8,11-14 | Titus 2:1-8,11-14**

你所教导的，应与健全的信仰相称。劝年长男子节制、庄重、审慎，在信德、爱德与坚忍上保持健全。年长妇女也要举止虔敬，不诽谤，不酗酒，教导美善，帮助年轻妇女爱护丈夫和子女，谨慎、贞洁，善理家务，服从丈夫，使天主的圣言不受毁谤。也劝青年男子自守。你自己在善行上作榜样，教导纯正而庄严，言语健全，无可指摘，使反对者无从诋毁，反觉羞愧。

天主拯救众人的恩宠已经显现，训练我们舍弃不敬虔与世俗欲望，在今世节制、公正、虔诚地生活，等候有福的盼望，等候我们伟大的天主和救主耶稣基督光荣显现。他为我们交付自己，使我们脱离不法，洁净我们，成为属于他、热心行善的子民。

Teach what accords with sound doctrine. Older men should be sober, dignified, prudent, and steadfast in faith, love, and endurance. Older women should live reverently, avoiding slander and enslavement to wine, and teach goodness. They should guide younger women to love their husbands and children, exercise self-control and purity, care for their homes, and be subject to their husbands, so that God's word is not dishonored. Encourage young men to be self-controlled. Make your own good deeds an example; teach with integrity and dignity, using wholesome speech that leaves opponents ashamed, with nothing evil to say.

God's saving grace has appeared for everyone. It teaches us to renounce irreligion and worldly cravings and to live soberly, uprightly, and devoutly now, while awaiting our blessed hope, the glorious appearing of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ. He gave himself to free us from lawlessness and purify a people belonging to him, eager for good works.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 17:7-10 | Luke 17:7-10**

耶稣对门徒说：谁有仆人耕地或牧羊回来，就立刻请他坐下吃饭呢？岂不是先叫他预备晚餐，束好衣服，伺候主人吃喝，然后自己才吃喝吗？仆人执行了命令，主人还须因此感谢他吗？你们也一样，完成所受的一切吩咐以后，仍该承认：我们是无用的仆人，不过尽了应尽的本分。

Jesus asked his disciples whether a master would invite a servant returning from plowing or shepherding to sit down immediately. Would he not first tell the servant to prepare supper, dress for service, and attend him while he ate and drank, before eating himself? Does carrying out an order place the master in the servant's debt? So, after fulfilling everything commanded, acknowledge that you are unworthy servants who have simply carried out your duty.

## 反省 | Reflection

真正的谦逊承认：人的功劳不能增添天主的美善，也不能换取救恩。天主使人自由；门徒应放下支配他人的欲望，以服侍弱小者回应他的爱。

True humility recognizes that our achievements neither increase God's goodness nor purchase salvation. God sets people free. Disciples respond to his love by relinquishing domination and serving those who are vulnerable.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

我们从天主领受一切，因此不能把服务当作向他索取报酬的资本。天天接纳并实践他的旨意，承认自己只是尽本分，能使心灵向他的慷慨敞开。

Everything comes to us as God's gift, so our service never makes him our debtor. Receiving and doing his will each day, without claiming special payment, is the humility that opens us to his generosity.

*本笃十六世，2010年10月3日讲道 | Benedict XVI, Homily, October 3, 2010；撮要与中译 | Summary and Chinese translation. [Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/10.html)*
