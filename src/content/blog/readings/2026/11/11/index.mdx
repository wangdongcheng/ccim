## 读经一 | First Reading

**弟铎书 3:1-7 | Titus 3:1-7**

提醒众人尊重执政者，服从合法权柄，准备实行各样善事；不要辱骂或争斗，却要温厚，向所有人显出谦和。我们从前也曾愚昧、悖逆、迷失，受欲望和享乐奴役，在恶意、嫉妒与彼此仇视中度日。然而，救主天主的良善与爱人之情显现了。他救我们，凭的是他的怜悯，并非我们所行的义事。他藉重生的洗涤与圣神的更新拯救我们，又藉我们的救主耶稣基督，把圣神丰厚地倾注在我们身上。这样，我们因恩宠成义，按照所怀的希望，成为永生的继承人。

Remind everyone to submit to rulers and authorities, obey, and be ready for every good work. They should speak evil of no one, avoid quarrels, and show gentleness and courtesy to all. We too were once foolish, disobedient, and astray, enslaved by desires and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful and hating one another. Then the goodness and loving kindness of God our Savior appeared. He saved us through mercy, rather than because of righteous deeds we had performed, through the washing of rebirth and renewal by the Holy Spirit. He poured out the Spirit richly through Jesus Christ our Savior, so that, justified by grace, we might inherit eternal life according to our hope.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

你在敌人面前为我设宴，以油傅我的头，使我的杯满溢。

**答：上主是我的牧者，我实在一无所缺。**

你的慈爱与恩惠终身伴随我，我愿长居上主的家中。

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 17:11-19 | Luke 17:11-19**

耶稣往耶路撒冷途中，经过撒玛黎雅与加里肋亚之间。他进入一个村庄时，十个癞病人远远站着呼喊，求耶稣师傅怜悯。耶稣看见他们，便叫他们去让司祭检验。他们上路以后，就得到洁净。其中一人看见自己痊愈，便大声光荣天主，回来俯伏在耶稣脚前感谢他；他是撒玛黎雅人。耶稣问：不是十个人洁净了吗？另外九人在哪里？竟只有这外邦人回来归光荣于天主吗？于是他对那人说：起来，去吧，你的信德救了你。

On the way to Jerusalem, Jesus traveled between Samaria and Galilee. At a village ten men with leprosy stood at a distance, calling to Jesus their Master for mercy. Seeing them, he told them to present themselves to the priests. They became clean as they went. One, realizing he was healed, returned praising God loudly and fell at Jesus' feet in thanksgiving. He was a Samaritan. Jesus asked whether ten had been cleansed, and where the other nine were. Had only this foreigner returned to honor God? He then told the man to rise and go: his faith had brought him salvation.

## 反省 | Reflection

撒玛黎雅人以感谢回应医治，因而与耶稣建立更深的关系。表达感恩使恩赐的经验走向圆满，也邀请我们把整个生命活成持续的感恩祭。

The Samaritan responds to healing with gratitude and enters a deeper relationship with Jesus. Expressing thanks brings the experience of receiving to fulfillment and invites us to make our whole life an enduring thanksgiving.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

痊愈的人容易专注所得的恩惠，却忘记赐恩者。回来感谢的撒玛黎雅人在耶稣内认出救主。我们既会向主祈求，也应学习回到他面前赞美感恩。

Those who are healed can become absorbed in the gift and forget its giver. By returning gratefully, the Samaritan recognizes Jesus as the one who saves. We should learn to return in praise as readily as we approach him with requests.
