2026年11月7日 · 星期六 | November 7, 2026 · Saturday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**斐理伯人书 4:10-19 | Philippians 4:10-19**

弟兄们，你们对我的关怀如今又有机会表现出来，使我在主内十分喜乐。你们原本就关心我，只是一直缺少机会。我这样说，并非由于生活欠缺，因为我已学会在各种处境中知足。我既懂得怎样度贫困的生活，也懂得怎样面对丰裕；无论饱足或饥饿、有余或不足，我都学到了其中的秘诀。靠着赐我力量的那一位，我能承担这一切。不过，你们愿意分担我的患难，确实做得很好。

斐理伯人，你们也知道，福音初传、我离开马其顿的时候，在收支上与我共同承担的教会，只有你们。甚至我还在得撒洛尼时，你们就不止一次送来我所需要的。我所寻求的，并不是礼物，而是那不断增加、记在你们账上的果实。

如今，我已收到一切，而且绰有余裕。厄帕洛狄托带来你们的馈赠，使我充足无缺；这馈赠如同馨香的祭献，是天主所悦纳的。我的天主必按他在基督耶稣内光荣的丰盛，补足你们所有的需要。

Brothers and sisters, I am deeply glad in the Lord that your concern for me has once again found expression. You cared all along, but had no opportunity to show it. I do not say this out of want, since I have learnt contentment whatever my circumstances. I know poverty and abundance alike; whether fed or hungry, well supplied or in need, I have learnt how to meet each situation. Through the one who gives me strength, I can face them all. Nevertheless, you did well to share my hardship.

You Philippians know that when the Gospel was first preached and I departed from Macedonia, yours was the only church to enter into partnership with me in giving and receiving. Even while I was in Thessalonica, you sent help for my needs more than once. What I seek is not the gift itself, but the fruit that increases to your credit.

I have now received everything and have more than enough. The gifts delivered by Epaphroditus have supplied me fully; they are like a sweetly fragrant offering, a sacrifice welcomed by God. In Christ Jesus, my God will meet every need of yours from the riches of his glory.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 112:1-2,5-6,8-9 | Psalm 112:1-2,5-6,8-9**

**答：敬畏上主的人真有福气。 | R. Blessed is the man who fears the Lord.**

敬畏上主、衷心喜爱他诫命的人多么有福！他的子孙将在地上兴盛，正直人的后代必蒙降福。

**答：敬畏上主的人真有福气。**

慷慨出借、秉公处理事务的人必享幸福。义人必能站立稳固，他的名将长存于人的记忆中。

**答：敬畏上主的人真有福气。**

他内心坚定，不再恐惧，终将看见仇敌败退。他广施财物，扶助贫困；他的正义长存，他的力量必在光荣中高举。

**答：敬畏上主的人真有福气。**

Happy is the person who reveres the Lord and takes great delight in his commands. His descendants will be strong in the land; the upright generation will receive blessing.

**R. Blessed is the man who fears the Lord.**

Good comes to the generous lender who conducts his affairs with justice. The righteous person will stand secure and be remembered for ever.

**R. Blessed is the man who fears the Lord.**

With a steadfast heart he has no fear, until he sees his adversaries overcome. He distributes his goods to the poor; his righteousness endures, and his strength is lifted high in honour.

**R. Blessed is the man who fears the Lord.**

**格林多人后书 8:9 | 2 Corinthians 8:9**

阿肋路亚。耶稣基督原是富有的，却为你们甘愿贫穷，使你们借着他的贫穷得到富足。阿肋路亚。

Alleluia. Though Jesus Christ possessed riches, he accepted poverty for your sake, so that through his poverty you might become rich. Alleluia.

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣路加福音 16:9-15 | Luke 16:9-15**

那时候，耶稣对门徒说：“我告诉你们，要善用这不义世界的钱财去结交朋友，好使钱财失效时，他们接待你们进入永恒的居所。在微小的事上可靠的人，在重大的事上也可靠；在微小的事上不诚实的人，在重大的事上也不诚实。如果你们连不义的钱财也管理得不忠信，谁会把真正的财富交托你们？如果你们对别人的东西不忠信，谁又会把属于你们的给你们？

“一个仆人无法服事两个主人：他会厌弃一个、爱另一个，或忠于一个、轻看另一个。你们不能把天主和钱财同时当作主人。”

爱钱的法利塞人听了这一切，便讥笑他。耶稣对他们说：“你们在人面前显出自己正义，天主却洞悉你们的心。人所推崇的事，在天主面前可能正是可憎的。”

Jesus said to his disciples: “Use the wealth of this unrighteous world to win friends, so that when it fails they may welcome you into dwellings that last for ever. Whoever proves trustworthy over something small will also prove trustworthy over much; dishonesty in small matters means dishonesty in great ones too. If you have not been faithful in handling unrighteous wealth, who will entrust you with true riches? If you have failed to care faithfully for another's property, who will give you what is your own?

“A servant cannot belong to two masters. He will reject one and love the other, or hold fast to one and scorn the other. You cannot make both God and money your master.”

The Pharisees, who were attached to money, heard this and ridiculed him. He told them: “You present yourselves as righteous before others, but God sees into your hearts. What people hold in high regard is detestable before God.”

## 反省 | Reflection

据[李建平神父当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-11-07)摘要（乐仁出版社供稿）：我们怎样使用金钱，显露了我们与天主的关系。钱财比起永生虽是小事，却考验人的忠信。法利塞人的问题，是外表敬虔而内心贪财；真正的虔诚也应落实在日常财物的运用上。善用钱财、服务他人，才能以生活表明自己事奉天主。

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau: Our handling of money reveals our relationship with God. Wealth is small beside eternal life, yet it tests our faithfulness. The Pharisees combined outward devotion with inward greed. Authentic devotion must also shape everyday financial choices. By putting possessions to good use in service of others, we show whom we truly serve.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

据[教宗良十四世2025年9月21日弥撒讲道](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/07.html)摘要：耶稣要求我们在天主与财富之间作出明确的生命选择。若把安全寄托于积蓄，便容易以算计、囤积和猜疑回应匮乏，忘记信赖与分享。天主赐下受造界的资源，是为了所有的人。由内心开始的皈依，使人张开双手施予；事奉天主的人能摆脱财富的奴役，以正义使财富成为共同的福祉。

Summary of Pope Leo XIV's homily of 21 September 2025: Jesus calls for a decisive choice about where our hearts belong. Treating wealth as our security can turn need into calculation, hoarding, and suspicion, instead of trust and mutual help. Creation's gifts are intended for everyone. Conversion begins within and opens our hands to give. Serving God brings freedom from money's domination and directs wealth towards the good of all.
