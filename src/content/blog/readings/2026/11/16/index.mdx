## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 1:1-4;2:1-5 | Revelation 1:1-4;2:1-5a**

这是天主交给耶稣基督的启示，为向仆人指明即将发生的事。他派天使传给若望，若望便为天主的话、耶稣基督的证言及自己所见的一切作证。诵读的人，以及聆听并遵行这预言的人，都是有福的，因为时期近了。若望致书亚细亚的七个教会：愿恩宠与平安，从今在、昔在、将来永在者，并从他宝座前的七神，临于你们。

主又命我写给厄弗所教会的使者：那握着七星、行于七盏金灯台之间的说，我知道你的工作、劳苦与坚忍；你不容忍恶人，试验那些假称宗徒的人，揭露他们的虚假。你为我的名忍受困难，未曾倦怠。但我要责备你：你离弃了起初的爱。回想你从何处跌落，悔改，重新做起初所做的事。

This is the revelation God gave Jesus Christ to show his servants what must soon occur. He communicated it through an angel to John, who testified to God's word, Jesus Christ's witness, and everything he saw. Blessed are the reader and those who hear and keep this prophecy, for the time is near. John greets the seven churches in Asia with grace and peace from the one who is, was, and is to come, and from the seven spirits before his throne.

To the angel of the church in Ephesus, the one holding seven stars and walking among seven golden lampstands says: I know your labor and endurance. You reject evil and have tested false apostles, exposing their lies. You have persevered for my name without growing weary. Yet you have abandoned your first love. Remember where you fell, repent, and return to your earlier deeds.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 18:35-43 | Luke 18:35-43**

耶稣快到耶里哥时，一个盲人坐在路边乞讨。他听见人群经过，便询问缘由，得知纳匝肋人耶稣经过，就呼喊达味之子耶稣怜悯他。前面的人斥责他，要他安静，他却更加呼求。耶稣停下来，叫人带他来，问他愿意得到什么。他求主使自己看见。耶稣说：看见吧，你的信德救了你。他立即恢复视力，跟随耶稣，光荣天主；群众见了，也一同赞美天主。

Near Jericho a blind man sat begging by the road. Hearing a crowd, he asked what was happening. Told that Jesus of Nazareth was passing, he cried to Jesus, Son of David, for mercy. Those in front rebuked him, but he called out still more insistently. Jesus stopped and ordered him brought forward, asking what he wanted. He asked the Lord for sight. Jesus granted it, saying that his faith had saved him. Immediately he could see and followed Jesus, glorifying God. The people who witnessed it also praised God.

## 反省 | Reflection

无视残障者的痛苦，也是一种看不见。耶稣在被排斥的人面前停下；愿意亲近十字架的门徒，也应停下来倾听、交谈和帮助，并从他们身上学习。

Ignoring disabled people's suffering is another kind of blindness. Jesus stops for those pushed aside. Disciples who approach the cross should likewise pause, listen, converse, and help, allowing the excluded to teach them.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

耶稣把路旁的盲人带到众人关注的中心，使宣讲福音的人也必须接纳受排斥者。主经过我们的生命，是慈悲的相遇，邀请我们亲近他，并认出需要安慰的人。

Jesus brings the roadside beggar into everyone's attention, requiring those announcing good news to include the excluded. His passage through our lives is an encounter with mercy, inviting us closer to him and toward people needing consolation.
