圣陈安勇乐司铎及同伴殉道（纪念） | Memorial of Saint Andrew Dung-Lac, Priest, and Companions, Martyrs

2026年11月24日 · 星期二 | November 24, 2026 · Tuesday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**默示录 14:14-19 | Revelation 14:14-19**

我若望看见一片白云，云上坐着一位相似人子的，头戴金冠，手持锋利的镰刀。另一位天使从圣殿出来，高声向云上那位呼喊，要他挥镰收割，因为时候已到，地上的庄稼已经成熟。坐在云上的那位便向地上挥镰，大地的庄稼就被收割了。

另一位天使也拿着锋利的镰刀，从天上的圣殿走出来。接着，掌管火的天使从祭坛出来，高声叫那持镰的天使收取地上葡萄树的果实，因为葡萄已经熟了。那天使向地上挥镰，收下葡萄，投入天主震怒的大榨酒槽。

I, John, saw a white cloud with someone like a son of man seated on it. He wore a golden crown and held a sharp sickle. Another angel emerged from the temple and called loudly to him to swing his sickle, for the harvest hour had arrived and the earth's crop was ripe. The one on the cloud swept his sickle over the earth, and its crop was gathered.

Another angel came out of the heavenly temple carrying a sharp sickle. Then the angel who had authority over fire came from the altar and called loudly to the angel with the sickle, telling him to gather the clusters from the earth's vines, for the grapes were ripe. He swept his sickle over the earth, gathered the grapes, and cast them into the great winepress of God's wrath.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 96:10-13 | Psalm 96:10-13**

**答：上主已经驾临，审判乾坤。 | R. The Lord comes to judge the earth.**

要向列邦宣告：上主为王！他使世界稳固，永不动摇。他将秉公判断万民。

**答：上主已经驾临，审判乾坤。**

愿诸天欢喜，大地踊跃，海洋和其中的一切发出欢声。愿田野和其中的一切欢腾，林中所有的树木也要欢唱。

**答：上主已经驾临，审判乾坤。**

他们要在上主面前欢唱，因为他来了，要审判大地。他将以正义审判世界，以信实治理万民。

**答：上主已经驾临，审判乾坤。**

**R. The Lord comes to judge the earth.**

Tell the nations that the Lord reigns. He has secured the world so that it cannot be shaken. He will judge the peoples with equity.

**R. The Lord comes to judge the earth.**

Let heaven rejoice and earth be glad. Let the sea roar with all it holds. Let the fields and everything in them exult, and all the trees of the forest sing for joy.

**R. The Lord comes to judge the earth.**

Let them sing before the Lord, for he is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness and the peoples with faithfulness.

**R. The Lord comes to judge the earth.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 21:5-11 | Luke 21:5-11**

有人谈论圣殿，赞叹装饰它的精美石料和还愿献礼。耶稣说，他们所看见的一切，日后都要被拆毁，连一块石头也不会留在另一块上。他们问老师，这些事何时发生，临近的时候有什么征兆。

耶稣提醒他们谨防受骗：许多人会冒用他的名字，自称是那一位，又声称时刻已经到了。不要跟随他们。当你们听见战争和叛乱，不要惊惶。这些事必须先发生，终局却不会马上来到。他又说，民族将起来攻击民族，国家将起来攻击国家。各地会有大地震、饥荒和瘟疫，也会出现可怕的景象和来自天上的大征兆。

Some people spoke admiringly of the fine stones and votive gifts adorning the temple. Jesus told them that the time would come when everything they saw would be demolished, with no stone left resting on another. They asked their teacher when this would happen and what sign would announce its approach.

Jesus warned them to guard against deception. Many would come in his name, claiming to be the one and declaring that the time had arrived. They were to stay away from such people. When you hear of wars and rebellions, do not be alarmed. These things must happen first, but the end will not come immediately. He went on to say that nation would rise against nation and kingdom against kingdom. Great earthquakes, famines, and plagues would strike in various places, and there would be fearful sights and great signs from heaven.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父提醒我们，有人借战争和灾难制造末日恐慌，断章取义地引用福音，诱使信友追随自称基督的人。面对这类宣称，门徒要保持警醒，谨守耶稣的劝告。基督的再来将是万民都能看见的光荣事件。

Fr. Li Jianping warns that some exploit wars and disasters to stir up fear about the end times, quoting the Gospel out of context to persuade believers to follow people who claim to be Christ. Disciples should remain alert and heed Jesus' counsel. Christ's return will be a glorious event visible to all peoples.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

良十四世指出，面对冲突、灾难和迫害，耶稣劝我们别让恐惧占据心灵，也别对受苦的人漠不关心。黑暗中，信德为人照亮希望。历史中的苦难终会过去，认出耶稣是救主的人将拥有长存的喜乐。

Leo XIV points to Jesus' invitation to resist fear amid conflict, disaster, and persecution, and to remain attentive to those who suffer. In dark times, faith keeps hope alive. The suffering of history will pass, while the joy of those who recognize Jesus as Savior will endure.
