## 读经一 | First Reading

**箴言 31:10-13,19-20,30-31 | Proverbs 31:10-13,19-20,30-31**

谁能寻得贤能的妻子？她的价值超过珍宝。丈夫心中信赖她，不会缺乏利益；她终身给他带来益处，而非损害。她寻找羊毛与麻，甘心用双手工作。她伸手拿纺锤，手指握住纺杆；也向穷苦者张开手，向有需要者伸出援手。妩媚会骗人，美貌终会消逝；敬畏上主的妇女才当受赞扬。让她享有劳作的成果，让她的作为在城门口为她赢得称誉。

Who can find a capable wife? Her worth surpasses jewels. Her husband entrusts his heart to her and lacks no gain. Throughout her life she brings him benefit rather than harm. She seeks wool and flax and works willingly with her hands. She handles the distaff and spindle, yet opens her hand to the poor and reaches out to the needy. Charm can deceive and beauty passes away; a woman who reveres the Lord deserves praise. Give her the fruit of her labor, and let her deeds commend her at the city gates.

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**得撒洛尼人前书 5:1-6 | 1 Thessalonians 5:1-6**

弟兄们，关于时日和期限，不必再给你们写信。你们清楚知道，主的日子会像夜间的贼来到。人说平安稳妥时，毁灭便突然临到，如产痛临到孕妇，无法逃避。但你们不在黑暗中，那日子不该像贼一样使你们措手不及；你们全是光明与白昼的子女，不属于黑夜与幽暗。因此，不要像别人那样沉睡，要醒寤自守。

Brothers and sisters, you need no letter about times and seasons. You know that the Lord's day arrives unexpectedly, as a thief comes at night. When people speak of peace and security, destruction overtakes them suddenly, like labor pains, without escape. But you do not belong to darkness, for that day to catch you unaware. You are children of light and daylight, not of night or darkness. Therefore, instead of sleeping as others do, remain awake and sober.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 25:14-30 | Matthew 25:14-30**

耶稣说：有个人临行远方，把仆人召来，将产业托付他们，按各人能力，一个五个塔冷通，一个两个，一个一个，然后出发。领五个的立即经营，又赚五个；领两个的也赚了两个；领一个的却挖地把主人的钱埋藏起来。

过了许久，主人回来结账。领五个的带来另外五个，领两个的也带来另外两个。主人分别称赞他们是良善忠信的仆人：既在少许事上忠信，就把更多事务托给他们，并邀他们分享自己的喜乐。

领一个的却说：我认定你严厉，在未播种处收割，在未撒种处聚敛；因为害怕，我把钱埋在地下，如今原物奉还。主人回答：你是懒惰的恶仆！既然如此想，为什么不把钱交给银庄，让我回来连本带息收回？于是他命人取走那个塔冷通，交给有十个的。凡有的，还要得到更多；没有的，连所有的也要被取去。那无用的仆人则被赶到外面的黑暗，那里有哀哭和切齿。

Jesus compared the kingdom to a man traveling abroad who entrusted his property to servants according to their abilities: five talents to one, two to another, and one to a third. The first promptly traded and gained five more; the second gained two more. The third dug a hole and concealed his master's money.

After a long time the master returned to settle accounts. The first presented his five additional talents; the second presented two. Each was welcomed as a good and faithful servant: faithfulness with a little would bring responsibility over much, and each was invited to share the master's joy.

The third said he considered the master harsh, harvesting where he had not sown. Fear had led him to bury the talent, which he now returned. The master called him wicked and idle: on that reasoning, he should have deposited the money with bankers to return it with interest. He ordered the talent given to the servant with ten. Those who have will receive abundance; those who have nothing will lose even what they possess. The useless servant was cast into outer darkness, where there is weeping and grinding of teeth.

## 反省 | Reflection

天主托付才能，是对人的信任。因害怕失败而埋藏资源，反而辜负这份爱。我们应勇敢善用所领受的机会和能力，以实际行动回应他。

God's entrusting of abilities expresses confidence in us. Burying resources through fear of failure betrays that love. We are invited to use our opportunities and capacities courageously and respond through action.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

埋藏恩赐的仆人不信任主人，也不信任自己；另两人则研究机会，审慎行动，愿意承担风险。信靠上主使人获得自由，创造美善；恐惧却使人停滞。

The servant who buried the gift trusted neither his master nor himself. The others examined opportunities, acted prudently, and accepted risk. Confidence in the Lord frees us to create goodness, while fear leaves us unable to act.
