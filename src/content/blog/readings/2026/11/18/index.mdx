## 读经一 | First Reading

**宗徒大事录 28:11-16,30-31 | Acts 28:11-16,30-31**

三个月后，我们搭上一艘在岛上过冬的亚历山大船，船头以宙斯双子为标记。到了息辣谷撒，我们停留三天，再绕行到勒基雍。翌日南风吹起，两天便到颇提约里。在那里遇到弟兄，受邀住了七天，然后前往罗马。当地弟兄听说我们的消息，远至阿丕约市场和三馆来迎接。保禄看见他们，感谢天主，精神振作。进罗马后，他获准单独居住，由一名士兵看守。

他在自租的住处住了整整两年，接待所有来访的人，大胆而自由地宣讲天主的国，教导有关主耶稣基督的事，没有人阻止。

After three months we sailed on an Alexandrian ship that had wintered on the island, with the Twin Brothers as its figurehead. We stopped at Syracuse for three days, then sailed around to Rhegium. A south wind arose the following day, and on the second day we reached Puteoli. Believers there invited us to stay seven days; afterward we proceeded toward Rome. Hearing about us, brothers came as far as the Forum of Appius and Three Taverns to meet us. Paul thanked God and took courage at seeing them. In Rome he was permitted to live separately, guarded by a soldier.

For two full years he stayed in his rented lodging, welcoming all who came. With boldness and without hindrance he proclaimed God's kingdom and taught about the Lord Jesus Christ.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 14:22-33 | Matthew 14:22-33**

耶稣催门徒上船，先往对岸，自己遣散群众。随后他独自上山祈祷，天晚了仍在那里。船已远离岸边，遇着逆风，被波浪冲击。夜间第四更，耶稣踏着海面走向他们。他们以为看见幽灵，惊恐大叫。耶稣立即安慰他们：鼓起勇气，是我，不要害怕。伯多禄说：主，若是你，就命我从水上到你那里。耶稣叫他来，他便下船，在水上朝耶稣走去。但察觉风势，心中害怕，开始下沉，呼求主救他。耶稣立刻伸手抓住他，问他为什么小信而疑惑。他们登船后，风便停止。船上的人俯伏朝拜，承认他确是天主子。

Jesus made the disciples embark and go ahead to the opposite shore while he dismissed the crowd. He then went up the mountain alone to pray, remaining there into the evening. Far from land, the boat struggled against waves and an opposing wind. In the fourth watch of the night Jesus approached across the water. The disciples mistook him for an apparition and cried out in terror. He immediately reassured them to take courage: it was he, and they need not fear. Peter asked the Lord, if it truly was he, to command him to approach on the water. Jesus called him, and Peter stepped out and walked toward him. Noticing the wind, he grew frightened, began sinking, and begged the Lord to save him. Jesus immediately caught him, asking why his little faith had given way to doubt. When they entered the boat the wind ceased. Those aboard worshiped him and confessed that he truly was God's Son.

## 反省 | Reflection

主人把资源交托仆人，表达了信任，也给予自由。好仆人认真回应，恶仆却以借口掩饰懈怠。天主似乎退隐时，我们仍能善用他赐下的资源，负责任地生活。

Entrusting resources expresses a master's confidence and grants freedom. Good servants respond responsibly; the idle servant hides behind excuses. Even when God seems hidden, we can use his gifts and take responsibility for our lives.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

领受恩赐之后，我们也蒙召成为别人的礼物。恐惧与错误的天主形象会令人埋藏才能；信德却邀请人勇于付出，分享食粮，使爱不断增长。

Having received gifts, we are called to become gifts for others. Fear and a distorted image of God can make us bury our abilities. Faith invites courageous self-giving, shared bread, and an increase of love.
