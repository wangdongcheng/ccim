## 读经一 | First Reading

**匝加利亚先知书 2:14-17 | Zechariah 2:10-13**

上主向熙雍女子宣告：你应当欢唱喜乐，因为我要来到，住在你们当中。那一天，许多民族将归向上主，成为他的子民；他要住在你们中间，你们就会认出，是万军的上主派遣我来到这里。

上主要在圣地以犹大为自己的产业，再次拣选耶路撒冷。所有血肉之躯，都应在上主面前安静肃立，因为他已从神圣的居所起身。

The Lord calls daughter Zion to sing joyfully: he is coming to make his home among her people. On that day many nations will attach themselves to him and become his people. His dwelling among them will make known that the Lord of hosts has sent his messenger.

Judah will be the Lord's own portion in the holy land, and Jerusalem will again be his chosen city. Let every mortal fall silent in his presence, for he has risen from his sacred dwelling.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**路加福音 1:46-55 | Luke 1:46-55**

**答：全能者给我做了奇事，他的名号何其神圣。 | R. The Mighty One has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

我的灵魂赞美上主；我的心灵欢跃于我的救主、天主。

**答：全能者给我做了奇事，他的名号何其神圣。**

他顾念了自己卑微的婢女；从今以后，世世代代都将宣认我的福分。大能的天主为我施行了伟大的作为，他的名神圣无比。

**答：全能者给我做了奇事，他的名号何其神圣。**

他的慈悲临于敬畏他的人，绵延世世代代。他伸展手臂，显出威力，使内心骄傲的人四散。

**答：全能者给我做了奇事，他的名号何其神圣。**

他使掌权者离开宝座，却举扬地位卑下的人；他让饥饿的人享用丰盛的美物，却使富人一无所得地离去。

**答：全能者给我做了奇事，他的名号何其神圣。**

他扶持自己的仆人以色列，始终记念慈悲；正如他向我们的祖先所许诺的，永远眷顾亚巴郎和他的后裔。

**答：全能者给我做了奇事，他的名号何其神圣。**

My soul praises the Lord; my spirit rejoices in God my Savior.

**R. The Mighty One has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

He has noticed his servant in her lowliness. From now on every generation will acknowledge my blessedness. The powerful God has acted greatly on my behalf; his name is sacred.

**R. The Mighty One has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

His compassion reaches those who reverence him throughout the generations. He has displayed his arm's strength, dispersing those whose inward thoughts are proud.

**R. The Mighty One has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

He has removed rulers from their seats and raised the lowly. He has satisfied hungry people with abundant gifts while dismissing the wealthy empty-handed.

**R. The Mighty One has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

He has supported Israel his servant, remembering mercy, just as he promised our ancestors: Abraham and his descendants for all time.

**R. The Mighty One has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 12:46-50 | Matthew 12:46-50**

耶稣仍在向群众讲话时，他的母亲和兄弟来到外面，希望与他说话。有人将这件事告诉耶稣，说他的母亲和兄弟正在外面等候，想见他。

耶稣向传话的人反问：谁是我的母亲，谁又是我的兄弟？接着，他伸手指向门徒，宣告他们就是自己的母亲和兄弟。凡承行他天上之父旨意的人，都与他成为兄弟、姊妹和母亲。

While Jesus continued speaking to the crowd, his mother and relatives waited outside, wishing to talk with him. Someone informed him that they were there and wanted to speak to him.

He asked the messenger who should be called his mother and his brothers. Pointing toward his disciples, he identified them as his family. Anyone who carries out his heavenly Father's will belongs to him as brother, sister and mother.

## 反省 | Reflection

撒杜塞人用婚姻难题质疑复活，显露他们以今世经验限制对永生的理解。耶稣指出，复活的生命超越现世制度。进入永恒的是爱；在天主内，这爱将达到今世尚不能实现的圆满。

The Sadducees' marriage puzzle reveals how earthly assumptions can restrict our understanding of resurrection. Jesus points to a life beyond present social arrangements. Love endures into eternity, where communion with God brings a fullness that earthly life cannot yet contain.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

耶稣使兄弟姊妹的关系超越血缘，向每一个人敞开。我们承行天主旨意，效法他所赐的爱，就进入这个家庭。圣父成为我们的父，圣神居于我们心中；耶稣甚至在十字架上，将自己的母亲赐给我们。这样的亲属关系要求全心奉献。

Jesus opens the relationship of brothers and sisters beyond ancestry to embrace everyone. Doing God's will through mutual love draws us into his family. His Father becomes ours, and the Spirit lives within us. From the cross he even shares his mother with us. This kinship calls for wholehearted self-giving.
