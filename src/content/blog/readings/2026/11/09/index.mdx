拉特朗大殿奉献日 | Dedication of the Lateran Basilica

2026年11月9日 · 星期一 | November 9, 2026 · Monday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄则克耳先知书 47:1-2,8-9,12 | Ezekiel 47:1-2,8-9,12**

天使带我回到圣殿入口。我看见水从殿的门限下面流出，向东而去，因为殿面朝东方。水从殿的右侧、祭坛南边流下。他又领我从北门出去，由外面绕到朝东的外门；水正在南侧涌流。

他告诉我：“这水流向东部地区，下到阿辣巴，进入盐海，使海水得到医治。河流所到之处，各样群集的生物都能存活，鱼类也将十分繁多；因为这水带来医治，所经之处便有生命。

“河的两岸将生长各种果树，叶子不会枯萎，果实不会断绝，每月都有新果，因为灌溉它们的水来自圣所。果实供人食用，叶子用以治病。”

The angel led me back to the temple entrance. I saw water coming from under its threshold and flowing eastwards, for the temple faced east. It ran down from the temple's right side, south of the altar. He then brought me out by the northern gateway and around outside to the outer gate facing east. There the water was emerging on the southern side.

He told me: “This water runs towards the eastern region, descends into the Arabah, and enters the sea of salt, making its waters wholesome. Wherever the river reaches, swarming creatures of every kind will live, and fish will abound. These waters bring healing; life will flourish wherever they go.

“On either bank will grow every kind of fruit tree. Their foliage will not wither, and their fruit will never cease. A fresh crop will come each month because the water nourishing them comes from the sanctuary. Their fruit will provide food, and their leaves will bring healing.”

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 46:2-3,5-6,8-9 | Psalm 46:1-2,4-5,7-8**

**答：河流要使天主的城邑欢乐，即至高者所住的至圣居所。 | R. A river gladdens the city of God, the holy dwelling of the Most High.**

天主是我们的庇护与力量，是患难中可靠的援助。因此，纵然大地变动、群山坠入海心，我们也不害怕。

**答：河流要使天主的城邑欢乐，即至高者所住的至圣居所。**

一道河流使天主的城欢喜，那是至高者的圣所。天主居于城中，她必稳固不摇；黎明一到，天主便来援助。

**答：河流要使天主的城邑欢乐，即至高者所住的至圣居所。**

万军的上主与我们同在，雅各伯的天主是我们的堡垒。你们来看看上主所做的事，他在地上施行了令人震惊的作为。

**答：河流要使天主的城邑欢乐，即至高者所住的至圣居所。**

God shelters and strengthens us, a help readily found in trouble. We shall therefore have no fear, even if the earth changes and mountains plunge into the heart of the sea.

**R. A river gladdens the city of God, the holy dwelling of the Most High.**

There is a river whose streams bring joy to God's city, the sacred dwelling of the Most High. God is within her, so she will stand firm; at the break of dawn he comes to her aid.

**R. A river gladdens the city of God, the holy dwelling of the Most High.**

The Lord of hosts stands beside us; Jacob's God is our stronghold. Come and look at the Lord's works, the astonishing desolations he has brought upon the earth.

**R. A river gladdens the city of God, the holy dwelling of the Most High.**

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**格林多人前书 3:9c-11,16-17 | 1 Corinthians 3:9c-11,16-17**

弟兄们，你们是天主所建造的房屋。凭着天主赐给我的恩宠，我如同熟练的建筑师，已经打下基础，别人继续在上面建造。然而，每个人都要谨慎，留心自己怎样建造。除了已经立下的耶稣基督，没有人能够另立根基。

你们岂不晓得，自己就是天主的殿，天主的圣神居住在你们内？毁坏天主圣殿的人，必受到天主的毁灭。天主的殿是神圣的，而这殿就是你们。

Brothers and sisters, you are the building God is raising. By the grace entrusted to me, I acted as a skilled master builder and laid its foundation; another continues the construction. Each person must take care over how he builds. No foundation can be laid in place of the one already established, Jesus Christ.

Do you not realise that you yourselves are God's sanctuary, with his Spirit living within you? Anyone who ruins God's sanctuary will face destruction from God. His sanctuary is holy, and you are that sanctuary.

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣若望福音 2:13-22 | John 2:13-22**

犹太人的逾越节将近，耶稣上耶路撒冷去了。他在圣殿范围内，看见有人贩卖牛、羊、鸽子，也有兑换钱币的人坐在那里。于是，他把绳索结成鞭子，连同牛羊把他们都赶出殿院；他倒出兑换者的钱币，掀翻了他们的桌子，又向卖鸽子的人说：“把这些搬走！不要把我父的家变作买卖的地方！”门徒想起圣经的话：对你圣殿的热爱将把我耗尽。

犹太人便问他：“你能显什么征兆，证明你有权这样做？”耶稣回答：“拆掉这座殿，我会在三天内将它重新建立。”他们说：“这座殿建了四十六年，你三天就能建立起来吗？”

耶稣所指的殿，其实是他自己的身体。因此，他由死者中复活以后，门徒记起他说过这话，就相信了圣经，也相信耶稣所说的话。

As the Jewish Passover drew near, Jesus travelled up to Jerusalem. In the temple precincts he found sellers of cattle, sheep, and pigeons, as well as money-changers seated at their business. Making a whip from cords, he drove them out together with the cattle and sheep. He scattered the changers' coins and overturned their tables. To those selling pigeons he said: “Remove these things! Stop turning my Father's home into a place of trade!” His disciples recalled the scriptural words about zeal for God's house consuming him.

The Jews challenged him to produce a sign establishing his authority for these actions. Jesus replied: “Pull down this sanctuary, and within three days I shall raise it again.” They objected that the temple had taken forty-six years to build, and asked how he could raise it in three days.

The sanctuary he meant was his own body. After he had risen from death, his disciples remembered these words and believed both the Scriptures and what Jesus had spoken.

## 反省 | Reflection

据[李建平神父当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-11-09)摘要（乐仁出版社供稿）：拉特朗大殿象征普世教堂，也指向作为天主子民、基督身体及圣神宫殿的教会。朝圣和圣堂固然可贵，真正的朝拜却在于以圣神和真理朝拜天父。信友聚集的意义，不只在某个地点，而在借基督、在圣神内与父相遇；教会的肢体应跟随基督这位元首行动。

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau: The Lateran Basilica represents churches everywhere and points to the Church as God's people, Christ's body, and the Spirit's dwelling. Pilgrimage and sacred buildings matter, yet true worship means approaching the Father in Spirit and truth. A Christian gathering finds its meaning in meeting the Father through Christ and in the Holy Spirit. The Church's members should act in union with Christ their head.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

据[教宗方济各2019年11月9日弥撒讲道](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/11/09.html)摘要：商贩占用的是外邦人也可进入的庭院，但天主愿自己的殿成为所有民族的祈祷之所。耶稣清理圣殿，明知会付出代价，并以三日重建圣殿指向自己的复活。罪使我们远离上主、损伤内在的圣殿，却不会使隔绝成为最后的结局。上主仍在人心开启向真、善、美的缝隙；即使遇上不信任与敌意，也要相信他有更新人心的力量。

Summary of Pope Francis's homily of 9 November 2019: The traders occupied the courtyard open to Gentiles, although God intended his temple for the prayer of every people. Jesus knew the cost of his action, and his promise to rebuild in three days pointed to his resurrection. Sin distances us from the Lord and damages the sanctuary within us, but separation need not have the final word. God continues to awaken desires for truth, goodness, and beauty. Even amid distrust or hostility, we should trust his power to renew the human heart.
