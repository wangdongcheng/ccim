## 读经一 | First Reading

**若望二书 4-9 | 2 John 4-9**

夫人，见到你的子女依照天父的命令在真理中生活，我非常欢喜。我如今请求你：我们要彼此相爱。这不是新写的命令，而是起初就领受的。爱就是按他的诫命行事；你们从起初所听见的，正是要在爱内生活。世上已有许多迷惑人的人，他们不承认耶稣基督在肉身中来到；这样的人就是骗子和反基督者。你们要警惕，别使我们的辛劳落空，而要得到完整的赏报。越过基督教训、不持守其中的人，没有天主；持守这教训的人，却同时拥有父与子。

Lady, I rejoice to find some of your children living by the truth, as the Father commanded. I ask that we love one another. This command is what we received at the beginning. Love means living according to his commandments; from the outset you heard that you should walk in love. Many deceivers have entered the world, refusing to acknowledge Jesus Christ coming in the flesh. Such a person is the deceiver and antichrist. Be watchful, lest our labor be lost, and seek the full reward. Anyone who goes beyond Christ's teaching instead of remaining in it does not have God. Whoever remains in that teaching has both Father and Son.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 119:1-2,10-11,17-18 | Psalm 119:1-2,10-11,17-18**

**答：遵行上主法律的人，真是有福。 | R. Blessed are those who follow the Lord's law.**

行为正直、依上主法律生活的人有福；遵守他的诫命，全心寻求他的人有福。

**答：遵行上主法律的人，真是有福。**

我全心寻求你，求你别让我偏离你的命令。我珍藏你的言语，免得冒犯你。

**答：遵行上主法律的人，真是有福。**

求你厚待仆人，使我存活并遵守你的话；开启我的眼，使我看见你法律中的奇妙。

**答：遵行上主法律的人，真是有福。**

**R. Blessed are those who follow the Lord's law.**

Happy are those whose way is blameless and who follow the Lord's law, keeping his testimonies and seeking him wholeheartedly.

**R. Blessed are those who follow the Lord's law.**

I seek you with my whole heart; keep me from wandering from your commands. I treasure your word within, so that I may not sin against you.

**R. Blessed are those who follow the Lord's law.**

Deal generously with your servant, that I may live and keep your word. Open my eyes to the wonders of your law.

**R. Blessed are those who follow the Lord's law.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 17:26-37 | Luke 17:26-37**

耶稣说：诺厄的日子怎样，人子的日子也怎样。人们照常吃喝婚嫁，直到诺厄进方舟，洪水便来，把他们全毁灭了。罗特的日子也一样：人们吃喝、买卖、种植、建造；罗特离开索多玛那天，天上降下火与硫磺，将他们毁灭。人子显现时也要如此。那天在屋顶的人不要下来取家中财物，在田里的人也不要回头。记住罗特的妻子！执意保存生命的将失去生命，舍弃生命的却使它存活。那夜两个人同床，一个被接去，一个留下；两个妇女一起磨粮，一个被接去，一个留下。门徒问这些事在哪里发生，耶稣回答：尸体所在，秃鹰也聚在那里。

Jesus compared the Son of man's days to Noah's. People went on eating, drinking, and marrying until Noah entered the ark; then the flood destroyed them. In Lot's time they likewise ate, drank, traded, planted, and built. On the day Lot left Sodom, fire and sulfur rained down and destroyed them. So it will be when the Son of man is revealed. Anyone on the roof must not come down to collect belongings, nor should someone in a field turn back. Remember Lot's wife. Those intent on preserving their life will lose it; those losing it will keep it alive. Two will share a bed that night: one will be taken and one left. Two women will grind together: one taken and one left. Asked where this would happen, he answered that vultures gather wherever the body lies.

## 反省 | Reflection

救恩与忠信发生在平凡生活中。同样的工作与作息，可以承载感恩、仁慈，也可以承载怨恨与伤害。与其推测末日时间，不如在每天的选择中守信行善。

Salvation and faithfulness take shape in ordinary life. Identical routines can contain gratitude and kindness or resentment and harm. Rather than calculate the end, remain faithful through everyday choices.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

主常隐藏于工作、偶遇和需要帮助者的面容中。日常生活也可能令我们麻木，以致忽略他的来访；因此应醒寤，在平凡处认出并迎接他。

The Lord is present in work, unexpected encounters, and people needing help. Everyday routine can also dull our awareness of his visit. Vigilance means recognizing and welcoming him within those ordinary circumstances.
