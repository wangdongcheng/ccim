## 读经一 | First Reading

**约伯传 1:6-22 | Job 1:6-22**

有一天，天主的众子前来侍立在上主面前，撒殚也在他们中间。上主问撒殚：“你从哪里来？”撒殚回答：“我在大地上来回巡游之后回来。”

上主说：“你注意到我的仆人约伯了吗？世上没有人像他那样纯全正直，敬畏天主，远避邪恶。”撒殚回答上主：“约伯难道是无缘无故敬畏天主吗？你不是四面保护了他、他的家庭和他所有的一切吗？你降福了他双手所做的工作，他的牲畜也遍布大地。可是，如果你伸手打击他所拥有的一切，他必会当面诅咒你。”

上主对撒殚说：“看，他所有的一切都在你手中，只是不可向他本人伸手。”撒殚便离开上主面前。

有一天，约伯的儿女正在长兄家中吃饭饮酒，一个报信的人来到约伯那里说：“牛正在耕田，母驴在旁吃草时，舍巴人突然袭来，抢走牲畜，用刀杀死仆人；只有我一人逃脱，前来报告。”

他还在说话，另一个人又来报告：“天火从天降下，焚毁了羊群和仆人；只有我一人逃脱，前来报告。”他还在说话，又有一人来报告：“加色丁人分成三队袭击骆驼，把它们掳走，又用刀杀死仆人；只有我一人逃脱，前来报告。”

那人还在说话，另一个人又来报告：“你的儿女正在长兄家中吃饭饮酒时，忽然有强风从旷野吹来，冲击房屋四角，房屋倒在年轻人身上，他们都死了；只有我一人逃脱，前来报告。”

约伯便起来，撕裂外衣，剃去头发，俯伏在地朝拜，说：“我赤身离开母胎，也要赤身归去；上主赏赐，上主收回，愿上主的名受赞美！”在这一切事上，约伯没有犯罪，也没有归罪于天主。

One day the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and Satan also came among them. The Lord asked Satan, “Where have you come from?” Satan answered, “From going to and fro upon the earth and walking up and down upon it.”

The Lord said, “Have you considered my servant Job? There is no one like him on earth: a blameless and upright man who fears God and turns away from evil.” Satan answered, “Does Job fear God for nothing? Have you not placed a hedge around him, his household, and all that he owns? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his possessions have increased throughout the land. But stretch out your hand and touch all that he has, and he will curse you to your face.”

The Lord said to Satan, “Behold, all that he has is in your power; only do not lay a hand upon the man himself.” Then Satan departed from the presence of the Lord.

One day Job’s sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother’s house. A messenger came to Job and said, “The oxen were plowing and the donkeys feeding beside them when the Sabeans attacked, carried off the animals, and struck down the servants with the sword. I alone escaped to tell you.”

While he was still speaking, another messenger came and said, “Fire fell from heaven and consumed the sheep and the servants. I alone escaped to tell you.” While he was still speaking, another came and said, “The Chaldeans formed three companies, raided the camels, carried them away, and struck down the servants with the sword. I alone escaped to tell you.”

While he was still speaking, another came and said, “Your sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother’s house when a great wind swept across the wilderness and struck the four corners of the house. It fell upon the young people, and they died. I alone escaped to tell you.”

Then Job arose, tore his robe, shaved his head, fell upon the ground, and worshiped. He said, “Naked I came from my mother’s womb, and naked shall I return. The Lord gave, and the Lord has taken away; blessed be the name of the Lord.” In all this, Job did not sin or charge God with wrongdoing.

## 反省 | Reflection

从第一个世纪到世界的终结，教会都面对一个事实：基督是教会的头，但教会并不等同基督，也不独占基督。天主圣言的种子深植于世界各处，总有游离于教会之外的个人或团体，以基督之名制胜邪恶。这种现象，或说事实，一度让教会困惑，正如当初也曾让宗徒们恼火一样。耶稣不让门徒去禁止那些善用祂名字的人，因为谁不反对基督，谁就是赞成基督。耶稣为什么这样说？到底哪里是教会的边界？圣奥斯定做了很好的回应：“有些人在教会之外，却属于教会；有些人在教会之内，却不属于教会。”

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

From the first century until the end of the world, the Church has faced this reality: Christ is the head of the Church, but the Church is not identical with Christ, nor does she possess Christ exclusively. The seed of God’s word is deeply planted throughout the world. There will always be individuals or groups outside the visible bounds of the Church who overcome evil in the name of Christ.

This phenomenon—or rather, this fact—has at times perplexed the Church, just as it once irritated the apostles. Jesus does not allow the disciples to stop those who make good use of his name, because whoever is not against Christ is for Christ. Why does Jesus say this? Where, then, are the boundaries of the Church? Saint Augustine offers a fitting response: “Some are outside the Church yet belong to her; others are inside the Church yet do not belong to her.”

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

耶稣谈过服务居首位之后，随即做了一件事来阐明这教导。我们已经看到，耶稣的行动比祂所用的话语更有力量。祂做了什么呢？祂领来一个孩子，把孩子安置在门徒中间，也就是最重要的位置、中心的位置（参阅第36节）。

在福音中，孩子所象征的，与其说是纯真，不如说是微小。因为小孩子和所有微小者一样，需要依靠他人、依靠成年人；他们需要接受别人的给予。耶稣拥抱这些孩子，并说：谁接纳一个微小者、一个孩子，就是接纳祂（参阅第37节）。我们最应当服务的人，是那些需要领受、却无法给予任何回报的人。（……）接纳那些处于边缘、被人忽视的人，就是接纳耶稣，因为祂在那里。在我们所服务的微小者和穷人身上，我们也领受天主温柔的拥抱。

亲爱的弟兄姊妹，面对福音的挑战，让我们问问自己：我这跟随耶稣的人，是否关心被人忽视的人？还是像那天的门徒一样，只在追求个人满足？我是否把人生理解为彼此竞争，不惜牺牲别人来为自己争取位置；还是相信居首位意味着服务？具体来说，我是否愿意把时间给予一个“微小者”，给予一个无力回报我的人？我是否关心那些无法给我任何回报的人，还是只关心自己的亲友？

（教宗方济各，三钟经，2021年9月19日）

After having spoken of the primacy of service, Jesus does something precisely to illustrate this. We have seen that Jesus’ actions are stronger than the words he uses. And what is that action? He takes a child and puts him in the midst of the disciples, at the center, in the most important place (cf. v. 36). In the Gospel, the child does not symbolize innocence so much as littleness. For like children, the little ones depend on others, on adults, they need to receive. Jesus embraces those children and says that those who welcome a little one, a child, welcome him (cf. v. 37). The ones who are to be served above all are: those in need of receiving who cannot give anything in return. (…) In welcoming those on the margins, the neglected, we welcome Jesus because He is there. And in the little one, in the poor person we serve, we also receive God’s tender embrace.

Dear brothers and sisters, challenged by the Gospel, let us ask ourselves: Am I, who follow Jesus, interested in the one who is neglected? Or am I rather seeking personal gratification, like the disciples that day? Do I understand life in terms of competing to make room for myself at others’ expense, or do I believe that being first means serving? And, concretely: do I dedicate time to a “little one”, to a person who has no means to pay me back? Am I concerned about someone who cannot give me anything in return, or only with my relatives and friends? (Pope Francis, Angelus, 19 September 2021)
