## 读经一 | First Reading

**格林多人前书 8:1-7,11-13 | 1 Corinthians 8:1-7,11-13**

弟兄们：关于祭过偶像的食物，我们都自以为拥有知识；可是知识会使人傲慢自大，爱德才能造就人。谁若以为自己已经知道了什么，其实还没有真正懂得应当怎样知道；谁若爱天主，天主就认识他。

至于祭过偶像的食物，我们知道世上的偶像本身算不得什么，也知道只有一个天主。纵使天上地下有许多被人称为“神”或“主”的，我们却只有一个天主——圣父；万物出于他，我们也归于他；也只有一个主——耶稣基督；万物藉着他而存在，我们也藉着他而存在。

但是，并不是人人都有这样的认识。有些人过去习惯敬拜偶像，因此吃这些食物时，仍把它看作祭过偶像的食物；他们的良心软弱，就受到伤害。

如果你的知识反而使那位软弱的弟兄跌倒——他可是基督为之舍命的人——那么你就是伤害弟兄软弱的良心，并因此得罪基督。所以，如果食物会使我的弟兄跌倒，我宁愿永远不吃肉，免得使他跌倒。

Brothers and sisters: with regard to food offered to idols, we may all think that we possess knowledge. Yet knowledge can inflate the ego, while love is what truly builds up. Anyone who imagines that they already know everything has not yet learned how knowledge should be used; but the one who loves God is known by God.

As for food offered to idols, an idol has no real divine existence, and there is only one God. Even though many beings are called gods or lords, for us there is one God, the Father, from whom all things come and for whom we live, and one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom all things exist and through whom we live.

Not everyone, however, has reached this understanding. Some remain shaped by their former association with idols, so when they eat such food they still regard it as connected with idol worship; because their conscience is weak, it is wounded.

If what you know becomes the means by which a weaker believer is ruined—the very brother or sister for whom Christ died—then you sin against them and against Christ by injuring a fragile conscience. Therefore, if food becomes an occasion for another believer to fall, Paul would rather abstain from meat altogether than become the cause of that fall.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 139:1-3,13-14,23-24 | Psalm 139:1-3,13-14,23-24**

**答：上主，求你引我迈向永生的道路。 | R. Lead me in the way everlasting.**

上主，你洞察我，也认识我；
我坐下或起身，你都知道，
我的思念即使在远处，你也明白。
我行走或休息，你都细察，
我的一切道路，你全都熟悉。

**答：上主，求你引我迈向永生的道路。**

是你形成了我的五内，
是你使我在母胎中成形。
我要赞美你，因为你创造我如此奇妙；
你的工程令人惊叹，
我的心灵深深知道这一点。

**答：上主，求你引我迈向永生的道路。**

天主，求你鉴察我，认识我的心；
考验我，洞悉我的思念。
看我是否走在错误的道路上，
并引领我走向永恒的道路。

**答：上主，求你引我迈向永生的道路。**

O LORD, you have examined me and you know me. You know when I sit and when I rise; even from far away, you understand my thoughts. You observe my journeys and my resting, and every path I take is known to you.

**R. Lead me in the way everlasting.**

You formed my inward being and knit me together in my mother’s womb. I praise you because my creation is filled with wonder; your works are marvelous, and I know this deeply.

**R. Lead me in the way everlasting.**

Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know what is within me. See whether I am walking along a harmful path, and guide me in the everlasting way.

**R. Lead me in the way everlasting.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 6:27-38 | Luke 6:27-38**

那时候，耶稣对听他说话的人说：你们要爱你们的仇人，善待恨你们的人；为诅咒你们的人祝福，为虐待你们的人祈祷。有人打你的面颊，你也把另一面转给他；有人拿走你的外衣，你连内衣也不要拒绝。有人向你求，就给他；有人拿走你的东西，不要强求取回。你们愿意别人怎样对待你们，你们也要怎样对待别人。

如果你们只爱那些爱你们的人，有什么值得称许的呢？罪人也爱那些爱他们的人。如果你们只善待那些善待你们的人，又有什么值得称许的呢？罪人也会这样做。如果你们只借给那些希望偿还的人，又有什么值得称许的呢？罪人也借给罪人，为要如数收回。

你们却要爱仇人，善待他们；借出去，不要期待回报。这样，你们的赏报必定丰厚，你们也将成为至高者的子女，因为他对忘恩负义和邪恶的人仍然慈善。你们要慈悲，如同你们的父慈悲一样。

不要判断别人，你们也就不受判断；不要定人的罪，你们也就不被定罪；宽恕别人，你们也会得到宽恕。你们要施予，也必有施予给你们：满满的一升，压紧、摇匀、甚至溢出来，倒在你们怀中。因为你们用什么尺度量给别人，也必用什么尺度量给你们。

Jesus said to those who were listening: love your enemies and act for the good of those who hate you. Bless those who curse you and pray for those who mistreat you. If someone strikes you, refuse retaliation; if someone takes your coat, do not cling even to your shirt. Give to the one who asks, and do not demand back what has been taken. Treat others in the way you would want them to treat you.

There is little credit in loving only those who love you, because even sinners do that. The same is true of doing good only to those who do good to you, or lending only when repayment is expected.

Instead, love your enemies, do good, and lend without making repayment your condition. Your reward will be great, and you will be children of the Most High, who is kind even to the ungrateful and the wicked. Be merciful as your Father is merciful.

Do not judge, and you will not be judged. Do not condemn, and you will not be condemned. Forgive, and you will be forgiven. Give, and generosity will return to you in overflowing measure. The standard you use in dealing with others will also be the standard used for you.
