2026年9月18日 · 星期五 | September 18, 2026 · Friday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**格林多人前书 15:12-20 | 1 Corinthians 15:12-20**

弟兄们：我们既然宣讲基督已从死人中复活，你们中间怎么有人说死人不能复活呢？如果没有死人复活，基督也就没有复活；如果基督没有复活，我们的宣讲便是空的，你们的信仰也是空的。这样，我们也成了为天主作假见证的人，因为我们曾为天主作证说：他使基督复活了；但如果死人不复活，他其实并没有使基督复活。

因为死人若不复活，基督也没有复活；基督若没有复活，你们的信仰便是徒然，你们仍在罪恶之中；那些在基督内安眠的人也就丧亡了。如果我们只在今生寄望于基督，我们便是众人中最可怜的。

但是，基督确实从死人中复活了，成了安眠者的初果。

Brothers and sisters: If Christ is proclaimed as raised from the dead, how can some among you say that there is no resurrection of the dead? If there is no resurrection of the dead, then Christ has not been raised. And if Christ has not been raised, our preaching is empty and your faith is empty. We would also be found false witnesses concerning God, because we testified that he raised Christ—whom he did not raise, if the dead are not raised.

For if the dead are not raised, neither has Christ been raised. And if Christ has not been raised, your faith is futile and you are still in your sins. Then those who have fallen asleep in Christ have perished. If our hope in Christ is for this life only, we are of all people most to be pitied.

But in fact Christ has been raised from the dead, the first fruits of those who have fallen asleep.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 17:4,6-8,15 | Psalm 17:1b,6-8,15**

**答：上主，愿我能以仰瞻你为幸福。 | R. Lord, may I find my joy in beholding your face.**

上主，求你聆听我的申诉，注意我的哀号；
请倾听我口中毫无虚伪的祈祷。

**答：上主，愿我能以仰瞻你为幸福。**

天主，我向你呼求，因为你必应允我；
求你侧耳俯听我的诉说。
求你彰显奇妙的慈爱，
以右手拯救投奔你、躲避仇敌的人。

**答：上主，愿我能以仰瞻你为幸福。**

求你保护我，有如眼中的瞳人；
让我藏身在你双翼的荫下。
我必因正义得见你的慈颜；
愿我醒来时，以仰瞻你为满足。

**答：上主，愿我能以仰瞻你为幸福。**

Hear my just cause, O Lord; attend to my cry;
give ear to my prayer from lips free of deceit.

**R. Lord, may I find my joy in beholding your face.**

I call upon you, for you will answer me, O God;
incline your ear to me and hear my words.
Show the wonder of your steadfast love,
O savior of those who seek refuge from their enemies at your right hand.

**R. Lord, may I find my joy in beholding your face.**

Guard me as the apple of your eye;
hide me beneath the shadow of your wings.
As for me, in righteousness I shall behold your face;
when I awake, I shall be satisfied in your presence.

**R. Lord, may I find my joy in beholding your face.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 8:1-3 | Luke 8:1-3**

那时候，耶稣走遍各城各村，宣讲天主国的喜讯。十二门徒同他一起，还有一些曾受恶魔侵扰或患病而获治愈的妇女：号称玛达肋纳的玛利亚，曾有七个魔鬼从她身上被赶出；黑落德的管家顾撒的妻子约安纳；苏撒纳；以及许多别的妇女。她们都用自己的财产供给耶稣和门徒。

At that time, Jesus traveled through towns and villages, preaching and bringing the good news of the Kingdom of God. The Twelve were with him, and also some women who had been healed of evil spirits and infirmities: Mary called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out; Joanna, the wife of Chuza, Herod's steward; Susanna; and many others. These women provided for Jesus and his disciples from their own means.
