## 读经一 | First Reading

**格林多人前书 9:16-19,22b-27 | 1 Corinthians 9:16-19,22b-27**

弟兄们：我若传福音，并没有什么可夸耀的，因为这是我必须履行的责任；我若不传福音，我就有祸了。若我甘心去做，我自有赏报；即使不是出于自己的选择，我仍是受托执行一项使命。

那么，我的赏报是什么呢？就是在传福音时不索取报酬，不完全使用福音赋予我的权利。我原是自由的，不属于任何人，却甘愿成为众人的仆人，为的是赢得更多的人。

对软弱的人，我就成为软弱的，为赢得软弱的人；我对各种人都设法成为他们中的一员，为的是尽可能救一些人。我所做的一切，都是为了福音，好使我也能分享福音所许诺的恩惠。

你们不知道在赛场上奔跑的人虽然很多，得到奖赏的却只有一个吗？你们也要这样奔跑，为赢得奖赏。参加竞赛的人凡事都有节制；他们这样做，是为了得到会朽坏的冠冕，我们却是为了那不会朽坏的冠冕。

所以，我奔跑不是漫无目标；我搏斗也不是像打空气。我克制自己的身体，使它服从我，免得我向别人宣讲以后，自己反而失去资格。

Brothers and sisters: if I proclaim the gospel, I have no reason to boast, because the task has been laid upon me; woe to me if I do not proclaim the gospel. If I do it willingly, I have a reward; if not willingly, I still carry out a commission entrusted to me.

What then is my reward? It is this: in proclaiming the gospel I make it available without charge and do not insist on every right the gospel might give me. Though free from all, I have made myself a servant to all, so that I might win more of them.

To the weak I became weak, in order to win the weak. I have become all things to all people, so that by every possible means I might save some. I do all this for the sake of the gospel, so that I too may share in its blessings.

Do you not know that in a race all the runners compete, but only one receives the prize? Run in such a way that you may win it. Athletes exercise self-control in everything; they do it to receive a perishable wreath, but we seek one that does not perish.

Therefore I do not run without purpose, nor do I fight as though beating the air. I discipline my body and bring it under control, so that after proclaiming the message to others I myself may not be disqualified.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 6:39-42 | Luke 6:39-42**

那时候，耶稣给门徒讲了一个比喻：“一个瞎子怎能给另一个瞎子领路呢？两个人岂不是都会掉进坑里吗？学生不能高过老师；但一个受过完整训练的学生，会像自己的老师一样。

“为什么你只看见弟兄眼中的木屑，却没有察觉自己眼中的大梁？你自己眼中有大梁，又怎能对弟兄说：‘弟兄，让我把你眼中的木屑取出来’呢？假善人！先把自己眼中的大梁取出来，那时你才会看得清楚，能够取出弟兄眼中的木屑。”

Jesus told his disciples a parable: “Can a blind person guide another blind person? Will not both fall into a pit? A disciple is not above the teacher, but everyone who is fully trained will become like the teacher.

"Why do you notice the speck in your brother’s eye but fail to recognize the beam in your own? How can you say, ‘Brother, let me remove the speck from your eye,’ while you do not see the beam in your own eye? Hypocrite! First remove the beam from your own eye; then you will see clearly enough to remove the speck from your brother’s eye." 🟥
