常年期第二十三主日 | Twenty-third Sunday in Ordinary Time

2026年9月6日 · 星期日 | September 6, 2026 · Sunday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄则克耳先知书 33:7-9 | Ezekiel 33:7-9**

上主这样说：“人子，我立你作以色列家族的警卫；你从我口中听到话，就应代我警告他们。

当我对恶人说：‘恶人，你必定要死！’如果你不开口警告恶人，使他离开自己的道路，那么那恶人要因自己的罪恶而死，但我要向你追讨他的血债。

如果你警告了恶人，叫他离开自己的道路，而他仍不肯回头，那么他要因自己的罪恶而死，而你却救了自己。”

So thou, O son of man, I have set thee a watchman unto the house of Israel; therefore thou shalt hear the word at my mouth, and warn them from me.

When I say unto the wicked, O wicked man, thou shalt surely die; if thou dost not speak to warn the wicked from his way, that wicked man shall die in his iniquity; but his blood will I require at thine hand.

Nevertheless, if thou warn the wicked of his way to turn from it; if he do not turn from his way, he shall die in his iniquity; but thou hast delivered thy soul.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 95:1-2, 6-7, 8-9 | Psalm 95:1-2, 6-7, 8-9**

**答：你们今天该听从他的声音，不要再心硬。 | R. To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your heart.**

请大家前来，向上主欢呼，
向拯救我们的磐石高声喝彩；
让我们怀着感恩来到他面前，
向他欢唱圣咏。

**答：你们今天该听从他的声音，不要再心硬。**

请大家前来叩首朝拜，
跪伏在创造我们的上主面前。
因为他是我们的天主，
我们是他牧场的人民，是他亲手牧放的羊群。

**答：你们今天该听从他的声音，不要再心硬。**

今天，你们若听到他的声音：
“不要再心硬，如同在默黎巴，
如同在旷野玛撒的那一天；
在那里，你们的祖先试探我、考验我，
虽然他们已经看见了我的作为。”

**答：你们今天该听从他的声音，不要再心硬。**

O come, let us sing unto the LORD:
let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation.

Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving,
and make a joyful noise unto him with psalms.

**R. To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your heart.**

O come, let us worship and bow down:
let us kneel before the LORD our maker.

For he is our God;
and we are the people of his pasture, and the sheep of his hand.

**R. To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your heart.**

To day if ye will hear his voice,
Harden not your heart, as in the provocation,
and as in the day of temptation in the wilderness:

When your fathers tempted me,
proved me, and saw my work.

**R. To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your heart.**

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**罗马书 13:8-10 | Romans 13:8-10**

弟兄们：除了彼此相爱以外，不要欠任何人的债，因为爱别人的，就满全了法律。

因为“不可奸淫，不可杀人，不可偷盗，不可贪恋”，以及其他任何诫命，都包含在这一句话中：“你应当爱近人如你自己。”

爱不加害于近人，所以爱就是法律的满全。

Owe no man any thing, but to love one another: for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.

For this, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Thou shalt not covet; and if there be any other commandment, it is briefly comprehended in this saying, namely, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

Love worketh no ill to his neighbour: therefore love is the fulfilling of the law.

## 福音前欢呼 | Alleluia

**格林多人后书 5:19 | 2 Corinthians 5:19**

阿肋路亚，阿肋路亚。

天主在基督内使世界与自己和好，并把和好的话交托给了我们。

阿肋路亚，阿肋路亚。

Alleluia, alleluia.

To wit, that God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself, not imputing their trespasses unto them; and hath committed unto us the word of reconciliation.

Alleluia, alleluia.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 18:15-20 | Matthew 18:15-20**

那时候，耶稣对门徒说：

“如果你的弟兄得罪了你，你要去，在只有你和他两个人的时候，指出他的过错。如果他听从你，你便赢得了你的弟兄。

如果他不听，你就再带一个或两个人同去，使一切事情凭两个或三个证人的口供得到证实。

如果他仍不听他们，就告诉教会；如果连教会也不听，就把他看作外邦人或税吏。

我实在告诉你们：凡你们在地上所束缚的，在天上也要被束缚；凡你们在地上所释放的，在天上也要被释放。

我又实在告诉你们：如果你们中有两个人在地上同心合意，无论求什么，我在天之父必要为他们成全。

因为无论在哪里，有两三个人因我的名聚集，我就在他们中间。”

Moreover if thy brother shall trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone: if he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother.

But if he will not hear thee, then take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.

And if he shall neglect to hear them, tell it unto the church: but if he neglect to hear the church, let him be unto thee as an heathen man and a publican.

Verily I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.

Again I say unto you, That if two of you shall agree on earth as touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father which is in heaven.

For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them. 🟥
