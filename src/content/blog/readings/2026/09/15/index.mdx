痛苦圣母 | Our Lady of Sorrows

2026年9月15日 · 星期二 | September 15, 2026 · Tuesday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**圣保禄宗徒致格林多人前书 12:12-14,27-31a | 1 Corinthians 12:12-14,27-31a**

弟兄们：正如身体只有一个，却有许多肢体；身体所有的肢体虽多，仍是一个身体；基督也是这样。

我们众人，不论是犹太人或希腊人，或为奴的或自由的，都因一个圣神受了洗，成了一个身体；又都领受了同一圣神。原来身体不只有一个肢体，而是有许多个。

你们便是基督的身体，各自都是肢体。天主在教会内所安排的，第一是宗徒，第二是先知，第三是教师；此外还有行奇迹的、有治病恩赐的、有协助人的、有管理人的、有说各种语言的。

难道人人都是宗徒吗？人人都是先知吗？人人都是教师吗？人人都能行奇迹吗？人人都有治病的恩赐吗？人人都能说各种语言吗？人人都能解释语言吗？你们应当热切追求更大的恩赐。

Brothers and sisters: As the body is one and has many members, and all its many members are one body, so it is with Christ.

For in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body—Jews or Greeks, slaves or free—and all were given to drink of one Spirit. The body does not consist of one member but of many.

You are the body of Christ, and individually members of it. In the Church God has appointed first apostles, second prophets, third teachers; then workers of miracles, those with gifts of healing, helpers, administrators, and speakers in various tongues.

Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all work miracles? Do all possess gifts of healing? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret? But earnestly desire the greater gifts.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 100(99):1b-2,3,4,5 | Psalm 100:1b-2,3,4,5**

**答：我们是上主的子民，是他牧放的羊群。 | R. We are his people, the sheep of his flock.**

普世大地，请向上主欢呼！请欢欣事奉上主；请兴高采烈，到他面前歌舞。

**答：我们是上主的子民，是他牧放的羊群。**

你们应明认上主是天主：他造成了我们，我们非他莫属；我们是他的子民，是他牧放的羊群。

**答：我们是上主的子民，是他牧放的羊群。**

请走进他的大门，向他谢恩；请进入他的庭院，向他赞颂。请感谢他，赞美他的圣名。

**答：我们是上主的子民，是他牧放的羊群。**

因为上主良善宽仁，他的慈爱直到永恒；他的忠信世世常存。

**答：我们是上主的子民，是他牧放的羊群。**

Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth! Serve the LORD with gladness; come into his presence with singing.

**R. We are his people, the sheep of his flock.**

Know that the LORD is God! It is he that made us, and we are his; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.

**R. We are his people, the sheep of his flock.**

Enter his gates with thanksgiving, and his courts with praise! Give thanks to him, bless his name!

**R. We are his people, the sheep of his flock.**

For the LORD is good; his steadfast love endures for ever, and his faithfulness to all generations.

**R. We are his people, the sheep of his flock.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣若望福音 19:25-27 | John 19:25-27**

那时候，耶稣的母亲和他母亲的姊妹、克罗帕的妻子玛利亚及玛利亚玛达肋纳，站在耶稣的十字架旁边。

耶稣看见母亲，又看见他所爱的门徒站在旁边，就对母亲说：「女人，看，你的儿子！」然后，又对那门徒说：「看，你的母亲！」从那时起，那门徒就把她接到自己家里。

Standing by the cross of Jesus were his mother, his mother’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene.

When Jesus saw his mother, and the disciple whom he loved standing near, he said to his mother, “Woman, behold, your son!” Then he said to the disciple, “Behold, your mother!” And from that hour the disciple took her into his own home.
