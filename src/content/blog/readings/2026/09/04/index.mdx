## 今日弥撒读经 — 2026年9月4日，星期五

**常年期第二十二周星期五 · 礼仪色：绿**

今天马耳他教区礼仪为：**格林多人前书 4:1–5；圣咏 37:3–6,27–28,39–40；福音前欢呼参咏19:8；路加福音 5:33–39。** 马耳他总教区与 Malta-local Universalis 今日资料一致。([Archdiocese of Malta][1])
万有真原今日弥撒页面目前无法直接抓取，因此今天依照马耳他当地礼仪结构；中文经文依《思高圣经》对应经节忠实表述。格前4章及路5章的思高文本也可由万有真原圣经库核对。([wanyouzhenyuan.cn][2])

### 读经一

**圣保禄宗徒致格林多人前书 4:1–5 / 1 Corinthians 4:1–5**

人应当把我们看作基督的服务员，以及受托管理天主奥秘的人。对于管理人，最重要的要求就是忠信。至于我，无论受你们审断，或受人的法庭审断，对我都算不了什么；甚至我也不审断自己。我虽然自觉良心无愧，却不能因此断定自己就是义人；真正审断我的，是主。因此，在时候尚未来到以前，不要急于判断任何人，只等待主的来临。祂将照亮隐藏在黑暗中的事，也将显露人心深处的意图；到那时，每一个人都要从天主那里得到应得的称誉。

> Let a man so account of us, as of the ministers of Christ, and stewards of the mysteries of God. Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful. But with me it is a very small thing that I should be judged of you, or of man's judgment: yea, I judge not mine own self. For I know nothing by myself; yet am I not hereby justified: but he that judgeth me is the Lord. Therefore judge nothing before the time, until the Lord come, who both will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts: and then shall every man have praise of God.

### 答唱咏

**圣咏 37:3–6, 27–28, 39–40 / Psalm 37:3–6, 27–28, 39–40**

**答：义人的救援来自上主。**

你要信赖上主，努力行善，安居在祂所赐的土地上，并忠诚生活。你要以上主为喜乐，祂必满足你内心真正的愿望。把你的道路托付给上主，依靠祂，祂必亲自成就；祂要使你的正义如晨光显露，使你的公道如正午一样明亮。

> **R. The salvation of the righteous is of the LORD.**
> Trust in the LORD, and do good; so shalt thou dwell in the land, and verily thou shalt be fed. Delight thyself also in the LORD; and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart. Commit thy way unto the LORD; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass. And he shall bring forth thy righteousness as the light, and thy judgment as the noonday.

**答：义人的救援来自上主。**

你要远离邪恶，努力行善，这样你便能长久安居。因为上主喜爱正义，决不遗弃祂忠信的人；祂要永远保护他们。

> **R. The salvation of the righteous is of the LORD.**
> Depart from evil, and do good; and dwell for evermore. For the LORD loveth judgment, and forsaketh not his saints; they are preserved for ever: but the seed of the wicked shall be cut off.

**答：义人的救援来自上主。**

义人的救援来自上主；在患难的时候，祂是他们的保障。上主扶助他们，拯救他们；祂救他们脱离恶人的手，使他们获得安全，因为他们投奔祂。

> **R. The salvation of the righteous is of the LORD.**
> But the salvation of the righteous is of the LORD: he is their strength in the time of trouble. And the LORD shall help them, and deliver them: he shall deliver them from the wicked, and save them, because they trust in him.

### 福音前欢呼

**圣咏 19:8 / Psalm 19:8**

**阿肋路亚，阿肋路亚。** 上主的训令是正直的，使人心欢乐；上主的命令是纯洁的，使人的眼目明亮。**阿肋路亚。**

> **Alleluia, alleluia.** The statutes of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart: the commandment of the LORD is pure, enlightening the eyes. **Alleluia.**

### 福音

**圣路加福音 5:33–39 / Luke 5:33–39**

那时候，经师和法利塞人对耶稣说：“若翰的门徒常常禁食祈祷，法利塞人的门徒也是这样，为什么你的门徒却又吃又喝呢？”耶稣回答他们：“新郎还和伴郎在一起的时候，你们怎能叫他们禁食呢？可是日子将要来到，新郎要从他们中被带走；到了那些日子，他们就要禁食了。”耶稣又给他们讲了一个比喻：“没有人从新衣服上撕下一块来补旧衣服；否则，不但新衣被撕破，从新衣上取下的布也和旧衣不相称。也没有人把新酒装进旧皮囊；否则，新酒会涨破皮囊，酒流出来，皮囊也毁坏了。新酒应该装在新皮囊里。喝惯了陈酒的人，也不会立刻想喝新酒，因为他说：‘还是陈酒好。’”

> And they said unto him, Why do the disciples of John fast often, and make prayers, and likewise the disciples of the Pharisees; but thine eat and drink? And he said unto them, Can ye make the children of the bridechamber fast, while the bridegroom is with them? But the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken away from them, and then shall they fast in those days. And he spake also a parable unto them; No man putteth a piece of a new garment upon an old; if otherwise, then both the new maketh a rent, and the piece that was taken out of the new agreeth not with the old. And no man putteth new wine into old bottles; else the new wine will burst the bottles, and be spilled, and the bottles shall perish. But new wine must be put into new bottles; and both are preserved. No man also having drunk old wine straightway desireth new: for he saith, The old is better.
