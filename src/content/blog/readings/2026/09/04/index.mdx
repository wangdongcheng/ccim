## 读经一 · First Reading

**格林多人前书 4:1–5**

弟兄姊妹们：

人应当把我们看作基督的仆人，以及受托管理天主奥秘的人。对于受托管理的人，所要求的就是忠信。

至于我，无论受到你们的审断，或受到人的法庭审断，对我来说都是极小的事；我甚至也不审断自己。虽然我的良心没有责备我，但这并不能因此证明我无罪；审断我的，是主。

所以，在时候来到以前，不要判断任何人，要等到主来临。祂要把隐藏在黑暗中的事显露出来，也要揭示人心深处的意念。那时，每个人都要从天主那里得到应有的称许。

**1 Corinthians 4:1–5 · KJV**

Let a man so account of us, as of the ministers of Christ, and stewards of the mysteries of God.

Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.

But with me it is a very small thing that I should be judged of you, or of man's judgment: yea, I judge not mine own self.

For I know nothing by myself; yet am I not hereby justified: but he that judgeth me is the Lord.

Therefore judge nothing before the time, until the Lord come, who both will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts: and then shall every man have praise of God.

## 福音 · Gospel

**路加福音 5:33–39**

那时候，经师和法利塞人对耶稣说：「若翰的门徒常常禁食祈祷，法利塞人的门徒也是这样；为什么你的门徒却吃喝呢？」

耶稣回答他们说：「新郎还与婚宴的客人在一起时，你们怎能叫他们禁食呢？但日子将要来到，新郎要从他们中被带走；到了那些日子，他们就要禁食。」

耶稣又给他们讲了一个比喻：没有人会从新衣服上撕下一块布，补在旧衣服上；否则，不但撕坏了新衣服，从新衣服撕下来的布也与旧衣服不相称。

同样，也没有人把新酒装进旧皮囊；否则，新酒会胀破皮囊，酒流出来，皮囊也毁坏。新酒必须装进新皮囊。

喝惯了陈酒的人，也不会马上想喝新酒，因为他说：「还是陈酒好。」

**Luke 5:33–39 · KJV**

And they said unto him, Why do the disciples of John fast often, and make prayers, and likewise the disciples of the Pharisees; but thine eat and drink?

And he said unto them, Can ye make the children of the bridechamber fast, while the bridegroom is with them?

But the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken away from them, and then shall they fast in those days.

And he spake also a parable unto them; No man putteth a piece of a new garment upon an old; if otherwise, then both the new maketh a rent, and the piece that was taken out of the new agreeth not with the old.

And no man putteth new wine into old bottles; else the new wine will burst the bottles, and be spilled, and the bottles shall perish.

But new wine must be put into new bottles; and both are preserved.

No man also having drunk old wine straightway desireth new: for he saith, The old is better.
