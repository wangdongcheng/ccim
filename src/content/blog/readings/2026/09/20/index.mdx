常年期第二十五主日 | Twenty-fifth Sunday in Ordinary Time

2026年9月20日 · 星期日 | September 20, 2026 · Sunday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**依撒意亚先知书 55:6-9 | Isaiah 55:6-9**

趁着还能找到上主的时候，你们应当寻找他；趁他仍在近处的时候，你们应当呼求他。

恶人应离开自己的道路，作恶的人应抛弃自己的思念，回到上主那里；上主必怜悯他。让他归向我们的天主，因为天主乐于宽恕。

上主说：“我的思念不是你们的思念，你们的道路也不是我的道路。正如高天远远超过大地，我的道路也远远超过你们的道路，我的思念也远远超过你们的思念。”

Seek the Lord while he may be found; call upon him while he is near.

Let the wicked abandon their way and the unrighteous forsake their thoughts. Let them return to the Lord, who will show mercy, and to our God, who forgives abundantly.

The Lord declares: “My thoughts are not your thoughts, nor are your ways my ways. As the heavens rise high above the earth, so my ways rise above your ways and my thoughts above your thoughts.”

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 145:2-3,8-9,17-18 | Psalm 145:2-3,8-9,17-18**

**答：上主亲近一切呼号他的人。 | R. The Lord is near to all who call upon him.**

上主，我要每日赞美你，
永远颂扬你的圣名。
上主伟大，极应受赞颂；
他的伟大深不可测。

**答：上主亲近一切呼号他的人。**

上主慈悲宽仁，
缓于发怒，富于慈爱。
上主善待万有，
怜爱他所创造的一切。

**答：上主亲近一切呼号他的人。**

上主的一切道路都是正义的，
他的一切作为都是慈爱的。
上主亲近一切呼号他的人，
亲近一切诚心呼号他的人。

**答：上主亲近一切呼号他的人。**

Every day I will bless you
and praise your name for ever.
Great is the Lord and greatly to be praised;
his greatness is beyond searching.

**R. The Lord is near to all who call upon him.**

The Lord is gracious and merciful,
slow to anger and rich in steadfast love.
The Lord is good to all,
and his compassion rests upon all he has made.

**R. The Lord is near to all who call upon him.**

The Lord is just in all his ways
and loving in all his works.
He is near to all who call upon him,
to all who call upon him sincerely.

**R. The Lord is near to all who call upon him.**

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**斐理伯人书 1:20-24,27 | Philippians 1:20c-24,27a**

弟兄姊妹们：无论我活着或死去，总愿基督在我身上受到显扬。因为对我来说，生活就是基督，死亡反而是收获。

可是，如果继续在肉身内生活，能使我的工作结出果实，我便不知道应当选择哪一样。我被这两者催迫着：我渴望离世与基督同在，因为这好得多；然而，为了你们，我留在肉身内更加必要。

无论如何，你们的生活应当与基督的福音相称。

Brothers and sisters: Whether through life or through death, my desire is that Christ may be honored in my body. For to me, living is Christ and dying is gain.

If I continue to live in the flesh, my labor can still bear fruit; therefore I cannot say which I would choose. I am drawn in both directions: I long to depart and be with Christ, which is far better, yet remaining in the flesh is more necessary for your sake.

Whatever happens, conduct yourselves in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 20:1-16 | Matthew 20:1-16**

那时候，耶稣给门徒讲了这个比喻：

“天国好比一个家主，清早出去，为自己的葡萄园雇用工人。他同工人议定一天一个银币，便派他们到葡萄园里去。

“大约第三时辰，他又出去，看见另有些人闲站在街市上，就对他们说：‘你们也到我的葡萄园里去吧！我会给你们合理的报酬。’他们便去了。

“约在第六时辰和第九时辰，他又出去照样办理。到了第十一时辰，他再次出去，看见还有人站在那里，便问他们：‘你们为什么整天闲站在这里？’他们回答：‘因为没有人雇用我们。’他便说：‘你们也到葡萄园里去吧！’

“到了傍晚，葡萄园主人对管事说：‘把工人叫来，从最后来的开始，直到最先来的，把工资发给他们。’那些第十一时辰才来的人，每人领了一个银币。

“最先来的以为自己必会多领一些，可是他们也每人领了一个银币。他们拿到工资以后，便埋怨家主说：‘最后来的这些人只工作了一个小时，你竟把他们和我们同等看待；我们可是整天劳苦，又忍受了炎热。’

“家主回答其中一人说：‘朋友，我没有亏待你。你不是同我议定一个银币吗？拿你的工资走吧！我愿意给这最后来的和给你的一样。难道我不能随意处理自己的财物吗？还是因为我慷慨，你就心生嫉妒？’

“这样，最后的，将成为最先的；最先的，将成为最后的。”

At that time, Jesus told his disciples this parable:

“The kingdom of heaven is like a landowner who went out early in the morning to hire laborers for his vineyard. After agreeing with them on a denarius for the day, he sent them into his vineyard.

“About the third hour he went out again and saw others standing idle in the marketplace. He told them, ‘You also go into the vineyard, and I will give you what is right.’ So they went.

“He went out again about the sixth and the ninth hours and did the same. About the eleventh hour he found others still standing there and asked, ‘Why have you stood here idle all day?’ They answered, ‘Because no one has hired us.’ He said to them, ‘You also go into the vineyard.’

“When evening came, the vineyard owner told his steward, ‘Call the laborers and pay them their wages, beginning with the last and ending with the first.’ Those hired at the eleventh hour came, and each received a denarius.

“When the first workers came, they expected to receive more, but each of them also received a denarius. After receiving it, they complained against the landowner: ‘These last workers labored for only one hour, yet you have made them equal to us, who bore the burden and heat of the whole day.’

“He answered one of them, ‘Friend, I am doing you no wrong. Did you not agree with me for a denarius? Take what belongs to you and go. I choose to give to this last worker as I give to you. Am I not permitted to do what I choose with what belongs to me? Or do you resent my generosity?’

“Thus the last will be first, and the first last.”

最后的，将成为最先的；最先的，将成为最后的。 | The last will be first, and the first last.
