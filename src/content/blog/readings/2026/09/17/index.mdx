## 读经一 | First Reading

**格林多人前书 15:1-11 | 1 Corinthians 15:1-11**

弟兄们：我愿你们重新认清我从前传给你们的福音；你们接受了它，也靠它站立。只要你们持守我所传的话，便会因这福音得救，否则你们的信便落了空。

我把自己所领受、也最为重要的事传给了你们：基督按照经书所载，为我们的罪而死，且被埋葬；又按照经书所载，第三天复活了。他先显现给刻法，继而显现给十二宗徒；以后一次显现给五百多位弟兄，其中大多数至今仍在，也有一些已经安眠。随后，他显现给雅各伯，又显现给众宗徒；最后，也显现给我这个仿佛未足月出生的人。

我原是宗徒中最小的，不配称为宗徒，因为我曾迫害天主的教会。然而，因天主的恩宠，我成了今日的我；他赐给我的恩宠没有落空。我比众人更加劳苦——其实不是我，而是天主的恩宠与我同在。总之，无论是我还是他们，我们都这样宣讲，你们也这样相信了。

Brothers and sisters: I remind you of the Gospel I preached to you. You received it, you stand firm in it, and through it you are being saved, provided you hold fast to the message and have not believed in vain.

I handed on to you what I had first received and what matters above all: Christ died for our sins in accordance with the Scriptures; he was buried; and on the third day he was raised in accordance with the Scriptures. He appeared first to Cephas and then to the Twelve. Afterward he appeared at one time to more than five hundred believers, most of whom were still living, though some had fallen asleep. Then he appeared to James, then to all the apostles, and last of all he appeared also to me, as to one born at the wrong time.

I am the least of the apostles and do not deserve that name, because I persecuted the Church of God. Yet by God's grace I am what I am, and his grace toward me has not been fruitless. I labored more abundantly than the others—though it was not I, but God's grace working with me. Whether it was I or they, this is what we preach, and this is what you believed.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 118:1-2,15-17,28,21 | Psalm 118:1-2,15-17,28,21**

**答：请你们颂谢上主，因为他美善宽仁。 | R. Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good.**

请你们颂谢上主，因为他美善宽仁，
他的慈爱永远常存。
愿以色列子民宣告：
他的慈爱永远常存。

**答：请你们颂谢上主，因为他美善宽仁。**

上主的右手获得胜利，
上主的右手把我高高举起。
我不至于死，仍要生存，
并要宣扬上主的作为。

**答：请你们颂谢上主，因为他美善宽仁。**

你是我的天主，我要称谢你；
你是我的天主，我要颂扬你。
我感谢你，因为你俯允了我，
并成为我的救援。

**答：请你们颂谢上主，因为他美善宽仁。**

Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good;
his steadfast love endures for ever.
Let Israel now proclaim:
his steadfast love endures for ever.

**R. Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good.**

The right hand of the Lord has triumphed;
the right hand of the Lord has raised me up.
I shall not die, but live,
and recount the deeds of the Lord.

**R. Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good.**

You are my God, and I give thanks to you;
you are my God, and I exalt you.
I thank you, for you have answered me
and have become my salvation.

**R. Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 7:36-50 | Luke 7:36-50**

那时候，一个法利塞人请耶稣同他吃饭，耶稣便到他家中赴席。城里有一个被视为罪人的妇人，听说耶稣在那里，就带来一玉瓶香液。她站在耶稣身后，靠近他的脚哭泣；泪水湿了他的脚，她便用头发擦干，不断亲吻他的脚，并抹上香液。

那位主人看见了，心里便想："这人若真是先知，应该知道触摸他的是谁，是怎样的女人；她是个罪人。"耶稣对他说："西满，我有话对你说。"他答道："老师，请说。"耶稣便说："一个债主有两个债户，一个欠五百银币，另一个欠五十。他们都无力偿还，债主便宽免了二人的债。那么，哪一个会更爱他呢？"西满回答："我想，是那获宽免较多的。"耶稣说："你判断得对。"

耶稣转向那妇人，对西满说："你看见这妇人吗？我进了你的家，你没有给我水洗脚，她却用眼泪湿了我的脚，又用头发擦干。你没有亲吻我，她却从我进来以后不断亲吻我的脚。你没有用油抹我的头，她却用香液抹我的脚。所以我告诉你：她许多的罪获得了赦免，因而显出深厚的爱；那获得赦免少的，所显的爱也少。"

耶稣遂对妇人说："你的罪已经赦免了。"同席的人彼此心想："这人是谁，竟然连罪也赦免？"耶稣又对妇人说："你的信德救了你，安心回去吧！"

At that time, one of the Pharisees invited Jesus to dine with him, and Jesus entered his house and took his place at table. A woman of the city, known as a sinner, learned that he was there and brought an alabaster jar of ointment. Standing behind him at his feet, she wept until her tears wet his feet. She wiped them with her hair, kissed them repeatedly, and anointed them with the ointment.

When the host saw this, he thought to himself, "If this man were a prophet, he would know who is touching him and what sort of woman she is—a sinner." Jesus said to him, "Simon, I have something to say to you." He answered, "Teacher, say it." Jesus told him of two debtors: one owed five hundred denarii and the other fifty. Since neither could pay, the creditor freely forgave them both. "Which of them will love him more?" Simon replied, "I suppose the one whose greater debt was forgiven." Jesus said, "You have judged rightly."

Turning toward the woman, Jesus said to Simon, "Do you see this woman? I entered your house, and you gave me no water for my feet; she has wet them with her tears and wiped them with her hair. You gave me no kiss; she has not ceased kissing my feet. You did not anoint my head with oil; she has anointed my feet with ointment. Therefore I tell you, her many sins have been forgiven, and so she has shown great love. But the one who is forgiven little loves little."

Then he said to the woman, "Your sins are forgiven." Those at table began asking among themselves, "Who is this, who even forgives sins?" But Jesus said to the woman, "Your faith has saved you; go in peace." 🟥
