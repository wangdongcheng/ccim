圣玛窦宗徒（圣史）庆日 | Feast of Saint Matthew, Apostle and Evangelist

2026年9月21日 · 星期一 | September 21, 2026 · Monday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 4:1-7,11-13 | Ephesians 4:1-7,11-13**

弟兄姊妹们：我这在主内被囚的人劝勉你们：你们的生活应当与所蒙受的召叫相称。凡事要谦逊、温和、忍耐，在爱德中彼此担待，以和平作为联系，竭力保持圣神所赐的合一。

只有一个身体和一个圣神，正如你们蒙召时同有一个希望；只有一个主、一个信德、一个洗礼；只有一个天主和众人之父，他超越众人，贯通众人，并在众人之内。

可是，我们每个人都按照基督恩赐的尺度领受了恩宠。他所赐的，有些人成为宗徒，有些人成为先知，有些人成为传福音者，有些人成为牧者和教师；这是为了装备圣徒去执行服务的工作，建树基督的身体，直到我们众人对天主子都有一致的信德和认识，成为成熟的人，达到基督圆满年龄的程度。

Brothers and sisters: I, a prisoner for the Lord, urge you to live in a manner worthy of the calling you have received. Be humble and gentle in everything; be patient, bearing with one another in love, and strive to preserve the unity given by the Spirit through the bond of peace.

There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were called to one hope. There is one Lord, one faith, one baptism, and one God and Father of all, who is above all, works through all, and is in all.

Grace, however, has been given to each of us according to the measure of Christ’s gift. He appointed some to be apostles, some prophets, some evangelists, and some pastors and teachers. Their task is to equip the saints for service and to build up the body of Christ, until all of us attain unity in faith and in knowledge of the Son of God, becoming mature and reaching the fullness found in Christ.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 19:2-5 | Psalm 19:1-4a**

**答：它们的声音传遍普世。 | R. Their voice goes out through all the earth.**

诸天陈述天主的光荣，
穹苍宣扬他双手的化工。
白昼向白昼传递言语，
黑夜向黑夜传授知识。

**答：它们的声音传遍普世。**

这不是言辞，也不是话语，
人听不见它们的声音；
可是它们的声音传遍大地，
它们的话传到世界尽头。

**答：它们的声音传遍普世。**

The heavens proclaim the glory of God,
and the firmament displays the work of his hands.
Day speaks its message to day,
and night imparts knowledge to night.

**R. Their voice goes out through all the earth.**

There is no speech and there are no words;
their voice cannot be heard.
Yet their message extends through all the earth,
and their words reach to the ends of the world.

**R. Their voice goes out through all the earth.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 9:9-13 | Matthew 9:9-13**

那时候，耶稣继续前行，看见一个名叫玛窦的人坐在税关上，便对他说：“跟随我！”玛窦就起来跟随了他。

后来，耶稣在屋里坐席，许多税吏和罪人也来到那里，同耶稣和他的门徒一起吃饭。法利塞人看见了，便问他的门徒：“你们的老师为什么同税吏和罪人一起吃饭？”

耶稣听见后说：“需要医生的不是健康的人，而是患病的人。你们去学习这句话的意义：‘我所喜爱的是仁爱，而不是祭献。’因为我来，不是为召叫义人，而是为召叫罪人。”

At that time, as Jesus continued on his way, he saw a man named Matthew sitting at the tax office. Jesus said to him, “Follow me.” Matthew rose and followed him.

Later, while Jesus was at table in the house, many tax collectors and sinners came and ate with him and his disciples. When the Pharisees saw this, they asked his disciples, “Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?”

Jesus heard them and replied, “Those who are healthy have no need of a physician, but those who are ill do. Go and learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy, not sacrifice.’ For I came not to call the righteous, but sinners.”

跟随我！ | Follow me.
