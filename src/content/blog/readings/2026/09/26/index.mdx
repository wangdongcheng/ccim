## 读经一 | First Reading

**训道篇 11:9-12:8 | Ecclesiastes 11:9-12:8**

少年人，你在青春时应当欢乐；在年轻的岁月里，要使自己的心愉快。你可以随心所欲，随眼所悦而行，但要知道：天主必会审判你所做的一切。要从心中除去烦恼，使身体远离痛苦，因为青春与少年都是短暂的。

趁你年轻，在灾祸的日子来到以前，在那些使你说“我不喜欢这些日子”的岁月临近以前，你要记住你的造主。不要等到太阳、光明、月亮和星辰失去光辉，雨后云彩又再出现；那时，看守房屋的人颤抖，强壮的人弯下腰，推磨的人因人数稀少而停工，从窗户向外观看的人眼目昏花，临街的门关闭，磨声变得低微；人因鸟鸣而惊醒，歌声也都衰弱。

那时，人惧怕高处，也害怕路上的危险；杏树开花，蚱蜢拖着身体前行，欲望也衰退。因为人将回到永远的家，哀悼者徘徊在街上。银链尚未折断，金灯尚未破碎，水罐尚未在泉旁打破，井边的轮子尚未损坏以前，你要记住造主。尘土将归回原来的大地，气息则归于赐予它的天主。

训道者说：虚而又虚，万事皆虚。

Rejoice, young man, while you are young, and let your heart be glad in the days of your youth. Follow the ways of your heart and what your eyes desire, but know that for all these things God will bring you into judgment. Remove anxiety from your mind and put pain away from your body, for youth and the dawn of life are fleeting.

Remember your Creator in the days of your youth, before the days of trouble come and the years draw near when you will say, “I find no pleasure in them”; before the sun, the light, the moon, and the stars grow dark, and the clouds return after the rain; when the keepers of the house tremble, the strong men stoop, the grinders cease because they are few, and those looking through the windows grow dim; when the doors toward the street are shut, the sound of grinding becomes low, one rises at the voice of a bird, and all the daughters of song are brought low.

Then people fear what is high and are terrified by dangers on the road; the almond tree blossoms, the grasshopper drags itself along, and desire fails. For man goes to his eternal home, while mourners walk through the streets. Remember him before the silver cord is snapped, the golden bowl is broken, the pitcher is shattered at the spring, or the wheel is broken at the well; then the dust returns to the earth as it was, and the spirit returns to God who gave it.

Vanity of vanities, says the Preacher; all is vanity.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 90:3-6,12-14,17 | Psalm 90:3-6,12-14,17**

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。 | R. In every age, O Lord, you have been our refuge.**

你使世人归于灰土，
又说：“亚当的子女，你们归来吧！”
因为一千年在你眼中，
好像刚刚过去的昨天，又像夜里的一更。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

你席卷他们，好像一场梦，
又像清晨新生的青草：
早晨生长茂盛，
晚上便萎谢枯干。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

求你教导我们数算自己的岁月，
使我们获得内心的智慧。
上主，求你回转！还要等到何时？
求你怜悯你的仆人。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

求你使我们清晨饱享你的慈爱，
使我们一生欢欣喜乐。
愿上主、我们的天主广施恩宠，
使我们双手的工作顺利成功。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

You turn man back to the dust,
and say, “Return, O children of men.”
For a thousand years in your sight
are like yesterday when it is past,
or like a watch in the night.

**R. In every age, O Lord, you have been our refuge.**

You sweep them away like a dream,
like grass renewed in the morning:
in the morning it flourishes and grows,
but in the evening it fades and withers.

**R. In every age, O Lord, you have been our refuge.**

Teach us to number our days,
that we may gain a heart of wisdom.
Return, O Lord! How long?
Have compassion on your servants.

**R. In every age, O Lord, you have been our refuge.**

Satisfy us in the morning with your steadfast love,
that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.
Let the favor of the Lord our God rest upon us,
and establish the work of our hands.

**R. In every age, O Lord, you have been our refuge.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 9:43-45 | Luke 9:43-45**

那时候，众人都惊奇于天主的伟大。当众人还在赞叹耶稣所做的一切时，祂对门徒说：“你们要把这些话牢牢记住：人子将被交在人的手中。”

但是门徒不明白这句话；这话的意义向他们隐藏起来，使他们不能领悟，而他们也不敢询问祂这句话的意思。

At that time, all were astonished at the majesty of God. While everyone was marveling at everything Jesus did, he said to his disciples, “Let these words sink into your ears: the Son of Man is to be delivered into the hands of men.”

But they did not understand this saying. Its meaning was concealed from them so that they could not perceive it, and they were afraid to ask him about it.

## 反省 | Reflection

人子将被交在人的手里，这是天父永恒的给予，圣三永远的救恩。圣子在时间之内空虚自己，取了如你我一样的形体，被交给人类，交给纳匝肋的一对朴实夫妻手里。人子将被交在人的手里，这是耶稣自己的选择。祂把自己交在若翰手中，加入罪人之列；把自己交在公议会和罗马人手里，任由他们所代表的人类权势嘲弄和倾轧，且被遗弃在十字架上。复活的基督把自己交在祂的奥体教会中，由祂的跟随者把祂的福音和德能带到世界各处，给人类带来生命的喜乐。基督也借感恩祭，把自己交在领受者手里，用自己的体血滋养人灵。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

The Son of Man will be delivered into the hands of men. This is the Father’s eternal gift and the everlasting salvation of the Holy Trinity. Within time, the Son emptied himself, took a form like yours and mine, and was entrusted to humanity—to the hands of a humble couple in Nazareth.

The Son of Man will be delivered into the hands of men. This is Jesus’ own choice. He placed himself in John’s hands and took his place among sinners; he placed himself in the hands of the Sanhedrin and the Romans, allowing the human powers they represented to mock and crush him, and he was abandoned upon the cross. The risen Christ entrusts himself to his Mystical Body, the Church, so that his followers may carry his Gospel and his saving power throughout the world and bring humanity the joy of life. Through the Eucharist, Christ also places himself into the hands of those who receive him, nourishing their souls with his Body and Blood.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

他们害怕十字架。伯多禄本人在凯撒勒雅斐理伯一带作出那庄严的信仰宣认之后，当耶稣再次谈到这件事时，便责谏主说：“主，绝对不可以！这事绝不能发生！”他害怕十字架。

然而，害怕十字架的不只是门徒，也不只是伯多禄；耶稣自己也害怕十字架。祂无法欺骗自己，祂知道将要发生什么。祂的恐惧如此强烈，以致在那个星期四晚上流下血汗。耶稣的恐惧如此强烈，祂所说的话几乎与伯多禄相同。几乎相同：“父啊，求祢从我这里撤去这杯”；但祂接着说：“愿祢的旨意承行。”区别就在这里。（……）

我们必须祈求恩宠，使我们在十字架来到时不要逃避。害怕是真的；十字架确实使我们恐惧。（……）在十字架上的耶稣身旁，最接近祂的是祂的母亲、祂的妈妈。让我们向她祈祷；我们应当求她赐给我们恩宠：不是除去我们的害怕——害怕十字架是自然的——而是不让我们因恐惧而惊慌失措、逃离十字架。她曾站在那里，也知道怎样站在十字架旁。

（教宗方济各，圣玛尔大之家弥撒讲道，2013年9月28日）

They were afraid of the cross. Peter himself, after that solemn confession in the region of Caesarea Philippi, when Jesus said this once again, rebuked the Lord, saying to Him: “No, never, Lord! Not this!” He was afraid of the cross. But it was not only the disciples, not only Peter; Jesus Himself was afraid of the cross. He could not deceive Himself; He knew. And so great was His fear that on that Thursday evening, He sweated blood. So great was Jesus’ fear that He spoke almost the same words as Peter. Almost: “Father, take this cup away from me”; but He added, “Your will must be done”. That is the difference. (…)

We must ask for the grace not to flee from the cross when it comes. With fear, it is true; it frightens us. (…) Very close to Jesus on the cross is His mother, His mama. Let us pray to her, and we would do well to ask her to grant us the grace not to take away our fear – it is natural to be afraid of the cross – but not to let us be terrified and flee from the cross. She was there, and she knows how to stand by the cross. (Pope Francis, Homily at Santa Marta, 28 September 2013)
