## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄则克耳先知书 18:25-28 | Ezekiel 18:25-28**

上主这样说：“你们却说：‘上主的作法不公平！’以色列家，请听：是我的作法不公平，还是你们的作法不公平呢？

义人若离弃正义而行恶，便会因所犯的罪而丧亡。恶人若离开自己所行的恶事，遵守法律，履行正义，便能保全自己的生命。因为他反省以后，离弃了所犯的一切罪过，所以他必定生存，不致丧亡。”

Thus says the Lord: “Yet you say, ‘The way of the Lord is not just.’ Hear now, O house of Israel: Is my way unjust, or is it not your ways that are unjust?

When a righteous person turns away from righteousness and commits evil, that person shall die because of the sin committed. But when a wicked person turns away from the wickedness committed and does what is lawful and right, that person shall preserve life. Because that person considered and turned away from all the transgressions committed, that person shall surely live and shall not die.”

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 25:4-9 | Psalm 25:4-9**

**答：上主，求你念及你的仁爱。 | R. Remember your mercy, O Lord.**

上主，请指示我你的道路，
教导我你的途径。
求你以真理引领并教训我，
因为你是救我的天主，我终日仰望你。

**答：上主，求你念及你的仁爱。**

上主，求你念及你的仁爱和慈悲，
因为它们自古常存。
求你不要记念我青春的罪过与过犯；
上主，求你按你的慈爱和美善垂念我。

**答：上主，求你念及你的仁爱。**

上主温良而正直，
因此指示罪人应走的道路；
祂引导谦卑的人履行正义，
教导谦逊的人遵循祂的道路。

**答：上主，求你念及你的仁爱。**

Make known to me your ways, O Lord;
teach me your paths.
Lead me in your truth and teach me,
for you are the God of my salvation;
for you I wait all the day.

**R. Remember your mercy, O Lord.**

Remember your mercy, O Lord,
and your steadfast love, for they are from of old.
Do not remember the sins of my youth or my transgressions;
according to your steadfast love remember me,
for the sake of your goodness, O Lord.

**R. Remember your mercy, O Lord.**

Good and upright is the Lord;
therefore he instructs sinners in the way.
He leads the humble in what is right
and teaches the humble his path.

**R. Remember your mercy, O Lord.**

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**斐理伯书 2:1-11 | Philippians 2:1-11**

弟兄们：如果你们在基督内得到鼓励，在爱德中得到安慰，在圣神内彼此相通，并有慈爱和怜悯，就要使我喜乐圆满：你们应意见一致，怀有同样的爱德，一心一意，思想同样的事。无论做什么，都不可出于自私或虚荣；反要谦逊地看别人胜过自己。各人不要只顾自己的事，也要关心别人的事。

你们应怀有基督耶稣所怀有的心情：祂虽具有天主的形体，却没有把与天主同等的地位视为应当把持的，反而空虚自己，取了奴仆的形体，成为人的模样；祂以人的形态出现，贬抑自己，服从至死，且死在十字架上。

因此，天主极其举扬祂，赐给祂一个超越一切名号的名，使天上、地上和地下的一切，一听到耶稣的名号都屈膝叩拜；使一切唇舌都明认耶稣基督是主，以光荣天主圣父。

Brothers and sisters: If there is any encouragement in Christ, any comfort from love, any fellowship in the Spirit, any affection and compassion, then complete my joy: be of the same mind, have the same love, be united in heart, and think as one. Do nothing from selfish ambition or conceit, but in humility regard others as better than yourselves. Let each of you look not only to your own interests but also to the interests of others.

Have among yourselves the mind that was in Christ Jesus: though he was in the form of God, he did not regard equality with God as something to be grasped. Instead, he emptied himself, taking the form of a servant and being born in human likeness. Found in human form, he humbled himself and became obedient unto death, even death on a cross.

Therefore God highly exalted him and bestowed upon him the name above every name, so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow—in heaven, on earth, and under the earth—and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 21:28-32 | Matthew 21:28-32**

那时候，耶稣对司祭长和民间长老说：“你们以为怎样？有一个人有两个儿子。他走到第一个儿子面前说：‘孩子，你今天到葡萄园里去工作吧！’他回答：‘我不去。’可是后来他悔悟了，便去了。

“父亲又去对另一个儿子说了同样的话。那儿子回答：‘父亲，我去。’但他实际上没有去。两人当中，哪一个履行了父亲的意愿呢？”

他们回答：“第一个。”

耶稣对他们说：“我实在告诉你们：税吏和娼妓要比你们先进入天主的国。因为若翰来到你们这里，走的是正义的道路，你们却不相信他；税吏和娼妓反而相信了他。你们虽然看见了，却仍没有悔悟，也没有相信他。”

At that time, Jesus said to the chief priests and elders of the people, “What do you think? A man had two sons. He went to the first and said, ‘Son, go and work in the vineyard today.’ The son answered, ‘I will not,’ but afterward he repented and went.

“The father went to the other son and said the same. That son answered, ‘I go, sir,’ but did not go. Which of the two did the will of his father?”

They answered, “The first.”

Jesus said to them, “Truly, I tell you: tax collectors and harlots are entering the kingdom of God before you. For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you did not believe him; but the tax collectors and harlots believed him. Even after you saw this, you did not afterward repent and believe him.”

## 反省 | Reflection

说不去而后悔悟了的，是指税吏、娼妓等罪人；说去但实际上没有去的，是指以公议会为代表的自以为义的宗教领袖。我们与天主的关系，并不建立在言语上，而是建立在我们的心灵和行动上。重要的不是过去，而是当下。如果过去的不堪激发我们重新皈依，那不堪就成了祝福；如果过去的优秀让现在的我们自负自大，那优秀就成了诅咒。让我们经验到天主之慈悲的，往往是我们见不得人的那些部分。税吏和罪人坦承自己的破碎，无可自夸，因为他们明白，自己依靠上主的怜爱。愿我们的优秀引我们感恩，我们的不堪引我们悔改。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

The one who said he would not go but later repented represents sinners such as tax collectors and prostitutes. The one who said he would go but did not actually go represents the self-righteous religious leaders embodied by the Sanhedrin. Our relationship with God is not founded upon words but upon our hearts and actions. What matters is not the past but the present.

If the shame of our past moves us to conversion, that shame becomes a blessing. If our past excellence makes us proud and self-important today, that excellence becomes a curse. Often it is precisely the parts of ourselves we are ashamed to show that enable us to experience God’s mercy. Tax collectors and sinners openly acknowledge their brokenness and have nothing in which to boast, because they know that they depend upon the Lord’s merciful love. May our strengths lead us to gratitude, and our failures lead us to repentance.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

如果你的心不是一颗悔改的心，如果你不聆听上主，不接受纠正，也不信赖祂，你就有一颗不肯悔改的心。然而，这些假善人听见耶稣谈及税吏和娼妓便感到冒犯，私下里却去找他们，或为放纵自己的情欲，或为同他们做生意——但这一切都在暗中进行——他们自称是“纯洁的”！上主不要这样的人。（……）

这些人无法接受救恩；他们把自己封闭在救恩之外。“我必在你中间留下一个谦卑贫苦的人民；他们必依赖上主的名。”他们一生都是如此。直到今天，这仍然是真实的，不是吗？当天主的圣善子民谦卑地生活，在对上主的信德和信赖中找到自己的财富——这些谦卑、贫苦而信赖上主的人，就是获得救恩的人；这就是教会应走的道路，不是吗？教会必须走这条路，而不是另一条不聆听上主声音、不接受纠正、也不信赖上主的道路。（……）

当我们能够对上主说：“主啊，这些是我的罪——不是他的，也不是她的，而是我的……它们是我的。求祢承担这些罪，这样我便会得救。”当我们能够这样做时，我们就会成为那美好的子民，成为那“谦卑贫苦、信赖上主之名的人民”。愿上主赐给我们这份恩宠。

（教宗方济各，2014年12月16日弥撒讲道）

If your heart is not a repentant heart, if you do not listen to the Lord, if you do not accept correction and do not trust in Him, you have an unrepentant heart. But these hypocrites who take offence at what Jesus says about tax collectors and prostitutes, yet then secretly went to them, either to indulge their passions or to do business – but all in secret – they were ‘pure’! And the Lord does not want them. (…) These people cannot receive Salvation. They are closed off to Salvation. “I will leave in the midst of you a people humble and lowly; they shall seek refuge in the name of the Lord”, for their whole lives.

And this remains true to this day, does it not? When we see God’s holy people who are humble, who find their riches in faith in the Lord, in trust in the Lord – the humble, lowly people who trust in the Lord: these are the saved, and this is the way of the Church, is it not? She must take this path, not the other path that does not listen to the voice, that does not accept correction and does not trust in the Lord. (…). When we are able to say to the Lord: “Lord, these are my sins – they are not his, or hers, they are mine… They are mine. Take them upon yourself, and so I shall be saved” – when we are able to do this, we shall be the beautiful people, the “humble and poor people”, who trust in the name of the Lord. May the Lord grant us this grace. (Pope Francis, Homily of 16 December 2014)
