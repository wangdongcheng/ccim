## 读经一 | First Reading

**圣保禄宗徒致格林多人前书 12:31-13:13 | 1 Corinthians 12:31-13:13**

弟兄们：你们要热切追求更大的恩赐。保禄指出一条更高超的道路：即使能说人间和天使的语言，能明白奥秘、拥有知识和移山的信德，甚至倾尽财物、舍弃生命，若没有爱，仍一无所成。

爱是含忍而慈祥的；不嫉妒，不夸张，不自大，不自私自利，也不计较人的过错。爱喜爱真理，包容、相信、盼望并忍耐一切。先知之恩、语言和知识都要过去；如今所认识的只是部分，到了圆满时，便要面对面地认识。现今存在的有信、望、爱这三样，其中最大的是爱。

Brothers and sisters: Strive eagerly for the greater gifts. Paul shows a still more excellent way: without love, eloquence in human or angelic tongues, prophetic insight, knowledge, faith that moves mountains, generosity, and even self-sacrifice amount to nothing.

Love is patient and kind. It is neither jealous nor boastful, not arrogant or self-seeking, and keeps no account of wrongs. It rejoices in the right; it bears, believes, hopes, and endures all things. Prophecy, tongues, and knowledge will pass away; our present knowledge is partial, but one day we shall see face to face. Faith, hope, and love abide, and the greatest of these is love.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 33:2-5,12-22 | Psalm 33:2-5,12,22**

**答：上主选为产业的国民真是有福！ | R. Blessed the people the Lord has chosen to be his own.**

请你们弹琴称谢上主，弹奏十弦琴歌颂上主；向他高唱新歌，在欢呼声中弹琴奏乐。

**答：上主选为产业的国民真是有福！**

因为上主的话句句真实，他的一切作为完全可靠。他爱护正义和公理；他的慈爱弥漫大地。

**答：上主选为产业的国民真是有福！**

尊上主为天主的民族真是有福；上主选为产业的国民真是有福。上主，求你向我们广施慈爱，满足我们对你的期待。

**答：上主选为产业的国民真是有福！**

Give thanks to the Lord with the lyre; make melody to him with the harp of ten strings. Sing to him a new song; play skillfully with loud shouts.

**R. Blessed the people the Lord has chosen to be his own.**

For the word of the Lord is upright; and all his work is done in faithfulness. He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the steadfast love of the Lord.

**R. Blessed the people the Lord has chosen to be his own.**

Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord, the people whom he has chosen as his heritage. Let thy steadfast love, O Lord, be upon us, even as we hope in thee.

**R. Blessed the people the Lord has chosen to be his own.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**圣路加福音 7:31-35 | Luke 7:31-35**

那时候，耶稣对群众说："我要把这一代的人比做什么呢？他们好像坐在街上的儿童，彼此呼唤说：'我们给你们吹笛子，你们却不跳舞；我们唱哀歌，你们却不啼哭。'"

耶稣接着说，洗者若翰来了，不吃饭，也不喝酒，人们便说他附魔了；人子来了，也吃也喝，人们又说他贪吃嗜酒，是税吏和罪人的朋友。但是，天主的智慧必由她的儿女得到证实。

At that time, Jesus said to the crowds, "To what then shall I compare the men of this generation, and what are they like? They are like children sitting in the market place and calling to one another, 'We piped to you, and you did not dance; we wailed, and you did not weep.'"

Jesus continued: John the Baptist came neither eating bread nor drinking wine, and people said that he had a demon. The Son of man came eating and drinking, and they called him a glutton and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners. Yet wisdom is justified by all her children. 🟥
