常年期第二十三周（星期三） | Wednesday of the Twenty-third Week in Ordinary Time

2026年9月9日 · 星期三 | September 9, 2026 · Wednesday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**格林多人前书 7:25-31 | 1 Corinthians 7:25-31**

弟兄们：关于守独身的人，我没有主的明令；不过，我既蒙受主的怜悯，成为可信靠的人，就提出自己的判断。

鉴于目前的困境，我认为人最好保持现状。你已经有妻子，就不要寻求摆脱；你目前没有妻子，就不要刻意寻求婚姻。即使你结婚，也没有犯罪；未婚女子若出嫁，同样没有犯罪。不过，结婚的人会经历现世生活的困苦，我愿意使你们免受这些压力。

弟兄们，我告诉你们：时间已经短促。从今以后，有妻子的要像没有妻子；哭泣的要像没有哭泣；欢乐的要像没有欢乐；购买的要像没有占有；使用这世界的，要像不依恋它，因为这个世界现有的形态正在消逝。

Now concerning virgins I have no commandment of the Lord: yet I give my judgment, as one that hath obtained mercy of the Lord to be faithful.

I suppose therefore that this is good for the present distress, I say, that it is good for a man so to be.

Art thou bound unto a wife? seek not to be loosed. Art thou loosed from a wife? seek not a wife.

But and if thou marry, thou hast not sinned; and if a virgin marry, she hath not sinned. Nevertheless such shall have trouble in the flesh: but I spare you.

But this I say, brethren, the time is short: it remaineth, that both they that have wives be as though they had none;

And they that weep, as though they wept not; and they that rejoice, as though they rejoiced not; and they that buy, as though they possessed not;

And they that use this world, as not abusing it: for the fashion of this world passeth away.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 45:11-12, 14-17 | Psalm 45:10-11, 13-16**

**答：女儿！请听，请看，也请细听。 | R. Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear.**

女儿，请听，请看，也要侧耳细听；
忘记你的民族和父家。
君王倾慕你的美丽；
他是你的主，你要朝拜他。

**答：女儿！请听，请看，也请细听。**

君王的女儿在宫内满有光华，
她的衣服以金线织成。
她穿着刺绣的礼服被引到君王面前；
陪伴她的童女也随她来到。

**答：女儿！请听，请看，也请细听。**

她们在欢欣喜乐中被引领，
一同进入君王的宫殿。
你的子孙将继承你的祖先；
你要立他们为王侯，遍及全地。

**答：女儿！请听，请看，也请细听。**

Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear;
forget also thine own people, and thy father's house;

So shall the king greatly desire thy beauty:
for he is thy Lord; and worship thou him.

**R. Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear.**

The king's daughter is all glorious within:
her clothing is of wrought gold.

She shall be brought unto the king in raiment of needlework:
the virgins her companions that follow her shall be brought unto thee.

**R. Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear.**

With gladness and rejoicing shall they be brought:
they shall enter into the king's palace.

Instead of thy fathers shall be thy children,
whom thou mayest make princes in all the earth.

**R. Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 6:20-26 | Luke 6:20-26**

那时候，耶稣举目望着自己的门徒说：

“你们贫穷的人是有福的，因为天主的国是你们的。

你们现在饥饿的人是有福的，因为你们将要饱足；你们现在哭泣的人是有福的，因为你们将要欢笑。

人们若为了人子的缘故憎恨你们、排斥你们、辱骂你们，并把你们的名字当作邪恶而弃绝，你们就是有福的。到那一天，你们应当欢喜踊跃，因为你们在天上的赏报十分丰厚；他们的祖先也曾这样对待先知。

可是，你们富有的人有祸了，因为你们已经得到了安慰。

你们现在饱足的人有祸了，因为你们将要饥饿；你们现在欢笑的人有祸了，因为你们将要哀恸哭泣。

当众人都称赞你们时，你们有祸了，因为他们的祖先也曾这样对待假先知。”

And he lifted up his eyes on his disciples, and said, Blessed be ye poor: for yours is the kingdom of God.

Blessed are ye that hunger now: for ye shall be filled. Blessed are ye that weep now: for ye shall laugh.

Blessed are ye, when men shall hate you, and when they shall separate you from their company, and shall reproach you, and cast out your name as evil, for the Son of man's sake.

Rejoice ye in that day, and leap for joy: for, behold, your reward is great in heaven: for in the like manner did their fathers unto the prophets.

But woe unto you that are rich! for ye have received your consolation.

Woe unto you that are full! for ye shall hunger. Woe unto you that laugh now! for ye shall mourn and weep.

Woe unto you, when all men shall speak well of you! for so did their fathers to the false prophets. 🟥
