## 读经一 | First Reading

**格林多人前书 3:1-9 | 1 Corinthians 3:1-9**

弟兄们：我从前对你们说话，还不能把你们当作属神的人，只能当作属血肉的人，当作在基督内的婴孩。我给你们喝的是奶，不是饭，因为那时你们不能吃；就是现在，你们还是不能，因为你们仍然是属血肉的人。你们中间既然有嫉妒和纷争，不正说明你们仍按俗人的方式行事吗？这人说：“我属保禄”，那人说：“我属阿颇罗”，这样，你们不是成了俗人吗？

其实，阿颇罗算什么？保禄算什么？我们不过是仆人，你们藉着我们获得了信仰；每人只是照主所交付的工作去做。我栽种了，阿颇罗浇灌了，然而，唯有天主使它生长。所以，栽种的和浇灌的都不算什么，一切都在于那使它生长的天主。栽种的和浇灌的目标相同，各人却要按自己的劳苦领受报酬。我们是天主的同工，你们是天主的田地，是天主的建筑物。

Brothers and sisters, I could not speak to you as spiritual people, but as people of the flesh, as infants in Christ. I fed you with milk rather than solid food, because you were not ready for it; even now you are not ready, for you are still governed by the flesh. While jealousy and strife remain among you, are you not living in a merely human way? When one says, “I belong to Paul,” and another, “I belong to Apollos,” are you not behaving like ordinary people?

What, then, is Apollos? What is Paul? They are servants through whom you came to believe, each carrying out the task the Lord assigned. I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth. Therefore neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything; only God, who gives the growth, matters. The planter and the waterer share one purpose, though each will receive wages according to that person’s labor. For we are God’s fellow workers; you are God’s field and God’s building.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 4:38-44 | Luke 4:38-44**

那时候，耶稣离开会堂，到了西满的家。西满的岳母正发高热，他们便为她祈求耶稣。耶稣走到她身边，呵叱热症，热症就退了；她立刻起来服侍他们。

日落以后，众人把所有患各种病症的人领到耶稣跟前；他给每个人覆手，治好了他们。也有魔鬼从许多人身上出来，喊着说：“你是天主子！”耶稣却叱责它们，不许它们说话，因为它们知道他是默西亚。

天一亮，耶稣就出去，到了荒野地方。群众寻找他，来到他那里，想挽留他，不让他离开。他却对他们说：“我也必须到别的城去传报天主国的喜讯，因为我正是为了这事被派来的。”于是，他常到犹太各会堂去宣讲。

At that time, Jesus left the synagogue and entered Simon’s house. Simon’s mother-in-law was suffering from a high fever, and they appealed to him on her behalf. He stood over her and rebuked the fever, and it left her. At once she rose and served them.

When the sun was setting, everyone who had friends or relatives sick with various diseases brought them to Jesus. He laid his hands upon each of them and healed them. Demons also came out of many, crying, “You are the Son of God!” But he rebuked them and would not allow them to speak, because they knew that he was the Christ.

At daybreak he departed and went to a lonely place. The crowds searched for him, came to him, and tried to keep him from leaving. But he said, “I must proclaim the good news of the kingdom of God to the other towns also, for I was sent for this purpose.” And he continued preaching in the synagogues of Judea.

## 反省 | Reflection

耶稣刚公开现身不久，民众对祂的认识，最多是一位先知、一位天主的人、一位大有能力的医治者。独有魔鬼清楚耶稣的真实身份——天主子，可是耶稣却禁止魔鬼说话。这里有所谓默西亚的秘密：要等到十字架上，天主子的身份才适合揭示；一位十字架上的至高者，将打碎人类对默西亚的虚假幻想。

再者，揭示耶稣身份的绝不该是魔鬼，而该是渴望天主救恩的人。魔鬼认识基督，却不是出自信、望、爱，不是出自对十字架奥迹的臣服；它反而诱使人远离十字架。圣奥斯定曾发出这样的警示：不要为与魔鬼同一层次的认知而夸口。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

Not long after Jesus began his public ministry, most people knew him at best as a prophet, a man of God, or a powerful healer. The demons alone clearly recognized his true identity as the Son of God, yet Jesus forbade them to speak. Here we encounter the so-called messianic secret: the identity of the Son of God could be properly revealed only upon the cross. The Most High hanging upon a cross would shatter humanity’s false expectations of the Messiah.

Moreover, Jesus’ identity should never be proclaimed by demons, but by people who long for God’s salvation. Demons know Christ, yet their knowledge does not arise from faith, hope, and love or from surrender to the mystery of the cross. Instead, they tempt people to flee from the cross. Saint Augustine therefore warns us not to boast of knowledge that rises no higher than the knowledge possessed by demons.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各指出，当安息日结束后，村民把所有病人和附魔者带到耶稣面前。耶稣治愈他们，却不许魔鬼揭露祂是基督。从传教之初，耶稣便特别亲近身体和心灵受苦的人；祂以言语和行动，使天父对受苦者的眷爱具体可见。

门徒不只是这项使命的旁观者。耶稣让他们参与其中，派遣他们，并赐给他们医治病人、驱逐魔鬼的能力。这使命在教会生活中延续至今。照顾各种病人并非教会可有可无的附加工作，而是教会使命不可分割的一部分：把天主的温柔带给受苦的人类。

（教宗方济各，三钟经，2021年2月7日）

Pope Francis notes that when the sabbath ended, the villagers brought all who were sick or possessed to Jesus. He healed them while forbidding the demons to reveal that he was the Christ. From the beginning of his ministry, Jesus showed a special closeness to those who suffer in body or spirit, making the Father’s loving concern visible through both words and deeds.

The disciples were not meant to remain spectators. Jesus involved and sent them, giving them authority to heal the sick and cast out demons. That mission continues in the Church today. Caring for every kind of illness is not an optional extra, but an integral part of the Church’s mission: bringing God’s tenderness to suffering humanity.

(Pope Francis, Angelus, 7 February 2021)
