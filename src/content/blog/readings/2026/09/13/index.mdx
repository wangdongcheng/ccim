常年期第二十四主日 | Twenty-fourth Sunday in Ordinary Time

2026年9月13日 · 星期日 | September 13, 2026 · Sunday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**德训篇 27:30-28:7 | Sirach 27:30-28:7**

愤怒和暴怒，二者都令人憎恶，罪人却紧紧抓住不放。

报复人的，必要遭到上主的报复；上主必会牢记他的罪过。

你要宽恕近人的过错；这样，当你祈祷时，你的罪也必蒙赦免。

一个人若对另一个人怀着怒气，又怎能向上主求医治？他若不怜悯与自己相同的人，又怎能为自己的罪求宽赦？他不过是血肉之躯，竟仍怀着怨恨；那么，谁会赦免他的罪呢？

你要记住生命的终结，停止仇恨；想起腐朽与死亡，不再犯罪。你要记住诫命，不要向近人发怒；记住至高者的盟约，宽容别人的过失。

Wrath and anger are both detestable, yet sinners cling tightly to them.

Those who take revenge will meet the Lord's vengeance, for the Lord keeps their sins in mind.

Forgive your neighbor's wrongdoing, and when you pray, your own sins will be forgiven.

If one person nurses anger against another, how can that person ask the Lord for healing? If someone shows no mercy to another person like himself, how can he seek pardon for his own sins? Though he is only flesh, he cherishes resentment; who, then, will forgive his sins?

Remember the end of your life and put hostility aside; remember decay and death, and cease from sin. Keep the commandments and do not be angry with your neighbor; remember the covenant of the Most High and overlook offenses.

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**罗马书 14:7-9 | Romans 14:7-9**

弟兄姊妹们：我们没有一个人为自己而生，也没有一个人为自己而死。我们若生活，是为主而生活；我们若死亡，是为主而死亡。所以，无论生或死，我们都是属于主的。

因为基督死而复生，正是为了作死者和生者的主。

Brothers and sisters: None of us lives for ourselves, and none of us dies for ourselves. If we live, we live for the Lord; and if we die, we die for the Lord. Therefore, whether we live or die, we belong to the Lord.

For this purpose Christ died and lived again, so that he might be Lord of both the dead and the living.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 18:21-35 | Matthew 18:21-35**

那时候，伯多禄前来问耶稣：“主啊，如果我的弟兄得罪了我，我该宽恕他多少次？七次吗？”

耶稣回答：“我告诉你，不是七次，而是七十个七次。

“天国好比一个君王，要同他的仆人清算账目。清算开始时，有人被带到他面前，欠了他一万塔冷通。那人无力偿还，主人便下令卖掉他、他的妻子儿女和他所有的一切，用来还债。

“那仆人俯伏在地，恳求说：‘请宽容我，我一定把一切还给你。’主人动了怜悯的心，释放了他，也免了他的债。

“那仆人出去后，遇见一个同伴，欠他一百德纳。他抓住同伴，扼着他的喉咙说：‘把你欠的还给我！’他的同伴便跪下哀求：‘请宽容我，我一定还你。’他却不肯，反而把同伴关进监牢，直到还清欠债。

“其他仆人看见所发生的事，非常悲伤，便把一切报告给主人。主人于是召来那仆人，对他说：‘恶仆！因为你哀求我，我免了你的全部债务；难道你不也应该怜悯你的同伴，如同我怜悯了你一样吗？’主人震怒，把他交给刑役，直到他还清全部债务。

“如果你们每个人不从心里宽恕自己的弟兄，我的天父也要这样对待你们。”

At that time Peter came to Jesus and asked, “Lord, if another member of the church sins against me, how often should I forgive? As many as seven times?”

Jesus answered him, “Not merely seven times, but seventy-seven times.

“The kingdom of heaven may be compared to a king who wished to settle accounts with his servants. As the reckoning began, a servant who owed ten thousand talents was brought before him. Since the servant could not pay, the master ordered that he, his wife and children, and all he possessed be sold so that payment could be made.

“The servant fell before him and pleaded, ‘Be patient with me, and I will repay everything.’ Moved with compassion, the master released him and canceled the debt.

“But that servant went out and found a fellow servant who owed him one hundred denarii. Seizing him by the throat, he demanded, ‘Pay what you owe.’ The fellow servant fell down and begged, ‘Be patient with me, and I will repay you.’ But he refused and had him imprisoned until the debt was paid.

“When the other servants saw what had happened, they were deeply distressed and reported everything to their master. Then the master summoned him and said, ‘You wicked servant! I forgave your entire debt because you pleaded with me. Should you not also have shown mercy to your fellow servant, just as I showed mercy to you?’ In anger, the master handed him over to be punished until the full debt was repaid.

“So my heavenly Father will also deal with you unless each of you forgives your brother or sister from the heart.” 🟥
