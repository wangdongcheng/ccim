## 读经一 | First Reading

**箴言 21:1-6,10-13 | Proverbs 21:1-6,10-13**

君王的心在上主手中，如同可引导的水流；上主愿意使它流向哪里，它便流向哪里。

人总以为自己的道路正直，然而衡量人心的却是上主。履行正义和公道，比祭献更蒙上主悦纳。

高傲的眼目和骄矜的心，以及恶人所炫耀的一切，都是罪恶。勤劳者的计划必带来丰足，急躁冒进的人却只会陷入贫乏。以欺骗的口舌积聚财富，不过是在追逐迅速消散的烟雾，并为自己布下死亡的陷阱。

恶人的心只渴望邪恶，对近人毫无怜悯。嘲讽者受到惩罚，纯朴的人便学会明智；智慧的人接受教导，知识就更加增长。

正义者留意恶人的家，知道恶人终必倾覆，陷入灾祸。谁掩耳不听穷人的呼求，将来自己呼求时，也不会得到回应。

The king’s heart is like a stream of water in the Lord’s hand; he directs it wherever he chooses.

Every person may consider their own way right, but the Lord weighs the heart. To practice righteousness and justice is more acceptable to the Lord than sacrifice.

Haughty eyes and a proud heart—the outward display of the wicked—are sin. The plans of the diligent surely lead to abundance, but reckless haste leads only to poverty. Wealth obtained by a lying tongue is a fleeting vapor and a deadly snare.

The soul of the wicked desires evil and shows no mercy to a neighbor. When a scoffer is punished, the simple learn wisdom; when the wise are instructed, they increase in knowledge.

The righteous one observes the house of the wicked and sees that the wicked are cast down to ruin. Whoever closes an ear to the cry of the poor will also cry out and receive no answer.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 119:1,27,30,34-35,44 | Psalm 119:1,27,30,34-35,44**

**答：上主，求你引导我走你诫命的道路。 | R. Guide me, Lord, in the way of your commands.**

品行无瑕、
遵行上主法律的人，真是有福。

**答：上主，求你引导我走你诫命的道路。**

求你使我明白你训令的道路，
我要默想你的奇妙化工。

**答：上主，求你引导我走你诫命的道路。**

我选择了忠信的道路，
把你的法令摆在我面前。

**答：上主，求你引导我走你诫命的道路。**

求你赐我明悟，使我遵守你的法律，
并全心全意持守不渝。

**答：上主，求你引导我走你诫命的道路。**

求你引导我走你诫命的途径，
因为这是我的喜乐。

**答：上主，求你引导我走你诫命的道路。**

我要不断遵守你的法律，
直到永远，世世无穷。

**答：上主，求你引导我走你诫命的道路。**

Blessed are those whose way is blameless,
who walk according to the law of the Lord.

**R. Guide me, Lord, in the way of your commands.**

Help me understand the way of your precepts,
and I will meditate upon your wonderful works.

**R. Guide me, Lord, in the way of your commands.**

I have chosen the way of faithfulness
and have set your ordinances before me.

**R. Guide me, Lord, in the way of your commands.**

Give me understanding, that I may keep your law
and observe it with my whole heart.

**R. Guide me, Lord, in the way of your commands.**

Lead me in the path of your commandments,
for in it I find delight.

**R. Guide me, Lord, in the way of your commands.**

I will keep your law continually,
for ever and ever.

**R. Guide me, Lord, in the way of your commands.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 8:19-21 | Luke 8:19-21**

那时候，耶稣的母亲和兄弟们来到他那里，因为人多，无法走到他跟前。

有人告诉耶稣：“你的母亲和你的兄弟站在外面，愿意见你。”

耶稣回答他们说：“听了天主的话而实行的，才是我的母亲和我的兄弟。”

At that time, the mother of Jesus and his brethren came to him, but they could not reach him because of the crowd.

Someone told him, “Your mother and your brethren are standing outside and wish to see you.”

Jesus answered them, “My mother and my brethren are those who hear the word of God and put it into practice.”

听了天主的话而实行的，才是我的母亲和我的兄弟。 | My mother and my brethren are those who hear the word of God and put it into practice.
