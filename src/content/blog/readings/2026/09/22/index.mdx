君王的心在上主手中，如同可引导的水流；上主愿意使它流向哪里，它便流向哪里。

人总以为自己的道路正直，然而衡量人心的却是上主。履行正义和公道，比祭献更蒙上主悦纳。

高傲的眼目和骄矜的心，以及恶人所炫耀的一切，都是罪恶。勤劳者的计划必带来丰足，急躁冒进的人却只会陷入贫乏。以欺骗的口舌积聚财富，不过是在追逐迅速消散的烟雾，并为自己布下死亡的陷阱。

恶人的心只渴望邪恶，对近人毫无怜悯。嘲讽者受到惩罚，纯朴的人便学会明智；智慧的人接受教导，知识就更加增长。

正义者留意恶人的家，知道恶人终必倾覆，陷入灾祸。谁掩耳不听穷人的呼求，将来自己呼求时，也不会得到回应。

The king’s heart is like a stream of water in the Lord’s hand; he directs it wherever he chooses.

Every person may consider their own way right, but the Lord weighs the heart. To practice righteousness and justice is more acceptable to the Lord than sacrifice.

Haughty eyes and a proud heart—the outward display of the wicked—are sin. The plans of the diligent surely lead to abundance, but reckless haste leads only to poverty. Wealth obtained by a lying tongue is a fleeting vapor and a deadly snare.

The soul of the wicked desires evil and shows no mercy to a neighbor. When a scoffer is punished, the simple learn wisdom; when the wise are instructed, they increase in knowledge.

The righteous one observes the house of the wicked and sees that the wicked are cast down to ruin. Whoever closes an ear to the cry of the poor will also cry out and receive no answer.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 8:19-21 | Luke 8:19-21**

有人告诉耶稣：“你的母亲和你的兄弟站在外面，愿意见你。”

## 反省 | Reflection

福音中的“耶稣的兄弟”，不应只按现代家庭关系的狭义来理解。在希伯来传统中，家族内同辈的男子也可称为兄弟；若耶稣另有亲生兄弟，祂在十字架上把圣母托付给若望便很难解释。教会由此护卫玛利亚终身童贞的信仰：基督由女人所生，显明祂真正的人性；由童贞女所生，彰显祂的天主性。既然天主能够创造万有、分开海水并施行奇迹，我们也应谦卑相信，祂能保存玛利亚的童贞。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

The “brethren of Jesus” in the Gospel should not be understood only in the narrow modern sense of immediate siblings. In the Hebrew tradition, as in traditional Chinese usage, male relatives of the same generation could also be called brothers. If Jesus had biological brothers, his entrusting Mary to John at the cross would be difficult to explain. The Church therefore safeguards faith in Mary’s perpetual virginity: Christ was born of a woman to reveal his true humanity, and born of a virgin to manifest his divinity. The God who creates, parts the sea, and works wonders can surely preserve Mary’s virginity.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

耶稣继续向群众讲话。祂爱百姓，也爱聚集的人群，甚至说：“这些跟随我的人，这一大群人，就是我的母亲和兄弟。”祂解释说：他们就是那些聆听天主圣言并付诸实行的人。

跟随耶稣有两个条件：聆听天主圣言，并付诸实行。这就是基督徒的生活，没有别的。就是这样简单、这样简单。

（……）每当我们这样做——打开福音，阅读一段经文，并问自己：“天主是否借着这段话对我说话？祂是否在向我传达什么？如果是，祂在对我说什么？”——这就是聆听天主圣言：不仅用耳朵聆听，也用心聆听，向天主圣言敞开心扉。

耶稣的仇敌也听祂讲话，却留在祂身旁，企图找出祂的错处，使祂失足，并削弱祂的权威。然而，他们从未问自己：“天主借着这圣言对我说什么？”

天主不只是泛泛地对所有人讲话；是的，祂对所有人讲话，但祂也亲自对我们每一个人讲话。福音正是为我们每一个人写的。（……）

上主始终播撒祂的圣言；祂所要求的，只是一颗敞开的心去聆听，以及一份愿意把圣言付诸实行的意愿。因此，让我们以圣咏的话作为今天的祈祷：“求你领我走你诫命的途径”，也就是走上祢圣言的道路，好使我在祢的引导下，学会把圣言付诸实行。

（教宗方济各，2014年9月23日弥撒讲道）

Jesus continued speaking with the people, and He loved the people and loved the crowd, so much so that He said, “These who follow me, that vast crowd, are my mother and my brothers; they are these”. And He explained: [They are] those who listen to the Word of God and put it into practice. These are the two conditions for following Jesus: listening to the Word of God and putting it into practice. This is Christian life, nothing more. Simple, simple.

(…) Every time we do this – we open the Gospel and read a passage, and ask ourselves: “Is God speaking to me with this, is He saying something to me? And if He is saying something, what is He saying?” – this is listening to the Word of God, listening to it with our ears and listening to it with our hearts. Opening our hearts to the Word of God. Jesus’ enemies listened to His words, but they stayed close to Him to try and find a mistake, to trip Him up, and to undermine His authority.

Yet they never asked themselves: “What is God saying to me in this Word?” And God does not speak merely to everyone: yes, He speaks to everyone, but He speaks to each one of us. The Gospel was written for each and every one of us. (…) The Lord sows His Word, always; He merely asks for an open heart to hear it and a willingness to put it into practice.

Let us therefore make today’s prayer that of the Psalm: “Lead me in the path of thy commandments”, that is, the path of Your Word, so that, with your guidance, I may learn to put it into practice. (Pope Francis, Homily of 23 September 2014)
