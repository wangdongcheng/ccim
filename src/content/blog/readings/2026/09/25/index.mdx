## 读经一 | First Reading

**训道篇 3:1-11 | Ecclesiastes 3:1-11**

万事都有定时，天下每一件事都有它的时刻：出生有时，死亡有时；栽种有时，拔除所栽种的也有时；杀戮有时，医治有时；拆毁有时，建筑有时；哭泣有时，欢笑有时；哀恸有时，舞蹈有时；抛石有时，堆石有时；拥抱有时，避免拥抱也有时；寻找有时，失落有时；保存有时，舍弃有时；撕裂有时，缝补有时；缄默有时，发言有时；爱有时，恨有时；战争有时，和平有时。

劳作者从自己的辛苦中能获得什么益处？我看见天主交给世人、使他们为之操劳的事务。天主所做的一切，在适当的时候都很美好；祂也把永恒的意识放在人心中，然而人始终不能完全明白天主从开始直到终结所做的工程。

For everything there is a season, and a time for every matter under heaven: a time to be born and a time to die; a time to plant and a time to uproot what was planted; a time to kill and a time to heal; a time to break down and a time to build up; a time to weep and a time to laugh; a time to mourn and a time to dance; a time to cast away stones and a time to gather them; a time to embrace and a time to refrain from embracing; a time to seek and a time to lose; a time to keep and a time to cast away; a time to tear and a time to sew; a time to keep silence and a time to speak; a time to love and a time to hate; a time for war and a time for peace.

What gain does the worker receive from all this toil? I have considered the task God has given the children of men to occupy them. He has made everything beautiful in its time, and he has placed a sense of eternity in the human heart; yet no one can fully discover the work God has done from beginning to end.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 9:18-22 | Luke 9:18-22**

有一天，耶稣独自祈祷，门徒同祂在一起。祂问他们：“群众说我是谁？”

他们回答：“有人说是洗者若翰；有人说是厄里亚；还有人说，是古时的一位先知复活了。”

耶稣又问：“你们说我是谁呢？”伯多禄回答：“是天主的受傅者。”

耶稣严厉嘱咐他们，不要把这事告诉任何人。祂又说：“人子必须受许多苦，被长老、司祭长和经师们排斥，并且被杀害；第三天祂必要复活。”

One day, as Jesus was praying alone with his disciples nearby, he asked them, “Who do the crowds say that I am?”

They answered, “John the Baptist; others say Elijah; and still others say that one of the ancient prophets has risen.”

Then he asked them, “But who do you say that I am?” Peter replied, “The Christ of God.”

Jesus strictly charged them to tell this to no one, saying, “The Son of Man must suffer many things, be rejected by the elders, chief priests, and scribes, be killed, and on the third day be raised.”

## 反省 | Reflection

孤独，源于不被了解，无人理解。毫无疑问，耶稣是四部福音书中最孤独的人，祂独有在天父那里拥有被理解的安慰。群众不了解耶稣，看祂只是众先知中的一位，宗徒们也不了解耶稣。对于耶稣的提问，伯多禄虽然说出了正确的答案，但不具有承载此答案的心态。耶稣禁止门徒宣扬默西亚的名，因为没有人能正确领悟默西亚的使命。耶稣是孤独的，人们只要自己心中想要的那种天主，被负卖、被遗弃、被处死的默西亚，是人们不能接受的。然而，“救恩来自那坐在宝座上的我们的天主，并来自羔羊！”它注定曲高和寡，能领略的就领略吧！

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

Loneliness springs from not being known, from having no one who understands. Without doubt, Jesus is the loneliest person in the four Gospels; only in the Father does he find the consolation of being understood. The crowds do not understand Jesus, seeing him merely as one among the prophets, and neither do the apostles understand him. Although Peter gives the correct answer to Jesus’ question, he does not yet possess the disposition needed to bear the weight of that answer. Jesus forbids the disciples to proclaim the name of the Messiah because no one can yet rightly grasp the Messiah’s mission.

Jesus is alone. People want only the kind of God fashioned by the desires of their own hearts; they cannot accept a Messiah who is betrayed, abandoned, and put to death. Yet “Salvation belongs to our God who sits upon the throne, and to the Lamb!” This truth is destined to be a lofty song appreciated by few; let those who can grasp it, grasp it.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

“人们说人子是谁？”（……）首先，对于耶稣的这个问题，有一种来自普通百姓的回答。在他们看来，纳匝肋人并非江湖骗子，而是一个正直的人：祂有勇气，善于言谈，也说正确的话，就像以色列历史上的其他伟大先知一样。（……）然而，对他们而言，祂只是一个人；因此，在危险来临、祂受难的时候，他们也离弃祂，失望而去。（……）

今天同样有许多环境，耶稣虽然作为一个人受到赏识，却被贬为某种富有魅力的领袖或超人。这不仅存在于非信徒当中，也存在于许多领洗的基督徒当中；他们最终在这种层面上陷入一种实际的无神论状态。

这就是托付给我们的世界。在这个世界里，正如教宗方济各多次教导我们的，我们蒙召为对救主耶稣的喜乐信德作见证。因此，我们也必须同伯多禄一起重申：“祢是默西亚，永生天主之子”（玛 16:16）。首先，我们必须在自己与主的个人关系中这样宣认，在每日不断皈依的道路上实践这宣认；然后，我们也要作为教会这样宣认，共同活出对主的忠信，并把喜讯带给所有的人。

（教宗良十四世，讲道，2025年5月9日）

“Who do people say that the Son of Man is?” (…) First, there is the (…) response to Jesus’ question: that of ordinary people. For them, the Nazarene is not a charlatan, but an upright man, one who has courage, who speaks well and says the right things, like other great prophets in the history of Israel. (…) Yet to them he is only a man, and therefore, in times of danger, during his passion, they too abandon him and depart disappointed. (…) Today, too, there are many settings in which Jesus, although appreciated as a man, is reduced to a kind of charismatic leader or superman. This is true not only among non-believers but also among many baptized Christians, who thus end up living, at this level, in a state of practical atheism.

This is the world that has been entrusted to us, a world in which, as Pope Francis taught us so many times, we are called to bear witness to our joyful faith in Jesus the Saviour. Therefore, it is essential that we too repeat, with Peter: “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God” (Mt 16:16). It is essential to do this, first of all, in our personal relationship with the Lord, in our commitment to a daily journey of conversion. Then, to do so as a Church, experiencing together our fidelity to the Lord and bringing the Good News to all. (Pope Leo XIV, Homily, 9 May 2025)
