常年期第二十五周（星期五） | Friday of the Twenty-fifth Week in Ordinary Time

2026年9月25日 · 星期五 | September 25, 2026 · Friday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**训道篇 3:1-11 | Ecclesiastes 3:1-11**

万事都有定时，天下每一件事都有它的时刻：出生有时，死亡有时；栽种有时，拔除所栽种的也有时；杀戮有时，医治有时；拆毁有时，建筑有时；哭泣有时，欢笑有时；哀恸有时，舞蹈有时；抛石有时，堆石有时；拥抱有时，避免拥抱也有时；寻找有时，失落有时；保存有时，舍弃有时；撕裂有时，缝补有时；缄默有时，发言有时；爱有时，恨有时；战争有时，和平有时。

劳作者从自己的辛苦中能获得什么益处？我看见天主交给世人、使他们为之操劳的事务。天主所做的一切，在适当的时候都很美好；祂也把永恒的意识放在人心中，然而人始终不能完全明白天主从开始直到终结所做的工程。

For everything there is a season, and a time for every matter under heaven: a time to be born and a time to die; a time to plant and a time to uproot what was planted; a time to kill and a time to heal; a time to break down and a time to build up; a time to weep and a time to laugh; a time to mourn and a time to dance; a time to cast away stones and a time to gather them; a time to embrace and a time to refrain from embracing; a time to seek and a time to lose; a time to keep and a time to cast away; a time to tear and a time to sew; a time to keep silence and a time to speak; a time to love and a time to hate; a time for war and a time for peace.

What gain does the worker receive from all this toil? I have considered the task God has given the children of men to occupy them. He has made everything beautiful in its time, and he has placed a sense of eternity in the human heart; yet no one can fully discover the work God has done from beginning to end.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 144:1b-2abc,3-4 | Psalm 144:1b-2abc,3-4**

**答：上主，我的磐石应受赞颂！ | R. Blessed be the Lord, my rock!**

上主，我的磐石应受赞颂！
祂是我的慈爱和堡垒，
是我的保障和救援，
是我的盾牌，是我的避难所。

**答：上主，我的磐石应受赞颂！**

上主，人算什么，你竟关怀他？
世人算什么，你竟眷顾他？
人不过像一口气息，
他的岁月有如掠过的阴影。

**答：上主，我的磐石应受赞颂！**

Blessed be the Lord, my rock!
He is my mercy and my fortress,
my stronghold and my deliverer,
my shield and my refuge.

**R. Blessed be the Lord, my rock!**

O Lord, what is man that you regard him,
or the son of man that you think of him?
Man is like a breath;
his days are like a passing shadow.

**R. Blessed be the Lord, my rock!**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 9:18-22 | Luke 9:18-22**

有一天，耶稣独自祈祷，门徒同祂在一起。祂问他们：“群众说我是谁？”

他们回答：“有人说是洗者若翰；有人说是厄里亚；还有人说，是古时的一位先知复活了。”

耶稣又问：“你们说我是谁呢？”伯多禄回答：“是天主的受傅者。”

耶稣严厉嘱咐他们，不要把这事告诉任何人。祂又说：“人子必须受许多苦，被长老、司祭长和经师们排斥，并且被杀害；第三天祂必要复活。”

One day, as Jesus was praying alone with his disciples nearby, he asked them, “Who do the crowds say that I am?”

They answered, “John the Baptist; others say Elijah; and still others say that one of the ancient prophets has risen.”

Then he asked them, “But who do you say that I am?” Peter replied, “The Christ of God.”

Jesus strictly charged them to tell this to no one, saying, “The Son of Man must suffer many things, be rejected by the elders, chief priests, and scribes, be killed, and on the third day be raised.”
