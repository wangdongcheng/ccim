## 读经一 | First Reading

**格林多人前书 15:35-37,42-49 | 1 Corinthians 15:35-37,42-49**

弟兄们：也许有人会问：“死人怎样复活？他们将带着怎样的身体回来？”

糊涂的人！你所播下的种子若不先死去，就不能获得新的生命；你所播下的，也不是后来长成的形体，而只是一粒赤裸的种子，或许是麦粒，或许是别的种子。

死人复活也是这样：播下的是可朽坏的，复活的是不可朽坏的；播下的是卑微的，复活的是光荣的；播下的是软弱的，复活的是强健的；播下的是属血气的身体，复活的是属神的身体。既然有属血气的身体，也就有属神的身体。

经上记载：“第一个人亚当成了有生命的人”；末后的亚当却成了赐予生命的神。属神的并不是首先出现的；先有属血气的，然后才有属神的。第一个人出于尘土，属于大地；第二个人来自天上。属于尘土的人怎样，凡属于尘土的也怎样；来自天上的那一位怎样，凡属于天上的也怎样。我们既带着那出于尘土者的肖像，也要带着那来自天上者的肖像。

Brothers and sisters: Someone may ask, “How are the dead raised, and what kind of body will they have?”

What is sown cannot receive new life unless it first dies. The seed that is planted is not the completed body that will appear, but only a bare grain—perhaps wheat or another kind of seed.

So it is with the resurrection. What is sown perishable is raised imperishable; what is sown without honor is raised in glory; what is sown in weakness is raised in power. A natural body is sown, but a spiritual body is raised. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual one.

Scripture says that the first man, Adam, became a living being, while the last Adam became a life-giving spirit. The spiritual did not come first; the natural came first, and afterward the spiritual. The first man came from the dust of the earth; the second man came from heaven. Those who belong to the earth resemble the earthly man, while those who belong to heaven resemble the heavenly man. Just as we have borne the likeness of the earthly man, we shall also bear the likeness of the heavenly one.

**答：我要在天主面前，生活在光明的人世间。 | R. I will walk before God in the light of life.**

我呼求你的那一天，仇敌必会退去；  
由此我知道，天主与我同在。

**答：我要在天主面前，生活在光明的人世间。**

我依靠天主，赞颂他的许诺；  
我信赖上主，赞美他的圣言。  
我依靠天主，无所畏惧；  
血肉之人又能把我怎样？

**答：我要在天主面前，生活在光明的人世间。**

天主，我必履行向你所许的愿，  
并向你奉献感恩之祭。  
因为你救我的性命脱离死亡，  
保护我的双脚不致跌倒，  
使我在天主面前，  
生活在光明的人世间。

**答：我要在天主面前，生活在光明的人世间。**

When I call upon you, my enemies turn back;  
by this I know that God is with me.

**R. I will walk before God in the light of life.**

In God, whose promise I praise,  
in the Lord, whose word I honor,  
I place my trust and will not fear.  
What can mortal man do to me?

**R. I will walk before God in the light of life.**

O God, I must fulfill my vows to you  
and offer you my sacrifice of thanksgiving.  
You have delivered my soul from death  
and kept my feet from falling,  
so that I may walk before you  
in the light of life.

**R. I will walk before God in the light of life.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 8:4-15 | Luke 8:4-15**

那时候，一大群人聚集起来，各城的人也来到耶稣跟前。他便用比喻说：

“有一个撒种的人出去撒种。他撒种时，有些种子落在路旁，被人践踏，天上的飞鸟把它吃掉了；有些落在石地上，刚长起来便枯萎了，因为没有水分；有些落在荆棘中，荆棘与它一同长大，把它窒息了；另有些落在好地里，长成后结出百倍的果实。”

耶稣说完这些话，便高声说道：“有耳能听的，就应当听！”

门徒问他这个比喻有什么意义。耶稣回答：“天主国的奥秘赐给你们认识；至于其他的人，却要借着比喻来听，使他们看而看不见，听而不明白。

“这个比喻的意义是：种子就是天主的话。落在路旁的，是那些听见了圣言的人；后来魔鬼来到，从他们心中夺走圣言，免得他们相信而得救。

“落在石地上的，是那些听见圣言便欣然接受的人；可是他们没有根，只相信一段时间，一遇到考验便退缩了。

“落在荆棘中的，是那些听见了圣言，却在生活的路上被忧虑、财富和享乐所挤压，因而结不出成熟果实的人。

“落在好地里的，是那些怀着善良诚实的心听取圣言、把它保存起来，并以坚忍结出果实的人。”

At that time, a great crowd gathered around Jesus, with people coming to him from many towns. He told them this parable:

“A sower went out to sow his seed. As he scattered it, some fell beside the path, where it was trampled and eaten by the birds. Some fell upon rock; after sprouting, it withered because it had no moisture. Some fell among thorns, which grew with it and choked it. Some fell into good soil, where it grew and produced a hundredfold.”

When he had said this, he called out, “Let anyone who has ears to hear listen!”

His disciples asked him what the parable meant. He answered, “Knowledge of the mysteries of the kingdom of God has been given to you. To others these things are told in parables, so that although they look, they may not truly see, and although they hear, they may not understand.

“This is the meaning of the parable: the seed is the word of God. Those beside the path are the people who hear; afterward the devil comes and takes the word from their hearts, lest they believe and be saved.

“Those upon the rock receive the word joyfully when they hear it, but it takes no root in them. They believe for a time, yet fall away when testing comes.

“The seed among thorns represents those who hear but, as they continue on their way, are overwhelmed by the cares, riches, and pleasures of life; their fruit never matures.

“The seed in good soil represents those who hear the word with a good and honest heart, hold it fast, and bear fruit through perseverance.” 🟥
