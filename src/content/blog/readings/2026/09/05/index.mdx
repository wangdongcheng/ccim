# 常年期第二十二周（星期六） | Saturday of the Twenty-second Week in Ordinary Time

2026年9月5日 · 星期六 | September 5, 2026 · Saturday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**格林多人前书 4:6b-15 | 1 Corinthians 4:6b-15**

弟兄们：你们要从我和阿颇罗身上学习，不要越过经上所记载的，免得你们彼此之间因偏爱某人而自高自大。谁使你与别人不同呢？你有什么不是领受来的？既然是领受来的，为什么还夸耀，好像不是领受的一样？

你们已经满足了，已经富足了；没有我们，你们竟然已经作王了！但愿你们真的作王，好使我们也能与你们一同作王。

我想，天主把我们这些宗徒列在最后，好像被判死刑的人，因为我们成了给世界、天使和世人观看的景象。我们为了基督成了愚妄的人，你们在基督内却是明智的；我们软弱，你们强壮；你们受尊敬，我们却受轻视。

直到现在，我们仍忍受饥饿、口渴、衣不蔽体、受人打击，并且居无定所。我们亲手劳作；受人咒骂时，我们祝福；受迫害时，我们忍耐；受毁谤时，我们以善言相劝。直到如今，我们仿佛成了世界的垃圾、众人的弃物。

我写这些，并不是要使你们羞愧，而是把你们当作我亲爱的孩子来劝戒。即使你们在基督内有无数导师，却没有许多父亲，因为是我借着福音，在基督耶稣内生了你们。

That ye might learn in us not to think of men above that which is written, that no one of you be puffed up for one against another.

For who maketh thee to differ from another? and what hast thou that thou didst not receive? now if thou didst receive it, why dost thou glory, as if thou hadst not received it?

Now ye are full, now ye are rich, ye have reigned as kings without us: and I would to God ye did reign, that we also might reign with you.

For I think that God hath set forth us the apostles last, as it were appointed to death: for we are made a spectacle unto the world, and to angels, and to men.

We are fools for Christ's sake, but ye are wise in Christ; we are weak, but ye are strong; ye are honourable, but we are despised.

Even unto this present hour we both hunger, and thirst, and are naked, and are buffeted, and have no certain dwellingplace;

And labour, working with our own hands: being reviled, we bless; being persecuted, we suffer it:

Being defamed, we intreat: we are made as the filth of the world, and are the offscouring of all things unto this day.

I write not these things to shame you, but as my beloved sons I warn you.

For though ye have ten thousand instructers in Christ, yet have ye not many fathers: for in Christ Jesus I have begotten you through the gospel.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 145:17-18, 19-20, 21 | Psalm 145:17-18, 19-20, 21**

**答：上主亲近一切呼求他的人。 | R. The LORD is nigh unto all them that call upon him.**

上主的一切行径都是正义的，
他的一切作为都是圣善的。
上主亲近一切呼求他的人，
亲近所有真诚呼求他的人。

**答：上主亲近一切呼求他的人。**

他满足敬畏他的人的愿望，
听见他们的呼求，并拯救他们。
上主保护一切爱他的人，
却要消灭所有作恶的人。

**答：上主亲近一切呼求他的人。**

愿我的口赞美上主；
愿一切有血肉的人，
永远赞颂他的圣名。

**答：上主亲近一切呼求他的人。**

The LORD is righteous in all his ways,
and holy in all his works.
The LORD is nigh unto all them that call upon him,
to all that call upon him in truth.

**R. The LORD is nigh unto all them that call upon him.**

He will fulfil the desire of them that fear him:
he also will hear their cry, and will save them.
The LORD preserveth all them that love him:
but all the wicked will he destroy.

**R. The LORD is nigh unto all them that call upon him.**

My mouth shall speak the praise of the LORD:
and let all flesh bless his holy name for ever and ever.

**R. The LORD is nigh unto all them that call upon him.**

阿肋路亚，阿肋路亚。

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 6:1-5 | Luke 6:1-5**

一个安息日，耶稣经过麦田，他的门徒掐下麦穗，用手搓着吃。

有些法利塞人对他们说：“为什么你们做安息日不许可做的事？”

耶稣回答他们说：“你们没有读过达味和跟随他的人饥饿时所做的事吗？他进入天主的殿，拿了供饼来吃，也分给跟随他的人；而这饼按照法律，除了司祭以外，别人是不可以吃的。”

耶稣又对他们说：“人子也是安息日的主。”

And it came to pass on the second sabbath after the first, that he went through the corn fields; and his disciples plucked the ears of corn, and did eat, rubbing them in their hands.

And certain of the Pharisees said unto them, Why do ye that which is not lawful to do on the sabbath days?

And Jesus answering them said, Have ye not read so much as this, what David did, when himself was an hungred, and they which were with him;

How he went into the house of God, and did take and eat the shewbread, and gave also to them that were with him; which it is not lawful to eat but for the priests alone?

And he said unto them, That the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath.
