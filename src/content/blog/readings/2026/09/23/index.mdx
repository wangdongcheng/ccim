## 读经一 | First Reading

**箴言 30:5-9 | Proverbs 30:5-9**

天主的每一句话都是精炼的；凡投靠他的，他必作护盾。不要在他的话上增添什么，免得他责斥你，显出你是说谎的人。

我向你求两件事，在我未死以前，请不要拒绝我：使虚假和谎言远离我；不要使我贫穷，也不要使我富有，只赐给我所需的食粮。免得我吃饱以后背弃你，说："上主是谁？"也免得我因贫穷而偷窃，亵渎我天主的名。

Every word of God proves true; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him. Add nothing to his words, lest he rebuke you and expose you as a liar.

Two things I ask of you; do not refuse them before I die. Keep falsehood and lying far from me. Give me neither poverty nor riches, but provide the food that I need. Otherwise, when I am full, I may deny you and say, "Who is the Lord?" Or, being poor, I may steal and profane the name of my God.

**答：上主，你的言语是我脚步前的明灯。**

Remove from me the way of falsehood,
and graciously teach me your law.
The law of your mouth is more precious to me

**R. Your word, O Lord, is a lamp to my feet.**

For ever, O Lord, your word

I hold back my feet from every evil way,
so that I may keep your word.

Through your precepts I gain understanding;
therefore I hate every false way.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 9:1-6 | Luke 9:1-6**

那时候，耶稣召集十二宗徒，赐给他们制伏一切魔鬼和医治疾病的能力与权柄，并派遣他们宣讲天主的国、治愈病人。

耶稣对他们说："你们上路时什么也不要带：不要带棍杖、口袋、食物或银钱，也不要带两件内衣。你们无论进入哪一家，就住在那里，直到离开当地。若有人不接待你们，你们离开那座城时，要把脚上的尘土拂去，作为反对他们的证据。"

于是，他们出发，走遍各乡村，到处宣讲福音、治愈疾病。

At that time, Jesus called the Twelve together and gave them power and authority over all demons and power to cure diseases. He sent them out to proclaim the kingdom of God and to heal the sick.

He told them, "Take nothing for your journey: no staff, bag, bread, or money, and do not take a second tunic. Whatever house you enter, remain there until you leave that place. Wherever people do not welcome you, leave that town and shake the dust from your feet as a testimony against them."

They departed and went through the villages, preaching the Good News and healing everywhere.

## 反省 | Reflection

从古至今，疾病一直纠缠着人类。哪里有疾病，哪里就有痛苦；哪里有痛苦，哪里就有向天呐喊的祈祷，哪里就召唤着人们彼此关爱、付出、医治。耶稣不仅自己医治病患，也召请和派遣门徒“医治各种疾病”。正如门徒驱魔的能力来自上主，医治的能力亦是如此。但门徒不能魔术般地使用此能力，他们该先像耶稣一样，感同身受于他人的痛楚，有勇气亲近他们，与他们交谈，倾听他们的故事，再呼求圣神的能力，以基督的心怀拥抱、覆手、医治他们。领受了上主的恩赐，基督徒就有责任做这个破碎世界的医治者。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

From ancient times to the present, illness has continually afflicted humanity. Wherever there is illness, there is suffering; wherever there is suffering, prayers cry out to heaven, calling people to care for one another, give of themselves, and bring healing. Jesus not only healed the sick himself but also called and sent his disciples to "cure every disease." Just as the disciples' authority to cast out demons came from the Lord, so did their power to heal. Yet the disciples could not use this power as though it were magic. They first had to follow Jesus' example: to share in the pain of others, courageously draw near to them, speak with them, and listen to their stories. Then they could call upon the power of the Holy Spirit and, with the heart of Christ, embrace them, lay hands upon them, and heal them. Having received the Lord's gifts, Christians have a responsibility to become healers in this broken world.

— Fr. Li Jianping; contributed by Le Ren Publishing House

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

没有停留，就没有出发。福音告诉我们，基督在派遣门徒执行使命之前，先“把他们叫到跟前”（参阅玛 10:1）。宣讲源于与主相遇；基督徒的一切行动，尤其是传教使命，都从这里开始，而不是从学院里学来的。不，不是这样！它始于与主相遇。事实上，为他作见证，就是把他彰显出来；可是，如果我们没有领受他的光，我们就会熄灭；如果我们不花时间与他相处，我们带给别人的便是自己，而不是他——我带去的是我自己，不是他——那么一切都会徒劳无功。因此，只有与他同在的人，才能把耶稣的福音带给别人。谁不与他同在，就不能传扬福音；他带去的只是观念，而不是福音。

同样，没有出发，也就没有停留。跟随基督并不是封闭在自己内的事：没有宣讲、服务和使命，我们与耶稣的关系便不会成长。（……）换句话说：我们领受了恩赐，因此蒙召献出自己；我们既然领受了恩赐，我们的圣召就是把自己作为礼物献给他人。我们内有身为天主子女的喜乐，就必须与那些尚未认识天主的弟兄姊妹分享！这正是宣讲的理由：走出去，把我们所领受的喜乐带给别人。（……）宣讲应当如此：与其谈论耶稣，不如把耶稣彰显出来。我们怎样彰显耶稣呢？就是以我们的见证。（教宗方济各，公开接见，2023年2月15日）

There is no going without staying : before sending the disciples forth on mission, Christ — the Gospel says — “calls them to himself” (cf. Mt 10:1). The proclamation is born from the encounter with the Lord; every Christian activity, especially the mission, begins from there. Not from what is learnt in an academy. No, no! It begins from the encounter with the Lord. Witnessing him, in fact, means radiating him; but, if we do not receive his light, we will be extinguished; if we do not spend time with him, we will bear ourselves instead of him — I am bringing myself and not him — and it will all be in vain. So only those who remain with him can bring the Gospel of Jesus. Anyone who does not remain with him cannot bear the Gospel. He will bring ideas, but not the Gospel. Equally, however, there is no staying without going. In fact, following Christ is not an inward-looking fact: without proclamation, without service, without mission, the relationship with Jesus does not grow. (…) In other words: we have a gift, so we are called to make a gift of ourselves; we have received a gift and our vocation is to make a gift of ourselves to others; there is in us the joy of being children of God, it must be shared with our brothers and sisters who do not yet know it! This is the reason for the proclamation. Going forth and bringing the joy of what we have received. (…) This is how one should proclaim: by showing Jesus rather than talking about Jesus. And how do we show Jesus? With our witness. (Pope Francis, General Audience, 15 February 2023)
