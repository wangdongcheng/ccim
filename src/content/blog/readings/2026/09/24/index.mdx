## 读经一 | First Reading

**训道篇 1:2-11 | Ecclesiastes 1:2-11**

训道者说：虚而又虚，虚而又虚，万事皆虚！人在太阳下辛勤劳作，究竟有什么益处？一代过去，一代又来，大地却永久常存。

太阳升起，太阳落下，又急速返回原处，再从那里升起。风吹向南方，又转向北方；不停旋转，周而复始。江河都流入大海，大海却总不满溢；江河仍流回它们所往之处，循环不息。

万事令人疲惫，无人能够尽述。眼睛看了仍不满足，耳朵听了也不厌足。过去发生的事还会再发生；从前做过的事还会再做。太阳之下没有新事。即使有人指着某事说："看，这是新的！"其实，在我们以前的世代，它早已存在。

人不再追念往昔的人；同样，将来的人也不会被后来者记念。

Vanity of vanities, says the Preacher; vanity of vanities, all is vanity. What does a person gain from all the labor done under the sun? One generation passes away and another comes, but the earth remains.

The sun rises and the sun sets, then hastens back to the place where it rises. The wind blows toward the south and turns toward the north; round and round it goes, returning on its circuits. All streams run to the sea, yet the sea is never filled; to the place from which they flow, the streams continue to return.

All things are wearisome beyond telling. The eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing. What has been will be again, and what has been done will be done again; there is nothing new under the sun. Even when someone says, "See, this is new," it has already existed in the ages before us.

Those of former times are no longer remembered, and those yet to come will not be remembered by the generations that follow them.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 90:3-6,12-14,17 | Psalm 90:3-6,12-14,17**

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。 | R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

你使世人归于灰土，说：
"亚当的子女，归来吧！"
一千年在你眼中，
如同刚刚过去的昨天，又像夜间的一更。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

你使他们如梦消逝；
他们好像清晨新生的青草：
早晨生长繁茂，
晚上便枯萎凋谢。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

求你教我们数算自己的岁月，
使我们获得内心的智慧。
上主，求你回顾！还要等待多久？
求你怜悯你的仆人。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

求你使我们清晨饱享你的慈爱，
让我们一生欢欣喜乐。
愿上主、我们的天主赐恩宠给我们；
求你使我们双手的工作顺利成功。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

You turn humanity back to dust,
saying, "Return, children of Adam."
For a thousand years in your sight
are like yesterday when it is past, or a watch in the night.

**R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

You sweep them away as in sleep;
they are like grass renewed in the morning:
in the morning it flourishes and grows,
but by evening it fades and withers.

**R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

Teach us to number our days,
that we may gain a heart of wisdom.
Return, O Lord! How long?
Have compassion on your servants.

**R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

Fill us in the morning with your steadfast love,
that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.
May the favor of the Lord our God rest upon us;
prosper the work of our hands.

**R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 9:7-9 | Luke 9:7-9**

那时候，分封侯黑落德听说了所发生的一切，便十分困惑，因为有人说："若翰从死人中复活了。"另一些人说："厄里亚显现了。"还有人说："古时的一位先知复活了。"

黑落德却说："若翰，我已经斩首了；那么，我听见这些事所指的这个人究竟是谁？"于是，他设法想见耶稣。

At that time, Herod the tetrarch heard about all that was happening and was greatly perplexed. Some were saying, "John has been raised from the dead." Others said, "Elijah has appeared," while still others claimed, "One of the ancient prophets has risen."

But Herod said, "John I beheaded. Who, then, is this man about whom I hear such things?" And he kept trying to see Jesus.

## 反省 | Reflection

黑落德本有机会保护若翰，让这位最后的先知继续宣讲天主的救恩；但他没有，竟让施洗者死在情妇黑落狄雅手里。黑落德也本有机会亲近耶稣，聆听这位比若翰更伟大者的福音，但他也没有抓住机会，任由走遍加里肋亚的耶稣从自己的封地离开。他想看看耶稣的心态，只是掌权者的百无聊赖，与渴慕天主无关。任何事物，只要危及他的权力和虚名，他都会毫不留情地使之消失。但是若翰是杀不死的：若翰死了，耶稣来了。若翰是黑落德的噩梦，耶稣也是世上所有“黑落德”的噩梦。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

Herod had the opportunity to protect John and allow this last prophet to continue proclaiming God's salvation. Yet he failed to do so and let the Baptist die at the hands of his mistress Herodias. Herod also had the opportunity to draw near to Jesus and hear the Gospel proclaimed by one greater than John. Once again, however, he let the opportunity pass, allowing Jesus—who traveled throughout Galilee—to leave his territory. His desire to see Jesus arose merely from a ruler's idle curiosity and had nothing to do with a longing for God. Anything that threatened his power or empty reputation, he would ruthlessly make disappear. But John could not truly be killed: John died, and Jesus came. John was Herod's nightmare, and Jesus remains the nightmare of every “Herod” in the world.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

我们是在日常生活的惯常事务中认识耶稣的。（……）是的，我们必须通过《教理》认识耶稣。然而，只在理智上认识祂还不够；那只是第一步。我们必须借着与祂交谈，在祈祷中跪下来向祂倾诉，从而认识耶稣。如果你不祈祷，不与耶稣交谈，你就不认识祂。你只是知道一些关于耶稣的事，却没有获得那由祈祷赋予人心的认识。

通过研读《教理》，我们在理智上认识耶稣；通过祈祷及与祂交谈，我们在心中认识耶稣。这对我们帮助很大，但仍然不够。（……）还有第三种认识耶稣的方法：成为祂的门徒，与祂同行，和祂一起走。

如果不把自己交托给耶稣，不把自己的生命投入其中，人就无法真正认识祂。当许多人——也包括我们自己——提出“这人究竟是谁？”这个问题时，天主圣言回答我们：“你想知道祂是谁吗？那么，就阅读教会对祂的教导，在祈祷中与祂交谈，并与祂同行，走祂所走的道路。这样，你就会认识这人究竟是谁。”这就是道路！

（教宗方济各，圣玛尔大之家弥撒讲道，2013年9月26日）

You come to know Jesus in the day-to-day routine of everyday life. (…) Yes, you must learn about Jesus from the Catechism. But it is not enough to know Him with your mind: that is just the first step. You must come to know Jesus through dialogue with Him, by speaking to Him, in prayer, on your knees. If you do not pray, if you do not speak to Jesus, you do not know Him. You know things about Jesus, but you do not have that knowledge which the heart gives you in prayer. Knowing Jesus with the mind comes from studying the Catechism; knowing Jesus with the heart comes from prayer and dialogue with Him. This helps us quite a lot, but it is not enough… There is a third way to get to know Jesus: it is discipleship. Going with Him, walking with Him. (…) One cannot get to know Jesus without committing yourself to Him, without staking your life on Him. When so many people—including ourselves—ask this question, “But who is this?”, the Word of God answers us: “Do you want to know who this is? Read what the Church tells you about Him, speak to Him in prayer and walk His path with Him. In this way, you will come to know who this man is”. This is the way! (Pope Francis, Homily at Santa Marta, 26 September 2013)
