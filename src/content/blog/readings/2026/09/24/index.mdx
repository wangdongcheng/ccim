## 读经一 | First Reading

**训道篇 1:2-11 | Ecclesiastes 1:2-11**

训道者说：虚而又虚，虚而又虚，万事皆虚！人在太阳下辛勤劳作，究竟有什么益处？一代过去，一代又来，大地却永久常存。

太阳升起，太阳落下，又急速返回原处，再从那里升起。风吹向南方，又转向北方；不停旋转，周而复始。江河都流入大海，大海却总不满溢；江河仍流回它们所往之处，循环不息。

万事令人疲惫，无人能够尽述。眼睛看了仍不满足，耳朵听了也不厌足。过去发生的事还会再发生；从前做过的事还会再做。太阳之下没有新事。即使有人指着某事说："看，这是新的！"其实，在我们以前的世代，它早已存在。

人不再追念往昔的人；同样，将来的人也不会被后来者记念。

Vanity of vanities, says the Preacher; vanity of vanities, all is vanity. What does a person gain from all the labor done under the sun? One generation passes away and another comes, but the earth remains.

The sun rises and the sun sets, then hastens back to the place where it rises. The wind blows toward the south and turns toward the north; round and round it goes, returning on its circuits. All streams run to the sea, yet the sea is never filled; to the place from which they flow, the streams continue to return.

All things are wearisome beyond telling. The eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing. What has been will be again, and what has been done will be done again; there is nothing new under the sun. Even when someone says, "See, this is new," it has already existed in the ages before us.

Those of former times are no longer remembered, and those yet to come will not be remembered by the generations that follow them.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 90:3-6,12-14,17 | Psalm 90:3-6,12-14,17**

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。 | R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

你使世人归于灰土，说：
"亚当的子女，归来吧！"
一千年在你眼中，
如同刚刚过去的昨天，又像夜间的一更。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

你使他们如梦消逝；
他们好像清晨新生的青草：
早晨生长繁茂，
晚上便枯萎凋谢。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

求你教我们数算自己的岁月，
使我们获得内心的智慧。
上主，求你回顾！还要等待多久？
求你怜悯你的仆人。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

求你使我们清晨饱享你的慈爱，
让我们一生欢欣喜乐。
愿上主、我们的天主赐恩宠给我们；
求你使我们双手的工作顺利成功。

**答：上主，从永远到永远，你作了我们的靠山。**

You turn humanity back to dust,
saying, "Return, children of Adam."
For a thousand years in your sight
are like yesterday when it is past, or a watch in the night.

**R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

You sweep them away as in sleep;
they are like grass renewed in the morning:
in the morning it flourishes and grows,
but by evening it fades and withers.

**R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

Teach us to number our days,
that we may gain a heart of wisdom.
Return, O Lord! How long?
Have compassion on your servants.

**R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

Fill us in the morning with your steadfast love,
that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.
May the favor of the Lord our God rest upon us;
prosper the work of our hands.

**R. Lord, you have been our refuge from age to age.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 9:7-9 | Luke 9:7-9**

那时候，分封侯黑落德听说了所发生的一切，便十分困惑，因为有人说："若翰从死人中复活了。"另一些人说："厄里亚显现了。"还有人说："古时的一位先知复活了。"

黑落德却说："若翰，我已经斩首了；那么，我听见这些事所指的这个人究竟是谁？"于是，他设法想见耶稣。

At that time, Herod the tetrarch heard about all that was happening and was greatly perplexed. Some were saying, "John has been raised from the dead." Others said, "Elijah has appeared," while still others claimed, "One of the ancient prophets has risen."

But Herod said, "John I beheaded. Who, then, is this man about whom I hear such things?" And he kept trying to see Jesus.
