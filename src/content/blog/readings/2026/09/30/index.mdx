## 读经一 | First Reading

**约伯传 9:1-12,14-16 | Job 9:1-12,14-16**

约伯答复他的朋友们说：“我确实知道事情是这样的，但是人怎能同天主理论呢？如果有人想同天主辩论，一千个问题，一个也不能回答。纵然天资聪敏，力量强大，谁能对抗天主，而能自保呢？

“他能移山，山却不知；他一发怒，把山翻倒。他震动大地的基础，大地的支柱立即摇撼。他出禁令，太阳就不升起来，星辰也发不出光辉。他独自伸展诸天，步行在海浪之上。他创造了北斗、猎户、昴星和南极的星群。他所做的大事不可了解，他奇异的作为不可胜数。他从我身旁经过，我看不见他；他走过去，我也没有觉察。他若强取，谁能阻止？谁敢问他：‘你在做什么？’

“我又怎敢回答天主，怎敢选择言词与他抗辩？我纵然有理，也不敢回答，唯有哀求我的审判官开恩。纵然他回答了我的呼声，我也不相信他会听我的祈求。”

Job answered his friends: “Indeed, I know that this is so; but how can a mortal be just before God? If anyone wished to dispute with him, that person could not answer him once in a thousand. God is wise in heart and mighty in strength; who has resisted him and remained unharmed?

“He moves mountains before they know it and overturns them in his anger. He shakes the earth from its place, and its pillars tremble. He commands the sun, and it does not rise; he seals up the stars. He alone stretches out the heavens and treads upon the waves of the sea. He made the Bear and Orion, the Pleiades and the chambers of the south. He does great things beyond understanding and wonders without number. He passes by me, and I do not see him; he moves on, but I do not perceive him. If he seizes something, who can stop him? Who can ask him, ‘What are you doing?’

“How, then, could I answer him or choose my words against him? Though I were in the right, I could not answer; I could only plead for mercy from my judge. If I called and he answered me, I would not believe that he was listening to my voice.”

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 9:57-62 | Luke 9:57-62**

耶稣和他的门徒在路上行走时，有一个人对他说：“你不论往哪里去，我都要追随你。”耶稣对他说：“狐狸有穴，天上的飞鸟有巢；人子却没有枕头的地方。”

耶稣又对另一个人说：“你追随我吧！”那人说：“主啊，请准我先去埋葬我的父亲。”耶稣对他说：“让死人去埋葬他们的死人吧！你该去传扬天主的国。”

又有一个人说：“主啊！我要追随你；但是请准我先去向我的家人告别。”耶稣对他说：“手扶着犁而往后看的，不适于进天主的国。”

As Jesus and his disciples were traveling along the road, someone said to him, “I will follow you wherever you go.” Jesus replied, “Foxes have holes, and the birds of the air have nests; but the Son of man has nowhere to lay his head.”

To another he said, “Follow me.” But the man answered, “Lord, first let me go and bury my father.” Jesus told him, “Leave the dead to bury their own dead; but as for you, go and proclaim the kingdom of God.”

Another said, “I will follow you, Lord; but first let me say farewell to those at home.” Jesus said to him, “No one who puts a hand to the plow and looks back is fit for the kingdom of God.”

## 反省 | Reflection

“让死人去埋葬死人”，这话听起来十分不近人情。但若那人的父亲尚在人世，他只是想等到父母离世、了无牵挂时才来跟随，那么他就会错过一切。“先回去安葬父亲”听起来合情合理，却很可能只是推脱的借口。

我们何尝不是如此？我们也常把借口合理化：“等我忙完这一阵，再去祈祷”“等我想通了，再去与人和好”“等我把钱挣够了，再去专注信仰”……可是永远不会有忙完、想通、挣够钱的一天，于是祈祷、和好、专注信仰的念头也就泥牛入海了。若内心有所触动，就马上行动，不要寻找托辞！

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

“Leave the dead to bury their own dead” can sound exceedingly harsh. Yet if the man’s father was still alive and he merely intended to wait until his parents had died and every obligation was gone before following Jesus, he would miss everything. “First let me bury my father” sounds reasonable, but it could easily be an excuse for delay.

Are we not often the same? We make our excuses appear reasonable: “I will pray after this busy period,” “I will reconcile with that person once I have sorted out my thoughts,” or “I will focus on faith after I have earned enough money.” Yet the day when everything is finished, understood, and financially secure never arrives, so our intentions to pray, reconcile, and attend to faith disappear without a trace. When your heart is moved, act at once; do not look for excuses.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各说，福音中的三位人物让我们看见，彻底跟随耶稣需要什么。第一人慷慨地许诺无论耶稣往哪里去都要跟随，但耶稣提醒他，人子没有安枕之处。第二人蒙耶稣亲自召叫，却请求先去安葬父亲；耶稣以刻意震撼人心的话，强调跟随祂、宣扬天国的首要地位，甚至高于家庭等极重要的现实。

第三人也愿意跟随，却先提出回家告别的条件。耶稣指出，跟随祂不能带着遗憾频频回首，而需要果断的德行。对基督和福音的热情，应落实为亲近弟兄姊妹的具体行动，尤其是关怀那些最需要接纳和照顾的人，正如耶稣自己所生活的那样。

（教宗方济各，三钟经，2019年6月30日）

Pope Francis explains that the three people in this Gospel show what is required to follow Jesus completely. The first offers an enthusiastic promise, but Jesus warns him that the Son of man has no secure place to rest. The second is personally called yet asks to bury his father first; Jesus’ deliberately challenging reply stresses that following him and proclaiming God’s kingdom take priority even over realities as important as family.

The third also wants to follow, but only after saying farewell at home. Jesus teaches that discipleship leaves no room for regretful backward glances; it demands decisiveness. Passion for Christ and the Gospel must become concrete acts of closeness to others, especially to those most in need of welcome and care, just as Jesus himself lived.

(Pope Francis, Angelus, 30 June 2019)
