## 第一篇读经

### 格林多人前书 3:18–23 / 1 Corinthians 3:18–23

谁也不要欺骗自己。你们中若有人自以为在这世上是聪明的，他应当成为世人眼中的愚人，好使自己真正成为有智慧的人。因为这世界的智慧，在天主面前原是愚妄的。经上记载：“祂使智者陷入自己的诡计。”又说：“上主知道智者的思念，知道它们都是虚妄的。”

所以，谁也不要拿人来夸耀，因为一切都是你们的：无论保禄、阿颇罗、刻法，或世界、生命、死亡，现在或将来，一切都是你们的；但你们属于基督，而基督属于天主。

> Let no man deceive himself. If any man among you seemeth to be wise in this world, let him become a fool, that he may be wise. For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written, He taketh the wise in their own craftiness. And again, The Lord knoweth the thoughts of the wise, that they are vain.
>
> Therefore let no man glory in men. For all things are yours; whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things to come; all are yours; and ye are Christ's; and Christ is God's.

**上主的圣言。**
**Thanks be to God.**

## 答唱咏

### 圣咏 24:1–6 / Psalm 24:1–6

**答：上主的山，谁能登上？心地纯洁的人，必蒙祂祝福。**

大地和其中的一切都属于上主，世界和居住其中的人也都属于祂。因为是祂把大地奠定在海洋之上，使它坚立于水流之上。

> The earth is the LORD'S, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein. For he hath founded it upon the seas, and established it upon the floods.

**答：上主的山，谁能登上？心地纯洁的人，必蒙祂祝福。**

谁能登上上主的山？谁能站在祂的圣所？就是手洁心清的人；他的心不趋向虚妄，也不以诡诈起誓。

> Who shall ascend into the hill of the LORD? or who shall stand in his holy place? He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart; who hath not lifted up his soul unto vanity, nor sworn deceitfully.

**答：上主的山，谁能登上？心地纯洁的人，必蒙祂祝福。**

这样的人必蒙上主降福，也必从拯救他的天主那里获得正义。这就是寻求祂的世代，是寻求雅各伯天主仪容的人。

> He shall receive the blessing from the LORD, and righteousness from the God of his salvation. This is the generation of them that seek him, that seek thy face, O Jacob. Selah.

**答：上主的山，谁能登上？心地纯洁的人，必蒙祂祝福。**

# 福音

## 圣路加福音 5:1–11 / Luke 5:1–11

有一天，耶稣站在革乃撒勒湖边，群众拥挤着祂，为要聆听天主的圣言。祂看见湖边停着两只船，渔夫已经离船上岸，正在洗网。耶稣上了其中一只属于西满的船，请他把船稍微划离岸边。祂便坐下，从船上教导群众。

> And it came to pass, that, as the people pressed upon him to hear the word of God, he stood by the lake of Gennesaret, and saw two ships standing by the lake: but the fishermen were gone out of them, and were washing their nets. And he entered into one of the ships, which was Simon's, and prayed him that he would thrust out a little from the land. And he sat down, and taught the people out of the ship.

讲完以后，耶稣对西满说：“划到深处去，撒下你们的网捕鱼吧！”西满回答说：“老师，我们整夜劳苦，什么也没有捕到；但既然祢这样说，我就撒网。”

> Now when he had left speaking, he said unto Simon, Launch out into the deep, and let down your nets for a draught. And Simon answering said unto him, Master, we have toiled all the night, and have taken nothing: nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net.

他们这样做了，竟捕到极多的鱼，连渔网都快要裂开。他们便招呼另一只船上的伙伴来帮助。他们来了，把两只船都装满了鱼，甚至船都快要沉下去。

> And when they had this done, they inclosed a great multitude of fishes: and their net brake. And they beckoned unto their partners, which were in the other ship, that they should come and help them. And they came, and filled both the ships, so that they began to sink.

西满伯多禄一看见这一切，就跪伏在耶稣膝前说：“主，请离开我吧，因为我是一个罪人。”因为他和同他一起的人，看见他们所捕获的鱼如此之多，都十分惊骇；西满的伙伴，载伯德的儿子雅各伯和若望，也同样惊讶。

> When Simon Peter saw it, he fell down at Jesus' knees, saying, Depart from me; for I am a sinful man, O Lord. For he was astonished, and all that were with him, at the draught of the fishes which they had taken: and so was also James, and John, the sons of Zebedee, which were partners with Simon.

耶稣却对西满说：“不要害怕！从今以后，你要成为捕人的人。”他们把船划到岸边以后，便舍弃了一切，跟随了耶稣。

> And Jesus said unto Simon, Fear not; from henceforth thou shalt catch men. And when they had brought their ships to land, they forsook all, and followed him.

**基督的福音。**
**Praise to thee, O Christ.**
