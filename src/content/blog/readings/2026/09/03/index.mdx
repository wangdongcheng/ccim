## 读经一 | First Reading

**格林多人前书 3:18-23 | 1 Corinthians 3:18-23**

弟兄们：谁也不要自欺。你们中间若有人自以为在这世代是有智慧的，就该变为愚笨，好使自己成为有智慧的。因为这世界的智慧，在天主看来是愚笨的。经上记载：“他以智者的诡计捕捉智者”；又说：“上主认透智者的思念，知道那些都是虚幻。”

所以，谁也不可拿人来夸口，因为一切都是你们的：无论是保禄、阿颇罗、刻法，或是世界、生命、死亡、现在、将来，一切都是你们的；你们却是基督的，而基督是天主的。

Brothers and sisters, let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you considers himself wise in this age, let him become a fool so that he may become truly wise. For the wisdom of this world is foolishness in God’s sight. As Scripture says, “He catches the wise in their craftiness,” and again, “The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise and knows that they are futile.”

Therefore, let no one boast about human leaders. All things are yours—whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas, the world or life or death, the present or the future—all belong to you; and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 5:1-11 | Luke 5:1-11**

有一天，耶稣站在革乃撒勒湖边，群众拥到他跟前，要听天主的道理。他看见两艘船停在湖边，渔夫已经下船，正在洗网。耶稣上了属于西满的那艘船，请他把船稍微划离岸边；他便坐下，从船上教训群众。

讲完以后，耶稣对西满说：“把船划到深处去，下网打鱼吧！”西满回答说：“老师，我们整夜劳苦，一无所得；但是照你的话，我要下网。”他们照样做了，网了许多鱼，连网也险些破裂。他们便招呼另一艘船上的同伴来帮助。伙伴来了，把两艘船都装得满满的，船甚至开始下沉。

西满伯多禄看见这事，就跪伏在耶稣膝前，说：“主啊，请你离开我吧！因为我是个罪人。”他和同伴们都因捕获这么多鱼而惊骇，西满的伙伴、载伯德的儿子雅各伯和若望也是一样。耶稣对西满说：“不要害怕！从今以后，你要做捕人的渔夫。”他们把船划到岸边，便舍弃一切，跟随了耶稣。

One day, while Jesus stood beside the Lake of Gennesaret, the crowd pressed around him to hear the word of God. He saw two boats by the shore; the fishermen had gone out of them and were washing their nets. Jesus entered the boat belonging to Simon and asked him to put out a little from the land. Then he sat down and taught the crowds from the boat.

When he had finished speaking, he said to Simon, “Put out into the deep and let down your nets for a catch.” Simon answered, “Master, we worked all night and caught nothing; nevertheless, at your word I will let down the nets.” When they had done so, they enclosed such a great number of fish that their nets began to break. They signaled to their partners in the other boat to come and help, and they filled both boats until they began to sink.

When Simon Peter saw this, he fell at Jesus’ knees and said, “Depart from me, Lord, for I am a sinful man.” He and everyone with him were astonished at the catch of fish, as were James and John, the sons of Zebedee, who were Simon’s partners. Jesus said to Simon, “Do not be afraid; from now on you will catch men.” When they brought the boats ashore, they left everything and followed him.

## 反省 | Reflection

跟鱼打交道，总比跟人相处容易；拥抱宠物，也总比拥抱人简单。“来跟随我！别再只接触鱼了，跟我接近人吧！”耶稣向西满和同伴发出的邀请，也是向后世每一位基督徒发出的。

四位渔夫的日子也算富足惬意，然而不谙渔道的耶稣，却向他们展示了全新的世界和能力。耶稣请他们把人生之舟划向深处，意味着基督不断邀请我们离开安乐窝，与祂一起走向世海中沉浮、难以相处、尚未认识天主的人群。这是天主的工程；谁接受这项挑战，谁就会像初期的渔夫门徒一样，经验到天国的能力和魅力！

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

Dealing with fish is always easier than dealing with people, and embracing a pet is often simpler than embracing another person. “Come, follow me! Do not remain occupied only with fish; come with me and draw close to people.” The invitation Jesus gave to Simon and his companions is also addressed to every Christian who comes after them.

The four fishermen already enjoyed a reasonably secure and comfortable life. Yet Jesus, who was no fisherman, revealed to them an entirely new world and a new power. By asking them to take the boat of their lives into deep water, Christ continually invites us to leave our comfort zones and go with him toward people who struggle in the sea of life, who may be difficult to serve, and who do not yet know God. This is God’s work. Whoever accepts the challenge will experience, like the first fishermen-disciples, the power and attraction of the kingdom of heaven.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

本笃十六世指出，首批门徒信赖耶稣、依靠祂的话，并在奇迹的陪伴下跟随祂。捕鱼以前，西满称耶稣为“老师”；奇迹以后，他称祂为“主”。天主召叫人的方式，并不首先考量被选者的才能，而是看他的信德；西满的回答正表达了这种信德：“照你的话，我要下网。”

伯多禄的经验虽然独特，却也代表每一位福音使徒的召叫：即使面对失败和困难，也不可灰心停止宣讲基督。司铎和献身生活的圣召尤其是天主的工程；人不是自己圣召的作者，而是回应天主的召叫。人的软弱无须害怕，因为天主的力量会在贫乏中行动，祂的慈悲能转化并更新一切。我们的责任是怀着信德撒网，其余的交给上主。

（教宗本笃十六世，三钟经，2013年2月10日）

Pope Benedict XVI explains that the first disciples followed Jesus by trusting him and relying upon his word, while signs accompanied their journey. Before the miraculous catch, Simon calls Jesus “Master”; afterward, he addresses him as “Lord.” God’s way of calling does not focus first on the abilities of those chosen, but upon their faith—the faith expressed when Simon agrees to lower the nets at Jesus’ word.

Peter’s experience is unique, yet it also represents the calling of every apostle of the Gospel: failures and difficulties must never discourage the proclamation of Christ. Vocations to priesthood and consecrated life are especially God’s work; people do not invent their own vocation but respond to a divine call. Human weakness need not be afraid, because God’s strength acts within our poverty and his mercy transforms and renews. Our task is to cast the nets in faith; the Lord will accomplish the rest.

(Pope Benedict XVI, Angelus, 10 February 2013)
