## 读经一 | First Reading

**达尼尔先知书 7:9-10,13-14 | Daniel 7:9-10,13-14**

我观看，看到有安置的宝座，那位万古常存者坐在上面。他的衣服像雪一般光亮，头发像羊毛那样洁白。他的宝座好似火焰，宝座的轮子如同烈火。一道火河涌出，从他面前流下。有千万的侍者服事他，有亿兆的人侍立在他面前；他开庭审判，展开了案卷。

我在夜间的异象中观看，看见一位像似人子者，乘云而来。他被引到那万古常存者面前，接受了权威、尊荣和王权，使各国、各族、说各种语言的人都事奉他。他的统治将永世常存，他的王国永不灭亡。

As I watched, thrones were set in place, and the Ancient of Days took his seat. His clothing was white as snow, and the hair of his head like pure wool. His throne was flames of fire, with wheels of burning fire. A stream of fire poured out before him. Thousands upon thousands served him, and countless hosts stood before him. The court sat in judgment, and the books were opened.

In the visions of the night I saw one like a son of man coming with the clouds of heaven. He was brought before the Ancient of Days and received dominion, glory, and kingship, so that every people, nation, and language should serve him. His dominion is everlasting and shall not pass away; his kingdom shall never be destroyed.

## 福音 | Gospel

**若望福音 1:47-51 | John 1:47-51**

那时候，耶稣看见纳塔乃耳向他走过来，就指着他说：“看哪，这的确是一个以色列人，他身上毫无诡诈。”纳塔乃耳对他说：“你怎么认识我呢？”耶稣回答说：“斐理伯叫你以前，你还在无花果树下时，我就看见你了。”

纳塔乃耳回答说：“老师！你是天主之子，你是以色列的王。”耶稣说：“因为我对你说：‘我看见你在无花果树下’，你就信了吗？你将来要看见比这个更大的事！”又对他说：“我实实在在告诉你们：你们要看见天门敞开，天主的天使在人子身上，上去下来。”

At that time, Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward him and said, “Behold, a true Israelite in whom there is no deceit!” Nathanael asked him, “How do you know me?” Jesus answered, “Before Philip called you, while you were beneath the fig tree, I saw you.”

Nathanael replied, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!” Jesus said, “Do you believe because I told you that I saw you beneath the fig tree? You will see greater things than these.” Then he said, “Truly, truly, I tell you: you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man.”

## 反省 | Reflection

天主所造世界的丰富，远不止我们的感官能捕捉的。天使没有物理意义上的形象，却是天主造物中异常活跃的一群。他们靠近上主，所以更加真纯。从《创世纪》的开篇，到《默示录》的结尾，在人类的舞台上，尤其在人类急需援助的时刻，常出现天使的身影，他们或保护、或引导、或传信、或惩罚、或医治，奉行上主的旨意，襄助人类。他们是“上下往来”的一群，将人类的需要带上去，把上主的慈爱带下来，来往于天人之间。如果你心灵的感官够敏锐，或可看到天使的翱翔，听到天使的歌声，感受到天使的常伴左右。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

The richness of the world God created extends far beyond what our senses can perceive. Angels have no form in the physical sense, yet they are extraordinarily active among God’s creatures. Because they remain close to the Lord, they are all the more pure and true. From the opening of Genesis to the conclusion of Revelation, angels often appear on the human stage, especially when people urgently need help. They protect, guide, carry messages, punish, and heal, fulfilling the Lord’s will and assisting humanity.

They are the ones who “ascend and descend,” carrying human needs upward and bringing the Lord’s loving kindness down, moving between heaven and earth. If the senses of your heart are attentive enough, perhaps you may see angels soaring, hear their song, and feel that they are always near.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

本笃十六世指出，耶稣称纳塔乃耳为毫无诡诈的真以色列人，这赞语唤起了圣咏中对心无欺诈之人的祝福。纳塔乃耳惊讶地问耶稣怎样认识自己；耶稣提到无花果树下那个无人知晓、却显然关系到他生命的重要时刻，使他感到自己被完全了解。

正因为耶稣熟悉他的内心和人生道路，纳塔乃耳明白自己可以信赖祂，并宣认耶稣是天主之子、以色列的君王。这份宣认，是依附耶稣、踏上门徒旅程的重要第一步。

（教宗本笃十六世，公开接见，2006年10月4日）

Benedict XVI observes that Jesus’ praise of Nathanael as a true Israelite without deceit recalls the psalmist’s blessing upon the person whose spirit is free of guile. Nathanael is astonished and asks how Jesus knows him. By referring to the hidden moment beneath the fig tree—a moment that clearly mattered deeply in Nathanael’s life—Jesus shows that he knows Nathanael completely.

Feeling understood and recognizing that Jesus knows the road of his life, Nathanael realizes that he can trust him. His confession of Jesus as the Son of God and King of Israel thus marks an important first step on the journey of becoming attached to Jesus.

(Pope Benedict XVI, General Audience, 4 October 2006)
