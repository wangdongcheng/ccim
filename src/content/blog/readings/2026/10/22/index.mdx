## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 3:14-21 | Ephesians 3:14-21**

弟兄们：为此，我跪在天父面前，天上地上的每一个家族都从祂得到名字。我求祂按照自己光荣的丰盛，藉圣神的大能，使你们内在的生命坚强起来；也愿基督藉着信德居于你们心内，使你们在爱德中深深扎根，稳固建基。

愿你们与全体圣徒一起，有力量领悟那长、阔、高、深，认识基督超越一切知识的爱，直到天主的全部丰盛充满你们。

天主能藉在我们内运行的力量，成就远远超过我们一切祈求和想象的事。愿祂在教会内，并在基督耶稣内，世世代代永受光荣。阿们。

For this reason I kneel before the Father, who gives every family in heaven and on earth its name. I ask that, out of his glorious abundance, he may give you inward strength through the power of his Spirit. May Christ make his home in your hearts through faith, and may love be the soil in which you take root and the foundation on which you stand.

Together with all the saints, may you receive the strength to grasp the breadth, length, height, and depth, and to experience Christ's love, which reaches beyond what knowledge can contain. May you thus be filled with God's entire fullness.

God's power is working within us, and he can accomplish immeasurably more than anything we request or imagine. May glory be his in the Church and in Christ Jesus, through every generation, eternally. Amen.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 32:1-2,4-5,11-12,18-19 | Psalm 33:1-2,4-5,11-12,18-19**

**答：上主的慈爱弥漫大地。 | R. The earth is full of the Lord's steadfast love.**

义人，你们应当因上主而欢呼；正直的人理当歌颂祂。弹起琴来感谢上主，用十弦琴奏乐赞美祂。

**答：上主的慈爱弥漫大地。**

上主所说的正直无误，所做的无不忠实。祂喜爱正义和公道，大地处处充满祂的慈爱。

**答：上主的慈爱弥漫大地。**

上主的旨意永不动摇，祂心中的计划代代长存。以上主为天主的民族多么有福！祂选作自己产业的人民多么有福！

**答：上主的慈爱弥漫大地。**

上主注视敬畏祂的人，照看那些寄望于祂慈爱的人；祂救他们脱离死亡，饥荒时也保全他们的生命。

**答：上主的慈爱弥漫大地。**

**R. The earth is full of the Lord's steadfast love.**

You who live justly, rejoice in the Lord; songs of praise are fitting for upright hearts. Thank him with the lyre and play music to him on the ten-stringed harp.

**R. The earth is full of the Lord's steadfast love.**

The Lord speaks what is right, and faithfulness marks everything he does. Justice and righteousness delight him; his enduring love fills the earth.

**R. The earth is full of the Lord's steadfast love.**

The Lord's purpose endures forever, and his heart's intentions remain through every generation. Happy the nation that has the Lord for its God, the people he has chosen to belong to him.

**R. The earth is full of the Lord's steadfast love.**

The Lord watches over those who revere him and place their hope in his enduring love. He rescues them from death and sustains their lives when famine comes.

**R. The earth is full of the Lord's steadfast love.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 12:49-53 | Luke 12:49-53**

那时候，耶稣对门徒说：「我来为把火投到大地上，我多么盼望它已经烧起来！我必须经历一场洗礼；在它完成以前，我心中何等迫切！

「你们以为我来，是要让地上从此太平吗？我告诉你们，我带来的反而是分裂。此后，同住一家里的五个人会彼此对立，三人与两人相争，两人与三人相争。

「父亲将与儿子对立，儿子与父亲对立；母亲与女儿对立，女儿与母亲对立；婆母与儿媳对立，儿媳与婆母对立。」

Jesus said to his disciples: “I came to cast fire upon the earth, and how eagerly I long for it to be burning already! There is a baptism I must undergo, and I am under great strain until it is fulfilled.

“Do you suppose my coming will bring peace on earth? I tell you, it will bring division instead. From now on, five people in one household will be at odds: three opposing two, and two opposing three.

“A father and his son will oppose one another, as will a mother and her daughter, and a mother-in-law and her daughter-in-law.”

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父反省摘要：耶稣以平凡的隐居生活亲近众人，又在宣讲与十字架上为人倾尽生命。祂的爱如火，亲近祂的人也会被点燃。追随基督，需要拒绝虚假的和谐，勇于为真理和正义作抉择，抵抗邪恶，归向天主。（[万有真原，乐仁出版社供稿](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-22)）

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection: Jesus shared ordinary human life in his hidden years, then gave himself completely through his ministry and the cross. His love kindles those who draw near him. Following Christ means rejecting false harmony, choosing truth and justice with courage, resisting evil, and turning toward God. ([WanYou ZhenYuan; contributed by Claretian Publications Macau](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-22))

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世三钟经讲话摘要：耶稣预告自己将在耶路撒冷遭受拒绝和十字架的苦难。行善、坚持真理，也可能招致敌意与压迫。祂邀请我们依靠祂，继续为所有人谋求益处，包括伤害我们的人；以爱忠于真理，不以报复回应凌辱。（[2025年8月17日三钟经，梵蒂冈新闻网当日选文](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/22.html)）

Summary of Pope Leo XIV's Angelus: Jesus anticipates rejection and the suffering of the cross in Jerusalem. Goodness and truth can likewise meet hostility and oppression. With his help, we must persevere in seeking everyone's good, including the good of those who hurt us, remaining loving and truthful rather than retaliating against insults. ([Angelus, 17 August 2025; selection in Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/22.html))
