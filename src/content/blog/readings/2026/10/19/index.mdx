## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 2:1-10 | Ephesians 2:1-10**

弟兄们：过去，你们陷在过犯和罪恶中，如同死去的人。那时，你们按这世界的风气生活，听从空中掌权者，就是如今仍在悖逆的人身上活动的恶神。我们从前也同他们一样，顺从肉性的欲望，任凭身体和心思的喜好支配自己；按本性，我们也和其他人一样，是应受义怒的子女。

然而，天主满怀慈悲，以深厚的爱爱了我们。即使我们因过犯而死，他仍使我们与基督一同获得生命——你们得救是由于恩宠。他使我们与基督一同复活，又使我们在基督耶稣内，与他一同安坐于天上。这样，他要在未来的世代，藉着在基督耶稣内给予我们的慈爱，显明他恩宠无可比拟的丰富。

你们因恩宠、藉信德而获救；这救恩不是你们自己取得的，而是天主所赐的礼物，也不是行为换来的，因此没有人可以夸耀。我们是天主的化工，在基督耶稣内被创造，为要实行善工；这些善工是天主预先安排，要我们在生活中践行的。

Brothers and sisters, wrongdoing and sin once left you dead. You followed this world's course and the ruler of the air, the spirit active among the disobedient. We too lived among them, obeying bodily cravings and the desires of body and mind. By nature, we shared humanity's condition under God's wrath.

Yet God, abundant in mercy and love, gave us life with Christ even when our trespasses had left us dead: salvation comes through grace. He raised us with Christ and seated us with him in heaven, in Christ Jesus. In the coming ages, his kindness toward us in Christ Jesus would reveal his grace's surpassing riches.

Grace saves you through faith. This is God's gift, no achievement of yours; works do not earn it, so nobody can boast. We are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus for the good deeds he prepared beforehand as our way of life.

怀着感恩进入他的门，以赞歌走进他的庭院；向他致谢，颂扬他的圣名。

**答：他创造了我们，我们属于他。**

因为上主是美善的，他的慈爱永不止息，他的忠信延续于世世代代。

**答：他创造了我们，我们属于他。**

**R. He made us, and we belong to him.**

Let every land shout joyfully to the Lord. Serve him gladly and come before him singing for joy.

**R. He made us, and we belong to him.**

**R. He made us, and we belong to him.**

The Lord is good; his steadfast love never ends, and his faithfulness reaches every generation.

**R. He made us, and we belong to him.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 12:13-21 | Luke 12:13-21**

那时，人群中有个人向耶稣请求：老师，请叫我的兄弟与我分产业。耶稣回答他：朋友，谁派我来为你们审判或分配家产呢？随后，他告诫众人：要警醒，提防各种贪心；即使财物丰厚，人的生命也不以家产为根基。

他给他们讲了一个比喻：一个富人的田地获得大丰收。他暗自盘算：我没有足够的地方存放收成，该怎么办？于是他决定：我要把原来的仓房拆掉，另盖更大的，将所有的粮食和财物存进去。然后，我就可以告诉自己：你已经积下足够享用多年的财富，可以安心休息，尽情吃喝快乐了。

天主却对他说：糊涂的人，今晚就要收回你的生命；你所备下的东西，到底会属于谁？那只顾为自己积聚财物，却不在天主前成为富有的人，也有同样的结局。

Someone in the crowd asked Jesus to tell his brother to divide their inheritance with him. Jesus replied by asking who had appointed him to judge their case or distribute their property. He then warned everyone to remain watchful against every kind of greed: even great abundance cannot make possessions the substance of a person's life.

He illustrated this with a parable. A wealthy man's land yielded an exceptional harvest. With nowhere to put it all, the man wondered what he should do. He decided to pull down his barns and replace them with larger ones, where he could gather all his grain and possessions. Then, he thought, he could assure himself that he had ample supplies for many years: he could take his ease, feast, drink and enjoy himself.

But God called him foolish: that very night his life would be required of him. Who, then, would receive everything he had prepared? Jesus said this is the fate of anyone who accumulates treasure for himself without becoming rich in relation to God.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父反省摘要：耶稣和教会并不敌视富人；问题在于人可能只顾占有，却不懂善用财富，最终反被财富控制。比喻中的富人不断囤积，以为能长久享受，却无法掌握生命和时间。当财物使人愈加贪婪、自私、封闭，甚至沉溺幻想时，人便已沦为财富的奴隶。（[万有真原，乐仁出版社供稿](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-19)）

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection: Jesus and the Church do not oppose wealthy people. The danger is possessing riches without using them well, until possessions control their owner. The rich man keeps hoarding, imagining lasting enjoyment, yet cannot command life or time. When wealth makes us increasingly greedy, selfish, closed to others and lost in fantasy, we have become its slaves. ([WanYou ZhenYuan; contributed by Claretian Publications Macau](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-19))

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世讲道摘要：生命的充实来自喜乐地领受和分享，购买、囤积与消费并不足够。我们应仰望天上的事，让世物帮助我们在爱德中亲近天主与弟兄姊妹，效法基督，学习怜悯、良善、谦逊、温柔、忍耐、宽恕与和平。圣神将天主的爱倾注于心，使我们更深明白望德不会使人失望。（[2025年8月3日讲道，梵蒂冈新闻网当日选文](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/19.html)）

Summary of Pope Leo XIV's homily: Life finds fullness in joyful receiving and sharing; acquisition and consumption cannot suffice. Looking to heavenly things, we should let earthly goods draw us closer to God and one another in charity. Following Christ, we grow in compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, patience, forgiveness and peace. The Spirit pours God's love into our hearts, deepening our understanding that hope will not disappoint. ([Homily, 3 August 2025; selection in Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/19.html))
