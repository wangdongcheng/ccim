## 读经一 | First Reading

**迦拉达人书 5:18-25 | Galatians 5:18-25**

弟兄们：你们若由圣神带领，便不在法律的辖制之下。肉欲所产生的行为十分明显：淫乱、污秽、放纵、崇拜偶像、邪术、敌意、争执、嫉妒、暴怒、自私的争夺、分裂、派系、妒恨、杀人、酗酒、狂宴，以及同类的行为。我以前已提醒你们，现在再提醒一次：惯行这些事的人不能继承天主的国。

圣神却使人结出这样的果实：爱德、喜悦、平安、坚忍、仁慈、良善、忠信、柔和、自制；法律并不反对这些。属于基督耶稣的人，已将肉身连同它的激情和欲望钉在十字架上。「如果我们因圣神而生活，就应该随从圣神的引导而行事。」

Brothers and sisters, when the Spirit leads you, you are no longer subject to the law. The deeds arising from the flesh are plain: sexual wrongdoing, uncleanness, unrestrained indulgence, worship of idols, magic, hostility, quarrelling, jealousy, angry outbursts, selfish rivalry, discord, factions, envy, killing, drunkenness, wild revelry, and similar conduct. I repeat my earlier warning: people who practise these things cannot inherit God's kingdom.

The Spirit, however, produces love, gladness, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, fidelity, gentleness, and restraint. No law forbids these qualities. Those who belong to Jesus Christ have nailed the flesh, together with its passions and cravings, to the cross. If the Spirit gives us life, let the Spirit also guide our steps.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 1:1-4,6 | Psalm 1:1-4,6**

**答：天主，跟随你的，必有生命之光。 | R. God, those who follow you will have the light of life.**

不接受恶人的劝诱，不踏上罪人的道路，不在嘲讽者中占一席的人，是有福的。他以遵行上主的法律为乐，日夜细思上主的教导。

**答：天主，跟随你的，必有生命之光。**

他像栽在流水旁的树，到了季节便结出果实，叶子也不枯萎；他所做的事，都能顺利成就。

**答：天主，跟随你的，必有生命之光。**

恶人却不是这样：他们如同糠屑，被风卷走。上主看顾义人的道路，恶人的道路却通向灭亡。

**答：天主，跟随你的，必有生命之光。**

**R. God, those who follow you will have the light of life.**

Happy is the person who refuses the advice of evil people, avoids the sinners' path, and does not join those who mock. Such a person finds joy in the Lord's law and ponders it throughout the day and night.

**R. God, those who follow you will have the light of life.**

That person resembles a tree rooted beside flowing water, bearing fruit at the proper season, with leaves that never fade. His undertakings flourish.

**R. God, those who follow you will have the light of life.**

Evil people are different: they resemble husks carried off by the wind. The Lord watches over the righteous on their way, while the path of the wicked ends in ruin.

**R. God, those who follow you will have the light of life.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 11:42-46 | Luke 11:42-46**

那时候，主说：你们法利塞人有祸了！你们连薄荷、芸香和各样园蔬也缴纳什一税，却把公义和对天主的爱置于一旁。你们应履行这些更重要的责任，同时也不忽略其他的。

你们法利塞人有祸了！你们爱在会堂中占首位，也爱听人在街市上向你们致敬。你们有祸了！你们好像没有标记的坟墓，人从上面走过，竟不知道。

一位法学士对他说：师傅，你这样说，也是在冒犯我们。耶稣回答：你们法学士也有祸了！你们把难以承受的担子放在人身上，自己却不愿伸出一根手指去碰这些担子。

At that time the Lord said: Woe to you Pharisees! You give a tenth even of mint, rue, and every garden herb, yet leave justice and love for God unattended. You ought to fulfil these duties while also observing the others.

Woe to you Pharisees! You want the places of greatest honour in the synagogues and respectful greetings in the marketplaces. Woe to you! You resemble graves without markers, which people tread upon without knowing they are there.

An expert in the law replied: Teacher, these words offend us as well. Jesus answered: Woe to you experts in the law too! You place loads upon people that are difficult to carry, yet will not move even a finger to touch those loads yourselves.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父指出：在耶稣时代的宗教团体中，法利塞人的信仰与耶稣相当接近，却受到严厉责斥。他认为，这或与福音写作时法利塞人继续承担传递信仰的责任，以及初期信友中也有法利塞人有关。他们虽重视信仰正统，却容易停留在法律的外表，忽略其精神，并追求名誉。耶稣与初期教会揭示这些弊病，正是出于关切。（据万有真原所载、乐仁出版社供稿的当日反省转述。）

Father Li Jianping observes that the Pharisees' faith was close to Jesus' teaching, yet they received severe rebukes. He suggests that this may reflect their continuing responsibility for transmitting the faith when the Gospel was written, and the presence of Pharisees among early Christians. Despite their concern for orthodoxy, they could cling to outward legal observance, miss its spirit, and seek prestige. Jesus and the early Church exposed these failings out of concern for them. (Paraphrased from the day's reflection supplied by Claretian Publications Macau on WanYou ZhenYuan.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各指出：把信仰缩减为一连串规定，会束缚人的良心，使人僵化，失去福音的喜乐和圣神的自由。天主的圣神带来自由；严苛主义却把救恩误作凭行为换取的报酬。成义以及耶稣的死亡与复活所带来的救赎，都是白白赐予的恩典，不能购买。（据梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录的2020年5月15日圣玛尔大弥撒讲话转述。）

Pope Francis warns that reducing faith to a list of prescriptions constrains consciences, fosters rigidity, and deprives people of Gospel joy and the Spirit's freedom. God's Spirit brings liberty. Rigidity instead treats salvation as something earned by fulfilling demands. Justification and the redemption brought by Jesus' death and resurrection are freely bestowed gifts; they cannot be bought. (Paraphrased from the Santa Marta homily of 15 May 2020, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
