## 读经一 | First Reading

**迦拉达人书 4:22-24,26-27,31;5:1 | Galatians 4:22-24,26-27,31;5:1**

弟兄们：经上记述，亚巴郎有两个儿子，一个由婢女生下，另一个由自由的妇人生下。婢女的儿子依肉体而生，自由妇人的儿子却因天主的许诺而生。这些事具有比喻的意义：两位妇人代表两个盟约，其中一个来自西乃山，使所生的子女处于奴役之下；这就是哈加尔。

天上的耶路撒冷却享有自由，她是我们的母亲。经上因此呼唤：未曾生育的女人，欢喜吧！没有经历产痛的女人，放声欢呼吧！因为孤独妇人的孩子，比有丈夫的妇人的孩子还要多。

所以，弟兄们，我们属于自由的妇人，并非婢女的子女。基督解救了我们，是要我们获得自由。你们要坚定站立，别再让奴役的轭束缚自己。

Brothers and sisters, Scripture tells us that Abraham had two sons, one born to an enslaved woman and the other to a free woman. The first was born through ordinary human generation; the second through God's promise. These events have an allegorical meaning: the women represent two covenants. One comes from Mount Sinai and brings forth children into slavery; this is Hagar.

The heavenly Jerusalem, however, is free and is our mother. Scripture calls upon the childless woman to rejoice and the woman who has never known labour to cry out joyfully, because the deserted woman will have more children than the woman with a husband.

We therefore belong to the free woman, brothers and sisters, rather than to the enslaved woman. For freedom Christ has set us free. Remain firm, and do not let yourselves be bound once more under slavery's yoke.

上主的仆人，一起赞颂他吧，

上主高于一切邦国，
他的荣耀超越诸天。

**答：愿上主的名永受赞美。**

谁能比得上上主、我们的天主？
他居于至高之处，
却俯身眷顾天空与大地。
他从尘土中扶起贫弱的人，
从灰堆中提拔困苦的人。

**答：愿上主的名永受赞美。**

Servants of the Lord, join in praising him;
give honour to his name.
May his name receive praise
now and through all eternity.

**R. May the Lord's name be praised for ever.**

Wherever the sun rises and sets,

his glory surpasses the heavens.

**R. May the Lord's name be praised for ever.**

Who can compare with the Lord our God?
From his dwelling on high,

**R. May the Lord's name be praised for ever.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 11:29-32 | Luke 11:29-32**

那时候，聚集的人越来越多，耶稣便说：这个世代邪恶，执意寻求征兆；但它所能得到的，只有约纳的记号。约纳怎样为尼尼微人成了记号，人子也要这样为这个世代成为记号。

到了审判时，南方的女王要与这个世代的人一同起来，指出他们的罪，因为她从遥远的地方赶来，聆听撒罗满的智慧；而在这里的，比撒罗满更大。

到了审判时，尼尼微人也要与这个世代一同起来，指出它的罪，因为他们听了约纳的宣讲便回心转意；而在这里的，比约纳更大。

As the gathering crowds grew larger, Jesus said: This generation is wicked and demands a sign. The only sign it will receive is Jonah's. Just as Jonah served as a sign for Nineveh's people, the Son of man will serve as a sign for this generation.

At the judgment, the queen of the South will stand alongside the people of this generation and show them to be guilty. She travelled from the world's farthest reaches to listen to Solomon's wisdom, and here is one greater than Solomon.

The people of Nineveh will likewise stand at the judgment with this generation and show its guilt. They turned from sin when Jonah preached to them, and here is one greater than Jonah.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父指出，尼尼微人愿意悔改，南方女王甘愿远行求智慧，耶稣身边的人却轻视他的教导，热衷异兆，忽略近人的需要与内心的转变。约纳的记号邀请我们效法那些外邦人，聆听圣言，回头归向上主。（[李建平神父，乐仁出版社供稿；意译摘要](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-12)）

Fr Li Jianping contrasts Nineveh's readiness to repent and the southern queen's costly search for wisdom with the resistance of Jesus' hearers. Fascination with extraordinary signs can distract us from our neighbours' needs and our own need to change. Jonah's sign invites us to learn from these Gentiles, listen to God's word and turn towards the Lord. (Fr Li Jianping, Claretian Publications Macau; translated summary.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

方济各教宗警惕只重外表完美、却不愿走出去传扬上主的宗教态度。约纳的记号指向基督的死亡与复活，彰显拯救我们的慈悲。善工是回应救恩的果实，不能取代天主的爱。我们虽是罪人，仍蒙召以谦卑温良的心跟随主。（[方济各教宗，2013年10月14日圣玛尔大之家弥撒讲话；意译摘要](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/12.html)）

Pope Francis warns against polished religious appearances that lack eagerness to bring others to the Lord. Jonah's sign points to Christ's death and Resurrection, revealing the mercy that saves us. Good works follow as our response to salvation; they cannot replace God's saving love. Sinners though we are, we are called to follow Jesus with humility and gentleness. (Pope Francis, Santa Marta, 14 October 2013; summary.)
