## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 1:1,3-10 | Ephesians 1:1,3-10**

保禄奉天主的旨意成为基督耶稣的宗徒，致书给厄弗所的圣徒，就是忠于基督耶稣的人。

愿我们的主耶稣基督的天主和父受赞颂！他在基督内，以天上的各种属神恩惠祝福了我们。在世界受造之前，他已在基督内选召我们，要我们在他面前成为圣洁、无可指摘的人。他怀着爱，依照自己旨意的美意，预定我们藉耶稣基督成为他的子女，使他在爱子内白白赐予我们的光荣恩宠受到赞扬。

在爱子内，我们因他的血蒙受救赎，罪过得以赦免；这是出于天主丰厚的恩宠，他慷慨地施予了我们。他以各种智慧和明达，让我们认识他旨意的奥秘，就是他在基督内所定的美好计划：当时期圆满时，使天上和地上的万有都在基督内归于一体。

Paul, called by God's will to be an apostle of Jesus Christ, writes to the holy people in Ephesus who are faithful to Christ Jesus.

Praise be to the God and Father of Jesus Christ our Lord! Through Christ he has given us every blessing of the Spirit in the heavenly realm. Before the world was founded, he chose us in Christ to stand before him in holiness, free from blame. In love, following his gracious purpose, he appointed us to become his children through Jesus Christ, so that the glorious grace he freely gave us in his beloved Son might be praised.

Through the Son's blood we are redeemed and our offences forgiven, because God's grace is so abundant. He poured that grace upon us and, with wisdom and understanding, revealed his hidden purpose. This is the gracious design he established in Christ: when the appointed time reaches its fulfilment, everything in heaven and on earth will be brought together in Christ.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 11:47-54 | Luke 11:47-54**

那时候，耶稣说：你们有祸了！你们为先知修造坟墓，杀害他们的却是你们的祖先。你们因而作了证，也认同祖先的行为：他们杀人，你们建墓。

为此，天主的智慧说：我要向他们派遣先知和宗徒，他们会杀害其中一些人，也会迫害他们。因此，自世界受造以来，所有先知所流的血，都要向这一代追讨；从亚伯尔的血，直到在祭坛与圣所之间遇害的匝加利亚的血，都包括在内。是的，我告诉你们，这一代必要为此负责。

你们法学士有祸了！你们夺去了使人认识天主的钥匙，自己没有进入，却又拦阻正在进入的人。

耶稣离开那里以后，经师和法利塞人开始猛烈地逼问他，用各种问题引他开口；他们伺机等候，要抓住他话中的把柄。

At that time Jesus said: Woe to you! You erect monuments for the prophets, although your ancestors murdered them. In doing so, you testify to their actions and approve them: they did the killing, and you put up the tombs.

For this reason God's Wisdom declared: I will send prophets and apostles to them; they will murder some and persecute others. Thus this generation will answer for the blood of every prophet shed since the world began, from Abel to Zechariah, who was killed between the altar and the sanctuary. Yes, I tell you, this generation will be held accountable.

Woe to you experts in the law! You have removed the key that opens the way to knowledge. You failed to enter, and you obstructed those who were going in.

After Jesus left, the scribes and Pharisees pressed him fiercely, drawing him into discussion on many subjects. They watched for an opportunity to seize upon something he said.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父提醒：精通天主法律的人，也可能以自己的见解遮掩法律的真正精神。学识既能帮助人认识天主，也可能伤害弱小者。耶稣严斥法学士，是因为他们认同迫害先知的行为，又使人民远离信仰。他们的虚伪、自负与骄傲堵塞了接纳天主的空间，并拒绝耶稣的见证。圣女大德肋撒的教导提醒我们：唯有放下自我，才有空间让天主完全充满自己。（据万有真原所载、乐仁出版社供稿的当日反省转述。）

Father Li Jianping warns that experts in God's law can obscure its spirit with their own opinions. Learning can lead people towards God, but can also harm the vulnerable. Jesus rebukes those who approve persecution of the prophets and turn people away from faith. Hypocrisy, pride, and self-righteousness leave no room for God and reject Jesus' witness. Saint Teresa's teaching reminds us that surrendering the self makes room to receive God fully. (Paraphrased from the day's reflection supplied by Claretian Publications Macau on WanYou ZhenYuan.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各指出：当人忘记救恩是白白赐予的恩典，忘记天主的亲近和慈悲，便失去了帮助人理解启示与天主心意的钥匙。这样的危险并不限于耶稣的时代，今天的宗教领袖也可能陷入其中。因此，我们应为牧者祈祷，使他们保存认识天主的钥匙，让自己和寻求进入的人，都能通过敞开的门。（据梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录的2017年10月19日圣玛尔大弥撒讲话转述。）

Pope Francis teaches that forgetting salvation's free gift, God's nearness, and his mercy deprives people of the means to grasp revelation and God's heart. Religious leaders today can fall into this danger too. We should therefore pray for pastors, that they preserve the key to understanding God and keep the doorway open both for themselves and for those seeking to enter. (Paraphrased from the Santa Marta homily of 19 October 2017, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
