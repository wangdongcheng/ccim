## 读经一 | First Reading

**斐理伯人书 1:18-26 | Philippians 1:18-26**

弟兄们：究竟怎样呢？无论动机虚假还是真诚，只要基督得到宣扬，我便为此喜乐，而且还要继续喜乐。我知道，藉着你们的代祷和耶稣基督之神的扶助，我所经历的一切必导向我的得救。

我热切期待、满怀希望：无论何事，都不致使我蒙羞；反而愿我始终勇敢，使基督如今和往常一样，藉我的身体受到尊崇，无论我生或死。因为对我而言，活着以基督为生命，死去便有所获得。

可是，若我仍活在肉身中，便能继续结出工作的果实；究竟选择什么，我也说不定。我被两方面牵引：我渴望离世，与基督相聚，那实在更好；但为你们的缘故，我留在肉身中更为必要。

既然这样确信，我知道自己仍将留下，继续陪伴你们众人，使你们的信德向前迈进，满怀喜乐。等我再次来到你们中间，你们便能因我而在基督耶稣内更加欢欣自豪。

Brothers and sisters, what matters is that Christ is made known. Whether the motives are sincere or a pretence, this gives me joy, and my joy will continue. With your intercession and the aid of Jesus Christ's Spirit, I know that my circumstances will lead to my deliverance.

My earnest hope is never to be disgraced, but to remain courageous. May Christ receive honour through my embodied life, now as before, whether I survive or die. For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain.

If I continue in this earthly life, my work can still bear fruit. I cannot tell which course I would choose. Two desires pull at me: leaving this life to be with Christ would be far better, yet your need makes it more necessary for me to stay.

With that conviction, I am confident of remaining alongside you all, helping your faith advance and bringing you joy in it. When I return to you, you will have still greater reason through me to rejoice in Christ Jesus.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 42:2-3,5 | Psalm 42:1-2,4**

**答：我的心渴望天主，永活的天主。 | R. My soul longs for God, the God who lives.**

天主，我的心切望亲近你，
正如鹿儿急切寻觅溪水。

**答：我的心渴望天主，永活的天主。**

我的心渴望天主，永活的天主；
何时才能来到他面前，瞻仰他的慈颜？

**答：我的心渴望天主，永活的天主。**

我倾吐心声，追忆往昔：
曾与群众同行，带领他们走向天主的殿宇；
大家欢呼感恩，
一同庆祝节日。

**答：我的心渴望天主，永活的天主。**

God, my whole being yearns for you,
as a deer searches eagerly for running water.

**R. My soul longs for God, the God who lives.**

I thirst for God, who is alive;
when may I enter his presence and look upon him?

**R. My soul longs for God, the God who lives.**

Pouring out my heart, I remember earlier days:
I walked among the crowds and guided them to God's house,
with joyful cries and grateful songs,
a whole assembly celebrating the feast.

**R. My soul longs for God, the God who lives.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 14:1,7-11 | Luke 14:1,7-11**

一个安息日，耶稣到一位法利塞人首领家里吃饭，众人一直注视着他。

他看见受邀的客人挑选尊贵的座位，便用一个比喻教导他们：“有人邀请你赴婚宴时，别先占上席，免得主人也邀请了比你更尊贵的客人。那请你们两人的主人若走过来，叫你把位置让给他，你就只好羞惭地退到末席。

“受邀请时，先到末席坐下吧。这样，主人来到时，或会邀请你说：‘朋友，到前面来坐。’你便在同席众人面前受到尊重。因为抬高自己的人将被降卑，谦抑自己的人却要被提升。”

One sabbath, Jesus went to eat at the house of a leader among the Pharisees, and everyone was watching him closely.

Seeing the guests select the most distinguished seats, he offered them a parable: “When someone invites you to a wedding celebration, do not claim the highest place. Another guest may have greater standing than you. Your host, who invited you both, might ask you to surrender your seat to that person. You would then have to withdraw in embarrassment to the lowest place.

“Instead, take the lowest seat when you arrive. Your host may then come and invite you forward as a friend, giving you honour before the other guests. Those who raise themselves up will be brought low; those who make themselves low will be raised.”

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父提醒，耶稣关注的是赴宴者的内心。故作谦虚以求抬举，仍是隐蔽的骄傲。真正的谦卑是在天主面前认清自己，无论坐在首席还是末席，都能安然自处，不靠别人的赞赏确定自身价值。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-31)摘要；原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

Father Li Jianping explains that Jesus addresses the guest's inner attitude. Pretending to be humble in order to win recognition remains concealed pride. True humility means knowing one's place before God and being at peace in any seat, without depending on others' admiration for a sense of worth. (Translated summary of the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

本笃十六世教宗指出，比喻教导我们在天主面前谦卑。基督在十字架上降至最低处，以谦卑救赎人类。我们应效法他，无偿照顾不能回报的人，在考验中忍耐，受冒犯时保持温良，在痛苦中服从天主。真正的福分是亲近基督。（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/31.html)的2010年8月29日三钟经讲话摘要。）

Pope Benedict XVI teaches that the parable calls us to humility before God. Christ descended to the cross and redeemed humanity through his humility. Following him means giving to those unable to repay us, meeting trials with patience and offences with gentleness, and obeying God amid suffering. Our deepest good is closeness to Christ. (Summary of the Angelus address of 29 August 2010, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
