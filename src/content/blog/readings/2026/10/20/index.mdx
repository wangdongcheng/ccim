## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 2:12-22 | Ephesians 2:12-22**

弟兄们：你们要记得，从前你们与默西亚无缘，被隔在以色列的团体之外，对恩许的盟约只是局外人；在世上既没有希望，也不认识天主。如今，你们这些曾经远离的人，因基督耶稣的血而得以亲近。

基督是我们的和平。他使原本分开的两方合而为一，以自己的身体拆除了隔在中间、造成仇恨的墙，也废除了使人分隔的律法条规，为在自己内创造一个新人。他藉十字架消灭仇恨，使双方成为一个身体，与天主和好。他把和平的喜讯传给远处的人，也传给近处的人；我们双方都因他、在同一圣神内，得以走向父。

因此，你们不再是外人或过客，而与圣徒同享公民身份，成为天主家中的人。你们建立在宗徒和先知的基础上，基督耶稣自己是角石。整个建筑在他内紧密结合，逐渐成长为属于上主的圣殿；你们也在他内共同被建造，成为天主藉圣神居住的地方。

Brothers and sisters, remember that you were once apart from Christ, outside Israel's community and strangers to the promised covenants. In this world you had no hope and were without God. Now, in Christ Jesus, his blood has brought near those who were once far away.

Christ is our peace. He united the two groups, breaking down in his flesh the wall of hostility between them. Setting aside the rules that divided them, he created one new humanity in himself and made peace. Through the cross he put hostility to death, reconciling both groups to God in one body. He proclaimed peace to those far away and to those near. Through him, both have access to the Father in one Spirit.

You are therefore strangers and visitors no longer. You share citizenship with the saints and belong to God's household. The apostles and prophets form its foundation, with Christ Jesus himself as the cornerstone. In him the whole structure is joined together and grows into a holy temple in the Lord. In him you too are being built together into a home for God through the Spirit.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 85:9-14 | Psalm 85:8-13**

**答：天主向自己的圣者宣布和平的纶音。 | R. The Lord speaks peace to his people.**

我要聆听上主天主所说的话：他向自己的子民、向归向他的圣者宣告和平。他的救恩临近敬畏他的人，他的光荣要居住在我们的土地上。

**答：天主向自己的圣者宣布和平的纶音。**

慈爱与忠信彼此相逢，正义与和平互相拥抱；忠信从大地萌生，正义由高天俯视。

**答：天主向自己的圣者宣布和平的纶音。**

上主必赐下美善，我们的土地也将结出果实；正义走在他前面，和平随着他的脚步。

**答：天主向自己的圣者宣布和平的纶音。**

**R. The Lord speaks peace to his people.**

I will listen to what the Lord God says: he promises peace to his people, to his faithful who turn to him. His salvation is close to those who fear him, and his glory will dwell in our land.

**R. The Lord speaks peace to his people.**

Steadfast love and faithfulness meet; righteousness and peace embrace. Faithfulness rises from the earth, while righteousness looks down from heaven.

**R. The Lord speaks peace to his people.**

The Lord will give what is good, and our land will bear its fruit. Righteousness goes before him and makes a path for his steps.

**R. The Lord speaks peace to his people.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 12:35-38 | Luke 12:35-38**

耶稣对门徒说：你们要束好腰，点亮灯，像等候主人从婚宴归来的仆人，听见他敲门便立刻开门。主人回来时，看见仆人仍然醒着，那些仆人是有福的。我实在告诉你们，主人会束起腰来，请他们坐席，亲自走近服侍他们。无论他在夜间哪一更回来，只要发现他们如此警醒，他们就是有福的。

Jesus told his disciples to be dressed and ready, with their lamps alight, like servants awaiting their master's return from a wedding feast. When he knocks, they can open the door at once. Blessed are the servants whom he finds awake when he arrives. Truly, he will make them sit at table, dress himself for service and come to wait on them. Whether he comes in the second watch or the third, blessed are those he finds ready.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父反省摘要：警醒不是不睡觉，而是无论主人何时回来，都忠于托付，做好分内之事。黑夜象征我们料想不到的时刻；在天主眼中，黑夜与白昼并无分别。好仆人不会在主人面前谨慎、背后放纵。天主信任并托付我们，更应许亲自服侍、安慰等候他的人。（[万有真原，乐仁出版社供稿](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-20)）

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection: Watchfulness is a way of living, not a refusal to sleep. Faithful servants carry out their duties whether or not the master is present and whatever the hour. Darkness represents an unexpected moment, but God is not bound by day or night. He entrusts his servants with responsibility and promises to serve and comfort those who await him. ([WanYou ZhenYuan; contributed by Claretian Publications Macau](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-20))

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各讲话摘要：束好腰、点亮灯，就是怀着永生的许诺，充分活在当下。真正的准备是把自己交托给天主和弟兄姊妹，而非追求掌声与成功。祈祷滋养我们与天父及他人的关系，使天主亲近我们，驱散孤独，注入希望；正如生命离不开呼吸，基督徒的生活也离不开祈祷。（[2021年11月20日讲话，梵蒂冈新闻网当日选文](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/20.html)）

Summary of Pope Francis's address: To keep our lamps burning is to live the present fully in light of eternal life's promise. Readiness means giving ourselves to God and to others, rather than measuring life by applause or success. Prayer nourishes these relationships, brings God close, releases us from isolation and gives us hope. As breathing sustains life, prayer sustains Christian life. ([Address, 20 November 2021; selection in Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/20.html))
