常年期第二十九周（星期五） | Friday of the Twenty-ninth Week in Ordinary Time

2026年10月23日 · 星期五 | October 23, 2026 · Friday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 4:1-6 | Ephesians 4:1-6**

弟兄们：我为主身受囚禁，恳请你们度与所蒙召叫相称的生活。你们要谦逊、温良，凡事忍耐，在爱德中彼此担待，以和平相连，努力维护圣神所赐的合一。

身体只有一个，圣神只有一个，正如你们蒙召时，共同怀有一个希望。主只有一个，信德只有一个，洗礼只有一个；天主只有一个，祂是众人的父，超越众人，藉众人而行动，也住在众人之内。

Brothers and sisters, as one imprisoned for the Lord, I appeal to you to shape your lives according to the calling you have received. Be humble and gentle, show patience, and bear with one another in love. Make every effort to safeguard the unity given by the Spirit, bound together in peace.

There is a single body and a single Spirit, just as your calling brings you a shared hope. There is one Lord, one faith, and one baptism; one God, the Father of everyone, who is above everyone, works through everyone, and dwells in everyone.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 23:1-6 | Psalm 24:1-6**

**答：上主，这样的人寻求祢的仪容。 | R. Lord, these are the people who seek your face.**

大地连同其中的一切，都属于上主；世界和居于其中的人，也属于祂。祂使大地稳立在海上，将世界安置在水流之上。

**答：上主，这样的人寻求祢的仪容。**

谁能登上上主的山？谁能站在祂的圣所？是那行为清白、心地纯洁，不把心交给虚妄，也不以谎言起誓的人。

**答：上主，这样的人寻求祢的仪容。**

这样的人要领受上主的祝福，由拯救他的天主获得正义。这就是追寻祂的人，就是雅各伯的天主啊，渴望瞻仰祢圣容的一代。

**答：上主，这样的人寻求祢的仪容。**

**R. Lord, these are the people who seek your face.**

The earth belongs to the Lord, together with everything it contains; the world and all who inhabit it are his. He established it upon the seas and secured it above the flowing waters.

**R. Lord, these are the people who seek your face.**

Who may climb the Lord's mountain, and who may stand in his holy dwelling? The person whose deeds are innocent and whose heart is pure, who does not give himself to falsehood or swear deceitfully.

**R. Lord, these are the people who seek your face.**

Such a person receives the Lord's blessing and righteousness from the God who saves him. These are the people who seek him, who long for your face, O God of Jacob.

**R. Lord, these are the people who seek your face.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 12:54-59 | Luke 12:54-59**

那时候，耶稣向群众说：「你们看到西边升起云彩，马上说：『快要下雨了。』雨便真的来了。南风一吹，你们又说：『天气要热了。』事情也果真如此。假善人啊！天地间的景象，你们懂得分辨；对于眼前这个时刻，你们怎么却不懂得辨认呢？

「你们为什么不自己判断什么是正当的？你与控告你的人一同去见官长时，要趁着还在路上，尽力与他解决争端，免得他把你拖到审判官面前，审判官将你交给差役，差役再把你关进监牢。我告诉你：只要还有最后一点欠款没有付清，你就出不来。」

Jesus said to the crowds: “As soon as you see a cloud coming up in the west, you say that rain is on its way, and rain comes. When the south wind blows, you say that the weather will be hot, and so it is. Hypocrites! You can read the appearance of earth and sky; how is it that you fail to recognize this present moment?

“Why do you not decide for yourselves what is right? When you are going with your accuser to the magistrate, work to settle your dispute while you are still on the road. Otherwise, he may drag you before the judge, who will hand you over to the officer, and the officer will put you in prison. I tell you, you will remain there until even the smallest outstanding coin has been paid.”

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父反省摘要：人能从云和风辨认天气，却可能对耶稣所显示的天国讯息迟钝，因为福音要求人悔改、与天主和好，并重新认识祂。我们熟悉经济、赛事和选举的动向，也当留心自己灵魂的状况。（[万有真原，乐仁出版社供稿](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-23)）

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection: We read the weather in clouds and wind, yet may overlook the Kingdom revealed in Jesus because his Gospel challenges us to repent, reconcile with God, and know him anew. Alongside our attention to markets, sports, and elections, we should attend to the movements of our souls. ([WanYou ZhenYuan; contributed by Claretian Publications Macau](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-23))

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各讲道摘要：基督徒应自由、无惧地辨认时代变化，不能只依赖传闻。静默观察、内心反思和祈祷，帮助我们认出上主要传达的讯息。我们要坚守基督和福音的真理，同时按时代的征兆不断调整自己的回应。（[2015年10月23日圣玛尔大之家讲道，梵蒂冈新闻网当日选文](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/23.html)）

Summary of Pope Francis's homily: Christians should discern changing times freely and courageously, instead of resting on hearsay. Quiet observation, reflection, and prayer help us recognize the Lord's message. While remaining firm in Christ and the Gospel's truth, we must continually adapt our response to the signs of the times. ([Homily at Santa Marta, 23 October 2015; selection in Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/23.html))
