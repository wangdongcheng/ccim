2026年10月24日 · 星期六 | October 24, 2026 · Saturday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 4:7-16 | Ephesians 4:7-16**

弟兄们：基督按照自己的恩赐，把恩宠分给我们每一个人。因此，经上说，他升到高处，带领俘虏，并将恩惠赐予人。既说他上升，就表示他也曾下降到地下深处。那位下降的，也就是升到诸天之上、要充满万有的基督。

他赐予不同的职务：宗徒、先知、传福音者、司牧和教师，使圣徒得到装备，从事服务，建立基督的身体，直到我们在信德上、在认识天主子上达到合一，成为成熟的人，长成基督圆满的身量。

如此，我们便不再像幼童，被各种教说吹动，被人的欺骗和诡计带入歧途。我们要在爱德中持守真理，在各方面向元首基督成长。整个身体由他联结，藉各关节彼此支援；每个肢体尽好自己的职分，身体便在爱德中增长、建立自己。

Brothers and sisters, Christ apportions grace to each person. Scripture describes him ascending with captives and distributing gifts. His ascent implies that he first descended into the earth's depths; this same Christ rose beyond every heaven to fill everything.

He appointed apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers to prepare the saints for service and strengthen his body. The goal is shared faith and knowledge of God's Son, maturity measured by Christ's fullness.

We should therefore cease to be children swept about by changing teachings and deceived by human schemes. Instead, let us speak the truth in love, growing in every way toward Christ, our head. He connects the whole body through its supporting joints. As each member fulfils its role, the body grows and develops in love.

耶路撒冷建成坚固相连的城邑；各支派，上主的各支派，都向这城登临。

**答：我们要高兴地进入上主的圣殿！**

他们遵照以色列所受的命令，前来称谢上主的圣名。审判的宝座设在那里，就是达味王室的宝座。

**答：我们要高兴地进入上主的圣殿！**

An invitation to the Lord's dwelling filled me with happiness. Jerusalem, we now stand inside your gates.

**R. Let us go with joy to the house of the Lord!**

Jerusalem is a city built into a firm whole. The tribes belonging to the Lord make their ascent there.

**R. Let us go with joy to the house of the Lord!**

They come to offer thanks to the Lord, obeying Israel's ordinance. There the royal house of David has established its seats of judgment.

**R. Let us go with joy to the house of the Lord!**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 13:1-9 | Luke 13:1-9**

那时候，有人向耶稣报告，有些加里肋亚人被比拉多杀害，他们的血与所献的祭品混在一起。耶稣问他们：这些人遭遇这样的灾祸，难道就证明他们比其他加里肋亚人更有罪吗？绝不是。然而，你们若不悔改，也都要丧亡。还有史罗亚的塔倒塌，压死了十八个人；难道他们比耶路撒冷的其他居民更有罪吗？绝不是。但你们若不悔改，也都要丧亡。

耶稣接着讲了一个比喻：有人在葡萄园里种了一棵无花果树，来寻找果实时，却一无所获。他对园丁说：我连续三年来查看这棵树，都没有找到果子。把它砍了吧，何必继续耗用土地？园丁却请求说：主人，请再给它一年，让我翻松四周的泥土，施上肥料。明年若能结果，自然很好；若仍不结果，你再砍掉它。

People reported to Jesus that Pilate had killed some Galileans, mixing their blood with their sacrifices. Jesus asked whether this meant they were more sinful than other Galileans. Certainly not; nevertheless, without repentance his listeners too would perish. Likewise, the eighteen killed when Siloam's tower collapsed were no more guilty than Jerusalem's other inhabitants. Again he warned that failure to repent would lead to their destruction.

He then described a man seeking fruit from a fig tree in his vineyard and finding none. After three fruitless years, the owner told his gardener to remove it rather than let it exhaust the soil. The gardener requested another year to loosen the earth and add manure. If the tree produced fruit the following year, it could remain; otherwise, the owner could have it cut down.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父提醒我们，别人的灾难应唤醒我们的良知，而非成为冷眼旁观或评断他人的理由。生命脆弱，世事难料；面对他人的苦难，我们也应反省自己。无花果树仍有受培育的机会，催促我们珍惜当下，悔改并结出善果。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-24)摘要，原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

Father Li Jianping urges us to let others' tragedies awaken our conscience rather than provoke indifference or judgment. Life is fragile and uncertain; another person's suffering also calls us to examine ourselves. The fig tree's remaining opportunity invites us to value the present, repent and bear good fruit. (Summary of the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各指出，无花果树象征冷漠、只顾自己而不行善的人；园主象征天父，园丁则象征不断为人类转求的耶稣。园丁悉心照料树木，显示天主愿意给人悔改的时间。即使我们的生命暂时没有结果，他仍以耐心和慈悲扶持我们，使我们能够改变，走向爱与正义。（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/24.html)的2019年3月24日三钟经讲话摘要。）

Pope Francis sees the barren tree as humanity absorbed in its own comfort and indifferent to others. The owner represents the Father, while the gardener represents Jesus interceding for us. His attentive cultivation reveals God's willingness to allow time for repentance. Even when our lives seem fruitless, divine patience and mercy sustain our capacity to change and grow in love and justice. (Summary of the Angelus of 24 March 2019, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
