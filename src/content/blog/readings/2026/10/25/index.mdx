常年期第三十主日 | Thirtieth Sunday in Ordinary Time

2026年10月25日 · 星期日 | October 25, 2026 · Sunday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**出谷纪 22:20-26 | Exodus 22:21-27**

上主这样说：不可欺负、压迫寄居的人，因为你们也曾在埃及作过外侨。不可苦待寡妇和孤儿。你们若伤害他们，他们向我求救，我必听见；我的义怒将使你们遭刀剑杀害，你们的妻子也会成为寡妇，儿女成为孤儿。

你若借钱给我子民中与你同住的穷人，不可像放债者那样向他取利。若收了邻人的外衣作抵押，务必在太阳落山前归还，因为那是他遮盖身体、夜间御寒的唯一衣物。没有它，他拿什么盖着睡觉呢？他向我呼求，我必垂听，因为我心怀慈悲。

The Lord says: Do not mistreat or exploit people from other lands; remember your own life as strangers in Egypt. Do not harm widows or orphans. If you do, I will hear their appeal. My anger will bring death by the sword upon you, leaving your wives widowed and your children without fathers.

When lending to a poor member of my people living beside you, do not demand interest. If a neighbor's cloak secures the loan, give it back before nightfall. It is the only covering available for sleep. Without it, how will that person stay covered? When the poor call to me, my compassion will move me to hear them.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 18:2-4,47,51 | Psalm 18:1-3,46,50**

**答：上主，我的力量！我爱慕你。 | R. Lord, my strength, I love you!**

上主，你赐我力量，我深爱你。你是护卫我的岩石、坚固的堡垒，也是拯救我的天主，我在你内得到庇护。

**答：上主，我的力量！我爱慕你。**

我的天主是我投靠的磐石，是我的盾牌、救援的力量和安全的高台。我呼求配受赞颂的上主，他便救我脱离敌人的手。

**答：上主，我的力量！我爱慕你。**

上主永远生活！愿我的磐石受赞美，愿拯救我的天主受尊崇！他使自己的君王获得伟大胜利，向受傅者达味及其子孙施予永不止息的慈爱。

**答：上主，我的力量！我爱慕你。**

Lord, you give me strength, and I cherish you. You are the rock that shelters me, my fortress and rescuer; in you, my God, I find safety.

**R. Lord, my strength, I love you!**

My God is the rock I trust, my shield, my saving strength and secure refuge. When I appeal to the Lord, deserving of every praise, he frees me from my foes.

**R. Lord, my strength, I love you!**

The Lord is living! Praise to my rock; honour to the God who saves me! He grants his king great victories and shows enduring kindness to David, his anointed, and to David's descendants forever.

**R. Lord, my strength, I love you!**

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**得撒洛尼人前书 1:5c-10 | 1 Thessalonians 1:5c-10**

弟兄们：你们知道，我们为了你们的益处，在你们中间怎样生活。你们效法我们，也效法主；在重重患难中，仍以圣神所赐的喜乐领受圣言，成为马其顿和阿哈雅所有信友的榜样。

上主的圣道从你们那里传扬出去，超越了马其顿和阿哈雅；你们对天主的信德已经广为人知，我们不必再加说明。人们自己述说你们如何接待我们，如何离弃偶像，归向永生的真天主并事奉他。你们期待他的圣子从天而来，就是天主使之由死者中复活、救我们免于将来震怒的耶稣。

Brothers and sisters, you remember how we lived among you for your good. Following our example and the Lord's, you welcomed his message amid severe hardship, sustained by the Spirit's joy. You thus became a pattern for believers throughout Macedonia and Achaia.

From you the Lord's message has spread beyond those regions; news of your trust in God has reached everywhere, leaving us nothing to add. Others recount your welcome and your abandonment of idols to serve the true, living God. You await his Son's coming from heaven: Jesus, raised by God from death, who rescues us from the approaching wrath.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 22:34-40 | Matthew 22:34-40**

那时候，法利塞人得知耶稣使撒杜塞人无言以对，就聚在一起。其中一位法律专家想试探他，便问：老师，法律中哪一条诫命最重要？

耶稣回答：要以你整个的心、整个的灵魂和全部心思，爱上主你的天主。这是首要而最大的诫命。第二条与它相似：你应当爱人如己。全部法律以及先知的教导，都以这两条诫命为根基。

Hearing that Jesus had left the Sadducees without an answer, the Pharisees assembled. One of their legal scholars tried to test him by asking which commandment held the highest place in the Law.

Jesus answered that loving God must engage the whole heart, soul and mind. This commandment comes first and is greatest. A second stands alongside it: You shall love your neighbor as yourself. The entire Law and the teaching of the prophets rest upon these two commands.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父从天主先爱我们出发，说明爱主与爱人的联系。天主赐下圣神，使我们能够领受并分享他的爱。因此，面对尚难亲近的人，我们可以先付出善意，把自己所领受的爱带给他们，唤起彼此生命中的美善。爱人如己，是回应天主先赐予我们的爱。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-25)摘要，原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

Father Li Jianping connects love of God and neighbor with God's initiative in loving us. Through the Spirit, we receive love and become able to share it. We can therefore approach difficult relationships with kindness first, passing on what we have received and awakening goodness in one another. Loving our neighbor responds to God's prior gift. (Summary of the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗本笃十六世指出，爱首先是天主赐予的恩惠，能在人心中生根成长。扎根于天主的爱，我们就能超越他人的表现，以基督的目光看见他们深处渴望被聆听、被关怀的需要。同时，敞开心接纳并服务别人，也使我们更认识天主的临在与美善。爱主与爱人彼此相连，不能分开。（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/25.html)的2012年11月4日三钟经讲话摘要。）

Pope Benedict XVI presents love first as God's gift, able to take root and grow within us. Rooted in that gift, we can see beyond another person's conduct and recognize, with Christ's compassion, their longing to be heard and cared for. Welcoming and serving others also opens us to God's presence and goodness. Love for God and neighbor belong together. (Summary of the Angelus of 4 November 2012, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
