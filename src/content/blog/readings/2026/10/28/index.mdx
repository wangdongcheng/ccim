圣西满及圣达陡宗徒（庆日） | Feast of Saints Simon and Jude, Apostles

2026年10月28日 · 星期三 | October 28, 2026 · Wednesday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 2:19-22 | Ephesians 2:19-22**

弟兄们：你们已不再是异乡的陌生人或寄居者，而与圣徒同属一国，成为天主家中的成员。宗徒和先知是你们的根基，基督耶稣亲自作这建筑的角石。

在他内，整座建筑紧密相连，不断成长，成为主内的圣殿。你们也在他内共同被建造，藉着圣神，成为天主居住的所在。

Brothers and sisters, you no longer live as outsiders or resident foreigners. You share citizenship with the saints and belong to God's family. Your foundation is the apostles and prophets, and Christ Jesus himself serves as the cornerstone.

In him the entire building fits together and grows into a holy sanctuary in the Lord. United with him, you too are being built together into a home where God dwells through the Spirit.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 19:2-5 | Psalm 19:1-4b**

**答：它们的声音传遍大地。 | R. Their voice reaches the whole earth.**

天空彰显天主的荣耀，苍穹展现他双手的作为；白昼向白昼传递讯息，黑夜向黑夜传授认识。

**答：它们的声音传遍大地。**

它们没有言辞，也没有话语，人耳听不见它们的声音；然而，它们的宣告遍及全地，它们的讯息抵达世界尽头。

**答：它们的声音传遍大地。**

The heavens make God's glory known; the sky displays the work of his hands. Each day passes its message to the next, and each night shares its knowledge with another.

**R. Their voice reaches the whole earth.**

They use neither speech nor words; no audible voice is heard. Yet their proclamation reaches every land, and their message extends to the world's farthest bounds.

**R. Their voice reaches the whole earth.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 6:12-16 | Luke 6:12-16**

在那些日子里，耶稣到山上祈祷，整夜与天主交谈。到了早晨，他召集门徒，从他们中拣选十二位，并给他们宗徒的名称。

他们是：耶稣称为伯多禄的西满，及他的兄弟安德肋；雅各伯、若望、斐理伯、巴尔多禄茂、玛窦、多默、阿耳斐的儿子雅各伯、被称为热诚者的西满、雅各伯的犹达，以及后来背叛耶稣的犹达斯依斯加略。

During those days Jesus went up a mountain to pray, remaining in prayer to God throughout the night. At daybreak he gathered his disciples, selected twelve of them, and gave them the name apostles.

They were Simon, to whom he gave the name Peter, and Andrew his brother; James, John, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, Simon known as the Zealot, Judas the son of James, and Judas Iscariot, who later betrayed him.

## 反省 | Reflection

“在拥抱他人之前，圣子也需要圣父的拥抱。”李建平神父以耶稣彻夜祈祷的画面，指出他在人群中施教、医治、安慰之后，也将一天的劳苦带到父面前，领受继续服务的力量。他在祈祷中迎接同行的门徒，又在黎明与他们一同出发。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-28)短引及摘要，原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

“Before embracing others, the Son too needs the Father's embrace.” Father Li Jianping reflects on Jesus praying through the night. After teaching, healing and consoling people, Jesus brings the day's weariness before his Father and receives strength to serve again. Prayer also prepares him to welcome companions and set out with them at dawn. (Translated excerpt and summary of the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各指出，耶稣在整夜祈祷后拣选宗徒，显示这一召叫出自他与父的交谈。宗徒日后的软弱，甚至犹达斯的背叛，也没有使天主的计划落空。我们祈祷软弱时，仍能信赖耶稣的代祷；即使在考验和犯罪之际，他此刻仍满怀爱意为我们祈求：“耶稣正在为我祈祷。”（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/28.html)的2021年6月2日公开接见讲话摘要及短引。）

Pope Francis explains that Jesus chose the apostles after praying all night: their calling arose from his conversation with the Father. Their later failures, including Judas's betrayal, did not undo God's purpose. When our own prayer falters, Christ's intercession sustains us. Even amid temptation and sin, his loving prayer accompanies us now: “Jesus is praying for me.” (Summary and short excerpt from the General Audience of 2 June 2021, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
