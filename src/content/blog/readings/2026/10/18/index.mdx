常年期第二十九主日（普世传教节） | Twenty-ninth Sunday in Ordinary Time (World Mission Sunday)

2026年10月18日 · 星期日 | October 18, 2026 · Sunday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**依撒意亚先知书 45:1,4-6 | Isaiah 45:1,4-6**

上主向他所傅立的居鲁士说：我扶持你的右手，使列国在你面前降服，使君王失去武力；我为你开启门户，不让城门关闭。

为了我的仆人雅各伯、我所拣选的以色列，我按名字召叫你，赐你尊号，尽管你还不认识我。我是上主，此外没有别的天主。你虽不认识我，我仍赐你力量，使日出日落各处的人都明白，除我以外再没有别的天主。我是上主，此外没有另一个。

The Lord addresses Cyrus, whom he has anointed: I take your right hand, bringing nations under your power and stripping kings of their strength. I open entrances ahead of you, keeping their gates from being shut.

For Jacob, my servant, and Israel, the people I chose, I summon you personally and bestow an honoured name on you, although you have not known me. I alone am the Lord; no other deity exists beside me. Though you do not know me, I equip you with strength, so that people throughout the world, from sunrise to sunset, may recognize that there is none beside me. I alone am the Lord.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 96:1,3-5,7-10 | Psalm 96:1,3-5,7-10**

**答：请赞颂上主的光荣威能。 | R. Praise the Lord's glorious might.**

普世大地，请以新歌颂扬上主；向列国传扬他的荣耀，向万民讲述他的奇妙作为。

**答：请赞颂上主的光荣威能。**

上主伟大，堪受极大的赞美；他超越众神，令人敬畏。万民所奉的神不过是偶像，唯有上主创造了诸天。

**答：请赞颂上主的光荣威能。**

各民族的家族，请将荣耀与能力归于上主；尊崇他的圣名，带着祭品来到他的庭院。

**答：请赞颂上主的光荣威能。**

请以圣洁的仪容朝拜上主；普世大地，在他面前肃然敬畏。向列邦宣告上主掌权，他必公正审判万民。

**答：请赞颂上主的光荣威能。**

**R. Praise the Lord's glorious might.**

Let the whole earth raise a fresh song to the Lord. Tell every nation of his glory and every people of his wonders.

**R. Praise the Lord's glorious might.**

The Lord's greatness deserves our highest praise; he inspires reverence beyond every god. The nations' gods are mere idols, whereas the heavens are the Lord's creation.

**R. Praise the Lord's glorious might.**

Families of every nation, acknowledge the Lord's splendour and power. Honour his holy name, bringing your gifts into his courts.

**R. Praise the Lord's glorious might.**

Bow before the Lord in holiness; let the earth tremble with reverence. Announce his kingship among the nations: his judgment of the peoples is just.

**R. Praise the Lord's glorious might.**

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**得撒洛尼人前书 1:1-5a | 1 Thessalonians 1:1-5a**

保禄、息耳瓦诺和弟茂德，致书给在天主父及主耶稣基督内的得撒洛尼教会：愿恩宠与平安赐给你们！

我们常为你们全体感谢天主，在祈祷中不断记念你们；在天主父面前，记起你们由信德而生的行动、为爱德付出的辛劳，以及因盼望主耶稣基督而有的坚定忍耐。天主所爱的弟兄们，我们知道他拣选了你们，因为福音传到你们那里，除了言语，还有德能、圣神的工作和满怀的确信。

Paul, Silvanus and Timothy write to the Thessalonian community, united with God our Father and with Jesus Christ our Lord: may grace and peace be yours.

We continually thank God for all of you and bring you into our prayers. Before our God and Father, we recall the deeds arising from your faith, the costly service inspired by love, and the perseverance sustained by hope in Jesus Christ our Lord. Brothers and sisters loved by God, we recognize his choice of you: the good news we brought reached you through more than speech. It came with divine power, the Holy Spirit's presence and deep assurance.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 22:15-21 | Matthew 22:15-21**

那时，法利塞人商议，要从耶稣的话里找出把柄。他们派自己的门徒与黑落德党人一起去见他，说：老师，我们知道你诚实，忠实教导天主的道路，不受别人左右，也不因人的地位而有所偏袒。那么，请告诉我们：向凯撒纳税，合不合法？

耶稣察觉他们的恶意，便责问这些伪君子为何试探他，并要他们拿出缴税用的钱币。他们递上一枚银币，耶稣问：上面的肖像和题字属于谁？他们回答：凯撒。耶稣于是告诉他们：属于凯撒的，要交还凯撒；属于天主的，要交还天主。

The Pharisees planned to catch Jesus through something he said. Their followers approached him together with supporters of Herod. They praised him as an honest teacher of God's way, independent of public opinion and unmoved by anyone's social standing. Then they asked whether paying the imperial tax was lawful.

Jesus recognized their malicious purpose. He challenged their hypocrisy and asked why they were testing him. He requested a coin used for the tax, and they handed him a denarius. He asked whose portrait and lettering appeared on it. They identified Caesar. Jesus then told them to return what belonged to Caesar to him, and to give God everything that belonged to God.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父反省摘要：凯撒的统治依靠政治权力与纳税服从；天主的主权却以爱进入人心。耶稣承认世上的责任，也指出人对天主更深的归属。天主不强迫人臣服，而邀请人以真诚、感恩、肖似耶稣的爱回应他的爱。（[万有真原，乐仁出版社供稿](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-18)）

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection: Caesar governs through political authority and the obligation to pay taxes; God's sovereignty reaches human hearts through love. Jesus acknowledges earthly duties while pointing to our deeper belonging to God. Rather than forcing submission, God invites an answer to his love through sincere gratitude and love modelled on Jesus. ([WanYou ZhenYuan; contributed by Claretian Publications Macau](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-18))

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

本笃十六世讲道摘要：钱币印着凯撒的肖像，每个人却带着天主的肖像，生命因此归于天主。耶稣的教导超越政教权限的区分，也揭示人的尊严。教会的使命，是宣讲天主，提醒人承认他的主权，并将自己的生命献给他。（[2011年10月16日讲道，梵蒂冈新闻网当日选文](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/18.html)）

Summary of Pope Benedict XVI's homily: The coin bears Caesar's likeness, but every person bears God's image and owes life to him. Jesus' teaching reaches beyond the distinction between political and religious authority to illuminate human dignity. The Church's mission is to proclaim God, recall his sovereignty, and invite people to offer him their lives. ([Homily, 16 October 2011; selection in Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/18.html))
