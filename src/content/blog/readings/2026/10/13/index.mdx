## 读经一 | First Reading

**迦拉达人书 5:1-6 | Galatians 5:1-6**

弟兄们：基督使我们摆脱奴役，是要我们活在自由中。因此，你们应坚定站立，别再让奴役的轭束缚自己。我保禄告诉你们：如果你们接受割损，基督便不能使你们获益。我再次郑重告诫每一个接受割损的人：他必须遵守整部法律。你们若想凭法律获得义德，就是与基督分离，从恩宠中跌落了。

我们却依靠圣神，凭信德等候所盼望的义德。在基督耶稣内，受割损或未受割损都不能决定什么；「唯有以爱德行事的信德，才算什么。」

Brothers and sisters, Christ has liberated us so that we may live in freedom. Remain steadfast, and do not let yourselves be placed under slavery's yoke again. I, Paul, tell you that if you accept circumcision, Christ will bring you no benefit. I solemnly repeat to everyone who accepts it that he must observe the entire law. Those who seek justification through the law have broken with Christ and fallen from grace.

We, however, rely on the Spirit and await by faith the righteousness for which we hope. In Christ Jesus, being circumcised or uncircumcised achieves nothing. What counts is faith put into action through love.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 119:41,43-45,47-48 | Psalm 119:41,43-45,47-48**

**答：上主，愿你以慈爱对待我。 | R. Lord, let your faithful love reach me.**

上主，求你向我显示慈爱，按你的许诺赐我救恩。不要让真理的话从我口中完全消失，因为我期待你的裁断。

**答：上主，愿你以慈爱对待我。**

我要不断遵行你的法律，直到永远。我将行走在自由宽广的道路上，因为我寻求你的训令。

**答：上主，愿你以慈爱对待我。**

你的诫命是我的喜悦，也是我所爱的。我向所爱的诫命举起双手，并细思你的法令。

**答：上主，愿你以慈爱对待我。**

**R. Lord, let your faithful love reach me.**

Lord, bring me your faithful love and the deliverance you have promised. Do not entirely remove truthful speech from my lips, for I place my hope in your judgments.

**R. Lord, let your faithful love reach me.**

I will observe your law without ceasing, for all eternity. I will walk in freedom, because I seek your instructions.

**R. Lord, let your faithful love reach me.**

I take joy in your commandments, which I love. I lift my hands toward those beloved commandments and ponder your statutes.

**R. Lord, let your faithful love reach me.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 11:37-41 | Luke 11:37-41**

耶稣讲话的时候，一个法利塞人邀请他用饭；他便进去，坐下赴席。那人见耶稣饭前没有先行洗手礼，感到惊讶。

主对他说：你们法利塞人把杯子和盘子的外面洗得干净，内里却满是掠夺与恶念。糊涂的人！创造外面的那一位，难道没有创造里面吗？应把里面的东西用来施舍；这样，你们的一切便洁净了。

While Jesus was speaking, a Pharisee invited him to a meal. Jesus went inside and took his place at the table. His host was surprised that he had not washed before eating.

The Lord told him: You Pharisees wash the exterior of cups and dishes, yet inwardly you are filled with extortion and evil. How foolish! The one who made the exterior also made the interior. Give what is within as alms, and everything will become clean for you.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父的反省指出：法利塞人以饭前洗手的礼规判断一个人是否忠于天主，但外在仪式不能代替内心的洁净。真正摆脱贪婪与邪恶，需要悔改和内在更新。（据万有真原所载、乐仁出版社供稿的当日反省转述。）

Father Li Jianping's reflection observes that the Pharisees treated ritual washing before meals as a test of faithfulness to God. Outward observance, however, cannot replace a clean heart. Freedom from greed and evil requires repentance and renewal within. (Paraphrased from the day's reflection supplied by Claretian Publications Macau on WanYou ZhenYuan.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各提醒我们：自由是应当珍惜的恩赐，不能只当作理所当然的权利。保禄劝迦拉达人守住领洗时得到的自由，别从基督的解放重新退回罪恶和法律主义的奴役。福音不能以耶稣之名强迫人或使人成为奴隶；基督使人自由。（据梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录的2021年10月6日公开接见讲话转述。）

Pope Francis teaches that freedom is a gift to cherish, rather than simply an entitlement. Paul urges the Galatians to preserve the freedom received in baptism, instead of returning from Christ's liberation to slavery under sin or legalism. Proclaiming the Gospel cannot mean coercing people or enslaving them in Jesus' name: Christ sets people free. (Paraphrased from the General Audience of 6 October 2021, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
