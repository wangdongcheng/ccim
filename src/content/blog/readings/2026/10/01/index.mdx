圣女婴孩耶稣德肋撒庆日 | Feast of Saint Thérèse of the Child Jesus, Virgin and Doctor of the Church

2026年10月1日 · 星期四 | October 1, 2026 · Thursday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**依撒意亚先知书 66:10-14c | Isaiah 66:10-14c**

凡爱耶路撒冷的人，都要同她一起欢乐；曾为她哀伤的人，如今要与她尽情欢喜。你们要从她安慰的怀中得到饱饫，从她丰盛的光荣中满饮喜乐。

因为上主这样说：“看，我要使平安像河水一样流向她，使万国的财富像满溢的溪流涌来。她的孩子要被抱在怀中，在膝上受到爱抚。正如母亲安慰自己的孩子，我也要这样安慰你们；你们要在耶路撒冷得到安慰。”

你们看见这一切，心里就要欢欣，身体也要像青草一样焕发生机；上主的大能必在他的仆人身上显明。

Rejoice with Jerusalem and be glad for her, all who love her; all who once mourned over her, now share fully in her joy. From her consoling breast you shall be nourished, and from her glorious abundance you shall drink with delight.

For thus says the Lord: “Behold, I will make peace flow to her like a river, and the wealth of the nations like an overflowing stream. Her children shall be carried in her arms and fondled upon her knees. As a mother comforts her child, so I will comfort you; in Jerusalem you shall find consolation.”

When you see this, your hearts shall rejoice and your bodies shall flourish like grass. The power of the Lord shall be made known among his servants.

上主，我的心不骄傲，
我的眼目也不高傲；
我不追求伟大的事，
也不妄想力所不及的事。

**答：上主，请保护我的心灵得享平安。**

我使自己的心灵安静平稳，
如同断乳的孩子偎依母亲；
我的心灵在我内，
正像那安睡的孩子。

**答：上主，请保护我的心灵得享平安。**

以色列，请仰望上主，
从现在直到永远。

**答：上主，请保护我的心灵得享平安。**

O Lord, my heart is not proud,
nor are my eyes raised too high;
I do not occupy myself with great matters
or with things beyond me.

**R. O Lord, keep my soul in peace.**

I have calmed and quieted my soul,
like a weaned child resting with its mother;
like that little child,
my soul is at peace within me.

**R. O Lord, keep my soul in peace.**

O Israel, hope in the Lord,
from this time forth and forevermore.

**R. O Lord, keep my soul in peace.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 18:1-4 | Matthew 18:1-4**

那时候，门徒来到耶稣跟前，问他说：“天国里究竟谁是最大的？”耶稣叫来一个小孩子，让他站在他们中间，然后说：“我实在告诉你们：你们若不回心转意，变得像小孩子一样，绝不能进入天国。所以，谁若自谦如同这个小孩子，他在天国里就是最大的。”

At that time the disciples came to Jesus and asked, “Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?” Jesus called a child, placed the child among them, and said, “Truly, I tell you: unless you turn and become like children, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven. Whoever humbles himself like this child is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven.”

## 反省 | Reflection

小德兰的心与耶稣的心彼此契合。她甘愿成为最小的，不倚靠自己的力量攀登通往天国的窄路，而让耶稣把她抱起，带她进入永恒。正因为承认自己的微小，她能怀着赤子般的自由与信赖，投入天父的怀抱。这正是耶稣所启示的天国秘诀：自谦如同小孩子。

小德兰也从耶稣的一生看见这条谦卑的道路：祂生于马槽、逃往异乡、隐居僻壤，以木匠的身份生活在平凡人中，选择末席，最终被列于罪犯之间，在羞辱中死去。基督的自我空虚，正是她所爱慕并愿意追随的道路；即使到了天堂，她仍渴望做最小的一个。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

Thérèse’s heart was closely united with the heart of Jesus. She willingly chose to be little, relying not on her own strength to climb the narrow road to heaven, but allowing Jesus to lift her and carry her into eternity. By acknowledging her littleness, she could entrust herself to the Father with the freedom and confidence of a child. This is the secret of the kingdom revealed by Jesus: to become humble like a little child.

Thérèse also recognized this path of humility throughout Jesus’ life. He was born in a manger, fled into a foreign land, lived hidden in an obscure place, worked as a carpenter among ordinary people, chose the lowest place, and finally was numbered among criminals and died in disgrace. Christ’s self-emptying was the way she loved and longed to follow; even in heaven, she still desired to remain the littlest one.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗本笃十六世指出，耶稣派遣七十二位门徒，表明传教使命并不只属于十二宗徒；在天主的田地里，每个人都有工作。门徒两人同行，好能彼此扶助，并为兄弟之爱作证。虽然他们像羔羊进入狼群，仍要在任何处境中成为和平的使者，信赖天主的照顾，并以医治彰显祂的慈悲。

即使遭到拒绝，门徒也不应报复，只须忠实地提醒人：天主的国已经临近。愿这段福音重新唤醒所有领洗者，使我们意识到自己都是基督的传教士，蒙召以言语和生活的见证为祂预备道路。

（教宗本笃十六世，三钟经，2007年7月8日）

Pope Benedict XVI explains that Jesus’ sending of the seventy-two shows that the mission was not reserved to the Twelve. “There is work for all in God's field.” The disciples go in pairs so that they may support one another and bear witness to brotherly love. Though sent like lambs among wolves, they are to bring peace in every circumstance, trust in God’s providence, and reveal his mercy by healing the sick.

Even when rejected, they are not to retaliate, but simply to testify that the kingdom of God has drawn near. May this Gospel awaken in every baptized person the awareness of being Christ’s missionary, called to prepare his way by both words and the witness of life.

(Pope Benedict XVI, Angelus, 8 July 2007)
