常年期第二十七周（星期四） | Thursday of the Twenty-seventh Week in Ordinary Time

2026年10月8日 · 星期四 | October 8, 2026 · Thursday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**迦拉达人书 3:1-5 | Galatians 3:1-5**

迦拉达人，你们怎么这样糊涂！耶稣基督被钉十字架的事，已经清楚地呈现在你们面前，究竟是谁使你们受了迷惑？我只问一件事：你们获得圣神，是由于履行法律，还是由于以信德接受所听到的福音？

你们竟如此不明白吗？既然靠圣神开始了，怎会如今转而靠肉性的力量完成？你们经历了那么多，难道全都落了空？但愿并非如此！天主不断赐予你们圣神，在你们中间行奇事，是因为你们履行法律，还是因为你们以信德接受了福音？

Galatians, how could you be so unwise? Jesus Christ's crucifixion was clearly set before you; who has led you astray? Answer this one question: was it through keeping the law that you received the Spirit, or through believing the message you heard?

How can you fail to understand? You began through the Spirit; will human strength now bring you to completion? Have all your experiences come to nothing—if they really have? When God gives you the Spirit and performs wonders among you, is this because you keep the law, or because you receive the message in faith?

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**路加福音 1:69-75 | Luke 1:69-75**

**答：上主，以色列的天主应受赞美，因为他眷顾了他的子民。 | R. Praise the Lord, the God of Israel, who has come to care for his people.**

他在仆人达味的家族中，为我们兴起强有力的救恩；这正应验了他自古藉圣先知所作的许诺。

**答：上主，以色列的天主应受赞美，因为他眷顾了他的子民。**

他救我们脱离敌人和一切恨我们者的手；他向我们的祖先施以怜悯，记得自己的神圣盟约。

**答：上主，以色列的天主应受赞美，因为他眷顾了他的子民。**

这是他向我们的先祖亚巴郎所立的誓约：我们既从敌手获救，就能无所畏惧，在他面前以圣洁和正义，一生事奉他。

**答：上主，以色列的天主应受赞美，因为他眷顾了他的子民。**

In the family of David, his servant, he has raised up mighty salvation for us, fulfilling what he declared long ago through his holy prophets.

**R. Praise the Lord, the God of Israel, who has come to care for his people.**

He rescues us from our enemies and everyone who hates us, showing compassion to our ancestors and remembering his sacred covenant.

**R. Praise the Lord, the God of Israel, who has come to care for his people.**

He swore to Abraham, our ancestor, that we would be freed from enemy hands and could worship him fearlessly, living before him in holiness and justice throughout our lives.

**R. Praise the Lord, the God of Israel, who has come to care for his people.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 11:5-13 | Luke 11:5-13**

耶稣向门徒讲述：假设你半夜到朋友家，请他借三个饼，因为有位远行的朋友刚到，你却没有食物招待他。屋里的朋友说，门已关好，孩子们也与他一起睡下了，不能起身给你。

耶稣说，即使友谊不足以使他起来，你不退缩地继续恳求，也会使他起身，把你所需要的给你。因此，你们祈求便会获得，寻找便会寻见，叩门便会有人开门；每个祈求者都获赐恩，每个寻找者都能寻见，每个叩门者都得到回应。

你们中有哪个父亲，会在儿子求饼时递给他石头，求鱼时递给他蛇，或求鸡蛋时递给他蝎子？你们虽有罪过，仍懂得把有益的东西送给孩子；天上的父岂不更愿意把圣神赐给向他祈求的人！

Jesus asked his disciples to imagine going to a friend's house at midnight for three loaves: a traveller has arrived, and there is no food to offer him. The friend inside protests that the door is locked and his children are in bed with him; he cannot rise to help.

Even if friendship does not move him, Jesus explains, the visitor's insistent appeal will bring him out of bed to supply what is needed. So make your requests, search, and knock: gifts will come, you will find, and doors will open. Whoever petitions receives; whoever searches finds; whoever knocks gains entry.

Would a father answer his child's request for bread with a stone, for fish with a snake, or for an egg with a scorpion? Even sinful parents understand how to provide good gifts. How much more readily will the Father in heaven grant the Holy Spirit to those who appeal to him!

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父的反省摘要：人会对别人的请求感到厌烦，天主却始终乐意聆听。恒心祈祷是子女向父亲倾诉、寻求照顾，也让我们学习他的教导，留心他的回应，而非要求他照自己的计划行事。

Summary of the reflection by Fr Li Jianping: People may tire of others' requests, but God always welcomes them. Persevering prayer expresses a child's affection and dependence on the Father. It helps us receive his guidance and notice his answers, rather than demand that he follow our plans.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世的话摘要：耶稣邀请我们信赖天父。祈求让我们承认一切恩惠来自他；寻找使我们顺从他的计划，走向圣德；叩门也邀请我们以纯洁、尊重和慷慨的爱分享恩赐。回顾喜乐与苦难，能看见天主如何培育信德和爱德。他始终以父亲的慈爱拥抱我们；生命的意义根植于他。（2025年10月9日，讲道）

Summary of Pope Leo XIV's words: Jesus invites confidence in the Father. Asking acknowledges that every gift comes from God; seeking means obediently pursuing his path to holiness; knocking also calls us to share our gifts with pure, respectful, generous love. Looking back on joy and suffering reveals how God develops faith and charity. His fatherly goodness continually embraces us, and our lives find meaning in him. (Homily, 9 October 2025)
