## 读经一 | First Reading

**斐理伯人书 1:1-11 | Philippians 1:1-11**

基督耶稣的仆人保禄和弟茂德，致书给居住在斐理伯、属于基督耶稣的全体圣徒，以及监督和执事：愿恩宠与平安由我们的父天主和主耶稣基督赐给你们。

每当记起你们，我便向天主致谢；每次为你们大家祈祷，我都满怀喜乐，因为从开始直到如今，你们一直与我共同承担福音的工作。我深信，在你们心中开创美善事业的天主，必使这事业不断成长，直到耶稣基督的日子达到圆满。

我这样关怀你们众人，本是应该的，因为你们常在我心中。不论我身受囚禁，或为福音申辩、证实福音，你们都与我一同分享恩宠。天主知道，我怎样怀着基督耶稣的深情，思念你们每一个人。

我为你们所求的是：愿你们的爱德日益增长，并伴随着认识和各样辨别的能力，好使你们能认出真正美好的事，在基督来临之日保持纯洁、无可指摘，藉耶稣基督结出丰盛的义德果实，使天主获得光荣与赞颂。

Paul and Timothy, who serve Christ Jesus, write to all God's holy people united with Christ in Philippi, together with their bishops and deacons. May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ grant you grace and peace.

Whenever you come to mind, I give God thanks. My intercession for every one of you is joyful, for you have shared in the Gospel's work from the beginning right up to the present. I trust that God, who set this good undertaking in motion among you, will carry it forward to its fulfilment when Jesus Christ comes.

Such affection for you all is fitting: you are dear to me, and you share the grace entrusted to me, both during my captivity and as I defend and establish the Gospel. God knows how deeply I long for every one of you with the tenderness of Christ Jesus.

I ask that your love may grow ever richer in understanding and sound judgment. Then you will recognize what truly matters and be found sincere and without fault when Christ comes. Through Jesus Christ, may your lives yield abundant righteousness, bringing God honour and praise.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 111:1-6 | Psalm 111:1-6**

**答：上主的作为何等伟大！ | R. How great are the deeds of the Lord!**

在正直者聚集的团体里，
我要全心向上主献上感谢。
他的工程伟大奇妙，
爱慕这些工程的人都用心寻求。

**答：上主的作为何等伟大！**

他的工作充满光辉和尊荣，
他的公义永不消逝。
他使奇妙的作为常被纪念，
上主满怀恩慈与怜悯。

**答：上主的作为何等伟大！**

他供养敬畏他的人，
时刻记念所订立的盟约。
他把列国的土地交给自己的子民，
使他们看见他大能的作为。

**答：上主的作为何等伟大！**

Among the upright gathered together,
I offer the Lord thanks with an undivided heart.
His mighty achievements invite the study
of everyone who delights in them.

**R. How great are the deeds of the Lord!**

Splendour and majesty mark his work;
his justice never passes away.
He keeps his wonders alive in our remembrance;
the Lord shows kindness and compassion.

**R. How great are the deeds of the Lord!**

He feeds those who revere him
and never forgets his covenant.
Giving his people the lands of the nations,
he has made his power known to them.

**R. How great are the deeds of the Lord!**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 14:1-6 | Luke 14:1-6**

一个安息日，耶稣到一位法利塞人首领家里用饭，在场的人密切注视着他。他面前有个人患了水肿病。

耶稣向法学士和法利塞人发问：“安息日医治病人，究竟合不合律法？”他们没有作声。耶稣于是扶住病人，使他痊愈，随后让他离去。

耶稣又问他们：“假使你们的儿子或牛在安息日掉进井里，难道你们不会马上把他拉上来吗？”他们对此无言以对。

On a sabbath, Jesus shared a meal in the home of a Pharisee who was a leader, while those present kept a close watch on him. A man suffering from dropsy stood before him.

Jesus put a question to the experts in the law and the Pharisees: “Does the law permit someone to be cured on the sabbath?” No one answered. Taking hold of the sick man, Jesus restored his health and sent him on his way.

He then asked them, “Suppose your son or your ox fell into a well on the sabbath. Would any of you delay pulling him out?” They had no answer to give.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父指出，若只顾规则而忘记眼前受苦的人，信仰便容易失去怜悯。耶稣看见每一个具体而平等的人，把病人的需要放在首位。原为人谋福的安息日，不应变成设陷阱、控告他人的工具。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-30)摘要；原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

Father Li Jianping warns that concern for rules can crowd out compassion for the suffering person before us. Jesus recognizes each person's equal dignity and puts the sick person's need first. The sabbath, intended for human good, must never become a device for trapping and accusing others. (Translated summary of the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

方济各教宗邀请我们让耶稣医治心灵的创伤。嫉妒和怨恨能使小小争执扩大，伤害整个家庭。我们应在祈祷中坦诚向主敞开心扉，求他医治自己；接受他的教导和医治之后，也能帮助别人走向和好。（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/30.html)的2015年2月8日讲道摘要。）

Pope Francis invites us to receive Jesus' healing for our inner wounds. Envy and resentment can magnify small quarrels and tear families apart. In prayer we can honestly open our hearts and ask him to heal us. Receiving his teaching and healing prepares us to help others find reconciliation. (Summary of the homily of 8 February 2015, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
