圣依纳爵·安提约基亚（主教、殉道） | Memorial of Saint Ignatius of Antioch, Bishop and Martyr

2026年10月17日 · 星期六 | October 17, 2026 · Saturday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 1:15-23 | Ephesians 1:15-23**

弟兄们：得知你们信靠主耶稣，又爱护众圣徒，我就常常为你们感谢天主，在祈祷中记念你们。我求我们的主耶稣基督的天主、光荣的父，赐你们智慧与启示的神恩，让你们更深认识他；愿他开启你们心灵的眼目，使你们明白他的召叫带来怎样的希望，他在圣徒中预备的光荣产业何等丰盛，他在信友身上施展的能力何等浩大。

这大能已在基督身上彰显：天主使他由死者中复活，在天上坐于自己右边，高居一切率领者、掌权者、异能者和宰制者之上，也超越今世来世的一切名号。天主使万有归服于他，又使他作教会至高的元首。教会是他的身体，充满那位使万有圆满者的丰盛。

Brothers and sisters, news of your trust in Jesus and care for the saints moves me to thank God and remember you in prayer. I ask the glorious Father, the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, to grant spiritual wisdom and insight into himself. May he open your inward sight to the hope of his call, the wealth of the glorious inheritance among his saints, and the extraordinary power at work in believers.

That power raised Christ from death and enthroned him beside the Father in heaven, beyond every ruler, authority, power, dominion, and title belonging to this world or the next. God subjected everything to Christ and appointed him supreme head of the Church. The Church is his body, the fullness of the one who brings everything to fullness.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 8:2-7 | Psalm 8:1-6**

**答：你令你的圣子统治你双手的化工。 | R. You have given your Son dominion over the work of your hands.**

上主，我们的天主，你的圣名在大地上何等尊荣！你的荣耀高于诸天；你使婴孩和乳儿的赞颂显出力量，令敌人和报复者止息。

**答：你令你的圣子统治你双手的化工。**

每当我仰望你手指造成的天空，以及你安置的明月星辰，我便想到：人算什么，你竟记念他？人子算什么，你竟如此眷顾他？

**答：你令你的圣子统治你双手的化工。**

你使他稍逊于天神，却以光荣尊贵为他加冠；你让他管理你亲手的创造，使一切归于他的脚下。

**答：你令你的圣子统治你双手的化工。**

Lord, our God, your name is glorious throughout the world! Your splendour rises above heaven. Through infants' voices you establish strength that silences enemies and those seeking vengeance.

**R. You have given your Son dominion over the work of your hands.**

As I gaze upon the sky your fingers fashioned, the moon and stars you set in place, I wonder why you remember humankind and care for the children of Adam.

**R. You have given your Son dominion over the work of your hands.**

You placed humanity just below the divine, bestowing a crown of honour and glory. You entrusted your creation to human stewardship and subjected everything to human rule.

**R. You have given your Son dominion over the work of your hands.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 12:8-12 | Luke 12:8-12**

那时候，耶稣向门徒说：谁在人前承认属于我，人子也会在天主的天使面前承认他；谁在人前否认我，也会在天主的天使面前被否认。出言冒犯人子的，可以蒙受宽赦；但亵渎圣神的，不能获得赦免。

当人把你们带到会堂、官长和掌权者面前，不必焦虑该怎样答辩、该说什么。到了那一刻，圣神自会教导你们应说的话。

Jesus told his disciples that those who openly acknowledge him will be acknowledged by the Son of man in the presence of God's angels. Those who disown him publicly will themselves be disowned there. A person who speaks against the Son of man can receive forgiveness, but blasphemy against the Holy Spirit will remain unforgiven.

When they are brought before synagogues, rulers, and authorities, they need not be anxious about their defence or search for words. At that very time the Holy Spirit will instruct them in what to say.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父指出，讲道与福传课程有助于沟通，但信仰见证的力量根源是圣神。分享福音也包括述说自己怎样经历天主、生命怎样因他而改变。常与天主相交的人，能够让内在的信仰自然流露，无须把见证完全寄托于表达技巧。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-17)转述，原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

Father Li Jianping observes that training can improve communication, yet the Holy Spirit supplies the strength for Christian witness. Sharing faith includes describing our encounter with God and the changes it brings. Living in communion with him allows that experience to find a natural voice, beyond reliance on technique. (Paraphrased from the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

圣若望保禄二世教宗教导，基督离开人世后，圣神仍在漫长的困苦岁月中陪伴教会，尤其在迫害中扶助信友。耶稣许下的助佑既属于宗徒，也属于历代为他受苦的人。许多基督徒亲身领受过这力量，整个教会也为圣神赐下的属灵胜利与喜乐作证。（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/17.html)的1991年3月13日公开接见讲话转述。）

Saint John Paul II teaches that the Spirit remains with the Church through the long trials following Christ's earthly life, especially during persecution. Jesus' promised assistance extends from the Apostles to everyone who suffers for him across the centuries. Many believers have experienced this strength, and the whole Church witnesses to the spiritual victory and joy it brings. (Paraphrased from the General Audience of 13 March 1991, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
