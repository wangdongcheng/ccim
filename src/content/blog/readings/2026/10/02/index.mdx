## 读经一 | First Reading

**出谷纪 23:20-23 | Exodus 23:20-23**

上主这样说：“看，我要派遣我的使者走在你前面，在路上保护你，并领你到我所预备的地方。你在他面前要谨慎，听从他的声音，不可反抗他；因为我的名在他身上，他不会宽赦你们的叛逆。

“如果你认真听从他的声音，履行我所吩咐的一切，我必敌对你的仇敌，攻击你的敌人。我的使者要走在你前面。”

Thus says the Lord: “Behold, I send an angel before you to guard you on the way and to bring you to the place I have prepared. Pay attention to him and listen to his voice; do not rebel against him, for my name is in him, and he will not pardon your transgression.

“But if you listen faithfully to his voice and do all that I command, I will oppose your enemies and resist those who resist you. My angel shall go before you.”

## 反省 | Reflection

一提到护守天使，有些人便把它当作只属于童年的想象。然而，圣经中的天使始终参与人类的救恩史。风、爱、原子和电子虽不能直接被肉眼看见，却不能因此被视为不存在；同样，对护守天使的信仰邀请我们超越感官的限制，不要把浩瀚的受造世界缩小成眼前所见的一切。

基督徒的救恩也不只是个人独自面对天主。从预报救主降生到宣告祂复活，从耶稣在旷野受试探到祂所讲的比喻，天使一次又一次出现在基督的生命与教导中。天主派遣他们，带着祂的恩宠与我们同行，作我们的朋友并守护我们；知道自己在这份眷顾中生活，实在令人平安。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿

When guardian angels are mentioned, some people dismiss them as an idea belonging only to childhood. Yet throughout Scripture, angels take part in the history of human salvation. Wind, love, atoms, and electrons cannot be seen directly with our eyes, but that does not make them unreal. In the same way, faith in guardian angels invites us to move beyond the limits of our senses and not reduce the vastness of creation to what is immediately visible.

Christian salvation is also more than an isolated individual standing before God. From the announcement of the Saviour’s birth to the proclamation of his resurrection, and from Jesus’ temptation in the wilderness to the parables he taught, angels repeatedly appear in Christ’s life and teaching. God sends them with his grace to accompany us, befriend us, and keep watch over us. To know that we live within such loving care is a profound source of peace.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各说，依照教会传统，我们每个人都有一位天使陪伴，在路上守护我们，也帮助我们辨别应当怎样行事。我们心里那提醒自己谨慎、纠正方向的声音，往往就是这位旅伴在劝告。拒绝聆听，仿佛是在对他说“走开”，这十分危险，因为人并不能总是正确地指导自己；圣神劝导我们，天使也在一旁帮助我们。

这不是关于天使的幻想，而是一项真实的信仰。我们可以省察：我怎样与自己的护守天使相处？我是否聆听他的提醒，在早晨问候他，在夜晚请他护守，并向他寻求帮助？他就在我们身旁，陪伴我们的旅程，并常常瞻仰天父的圣容。

（教宗方济各，圣玛尔大之家弥撒讲道，2014年10月2日）

Pope Francis teaches that, according to the Church’s tradition, each person has an angel who watches over and accompanies us, helping us discern how we should act. The inner prompting that warns us to be careful or correct our direction may be the counsel of this travelling companion. Refusing to listen is like sending that companion away, which is dangerous because no one can always guide oneself rightly; the Holy Spirit counsels us, and the angel also assists us.

This is not a fanciful idea about angels, but a reality of faith. We can ask ourselves: What is my relationship with my guardian angel? Do I listen, greet him in the morning, ask for protection at night, and seek his advice? As Pope Francis reminds us, “He is by my side,” accompanying our journey and always beholding the Father’s face in heaven.

(Pope Francis, Homily at Santa Marta, 2 October 2014)
