## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 1:11-14 | Ephesians 1:11-14**

弟兄们：天主依照自己的旨意安排万事；按照他在基督内所定的计划，我们这些最先寄望于基督的人，已蒙预定和召选，要以生活颂扬他的光荣。

你们也在基督内听见了真理的圣言，就是带给你们救恩的福音，并且信从了他，因而领受了所恩许的圣神作为印记。这圣神是我们将来承受基业的凭据，直到完全获得那基业，使天主的光荣受到赞颂。

Brothers and sisters: God brings everything to fulfilment in keeping with his own will and purpose. In Christ he appointed us, who were the first to place our hope in him, to live in a way that brings glory to God.

You too heard the message of truth in Christ, the good news through which you are saved, and put your faith in him. God marked you with the Holy Spirit he had promised. The Spirit is the pledge of the inheritance awaiting us, until we come into its full possession, so that God's glory may be praised.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**答：上主选为产业的国民真是有福！ | R. Blessed are the people the Lord has chosen as his own!**

义人，请因上主而欢欣；心地正直的人理应歌颂他。请以琴声向上主致谢，用十弦乐器奏出赞歌。

**答：上主选为产业的国民真是有福！**

上主的言语正直可信，他所行的一切都忠实可靠。他喜爱公义与正直，大地处处充满他的慈爱。

**答：上主选为产业的国民真是有福！**

奉上主为天主的民族是有福的，他召选为自己产业的子民是有福的。上主从天上垂顾，看见世间的每一个人。

**答：上主选为产业的国民真是有福！**

**R. Blessed are the people the Lord has chosen as his own!**

Let those who do right find joy in the Lord; upright hearts should sing his praise. Offer him thanks with the lyre and make music for him on an instrument of ten strings.

**R. Blessed are the people the Lord has chosen as his own!**

What the Lord says is right, and everything he does is trustworthy. Justice and righteousness are dear to him; his enduring kindness fills the world.

**R. Blessed are the people the Lord has chosen as his own!**

Happy is the nation that acknowledges the Lord as God, the people he has made his own inheritance. From heaven the Lord watches over every member of the human family.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 12:1-7 | Luke 12:1-7**

那时候，聚集的群众成千上万，拥挤得彼此践踏。耶稣先向门徒讲话，劝他们提防法利塞人的酵母，也就是虚伪。他说：凡被掩盖的，终会显露；凡被隐藏的，终会为人所知。所以，你们暗中所说的话，将在光明中被人听到；你们在内室低声说的话，将从屋顶传扬出去。

耶稣又说：我的朋友们，不要畏惧那些只能杀害身体、此后便无能为力的人。我要告诉你们应当敬畏谁：应当敬畏那位在取去性命以后，还有权把人投入地狱的。是的，你们应当敬畏他。

五只麻雀虽只卖两文钱，却没有一只被天主遗忘。连你们头上的每一根头发，他也都知道。因此不必害怕；在天主眼中，你们远比许多麻雀珍贵。

A crowd numbering many thousands had gathered, pressing so tightly that people trampled one another. Jesus addressed his disciples first, warning them against the Pharisees' leaven, by which he meant hypocrisy. Everything concealed would eventually come into the open, and every secret would become known. Words spoken under cover of darkness would be heard in daylight; private whispers would be announced from rooftops.

He called them his friends and urged them not to be frightened of people whose power ends with killing the body. Rather, they should fear the one whose authority extends beyond death to casting into hell. It is this one, he insisted, whom they should fear.

Five sparrows cost only two pennies, yet God forgets none of them. He even knows the number of hairs on their heads. They need not be afraid: they matter far more than many sparrows.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父从清晨的鸟鸣想到天主对受造物的照顾：麻雀赖以栖息、觅食和飞翔的一切，都是恩赐。连微小的雀鸟也蒙眷顾，我们更可以把生命托付给天主。与其任由忧虑和恐惧占据内心，不如从雀鸟和发丝这些细小事物，学习信赖上主。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-16)转述，原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

Father Li Jianping hears in morning birdsong a reminder of God's care. A sparrow's shelter, nourishment, and ability to fly are gifts. If such small creatures receive God's attention, we can entrust our lives to him with still greater confidence. Birds and even our hair invite us to learn trust instead of letting worry and fear govern us. (Paraphrased from the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世指出，宣讲福音始于每个人亲自与基督相遇。使徒工作的力量来自圣神在人心中的行动和真诚的回应，也来自把默观所得分享给别人。面对仇恨、傲慢和灰心，门徒应以爱、温柔和恒心回应；与耶稣的深厚关系，使我们能够不断向世界传递希望、爱与和平。（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/16.html)的2026年6月21日三钟经讲话转述。）

Pope Leo XIV explains that evangelization begins with each person's encounter with Christ. Apostolic work draws its strength from the Spirit's action and our sincere response, sharing with others what prayer has allowed us to contemplate. Disciples meet hatred with love, arrogance with gentleness, and discouragement with perseverance. Deep communion with Jesus sustains their witness to hope, love, and peace. (Paraphrased from the Angelus of 21 June 2026, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
