## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 3:2-12 | Ephesians 3:2-12**

弟兄们：你们想必听说，天主为了你们，将管理祂恩宠的职分交给了我。祂以启示使我认识基督的奥秘；我先前已简略写过，你们读了便能明白我对此奥秘的认识。往昔世代的人未曾这样知晓它，如今圣神却把它启示给基督的宗徒和先知：外邦人藉福音在基督耶稣内与以色列人同作继承人，同属一个身体，同享天主的恩许。

天主以大能施恩，使我成为这福音的仆役。我虽是众圣徒中最微小的，却蒙赐恩宠，向外邦人传扬基督无可测度的丰富，并使众人看见这奥秘如何实现。它历代隐藏在创造万有的天主内，为使天上的掌权者如今藉教会认识天主丰富多样的智慧。这是天主在我们的主基督耶稣内实现的永恒计划；我们在祂内，因信靠祂，可以坦然、满怀信心地来到天主面前。

Brothers and sisters, you have heard that God entrusted me with the stewardship of his grace for your sake. By revelation he made known to me the mystery of Christ, about which I wrote briefly before. Reading that, you can understand my insight into it. Earlier generations did not know this mystery as Christ's holy apostles and prophets now know it through the Spirit: through the gospel, the Gentiles share the inheritance, belong to the same body, and share the promise in Christ Jesus.

I became a servant of this gospel by the gift of God's grace and the working of his power. Though I am the least of all the saints, he gave me grace to announce Christ's unfathomable riches to the Gentiles and to bring to light the unfolding of the mystery hidden for ages in God, who created all things. Thus, through the Church, God's many-sided wisdom is now made known even to the powers in heaven. This fulfills his eternal purpose in Christ Jesus our Lord. In him, through faith, we can approach God freely and with confidence.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 12:32-48 | Luke 12:32-48**

那时候，耶稣对门徒说：「你们这小羊群，不要害怕，因为你们的父乐意把天国赐给你们。变卖所有的，施舍给有需要的人；为自己预备不会破旧的钱囊，将财宝积存天上，那里没有贼偷，也没有虫蛀。你们的财宝在哪里，你们的心也在哪里。

「你们要束好腰，点着灯，像等候主人从婚宴归来的仆人；他一敲门，就立刻开门。主人来到时若看见他们醒着，那些仆人是有福的。我实在告诉你们：主人会束上腰，请他们坐席，亲自来服侍他们。即使他在深夜或黎明来到，只要看见他们这样警醒，他们就是有福的。你们要知道：家主若晓得贼何时来，必会守候，不让自己的房屋被挖穿。你们也要准备好，因为人子要在你们料想不到的时刻来临。」

伯多禄问：「主啊，这比喻是对我们说的，还是对众人说的？」主回答：「谁是那位忠信而精明的管家，受主人委派管理家仆，按时分给他们食物呢？主人回来时，若看见他这样做，那仆人是有福的。我实在告诉你们：主人会派他管理自己的一切财产。

「但若那仆人心想：『我的主人还要很久才回来』，便殴打男女仆人，只顾吃喝醉酒，主人将在他意想不到的日子、不知道的时刻回来，严厉惩处他，使他与不忠信的人同列。知道主人意愿却不准备、也不照着去做的仆人，必受重罚；不知道而做了该受惩罚的事的，受罚较轻。多给谁，就向谁多要；多托付给谁，就向谁多索取。」

Jesus said to his disciples: “Do not be afraid, little flock, for your Father delights in giving you the kingdom. Sell what you have and give to those in need. Make purses that will not wear out and store up treasure in heaven, where no thief can take it and no moth can destroy it. Where your treasure is, your heart will be there too.

“Keep your belts fastened and your lamps burning. Be like servants awaiting their master’s return from a wedding feast, ready to open the door as soon as he knocks. Blessed are those servants whom he finds awake. Truly, he will dress to serve, seat them at table, and wait on them himself. Whether he comes late at night or toward dawn, they are blessed if he finds them ready. Remember that a householder who knew when a thief was coming would stay awake and prevent a break-in. You too must be ready, for the Son of Man will come at an hour you do not expect.”

Peter asked, “Lord, is this parable for us or for everyone?” The Lord replied, “Who is the faithful and wise steward whom the master sets over his household to give the others their food at the right time? Blessed is that servant if the master returns and finds him doing this. Truly, the master will put him in charge of all his possessions.

“But if that servant tells himself that his master is delayed, then begins to beat the men and women servants and to feast and get drunk, the master will come on a day and at an hour he does not expect. He will punish him severely and place him among the unfaithful. A servant who knew his master’s will but made no preparations and did not obey it will receive a severe punishment. One who did not know, yet did what deserved punishment, will receive a lighter one. Much will be required of the one given much; still more will be asked of the one entrusted with more.”

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父反省摘要：蜜蜂采花粉酿蜜，却不占有花；雀鸟借树枝筑巢，却不占有树。人若一味追求资源、财富与权位，把受托之物当成自己的私产，便容易变得贪婪而失职。忠信的管家记得主人是谁，并照料其他仆人；失职的管家却趁主人不在时挥霍财物、欺压同伴。所得的能力、地位和资源越多，所承担的责任也越大。（[万有真原，乐仁出版社供稿](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-21)）

Summary of Fr Li Jianping’s reflection: A bee gathers pollen without claiming the flower, and a bird builds a nest without claiming the tree. People, however, may treat entrusted resources, wealth, and authority as possessions to hoard. The faithful steward remembers the master and cares for fellow servants; the unfaithful one squanders what is entrusted to him and abuses others. Greater ability, position, and resources bring greater responsibility. ([WanYou ZhenYuan; contributed by Claretian Publications Macau](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-21))

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世禧年接见讲话摘要：耶稣向领受许多的门徒要求更多，是信任与友谊的标记；祂知道他们能够回应。主把天主之爱的火带到世上，也唤醒人心的渴望。真正的平安不是无所作为的安静。面对不义、不平等、被践踏的尊严和被迫沉默的弱小者，福音召唤我们表明立场，以行动见证希望。（[2025年11月22日禧年接见，梵蒂冈新闻网当日选文](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/21.html)）

Summary of Pope Leo XIV’s Jubilee Audience: Jesus asks much of disciples who have received much, a sign of his trust and friendship. He brings the fire of God’s love and stirs our hearts to respond. His peace is more than passive quiet. Faced with injustice, inequality, violated dignity, and the silencing of vulnerable people, hope calls us to take a stand and show through our actions that things can change. ([Jubilee Audience, 22 November 2025; selection in Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/21.html))
