## 读经一 | First Reading

**依撒意亚先知书 25:6-10a | Isaiah 25:6-10a**

在这座山上，万军的上主要为各民族预备丰盛的宴席，有肥美的食物和经过澄清的陈酒。他要除去笼罩万民的帷幕，掀开覆盖列国的殓布。他要彻底消灭死亡；吾主上主要擦干众人面上的泪水，从全地除去自己子民所受的羞辱。这是上主亲口所许诺的。

到了那一天，人们会说：看，我们等候的天主就在这里，他来拯救我们了！这就是我们一直信赖的上主，让我们因他施行的救恩而欢喜踊跃。因为上主的手要安住在这座山上。

On this mountain, the Lord of hosts will prepare a lavish banquet for every people, with rich food and well-aged, carefully refined wine. Here he will remove the covering that hangs over all peoples and the veil spread across the nations. Death will be destroyed for ever. The Lord God will dry the tears on every face and remove his people's disgrace throughout the earth. The Lord has declared it.

On that day people will say: Here is our God, whom we awaited for deliverance! Here is the Lord in whom we placed our hope. Let us celebrate with joy the salvation he has brought, for the Lord's hand will remain upon this mountain.

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**斐理伯人书 4:12-14,19-20 | Philippians 4:12-14,19-20**

弟兄们：我懂得怎样在贫困中生活，也懂得怎样面对富足。在各种处境中，不论吃饱还是挨饿、丰裕还是缺乏，我已学会面对这一切的秘诀：那位赐我力量的，使我能应付所有境遇。不过，你们愿意与我分担患难，实在做得好。

我的天主要按他在基督耶稣内荣耀的丰盛，供应你们所有的需要。愿我们的天主与父，永远受光荣。阿们。

Brothers and sisters, I have learned to live with little and to live with plenty. In every situation, whether well fed or hungry, prosperous or lacking necessities, I have learned how to face it: the one who gives me strength enables me to meet every circumstance. Even so, you acted generously by sharing my affliction.

My God will provide for all your needs from his glorious abundance in Christ Jesus. May our God and Father receive glory through all eternity. Amen.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 22:1-14 | Matthew 22:1-14**

那时候，耶稣再次以比喻向司祭长和民间长老讲话：天国就像一位国王，为自己的儿子举行婚宴。他差遣仆人通知受邀的人前来，他们却不肯赴宴。

国王又派出别的仆人，让他们告诉宾客：宴席已准备妥当，公牛和肥畜都已宰好，一切齐备，来参加婚宴吧！那些人却把邀请当作无关紧要的事，各自离开，有人去田里，有人忙自己的买卖。其余的人竟捉住仆人，羞辱他们，并把他们杀了。国王愤怒，派军队消灭那些杀人者，烧毁他们的城。

接着，国王对仆人说：婚宴已经准备好了，但原先受邀的人不配赴宴。你们到各条大道去，把遇到的人都请来。仆人便出发，将所遇到的人，无论善恶，都聚集起来，婚宴大厅就坐满了宾客。

国王进来察看宾客，发现有人没有穿婚宴礼服，便问他：朋友，你没有穿礼服，怎么进来了？那人无法回答。国王于是吩咐侍从，把他的手脚捆住，抛到外面的黑暗中；那里充满哭泣和切齿。因为蒙召的人很多，获选的人却少。

Jesus again addressed the chief priests and the elders through a parable. The kingdom of heaven is like a king holding a wedding banquet for his son. He sent servants to summon the invited guests, but they refused to attend.

The king dispatched more servants with this message: The meal is prepared; the oxen and fattened calves have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come and celebrate the wedding! The guests dismissed the invitation and left, some for their farmland and others for their trade. The remaining guests seized the servants, humiliated them and killed them. In anger, the king sent soldiers to destroy the murderers and set their city ablaze.

Then he told his servants: The banquet is ready, but the original guests proved unworthy. Go to the main roads and invite everyone you encounter. The servants gathered all the people they met, whether bad or good, until the banquet hall was crowded with guests.

When the king entered to inspect the guests, he noticed someone without wedding attire. He asked: Friend, how did you enter without the proper garment? The man could give no answer. The king ordered the attendants to bind his hands and feet and throw him into the darkness outside, a place of weeping and grinding teeth. Many receive the call, but few are chosen.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父提醒我们，生命首先是天主赐予的恩惠。人能靠努力获得物质财富，却不能把真理、爱与宽恕当作报酬赚来，只能开放自己去接受。耶稣的婚宴比喻邀请我们怀着喜乐回应天主；接受或拒绝圣言的召叫，也塑造着我们成为怎样的人。（[李建平神父，乐仁出版社供稿；意译摘要](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-11)）

Fr Li Jianping reflects on life as a gift from God. Effort can secure material wealth, but truth, love and forgiveness cannot be earned as wages; we receive them with openness. Jesus' wedding banquet parable invites a joyful response to God. Our acceptance or refusal of the Word's call also shapes the people we become. (Fr Li Jianping, Claretian Publications Macau; translated summary.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

方济各教宗指出，比喻中的国王是一位慷慨的父亲，愿意让众人分享他的喜乐。他邀请，却不强迫。天主同样召叫我们与他、也与彼此共融，尊重我们自由的回应。赴宴需要我们付出时间，并以行动答应邀请。天父所愿意建立的是父子关系；他的全能不会取消人的自由，因为他的爱始终尊重我们的选择。（[方济各教宗，2023年10月15日三钟经讲话；意译摘要](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/11.html)）

Pope Francis describes the king as a generous father who wants others to share his joy. His invitation involves no coercion. God likewise calls us into communion with him and one another while respecting our freedom. Attending requires time and an active willingness to accept. The Father seeks a relationship with his children, and his almighty power does not override our consent: his love honours our choice. (Pope Francis, Angelus, 15 October 2023; summary.)
