2026年10月9日 · 星期五 | October 9, 2026 · Friday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**迦拉达人书 3:7-14 | Galatians 3:7-14**

弟兄们，你们应当明白：凭信德生活的人，才是亚巴郎的子孙。圣经预先指出，天主要使外邦人因信德而成义，所以早已向亚巴郎宣布喜讯：所有民族都将在他内蒙受祝福。因此，信靠天主的人，也与充满信德的亚巴郎一起蒙福。

凡把希望寄托于履行法律的人，却处在诅咒之下；因为经上说，谁不坚持实行法律书所载的一切，就要受诅咒。没有人能靠法律在天主面前成义，这是清楚的，因为“义人因信德而生活”。法律的原则却不是信德，而是要求人实行其中的规定，藉此生活。

基督为我们承担了诅咒，将我们从法律的诅咒中赎回；经上把悬在木架上的人称为受诅咒者。这样，亚巴郎所得的祝福便在耶稣基督内临到外邦人，我们也能藉信德领受天主所许诺的圣神。

Brothers and sisters, understand that Abraham's children are those who have faith. Scripture foresaw that God would make the Gentiles righteous through faith, and announced good news to Abraham beforehand: every nation would receive blessing through him. Believers therefore share the blessing given to faithful Abraham.

Those who depend on carrying out the law face its curse, for Scripture places under a curse everyone who fails to keep every requirement in the book of the law. Clearly the law cannot make anyone righteous before God: the righteous receive life through faith. The law proceeds on another basis, promising life to the person who performs its commands.

Christ bought our freedom from that curse by bearing it himself on our behalf; Scripture calls the person hung upon a tree accursed. Through Jesus Christ, Abraham's blessing could thus reach the Gentiles, and through faith we could receive the Spirit whom God promised.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 111:1-6 | Psalm 111:1-6**

**答：上主永远怀念自己的盟约。 | R. The Lord always remembers his covenant.**

我要在正直者聚集的会众中，全心感谢上主。他的工程何等伟大，喜爱这些工程的人都细心察看。

**答：上主永远怀念自己的盟约。**

他的作为满有尊荣和威严，他的正义永不消逝。他使奇妙的工程留在人们的记忆中；上主充满慈爱与怜悯。

**答：上主永远怀念自己的盟约。**

他供应敬畏他的人食粮，始终记得所立的盟约。他将列邦的产业赐给自己的子民，使他们看见他工程的大能。

**答：上主永远怀念自己的盟约。**

Among the upright gathered together, I will thank the Lord with all my heart. His deeds are magnificent; everyone who delights in them examines them with care.

**R. The Lord always remembers his covenant.**

Honor and splendor mark his deeds, and his righteousness lasts without end. He ensures that his wonders are remembered; the Lord shows kindness and compassion.

**R. The Lord always remembers his covenant.**

He supplies food to those who revere him and never forgets his covenant. By granting his people the inheritance of the nations, he makes the might of his deeds known to them.

**R. The Lord always remembers his covenant.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 11:15-26 | Luke 11:15-26**

那时候，有人指责耶稣，说他藉魔王贝耳则步的力量赶走魔鬼；另一些人为了试探他，要求他显示一个来自天上的征兆。

耶稣洞悉他们的思想，便说：一个王国若内部争斗，就会荒废，房屋也相继倒塌。撒殚若与自己作对，它的王国怎能站立？你们竟说我藉贝耳则步驱魔。假如我真靠他驱魔，你们的子弟又靠谁呢？他们将判明你们的错误。但若我藉天主的手指驱魔，天主的国便已来到你们中间。

一个全副武装的壮士守卫自己的宅院时，财物可以安然保存。但更强的人若来攻打并胜过他，就会拿走他信赖的武装，分配所夺得的财物。谁不与我站在一起，就是与我为敌；谁不与我共同聚集，就是使人离散。

不洁之神离开一个人后，走过无水之地寻找安歇之处，却找不到，便决定返回先前离开的住所。回来时，见那里已经扫净、整理好了，就再带来七个比自己更恶的邪神，一同进去居住。那人的最终处境，竟比起初更加恶劣。

Some accused Jesus of expelling demons with the help of Beelzebul, their prince. Others tested him by demanding a heavenly sign.

Knowing their thoughts, Jesus replied that internal conflict ruins a kingdom and brings houses down. If Satan fights himself, how can his kingdom survive? They claimed that Beelzebul empowered him; but then whose power enabled their own sons to expel demons? Those sons would judge their accusation. If God's finger empowered Jesus, however, God's kingdom had already reached them.

An armed strong man keeps his property secure while guarding his palace. But a stronger attacker who defeats him removes the armor he depended upon and shares out the captured goods. Anyone who takes no stand with Jesus opposes him; anyone who does not help him gather causes scattering.

After leaving a person, an unclean spirit travels through dry places seeking rest. Finding none, it resolves to return to its former dwelling. It discovers the house swept and arranged, then brings seven spirits more wicked than itself to settle there together. That person's final condition becomes worse than the original one.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父的反省摘要：魔鬼危害人的灵魂，而耶稣驱魔显示天国的大能已临在。把这能力归于魔王，是把解救人的行动误认为邪恶内部的争斗。基督凭天主的力量进入仇敌的领域，释放受困的人。反省引用圣纽曼的思想：耶稣在苦难中甘愿承受黑暗的攻击，为使受奴役的人获得自由。

Summary of the reflection by Fr Li Jianping: Evil threatens human souls, while Jesus' exorcisms reveal the power of God's kingdom. Attributing that liberation to the demonic prince mistakes rescue for conflict within evil itself. Christ enters the enemy's domain with God's power to free its captives. Drawing on Saint Newman's thought, the reflection presents Jesus' suffering as his voluntary submission to darkness's assault for the liberation of those enslaved by it.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗方济各的话摘要：皈依使我们辨认邪恶、选择良善，但邪恶也会改变策略，以不易识破的伪装再次接近我们。因此，我们需要警醒，留心自己和教会。耶稣关于邪神返回整洁屋子的比喻，提醒我们不要以为曾经摆脱的诱惑永远消失。它可能以更文雅、更精致的形式重来。每日省察能帮助我们识别这些隐藏的诱惑，守护内心。（2022年12月22日，向罗马教廷发表讲话）

Summary of Pope Francis' words: Conversion teaches us to recognize evil and choose good, yet evil responds by disguising itself more subtly. Vigilance over ourselves and the Church is therefore necessary. Jesus' image of spirits returning to a tidy house warns against assuming that temptations once overcome have disappeared permanently. They may return looking sophisticated and respectable. A daily examination of conscience helps expose these concealed temptations and protect our inner life. (Address to the Roman Curia, 22 December 2022)
