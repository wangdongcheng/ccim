## 读经一 | First Reading

**约伯传 42:1-3,5-6,12-17 | Job 42:1-3,5-6,12-17**

约伯向上主回答：“我承认你无所不能，没有一个计划会落空。是谁以无知的话遮蔽你的谋略？正是我谈论了自己不懂的事，那些奇妙的事远远超出我的认识。过去我只是听说过你，如今却亲眼见到了你；所以我厌弃自己，在尘埃和灰烬中悔改。”

上主赐福约伯晚年的生活，胜过他起初所得的。他有一万四千只羊、六千匹骆驼、一千对牛和一千头母驴。他还有七个儿子和三个女儿，女儿依次名叫耶米玛、刻漆雅和刻冷哈普客。那地方没有女子比约伯的女儿更美；父亲也让她们同弟兄一起继承家产。此后约伯又活了一百四十年，看见子孙直到第四代，最后年老寿满而逝。

Job replied to the Lord: “I acknowledge that your power has no limit and your plans cannot fail. Who has clouded your counsel with ignorance? I have spoken about wonders beyond my understanding. Previously I knew you only through what I heard; now I have seen you. I therefore reject my presumption and repent amid dust and ashes.”

The Lord gave Job greater blessings in his later years than at the beginning. His possessions included fourteen thousand sheep, six thousand camels, a thousand pairs of oxen, and a thousand female donkeys. He also had seven sons and three daughters, named Jemimah, Keziah, and Kerenhappuch. No women in the land surpassed his daughters in beauty, and their father included them in the inheritance alongside their brothers. Job lived another hundred and forty years and saw four generations of descendants. He died in old age, having lived a full life.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 119:66,71,75,91,125,130 | Psalm 119:66,71,75,91,125,130**

**答：上主，求你给你的仆人显示你的慈颜。 | R. Lord, let your face shine upon your servant.**

求你赐我判断的智慧和知识，
因为我信赖你的诫命。
苦难使我获益，
帮助我学习你的法令。

**答：上主，求你给你的仆人显示你的慈颜。**

上主，我知道你的裁断公正，
你使我受苦，也出于你的忠信。
天地至今依照你的安排而立，
因为一切受造物都事奉你。

**答：上主，求你给你的仆人显示你的慈颜。**

我是你的仆人，求你开启我的心智，
使我认识你的约法。
你的话一经阐明便发出光亮，
使纯朴的人也能领悟。

**答：上主，求你给你的仆人显示你的慈颜。**

Grant me wisdom to judge rightly and knowledge,
for I place my trust in your commands.
My suffering has benefited me
by helping me learn your statutes.

**R. Lord, let your face shine upon your servant.**

Lord, I acknowledge the justice of your judgments;
even my affliction comes through your faithfulness.
Heaven and earth endure according to your decree,
for everything you made serves you.

**R. Lord, let your face shine upon your servant.**

As your servant, I ask for insight
so that I may understand your testimonies.
When your words are opened up, they bring light
and give understanding to those who are simple.

**R. Lord, let your face shine upon your servant.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 10:17-24 | Luke 10:17-24**

七十二位门徒喜乐地归来，向耶稣报告：“主，我们奉你的名行动，连恶魔也听从我们！”耶稣回答：“我曾看见撒殚如闪电般自天坠落。我给了你们权柄，能踏过毒蛇和蝎子，胜过仇敌的一切力量，没有什么能伤害你们。你们的喜乐应当在于名字已被记在天上，而不只在于邪灵听从你们。”

就在那时，耶稣在圣神内满怀喜乐，说：“父啊，天地的主宰，我向你感恩；你将这些事向智慧明达的人隐藏，却让小孩子认识。父啊，这是你所喜悦的。父已经把一切交托给我。只有父认识子，只有子和子愿意向他启示的人认识父。”

随后，耶稣私下转向门徒说：“你们的眼睛能看见这一切，真是有福！许多先知和君王渴望看见你们所见的，却未能看见；渴望听见你们所听的，却未能听见。”

The seventy-two disciples came back delighted: “Lord, when we act in your name, even demons obey us!” Jesus answered: “I saw Satan descend from heaven like a lightning flash. I have empowered you to tread on snakes and scorpions and overcome every force of the enemy; nothing will injure you. Your joy should be that your names have been inscribed in heaven, rather than simply that evil spirits obey you.”

At that moment Jesus rejoiced in the Holy Spirit: “Father, sovereign over heaven and earth, I give you thanks. You have concealed these things from the wise and discerning and made them known to little children. Father, this accords with your delight. My Father has entrusted everything to me. Only the Father knows the Son; only the Son, and those to whom he chooses to disclose him, know the Father.”

He then spoke privately to his disciples: “How blessed you are to see this! Many prophets and kings longed to see and hear what you experience, but never did.”

## 反省 | Reflection

门徒能够驱魔，力量来自他们所呼求的耶稣。耶稣邀请他们怀有更深的喜乐：他们已与祂相连，成为天主子的门徒、同伴和见证人，名字被记在天上。若只陶醉于自己的能力，喜乐便容易变成骄傲，使人忘记天主，反而陷入邪恶的圈套。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿（忠实转述）

The disciples can drive out demons because of Jesus, whose name they invoke. He invites them to a deeper joy: they belong to him as disciples, companions, and witnesses, and their names are recorded in heaven. Delight in their own abilities alone could turn into pride, make them forget God, and leave them vulnerable to the evil they have overcome.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau (faithful paraphrase)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

本笃十六世指出，耶稣的欢欣祈祷表达了感恩与赞美。祂称天主为父，显明祂作为圣子与父持续而亲密的共融，这也是祂一切祈祷的根源。圣子喜悦天父向心地纯朴的人揭示奥秘，祂的意愿与父完全一致。天主的启示超越世间对学识和权势的看法，将奥秘赐给小孩子。

（教宗本笃十六世，2011年12月7日公开接见；忠实转述）

Benedict XVI explains that Jesus’ joyful prayer gives thanks and praise. In addressing God as Father, Jesus expresses the intimate and enduring communion of the Son with the Father, which grounds all his prayer. The Son welcomes the Father’s decision to reveal divine mysteries to people with simple hearts; their wills are united. Revelation goes beyond worldly assumptions about learning and power: God makes his mysteries known to little children.

(Pope Benedict XVI, General Audience, 7 December 2011; faithful paraphrase)
