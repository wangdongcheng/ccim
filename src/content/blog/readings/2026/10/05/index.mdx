## 读经一 | First Reading

**迦拉达人书 1:6-12 | Galatians 1:6-12**

弟兄们，我十分惊讶，你们竟这样快就背离那以基督恩宠召叫你们的天主，转向别的福音。其实并不存在另一种福音，只是有人扰乱你们，想歪曲基督的福音。

即使我们自己，或来自天上的天使，向你们传讲与我们先前所传不同的福音，也应受诅咒。我再重申以前的话：凡传讲与已领受的福音相反之道的人，都应受诅咒。

我所求的是人的赞许，还是天主的悦纳？若我仍以取悦人为目标，就不能作基督的仆人。弟兄们，你们要知道，我所传的福音并非人的构想；它不是从人领受，也不是由人教给我的，而是耶稣基督亲自启示给我的。

Brothers and sisters, I am amazed that you have so quickly abandoned God, who called you through Christ's grace, and turned to a different gospel. No alternative gospel exists; certain people are unsettling you and trying to distort Christ's message.

Even if we ourselves, or a heavenly angel, proclaimed a message opposed to what we first preached, that messenger would deserve condemnation. I repeat my earlier warning: anyone contradicting the gospel you received deserves that condemnation.

Whose approval am I seeking, people's or God's? If pleasing people were still my purpose, I could not serve Christ. Understand, brothers and sisters, that my gospel was not devised by human beings. No person handed it on to me or taught it to me; Jesus Christ made it known through revelation.

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 10:25-37 | Luke 10:25-37**

一位法学士起来试探耶稣，问怎样才能承受永生。耶稣请他说明法律的教导。他答：要用全部心灵、力量和思想爱上主、自己的天主，也要爱近人如同自己。耶稣肯定他的回答，嘱咐他实践，便能获得生命。那人想证明自己有理，又问谁算是自己的近人。

耶稣讲道：一位旅人从耶路撒冷往耶里哥去，遭强盗抢劫、剥衣、殴打，被丢下奄奄一息。路过的司祭看见了，绕到另一边走开；肋未人也一样。但一位撒玛黎雅旅人心生怜悯，上前用油和酒处理伤处，加以包扎，让他骑上自己的牲口，送到客店照料。次日，他给店主两个银币，托付继续看护，并答应归来时偿还额外费用。

耶稣问，三人中谁成了遇劫者的近人。法学士答，是向他施怜悯的人。耶稣说：“你也照样去做吧！”

A legal expert tested Jesus by asking how to inherit eternal life. Jesus asked him to explain the Law. He answered: love God with your entire heart, soul, strength, and mind, and love your neighbor as yourself. Jesus affirmed this answer and promised life through practicing it. Seeking to justify himself, the man asked who counted as his neighbor.

Jesus described a traveler descending from Jerusalem to Jericho. Robbers stripped and battered him, abandoning him near death. A passing priest saw him but kept to the opposite side; a Levite did the same. A Samaritan traveler, however, felt compassion. He treated and bandaged the injuries with oil and wine, placed the man on his own animal, and took him to an inn for care. Next morning he gave the innkeeper two denarii, requesting continued care and promising reimbursement for further expenses upon returning.

Jesus asked which traveler became the victim's neighbor. The expert identified the one who acted mercifully. Jesus told him, “Go and do likewise.”

## 反省 | Reflection

法学士熟悉法律，却没有把握其指向天主圣言的精神。若总从自己出发，追问谁才算近人，就容易用种族、肤色、信仰或文化划定爱的边界。耶稣邀请我们转换问题：自己愿意成为谁的近人？我们是否愿意向每个相遇的人伸出仁爱的手？祂降来时，便亲自成为相遇者的近人，活出了善心撒玛黎雅人的慈悲。

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿（忠实转述）

The legal expert knows the Law but misses its purpose of preparing the way for God's living Word. Beginning with ourselves and deciding who qualifies as a neighbor can make race, color, religion, or culture boundaries to love. Jesus invites a different question: whose neighbor are we willing to become? Will we meet others with charity? In coming among us, he became a neighbor to those he encountered, embodying the Samaritan's compassion.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau (faithful paraphrase)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

良十四世教宗指出，比喻中崎岖的道路像人的生命旅程。面对相遇者的脆弱，我们可以选择照料，也可以装作没有看见。司祭与肋未人事奉圣殿，却没有停下；参加敬礼并不自动使人慈悲，因为怜悯首先是我们作为人的召叫。

撒玛黎雅人虽属于受轻视的民族，却因为看见另一个人需要援助而停步。当我们认出路边的伤者也代表自己，并记起耶稣曾多次停下来照顾我们，就更能中断自己的行程，以怜悯回应他人的需要。

（教宗良十四世，2025年5月28日公开接见；忠实转述）

Pope Leo XIV compares the parable's rough road to our journey through life. Encounters with another person's vulnerability reveal our choices: offer care or ignore the need. The priest and Levite serve in the Temple but fail to stop. Participation in worship does not by itself produce compassion; mercy first belongs to our calling as human beings.

The Samaritan belongs to a despised community, yet stops simply because another person needs help. We become more ready to interrupt our journey when we recognize ourselves in the wounded traveler. Remembering how often Jesus has stopped to care for us can deepen our compassion toward others.

(Pope Leo XIV, General Audience, 28 May 2025; faithful paraphrase)
