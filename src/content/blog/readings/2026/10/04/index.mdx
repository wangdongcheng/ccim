常年期第二十七主日 | Twenty-seventh Sunday in Ordinary Time

2026年10月4日 · 星期日 | October 4, 2026 · Sunday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**依撒意亚先知书 5:1-7 | Isaiah 5:1-7**

我要为所爱的朋友唱一首葡萄园之歌。他的园子在肥沃的山冈上；他松土清石，种下上好的葡萄树，建起瞭望台，凿好榨酒池。他期待丰美的果实，收获的却是野葡萄。

耶路撒冷的居民和犹大人，请来评断园主与葡萄园之间的事：他已经尽心照料，还有什么该做而没做？为何期待好果实，园子却只结野葡萄？

现在园主要说出自己的决定：撤掉篱笆，让园子遭吞噬；拆去围墙，任它被践踏。他要让它荒芜，不再修剪耕作，荆棘蒺藜任意生长，连云彩也不再给它降雨。

这园子代表万军上主的以色列家，犹大人就是他喜爱的栽种。他寻找公义，眼前却是流血；他期待正直，耳中却满是受苦者的呼号。

I will sing a vineyard song for my beloved friend. On a rich hillside he prepared the soil, removed stones, planted fine vines, erected a lookout tower, and cut a wine vat. He hoped for good fruit, yet gathered only wild grapes.

People of Jerusalem and Judah, decide between the owner and his vineyard. What care has he withheld? Why has such thorough tending produced worthless fruit?

The owner announces his decision: he will strip away the hedge and expose the vineyard to destruction, demolish its wall and leave it to trampling. Untended and uncultivated, it will become a wilderness of thorns. Even the clouds will be forbidden to water it.

The vineyard represents Israel, belonging to the Lord of hosts; Judah is the planting he cherishes. He sought justice but encountered bloodshed; he expected righteousness but heard the cries of those wronged.

## 读经二 | Second Reading

**斐理伯人书 4:6-9 | Philippians 4:6-9**

弟兄们，不要让忧虑支配你们；无论遇到什么，都怀着感恩向天主祈祷，把自己的需要交托给他。他赐下的平安超越人的理解，会在基督耶稣内守护你们的心灵与思想。

凡是真诚、庄重、公正、纯洁、可爱、值得敬重的事，一切美德和堪受赞扬的事，都应成为你们默想的对象。你们从我学到、领受、听见和看见的一切，要付诸行动；那赐平安的天主就必与你们同在。

Brothers and sisters, do not surrender to anxiety. Whatever the situation, bring your needs before God through thankful prayer and earnest petition. His peace, beyond human comprehension, will protect your inner life and thoughts in Christ Jesus.

Give your attention to truth, dignity, justice, purity, loveliness, and everything deserving respect. Ponder all that is excellent and praiseworthy. Put into practice what you learned and received from me, what you heard me teach and saw me do. The God of peace will be with you.

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 21:33-43 | Matthew 21:33-43**

耶稣向司祭长和民间长老讲述另一个比喻：一位家主栽好葡萄园，围起篱笆，掘出榨酒池，筑起瞭望台，然后租给佃户，自己远行。

收获将近，他派仆人去取应得的果实；佃户却殴打一个，杀害另一个，又用石头打死一个。他再派更多仆人，仍遭同样的待遇。最后，他派自己的儿子，心想他们会敬重他。佃户却见他是继承人，商议杀掉他，好夺取产业；他们将他拖到园外杀害了。

耶稣问园主回来会怎样处置这些人。他们回答，他会严惩这些恶人，使他们惨死，再把园子租给按季交付收成的人。

耶稣接着问，他们是否读过圣经：被建筑者舍弃的石头，竟成为屋角的基石；这是上主所成就的，在我们眼中奇妙可惊。因此，天主的国将从他们那里收回，交给能结出天国果实的民族。

Jesus told the chief priests and elders another parable. An owner established a vineyard with a hedge, a wine press, and a tower, entrusted it to tenants, and travelled abroad.

Near harvest he sent servants for his share. The tenants assaulted one, murdered another, and stoned a third. Further servants met the same fate. Finally he sent his son, expecting respect. Recognizing the heir, the tenants plotted to kill him and seize his inheritance. They dragged him outside and murdered him.

Jesus asked what the returning owner would do. His listeners answered that he would bring those wicked men to a terrible death and appoint tenants who delivered the harvest when due.

Jesus recalled Scripture: the stone discarded by builders became the cornerstone, an astonishing work of the Lord. He declared that God's kingdom would be removed from them and entrusted to a people bearing its fruit.

## 反省 | Reflection

这比喻流露出耶稣面对死亡的悲痛：祂已来到耶路撒冷，敌意日增。佃户占据园主的产业，儿子的到来反而给了他们夺取继承权的机会。天主已经赐下法律、不断派遣先知，最后仍把爱子交付给人；佃户却忘记主人，只剩贪欲。这使我们反问自己：还有愿意忠实回应园主的佃户吗？

—— 李建平神父；乐仁出版社供稿（忠实转述）

The parable conveys Jesus' sorrow as death approaches: he has reached Jerusalem, where hostility is growing. Tenants who have appropriated the vineyard see the son's arrival as an opportunity to seize his inheritance. God has given the Law and repeatedly sent prophets; finally he entrusts his beloved Son to humanity. Yet the tenants have forgotten their master and yielded to greed. We are left to ask whether tenants still exist who will faithfully respond to the owner.

— Fr. Li Jianping; provided by Claretian Publications Macau (faithful paraphrase)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

方济各教宗借葡萄园佃户的悲剧提醒我们：人若误以为一切都靠自己，便忘了生命的根基是天主白白赐予的恩宠。不再感恩，人就难以怀着蒙爱、获救的喜乐面对自己的处境与限度，反而陷入不需要爱与救恩的错觉。

不愿接受爱的人，会被贪欲和胜过他人的渴望束缚。不满、埋怨、误解与嫉妒由此而生，怨恨又可能带人走向暴力。忘恩会破坏和平，而一句真诚的感谢，却能帮助我们重获和平。

（教宗方济各，2023年10月8日三钟经；忠实转述）

Pope Francis draws on the tragedy of the vineyard tenants to warn against imagining that we owe everything to ourselves. Such thinking obscures life's foundation: the good we receive is God's freely given grace. Forgetting gratitude, we face our circumstances and limitations without the joy of being loved and saved, imagining instead that we need neither love nor salvation.

Refusing to receive love leaves us captive to greed and the desire to surpass others. Discontent, complaints, misunderstandings, and envy grow from this attitude; resentment can then lead to violence. Ingratitude destroys peace, while a sincere expression of thanks can help restore it.

(Pope Francis, Angelus, 8 October 2023; faithful paraphrase)
