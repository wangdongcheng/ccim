## 读经一 | First Reading

**迦拉达人书 1:13-24 | Galatians 1:13-24**

弟兄们，你们知道我从前奉行犹太教时的生活：我曾猛烈迫害天主的教会，设法毁灭她。在本族同龄的人中，我在犹太教方面更为突出，对祖先传下的传统格外热心。

然而，天主在我出生以前就选定了我，又以恩宠召叫我。他愿意把自己的圣子启示给我，好使我向外邦人宣讲他。我没有立即向任何人请教，也没有去耶路撒冷拜访那些先于我成为宗徒的人；我去了阿拉伯，随后回到大马士革。

三年以后，我才到耶路撒冷认识刻法，在他那里住了十五天。其他宗徒，我只见到主的兄弟雅各伯。我在天主面前郑重保证：写给你们的这些事，没有虚假。

后来，我去了叙利亚和基里基雅。犹太境内属于基督的各教会，当时还不认识我本人。他们只是听说，从前迫害他们的人，如今竟宣讲起他曾企图摧毁的信仰。因此，他们为了我所发生的转变而赞颂天主。

Brothers and sisters, you know how I once lived as a follower of Judaism: I fiercely persecuted God's Church and sought to destroy it. I advanced beyond many Jews of my own age, with an exceptional zeal for the traditions handed down by my ancestors.

Yet God had set me apart before I was born and called me by his grace. He chose to reveal his Son in me so that I might proclaim him among the Gentiles. I did not immediately seek anyone's advice or visit Jerusalem to meet those who had become apostles before me. Instead, I went to Arabia and afterwards returned to Damascus.

Three years later, I went to Jerusalem to become acquainted with Cephas and stayed with him for fifteen days. Of the other apostles, I saw only James, the Lord's brother. Before God, I solemnly affirm that what I am writing is true.

I then travelled to Syria and Cilicia. The churches of Christ in Judea still did not know me personally. They had only heard that their former persecutor was now proclaiming the faith he had once tried to eradicate. Because of what had happened to me, they praised God.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 139:1-3,13-15 | Psalm 139:1-3,13-15**

**答：天主，求你引导我，走向永生的道路。 | R. God, guide me along the everlasting way.**

上主，你察看了我，彻底认识我；
无论我坐下或起身，你都知道，
即使相隔遥远，你也明白我的思想。
我行路或休息，都在你的察看之中，
你熟知我生活的每一条道路。

**答：天主，求你引导我，走向永生的道路。**

是你形成了我的五内，
在母胎中把我编织成形。
我为你奇妙而令人敬畏的创造感谢你；
你的作为如此美妙，
我的心深深知道。

**答：天主，求你引导我，走向永生的道路。**

我在隐秘中被塑造，
仿佛在大地深处被编织时，
我的骨骸并没有向你隐藏。

**答：天主，求你引导我，走向永生的道路。**

Lord, you have searched me and know me completely.
You know when I sit and when I rise;
even from afar, you understand my thoughts.
You examine my journeys and my resting places;
every path of my life is familiar to you.

**R. God, guide me along the everlasting way.**

You formed my inward being
and wove me together in my mother's womb.
I thank you for creating me with such awe-inspiring wonder.
Your works are marvellous,
and I know this deep within me.

**R. God, guide me along the everlasting way.**

When I was being formed in secret,
woven together as in the depths of the earth,
my frame was never hidden from you.

**R. God, guide me along the everlasting way.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 10:38-42 | Luke 10:38-42**

耶稣与门徒继续行路，来到一个村庄。一位名叫玛尔大的妇人邀请他到自己家中。她的妹妹玛利亚坐在主脚旁，专心听他讲话。

玛尔大被许多招待的工作牵住了心神，就走来向主诉说：妹妹留下她独自服侍，难道主不在意吗？她请求主吩咐妹妹来帮忙。

主亲切地两次呼唤玛尔大的名字，指出她为许多事情忧虑不安，而真正必需的只有一件。玛利亚已经选了更好的一分，这一分不会被夺走。

As Jesus and his disciples continued their journey, he entered a village. A woman named Martha invited him into her home. Her sister Mary sat at the Lord's feet, attentively listening to his teaching.

Martha was distracted by the many tasks of serving. She approached the Lord and complained that her sister had left her to do everything alone. Did he not care? She asked him to tell Mary to help her.

The Lord addressed Martha twice by name and told her that she was troubled and anxious about many things, although only one was necessary. Mary had chosen the better portion, and it would not be taken away from her.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父反省摘要（[万有真原，乐仁出版社供稿](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-06)）：玛尔大热诚服侍，让客人感到家的温暖；虽然忙碌使她焦躁，耶稣仍以朋友的坦诚待她。玛利亚则安静聆听。教会同时需要殷勤接待的人与专心听主的人。

Summary of Fr Li Jianping's reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau through WanYou ZhenYuan: Martha's generous service makes guests feel at home. Though her busyness sometimes leaves her irritable, Jesus treats her with the frankness of a friend. Mary listens quietly. The Church needs both those who offer attentive hospitality and those who listen closely to the Lord.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世，2025年7月20日三钟经讲话摘要（[梵蒂冈新闻网](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/06.html)）：接待别人和接受接待都需要谦逊与开放。玛尔大的慷慨必须为与耶稣相处留出空间；玛利亚认出了最重要的事。我们也要休息，学习迎接别人并接受他们的款待。与天主、他人及大自然的相遇是白白的恩赐；我们不仅能给予，也有许多可以领受。

Summary of Pope Leo XIV's Angelus address of 20 July 2025: Both offering and receiving hospitality require humility and openness. Martha's generosity must leave room for time with Jesus; Mary recognizes what matters most. We too need rest and must learn to welcome others and accept their welcome. Encounters with God, other people and nature are freely given. We have much to receive as well as to give.
