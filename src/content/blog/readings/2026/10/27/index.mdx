## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 5:21-33 | Ephesians 5:21-33**

弟兄们：出于对基督的敬畏，你们应彼此顺服。妻子应顺服自己的丈夫，如同顺服主。丈夫是妻子的头，犹如基督是教会的头，也是这身体的救主。教会怎样顺服基督，妻子也应这样在各事上顺服丈夫。

丈夫要爱妻子，效法基督对教会的爱：他为教会舍身，以水和圣言洗净她，使她成圣，好将教会带到自己面前，光耀、圣洁，毫无污点、皱纹或任何缺陷。丈夫同样该爱妻子，如同爱自己的身体；爱妻子，就是爱自己。人从不憎恨自身，反而养育、珍惜自己的身体，正如基督照顾教会，因为我们是他身体的肢体。

为此，人要离开父母，与妻子结合，两人成为一个身体。这是极深的奥秘，我所说的是基督与教会。总之，每个丈夫都该爱妻子如同自己，妻子也该尊敬丈夫。

Brothers and sisters, let reverence for Christ move you to mutual submission. Wives should submit to their husbands as they do to the Lord. The husband stands as his wife's head, just as Christ heads the Church, his body, which he saves. As the Church submits to Christ, wives should likewise submit to their husbands in everything.

Husbands must love their wives with the self-giving love Christ showed the Church. He surrendered himself to make her holy, cleansing her with water and the word, and presenting her radiant, holy and faultless, free of stains or wrinkles. Husbands should similarly cherish their wives as their own bodies. Loving one's wife means loving oneself. People nourish and care for their bodies rather than hate them; Christ cares for the Church likewise, since we belong to his body.

Thus a man leaves his parents, unites with his wife, and they become one body. This profound mystery concerns Christ and the Church. Each husband, then, must love his wife as himself, and each wife respect her husband.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 128:1-5 | Psalm 128:1-5**

**答：敬畏上主的人真是有福。 | R. Happy are those who revere the Lord.**

敬畏上主、遵循他道路的人有福了。你要享用亲手辛劳所得，生活幸福，万事顺遂。

**答：敬畏上主的人真是有福。**

你的妻子在家中如丰产的葡萄藤；孩子围坐在你的桌旁，好像生机盎然的橄榄幼苗。

**答：敬畏上主的人真是有福。**

看，敬畏上主的人将这样蒙福。愿上主从熙雍赐福于你，使你一生看见耶路撒冷的兴盛。

**答：敬畏上主的人真是有福。**

Happy are people who revere the Lord and follow his paths. You will enjoy what your hands have earned, finding happiness and well-being.

**R. Happy are those who revere the Lord.**

Your wife within your home resembles a vine heavy with fruit; your children gathered at the table are like young olive plants.

**R. Happy are those who revere the Lord.**

This is the blessing awaiting those who revere the Lord. May he send you blessing from Zion, and may you witness Jerusalem flourishing throughout your life.

**R. Happy are those who revere the Lord.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 13:18-21 | Luke 13:18-21**

那时候，耶稣问：天主的国像什么？我可用什么来作比喻呢？它像一粒芥菜种，有人把它种进自己的园子；它渐渐长成树木，空中的鸟儿就在枝间安家。

他又说：我还可用什么比喻天主的国呢？它像酵母，妇人把它拌入三斗面粉，直至整团面都发起来。

Jesus asked what might illustrate God's kingdom. He compared it to a mustard seed planted in someone's garden. It grew into a tree, and birds found a home among its branches.

He offered another comparison: a woman worked leaven into three measures of flour, and its action spread until the entire dough had risen.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父提醒我们，天国并非遥不可及。种子生长、枝头栖鸟、面团发酵，这些平常景象帮助我们认出天主安静而真实的行动。愿意留心观察的人，也能发现天国正在自己的生命中生长。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-27)摘要，原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

Father Li Jianping explains that the kingdom is close to ordinary life. Growing seeds, nesting birds and rising dough reveal how quietly and truly God acts among us. Attentive openness helps us recognize the kingdom growing within our own lives. (Summary of the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世指出，信德使生活成为天主救恩的工具，其力量谦逊而不强迫人。即使信德微小，也承载天主的爱。当我们以福音的怜悯照顾受苦者，在日常言行中服务他人、不追求私利，救恩便如种子渐渐生长。（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/27.html)的2025年10月5日讲道摘要。）

Pope Leo XIV describes faith as a humble power that makes our lives instruments of God's saving work. Even small faith carries divine love. Salvation grows quietly when we compassionately care for suffering people and serve others in ordinary actions, seeking their good rather than our advantage. (Summary of the homily of 5 October 2025, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
