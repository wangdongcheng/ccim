## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 4:32—5:8 | Ephesians 4:32–5:8**

弟兄们：要以善意和怜悯彼此相待，互相宽恕，正如天主已在基督内宽恕你们。你们既是天主钟爱的儿女，就该效法他。你们要实践爱德，像基督爱我们，为我们交出自己，成为献给天主的馨香祭品。

淫乱、不洁和贪婪，在圣徒中连提说也不合宜；污秽的言语、愚妄的谈论和轻佻的笑话，都该让位给感恩。要知道，淫乱、不洁或贪婪的人，不能承受基督与天主国度的产业；贪婪的人所崇拜的正是偶像。

不要被空洞的话欺骗：这些罪使天主的义怒临到悖逆的人。因此，不要参与他们的行为。从前你们属于黑暗，如今却在主内成为光明，生活也该显出光明之子的样子。

Brothers and sisters, treat each other with kindness and compassion. Forgive as God has forgiven you through Christ. As God's dearly loved children, follow his example. Let love guide your lives, following Christ, who surrendered himself for us as a sweet-smelling sacrifice to God.

Sexual wrongdoing, uncleanness and greed have no place even in the conversation of God's holy people. Replace obscene speech, foolish chatter and coarse jokes with thanksgiving. Understand that those given to sexual sin, impurity or greed have no share in the kingdom belonging to Christ and God; greed amounts to idol worship.

Do not be misled by hollow arguments. Such conduct brings God's anger upon the disobedient, so take no part in it with them. You formerly belonged to darkness; through the Lord you now belong to light. Live accordingly, as his children of light.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 1:1-4,6 | Psalm 1:1-4,6**

**答：全心依靠上主的人真是有福。 | R. Happy are those who place their trust in the Lord.**

不听从恶人的主张，不走罪人的道路，不与讥诮者同坐，这样的人有福了。他以遵守上主的法律为乐，日夜细想上主的训诲。

**答：全心依靠上主的人真是有福。**

他如栽在流水旁的树，到了季节便结出果实，叶子不致枯萎；他所从事的，都得到成全。

**答：全心依靠上主的人真是有福。**

恶人却像随风飘散的糠屑。上主看顾义人所走的路，恶人的道路却通向毁灭。

**答：全心依靠上主的人真是有福。**

Happy is the person who rejects wicked advice, avoids the path of sinners and refuses the company of mockers. Such a person delights in God's law and reflects on it night and day.

**R. Happy are those who place their trust in the Lord.**

That person resembles a tree beside flowing water, bearing fruit at the proper season, its foliage never fading; its work flourishes.

**R. Happy are those who place their trust in the Lord.**

The wicked instead resemble husks scattered by wind. The Lord watches over the righteous on their journey, while the wicked follow a road to ruin.

**R. Happy are those who place their trust in the Lord.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 13:10-17 | Luke 13:10-17**

一个安息日，耶稣正在会堂教导。有位妇人受病魔折磨已有十八年，腰背弯曲，无法挺直。耶稣看见她，就叫她前来，告诉她已从病患中获得释放，并把手按在她身上。她立即挺直身体，赞颂天主。

会堂长因耶稣在安息日治病而恼怒，向群众说：工作有六天，你们该在那些日子来求医，不该在安息日来。

主回答：你们这些假善人！难道你们在安息日，不也解开槽边的牛或驴，牵去喝水吗？这妇人是亚巴郎的女儿，被撒殚捆绑十八年，难道不该在安息日解除她的束缚吗？他说了这些话，反对他的人都羞愧了；群众则为他所行的一切奇事欢欣。

On a sabbath Jesus was teaching in a synagogue. A woman there had suffered under an infirmity-causing spirit for eighteen years; she remained bent and could not stand upright. Seeing her, Jesus called her forward and announced her release from illness. He placed his hands on her, and she immediately straightened up, praising God.

Angry that Jesus had healed on the sabbath, the synagogue leader told the crowd to seek healing during the six working days instead.

The Lord challenged their hypocrisy: even on the sabbath they untied their cattle or donkeys and took them to drink. Surely this woman, Abraham's daughter, deserved release on that day from the bondage Satan had imposed for eighteen years. His opponents were ashamed, while the crowd celebrated all the wonderful things he was doing.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父指出，安息日原是天主赐人自由与休息的日子。耶稣医治妇人，正实现了这份恩赐。会堂长能接受牲畜在安息日得到照顾，却反对人获得释放；他的愤怒反使自己陷入束缚。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-26)摘要，原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

Father Li Jianping presents the sabbath as God's gift of freedom and rest. Jesus fulfills its purpose by healing the woman. The synagogue leader accepts care for animals that day but opposes a person's liberation. His anger becomes a bondage of his own. (Summary of the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

教宗良十四世以弯腰的妇人说明，人可能被自我中心限制。生命中许多重要的恩惠来自他人的教导、相遇和经历；接纳这些恩惠能拓宽眼光。与基督相遇，更使人走出封闭，重新挺立，怀着希望看待生命。（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/26.html)的2025年10月27日讲道摘要。）

Pope Leo XIV sees the bent woman as an image of confinement within ourselves. Essential gifts reach us through other people, learning and experience. Receiving them widens our vision. Encountering Christ frees us from inward confinement, helping us stand upright again and face life with hope. (Summary of the homily of 27 October 2025, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
