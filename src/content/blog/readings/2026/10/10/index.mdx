2026年10月10日 · 星期六 | October 10, 2026 · Saturday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**迦拉达人书 3:22-29 | Galatians 3:22-29**

弟兄们：圣经把万有都归在罪的拘禁之下，为使那因信耶稣基督而来的恩许，赐给有信德的人。

信德尚未来到时，我们被法律看守着，等候将要显明的信德。因此，法律曾作我们的监护者，引我们走向基督，使我们因信德获得成义。如今信德已经来到，我们便不再受这监护者管辖。

你们因信德，在基督耶稣内都是天主的子女。凡受洗归入基督的，都已披戴基督。在他内，犹太人与希腊人、奴隶与自由人、男人与女人之间的区别，不再使你们彼此分离：你们在基督耶稣内同属一体。既然属于基督，你们就是亚巴郎的子孙，也按天主的恩许承受产业。

Brothers and sisters, Scripture places everything under the captivity of sin, so that the promise received through faith in Jesus Christ may reach those who believe.

Before faith arrived, the law held us in custody while we awaited its revelation. The law therefore guarded and guided us until Christ, so that faith might bring us justification. With faith now present, that guardianship has ended.

Through faith, you are God's children in Christ Jesus. Baptism into Christ means being clothed with him. Jewish or Greek origin, slavery or freedom, and being male or female no longer divide your unity in Christ Jesus. Belonging to Christ makes you Abraham's descendants and inheritors of the promise.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 104(105):2-7 | Psalm 105:2-7**

**答：上主永远纪念他的盟约。 | R. The Lord remembers his covenant for ever.**

向上主献唱，颂扬他的一切奇妙作为；
以他的圣名为光荣，
让寻求他的人心中充满欢乐。

**答：上主永远纪念他的盟约。**

寻求上主，依靠他的力量，
不断寻觅他的面容；
记住他所行的奇迹，
以及他口中发出的裁断。

**答：上主永远纪念他的盟约。**

他的仆人亚巴郎的后裔，
他拣选的雅各伯的子孙，
上主是我们的天主，
他的审判遍及整个大地。

**答：上主永远纪念他的盟约。**

Offer songs of praise to the Lord;
recount his marvellous deeds.
Take pride in his holy name;
may those seeking him rejoice in their hearts.

**R. The Lord remembers his covenant for ever.**

Turn to the Lord and his power;
continue seeking his face.
Keep his wondrous deeds in mind,
his miracles and the judgments he has spoken.

**R. The Lord remembers his covenant for ever.**

You descendants of Abraham, his servant,
you children of Jacob, whom he chose:
the Lord is our God,
and his judgments reach the whole earth.

**R. The Lord remembers his covenant for ever.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 11:27-28 | Luke 11:27-28**

耶稣讲话时，群众中一位妇女提高声音，称赞那位怀孕生下他、又以乳汁哺育他的母亲真有福气。

耶稣却指出：真正更有福气的，是聆听天主的圣言并照着实行的人。

As Jesus spoke, a woman in the crowd called out, praising the blessedness of the mother who had carried him and nursed him.

Jesus answered that the greater blessing belongs to people who listen to God's word and live according to it.

## 反省 | Reflection

玛利亚的福气来自她信赖天主、接受圣神引导，并以整个生命承行主旨。她作为母亲的尊荣，离不开聆听和实行圣言的信德。天主公正地向所有人敞开这条道路：凡像她一样慷慨、真诚地回应圣言的人，也能让圣言在自己的生命中结果，分享这份福气。（[李建平神父，乐仁出版社供稿；意译摘要](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-10)）

Mary's blessedness arises from trusting God, welcoming the Spirit's guidance, and giving her life to God's will. Her honour as Jesus' mother is inseparable from a faith that listens and acts. God's justice opens this path to everyone: by responding generously and sincerely as Mary did, we too can bring God's word to fruition in our lives and share her blessedness. (Fr Li Jianping, Claretian Publications Macau; translated summary.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

方济各教宗指出，跟随耶稣的生活以聆听圣言和实行圣言为中心。读福音时，应以开放的心询问天主正在向自己说什么，而非挑剔话语的漏洞。福音也是赐给每一个人的。上主不断播撒圣言，邀请我们敞开心扉，并愿意行动；我们求他引导，使我们学会实践。（[方济各教宗，2014年9月23日圣玛尔大之家讲道；意译摘要](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/10.html)）

Pope Francis presents discipleship as listening to God's word and acting upon it. Reading the Gospel calls for an open heart that asks what God is saying personally, rather than looking for faults. Its message addresses each person. The Lord keeps sowing his word and invites receptive hearts and a willingness to act. We ask his guidance to learn how to live it. (Pope Francis, Santa Marta, 23 September 2014; summary.)
