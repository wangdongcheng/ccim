2026年10月29日 · 星期四 | October 29, 2026 · Thursday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**厄弗所人书 6:10-20 | Ephesians 6:10-20**

弟兄们：最后，你们要在主内，倚靠他强大的德能，获得力量。把天主所赐的整副装备穿戴齐全，才能抵御魔鬼的诡计。我们争战的对象，并非有血有肉的人，而是率领者、掌权者、支配这黑暗世界的势力，以及天界的邪恶神灵。因此，要拿起天主的全副装备，好在邪恶来临之日抵挡得住，尽了一切努力之后，仍能坚定站立。

所以，要站稳脚步：让真理成为束腰的带子，以正义护卫胸膛，以准备传扬和平福音的心志装备双脚。还要持守信德，如同举起盾牌，熄灭恶者射来的一切燃烧的箭；以救恩作头盔，以天主的圣言作圣神的利剑。

在任何时刻，都要在圣神内，以各样祷告和恳求向天主祈祷；为此要持续警醒，不懈地为所有圣徒代祷。也请为我祈求，使我开口时获得合宜的言语，勇敢揭示福音的奥秘。我为这福音作使者，如今身负锁链；愿我仍能勇敢宣讲，尽我应尽的本分。

Brothers and sisters, draw strength from your union with the Lord and his mighty power. Wear every piece of the equipment God provides, so that the devil's schemes cannot overthrow you. Our conflict is with no human opponent, but with rulers, authorities, the powers governing this world's darkness, and evil spiritual forces in the heavenly realm. Take up all God's equipment, then, to resist when evil strikes and remain standing after doing everything required.

Stand ready: fasten truth around your waist, protect your chest with righteousness, and prepare your feet for the good news of peace. Carry faith like a shield to extinguish every burning arrow the evil one sends. Let salvation protect your head, and take the Spirit's blade, which is God's message.

In every circumstance, offer every kind of prayer and petition in the Spirit. Remain awake to this task, persevering in intercession for all God's holy people. Pray for me as well, that words may be given me when I speak, enabling me to reveal the Gospel's mystery without fear. I represent that Gospel even in chains; ask that I may proclaim it courageously, fulfilling my duty to speak.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**圣咏 144:1-2,9-10 | Psalm 144:1-2,9-10**

**答：愿上主、我的磐石，受赞美。 | R. Let us praise the Lord, our rock.**

上主是我的磐石，我要赞美他；
他教导我的双手迎战，
训练我的手指应付争斗。

**答：愿上主、我的磐石，受赞美。**

他是我的磐石和堡垒，
是保护我、解救我的坚城；
他如盾牌，我投奔他寻求庇护，
他使众民归服于我。

**答：愿上主、我的磐石，受赞美。**

天主，我要为你唱一首新曲，
以十根弦的琴向你奏响赞歌；
你赐给君王胜利，
救拔你的仆人达味。

**答：愿上主、我的磐石，受赞美。**

I offer praise to the Lord, my rock;
he teaches my hands to fight
and prepares my fingers for battle.

**R. Let us praise the Lord, our rock.**

He is my rock, my fortress,
my secure refuge and rescuer.
He shields me, and I seek safety in him;
he brings the peoples under my rule.

**R. Let us praise the Lord, our rock.**

God, I will offer you a fresh song,
accompanying my praise on a harp of ten strings.
You grant triumph to kings
and bring your servant David to safety.

**R. Let us praise the Lord, our rock.**

## 福音 | Gospel

**路加福音 13:31-35 | Luke 13:31-35**

就在那时，有些法利塞人走近耶稣，劝他离开，因为黑落德打算杀害他。

耶稣回答：“去把我的话带给那只狐狸：今天和明天，我仍要驱逐魔鬼，医治病人，第三天便完成我的路程。然而今天、明天，以及随后的一天，我仍须继续前行，因为先知不会在耶路撒冷之外丧命。

“耶路撒冷啊，耶路撒冷！你杀死先知，又用石头打死派遣到你这里来的使者。我多少次愿意把你的子女聚在一起，像母鸡把雏鸡收在双翼之下，你们却不肯。看，你们的家已被舍弃。我告诉你们，在你们欢呼赞颂那位以上主的名来临者以前，你们不会再看见我。”

At that moment, some Pharisees approached Jesus and urged him to leave, warning that Herod intended to have him killed.

Jesus replied, “Take this answer to that fox: today and tomorrow I continue freeing people from demons and healing the sick; on the third day I bring my work to completion. Yet I must keep travelling today, tomorrow and the following day, since Jerusalem is where a prophet must meet his death.

“Jerusalem, Jerusalem! You murder the prophets and stone the messengers sent to you. Again and again I wished to bring your children together, like a mother hen drawing her chicks beneath her wings, but you refused. Look: your home has been abandoned. You will not see me again, I tell you, before you welcome with blessing the one arriving in the Lord's name.”

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父借狐狸与母鸡的形象，对照黑落德的残暴与耶稣的护佑。耶稣明知前往耶路撒冷意味着受难，仍继续使命，更为拒绝救恩的人民忧伤。他们若拒绝这份庇护，便如离开母鸡的幼雏，暴露在毁灭之中。（据[万有真原当日反省](https://www.wanyouzhenyuan.cn/index.php?m=daily&stype=thought&date=2026-10-29)摘要，原文由乐仁出版社供稿。）

Father Li Jianping contrasts Herod's cruelty with Jesus' protection through the images of fox and hen. Knowing that Jerusalem means suffering, Jesus continues his mission and grieves for those refusing salvation. Rejecting his shelter leaves them like chicks separated from their mother, exposed to destruction. (Translated summary of the day's WanYou ZhenYuan reflection, supplied by Claretian Publications Macau.)

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

方济各教宗强调，魔鬼并非虚构，信徒需要天主所赐的装备，尤其以真理抵抗诱惑。属灵争战虽需克服怠惰和私欲，却使人分享主白白赐下的救恩与喜乐。即使跌倒，也不要灰心，应因主的陪伴而重新振作。（据[梵蒂冈新闻网当日选录](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/word-of-the-day/2026/10/29.html)的2014年10月30日圣玛尔大之家讲道摘要。）

Pope Francis teaches that the devil is real and that believers need God's equipment, particularly truth, to resist temptation. Spiritual struggle means overcoming laziness and disordered desires, yet it brings the joy of the Lord's freely given salvation. Our failures need not discourage us: his presence gives us courage to persevere. (Summary of the Santa Marta homily of 30 October 2014, selected by Vatican News for this day.)
