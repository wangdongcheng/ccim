玫瑰圣母 | Memorial of Our Lady of the Rosary

2026年10月7日 · 星期三 | October 7, 2026 · Wednesday

## 读经一 | First Reading

**宗徒大事录 1:12-14 | Acts 1:12-14**

宗徒们离开橄榄山，返回耶路撒冷；这山在城的近处，距离约为安息日准许行走的路程。他们进城后，来到居住的楼上房间。在那里的是伯多禄、若望、雅各伯、安德肋、斐理伯、多默、巴尔多禄茂、玛窦、阿耳斐的儿子雅各伯、热诚者西满，以及雅各伯的犹达。

他们与几位妇女、耶稣的母亲玛利亚和他的弟兄，在祈祷中保持同一的心志，恒心相聚。

The apostles came back to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives, which lay nearby, within the distance permitted for a sabbath journey. In the city they went upstairs to their lodging. Those present were Peter, John, James, Andrew, Philip, Thomas, Bartholomew, Matthew, James the son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas the son of James.

United in purpose, they persevered in prayer with the women, with Jesus' mother Mary, and with his brothers.

## 答唱咏 | Responsorial Psalm

**路加福音 1:46-55 | Luke 1:46-55**

**答：大能的天主为我行了大事，他的名字是圣的。 | R. The mighty God has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

我的灵魂赞颂上主；我的心因天主、我的救主而喜悦。

**答：大能的天主为我行了大事，他的名字是圣的。**

他看顾了自己卑微的婢女；从今以后，世世代代都要称我有福。他为我成就了大事，他的名是圣的。

**答：大能的天主为我行了大事，他的名字是圣的。**

他的怜悯世代临于敬畏他的人。他伸出强有力的手臂，使内心骄傲的人四散。

**答：大能的天主为我行了大事，他的名字是圣的。**

他使在位的权贵降下，却举起卑微的人；他以美好的恩惠满足饥饿者，使富有者空着手离去。

**答：大能的天主为我行了大事，他的名字是圣的。**

他援助自己的仆人以色列，始终记得施予仁慈，履行了向祖先的许诺：永远眷顾亚巴郎和他的后裔。

**答：大能的天主为我行了大事，他的名字是圣的。**

My whole being praises the Lord; my heart delights in God, who saves me.

**R. The mighty God has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

He has looked kindly on his humble servant. Every generation to come will call me blessed: the Mighty One has acted wonderfully for me, and his name is holy.

**R. The mighty God has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

His compassion reaches those who fear him throughout the generations. With his powerful arm he has dispersed those whose hearts are proud.

**R. The mighty God has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

He has brought rulers down and raised the lowly. He has satisfied the hungry with good gifts and let the wealthy depart with nothing.

**R. The mighty God has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

He has come to the aid of Israel, his servant, remembering his mercy and the promise he made to our ancestors, to Abraham and his descendants for all time.

**R. The mighty God has worked wonders for me; his name is holy.**

## 福音前欢呼 | Alleluia

**路加福音 11:28 | Luke 11:28**

阿肋路亚，阿肋路亚！听了天主的话而遵行的人是有福的。阿肋路亚！

Alleluia, alleluia! Blessed are those who hear God's word and put it into practice. Alleluia!

## 福音 | Gospel

**玛窦福音 12:46-50 | Matthew 12:46-50**

耶稣还在向群众讲话时，他的母亲和弟兄来到外面，希望与他谈话。有人把他们在外等候、想见他的事告诉了他。

耶稣问那传话的人，谁是他的母亲，谁是他的弟兄。随后，他向自己的门徒伸出手，指出他们就是他的母亲和弟兄；凡实行他天上父亲旨意的人，都与他亲如弟兄、姊妹和母亲。

As Jesus continued addressing the crowd, his mother and brothers waited outside, wanting to speak with him. Their request was brought to him.

Jesus asked the messenger who his mother and brothers were. Then he extended his hand toward the disciples and identified them as his family: everyone who carries out the will of his heavenly Father belongs to him as brother, sister, and mother.

## 反省 | Reflection

李建平神父的反省摘要：门徒渴望进入耶稣祈祷的奥秘。天主经让我们认识他所关心的事和他的使命，也引导教会的生活。以这篇祷文祈求，就是与基督同心，向往天国、寻求恩宠，并在生活中参与他的工作。

Summary of the reflection by Fr Li Jianping: The disciples long to share in the mystery of Jesus' prayer. The Our Father reveals his concerns and mission and guides the Church's life. Praying it unites believers with Christ in their longing for God's kingdom, their search for grace, and their participation in his work.

## 教宗的话 | The words of the Popes

圣若望保禄二世的话摘要：天主赐予的恩宠使人有勇气回应召叫。玛利亚的信靠也能帮助我们面对生活，以及婚姻、母职或司铎职务。效法她，忠诚地答应天主，会为他人带来喜乐、生命与祝福，使家庭、堂区和社会的生活更有人性。（1982年3月25日，向宗座罗马大修院致辞）

Summary of Saint John Paul II's words: God's grace gives people courage to answer their vocation. Mary's trust can sustain us in life, marriage, motherhood, and priesthood. Following her example, our faithful consent to God brings others joy, life, and blessing, helping families, parishes, and society grow more humane. (Address to the Pontifical Roman Major Seminary, 25 March 1982)
