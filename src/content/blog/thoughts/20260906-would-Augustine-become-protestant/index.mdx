> 如果圣奥斯定活在宗教改革时代，他会加入新教吗？

> If Saint Augustine Had Lived During the Reformation, Would He Have Become Protestant?

## 一、问题：一个属于古代公教会的圣人，会不会在十六世纪选择宗教改革？

这是一个很有意思的反事实历史问题：

**如果圣奥斯定不是生活在四、五世纪，而是生活在马丁·路德所处的十六世纪，当他亲眼看到当时西方教会中的腐败、赎罪券争议、关于功德与恩宠的混乱，以及路德所提出的宗教改革主张时，他会不会离开罗马教会，加入新教？**

更具体地说：奥斯定会不会认为，路德对于**原罪、恩宠、人的无力、信德与救恩**的理解比当时某些天主教神学和实践更接近他自己？如果答案是肯定的，那么他会不会进一步接受**唯独圣经、唯独因信、拒绝教宗权威以及与罗马教会分裂**这些宗教改革的核心结果？

我的判断是：

**大概率不会。至少，他大概不会以“离开公教会、加入路德宗”的方式回应宗教改革。**

但是，这并不意味着奥斯定会简单站在十六世纪教会制度的一边反对路德。恰恰相反，他很可能会对路德提出的若干核心问题产生非常强烈的共鸣，并且成为一个极其严厉的天主教改革者。

真正有意思的地方就在这里：

**奥斯定可能非常赞同路德提出的某些“问题”，却不接受路德最后给出的“分裂这个答案”。**

---

**1. The Question: Would a Saint of the Ancient Catholic Church Have Chosen the Reformation?**

This is a fascinating counterfactual question:

**If Saint Augustine had lived not in the fourth and fifth centuries but in the sixteenth century, during the age of Martin Luther, how would he have reacted to the corruption of the Western Church, the controversy over indulgences, confused ideas about merit and grace, and the theological claims of the Reformation? Would he have left communion with Rome and become Protestant?**

More specifically, Augustine might well have thought that Luther's teaching on **original sin, grace, human inability, faith, and salvation** was closer to his own theology than some of the theology and popular religious practices of the late medieval Church. But would that sympathy have led him further—to accept **sola Scriptura, sola fide in its Lutheran formulation, rejection of papal authority, and separation from the Roman Church**?

My judgment is:

**Probably not. At least, he would probably not have responded by leaving the Catholic Church and becoming Lutheran.**

That does not mean Augustine would simply have defended every feature of the sixteenth-century ecclesiastical establishment against Luther. Quite the opposite: he might have sympathized strongly with some of Luther's central criticisms and become an exceptionally severe Catholic reformer.

That is where the thought experiment becomes most interesting:

**Augustine might have agreed with many of Luther's “questions” while rejecting Luther's final “answer” of ecclesial separation.**

---

## 二、为什么路德会让奥斯定感到如此熟悉？——恩宠、原罪与人的无力

首先必须承认：**马丁·路德本身就是深受奥斯定影响的人。**

路德是一位奥斯定会修士。他对于圣保禄、原罪、恩宠、人的意志以及救恩的理解，很大程度上是在奥斯定传统中形成的。

尤其是晚年的奥斯定，在与白拉奇主义的争论中越来越强烈地强调：

人不能先靠自己的力量成为义人，然后再获得天主的恩宠。

不是人先迈出决定性的第一步，天主才来帮助他。

相反，是天主的恩宠首先临到人，唤醒、医治并释放人的意志，使人能够相信、爱天主并行善。

因此，奥斯定会非常赞同这样的基本原则：

> 救恩首先是天主的作为，而不是人的成就。

人的善工不能成为一种让天主“欠我们救恩”的资本。

甚至人的信德本身，也不能简单理解为一个完全独立于恩宠之外、由人自己先产生的功劳。

从这个意义上说，如果奥斯定亲耳听到路德批判一种过度交易化的宗教观——仿佛人的功德、金钱、宗教行为能够通过某种计算方式换取救恩——他很可能不仅不会反对路德的问题意识，甚至会认为：

**这里确实触及了福音的核心。**

奥斯定的一生本来就是在不断反对一种思想：

> 人可以凭自己先把自己变成值得天主拯救的人。

因此，在“恩宠的优先性”这一点上，奥斯定与宗教改革之间确实存在真实而深刻的亲缘关系。

---

**2. Why Would Luther Have Sounded So Familiar to Augustine? Grace, Original Sin, and Human Inability**

It must first be acknowledged that **Martin Luther himself was profoundly influenced by Augustine**.

Luther was an Augustinian friar. His understanding of Saint Paul, original sin, grace, the human will, and salvation developed to a considerable degree within an Augustinian theological tradition.

The later Augustine, especially in his controversies against Pelagianism, increasingly emphasized that human beings cannot first make themselves righteous by their own power and then receive divine grace as a reward.

Human beings do not take the decisive first step and then receive God's assistance.

Rather, God's grace comes first. Grace awakens, heals, and liberates the human will, enabling a person to believe, to love God, and to do good.

Augustine would therefore have strongly affirmed a fundamental principle:

> Salvation is first of all God's work, not man's achievement.

Good works cannot become a kind of spiritual capital by which God becomes indebted to us.

Even faith itself cannot simply be understood as a meritorious act generated independently by the human person before grace.

In this sense, if Augustine had heard Luther attacking an excessively transactional understanding of religion—as though merit, money, and religious acts could be calculated and exchanged for salvation—he might not merely have sympathized with Luther's concern. He might have believed that Luther was touching something central to the Gospel.

Much of Augustine's theological life was devoted to rejecting precisely this idea:

> Human beings cannot first make themselves worthy of being saved by God.

There is therefore a real and profound affinity between Augustine and the Reformation when it comes to the **primacy of grace**.

## 三、奥斯定会支持“唯独因信”吗？

这可能是整个问题中最容易被简单化的一点。

如果直接问：

**“奥斯定是否相信唯独因信称义？”**

最准确的回答不是简单的“是”或“不是”，而是必须先说明这里的“唯独因信”究竟是什么意思。

如果它的意思是：

> 人得救不是因为先有自己的功劳；救恩完全来自天主白白赐予的恩宠，人只能依赖基督，而不能靠自己的行为赚取最初的称义，

那么奥斯定会非常接近这一思想。

甚至可以说，路德主义之所以能够如此大量地引用奥斯定，正是因为奥斯定确实给这种思想提供了非常强的神学资源。

但是如果“唯独因信”进一步被解释为：

> 称义只是天主在外在法律意义上宣布罪人为义人，而人的内在更新、爱德、圣化以及圣事生活与称义本身没有真正内在关系，

那么奥斯定大概就不会同意。

因为在奥斯定那里，恩宠不只是改变天主“如何看待罪人”。

恩宠也真正改变罪人本人。

天主不是仅仅说：

**“我把你当作义人。”**

恩宠同时在人的内部工作：

医治意志，

洁净欲望，

赐下爱德，

使人逐渐真正成为义人。

因此，奥斯定的称义思想具有非常明显的**医治性和转化性**。

救恩不仅是地位改变，也是生命改变。

这也是奥斯定与后来某些非常严格的“纯法庭式称义”表述之间的重要距离。

---

**3. Would Augustine Have Accepted *Sola Fide*?**

This is perhaps the easiest part of the entire question to oversimplify.

If we ask directly:

**“Did Augustine believe in justification by faith alone?”**

the most accurate answer is neither a simple yes nor a simple no. We must first ask what exactly is meant by *sola fide*.

If it means:

> Human beings are not saved because they first possess merit of their own; salvation comes entirely from God's gratuitous grace, and the sinner must rely upon Christ rather than earn initial justification by works,

then Augustine comes very close to this principle.

Indeed, one reason Lutheran and Reformed theologians have appealed so frequently to Augustine is that his theology provides powerful resources for precisely this conviction.

But if *sola fide* is further taken to mean:

> Justification is exclusively an external or forensic declaration in which God regards the sinner as righteous, while interior renewal, charity, sanctification, and sacramental life have no intrinsic connection with justification itself,

then Augustine would probably resist such a formulation.

For Augustine, grace does not merely change the way God “regards” the sinner.

Grace truly changes the sinner.

God does not merely say:

**“I shall count you as righteous.”**

Grace also works within the person:

healing the will,

purifying desire,

giving charity,

and gradually making the person genuinely righteous.

Augustine's understanding of justification therefore has a strongly **healing and transformative** character.

Salvation is not merely a change of status. It is also a transformation of life.

This marks an important distance between Augustine and some later, strictly forensic formulations of justification.

---

## 四、自由意志：奥斯定可能赞同路德的问题，却未必赞同他的全部表达

在自由意志问题上，奥斯定与路德之间同样既接近又有距离。

晚年的奥斯定对堕落之后人的处境极其悲观。

人的意志不是中立的。

罪并不仅仅使人偶尔犯错，而是深入伤害了人的意志，使人无法单靠自己的自然能力重新回到天主那里。

这一点与路德《论意志的束缚》中的基本直觉非常接近。

奥斯定很可能会强烈同意：

> 人不能成为自己的救主。

然而，奥斯定通常不会因此认为“意志已经不存在”。

更准确地说：

人仍然愿意，

仍然选择，

仍然行动，

但是人的意志已经受伤、扭曲，并且受到罪的奴役。

恩宠的工作不是消灭意志，而是**医治意志，使它重新获得真正的自由。**

因此，奥斯定的思想更接近：

> 自由意志不是恩宠的竞争者；真正的自由反而是恩宠的结果。

所以，如果他阅读路德《论意志的束缚》，他可能会非常赞同其中反对“人靠天然能力自我拯救”的部分，却未必接受路德每一种最激烈的修辞和神学表达。

---

**4. Free Will: Augustine Might Have Agreed with Luther's Diagnosis Without Accepting Every Formulation**

On free will, Augustine and Luther are again both close to and distant from one another.

The later Augustine had an extraordinarily pessimistic view of the human condition after the Fall.

The will is not morally neutral.

Sin does not merely cause occasional bad choices. It wounds the will deeply and leaves human beings unable, by their natural power alone, to return to God.

This is very close to the fundamental intuition behind Luther's *The Bondage of the Will*.

Augustine would almost certainly have agreed strongly that:

> Man cannot be his own savior.

Yet Augustine does not normally conclude that the human will has simply ceased to exist.

Rather:

human beings still will,

still choose,

still act,

but the will has been wounded, distorted, and enslaved by sin.

Grace does not destroy the will. Grace **heals the will and makes genuine freedom possible again**.

Augustine's logic is therefore closer to this:

> Free will is not the rival of grace; true freedom is itself the fruit of grace.

If Augustine had read Luther's *The Bondage of the Will*, he might therefore have strongly approved of its attack on human self-salvation while remaining uncomfortable with some of Luther's strongest formulations.

## 五、真正使奥斯定难以成为新教徒的，是他的教会论

如果说恩宠论让奥斯定和路德彼此靠近，那么**教会论很可能就是两者真正分开的地方。**

奥斯定不是一个认为基督信仰可以脱离可见教会而存在的神学家。

他的基督宗教具有强烈的：

**历史性、可见性、共同体性和圣事性。**

主教、圣事、使徒传统、教会共融，都不是附加在“真正信仰”外面的制度包装。

它们属于教会本身。

奥斯定有一句后来极其著名的话：

> 若不是公教会的权威推动我，我甚至不会相信福音。

这句话不能简单解释成 **教会高于圣经**。

它产生于奥斯定与摩尼教争论的特殊背景中，因此不能被当成后世教宗制度的简单证明文本。

但是它至少非常清楚地说明：

**奥斯定并不想象一个个人脱离历史教会，然后仅凭自己手中的圣经重新建立基督宗教。**

对奥斯定来说，《圣经》本身就是在一个历史共同体中被保存、辨认、传承和解释的。

于是，如果把奥斯定放到十六世纪，让他看到：

路德这样解释经文，

慈运理那样解释，

加尔文提出另一套系统，

重洗派又作出完全不同的判断，

他很可能会提出一个非常奥斯定式的问题：

> 那么最终由谁判断？

这未必意味着他会赞成后来天主教关于教宗权威的一切具体发展。

但是他很难接受一种彻底个人化的基督宗教：

**圣经在这里，而我自己就是最终解释者。**

---

**5. The Greatest Obstacle to Augustine Becoming Protestant Would Be His Ecclesiology**

If Augustine's doctrine of grace brings him close to Luther, **his ecclesiology is probably where the deepest separation would emerge**.

Augustine was not a theologian who imagined Christianity existing independently of the visible Church.

His Christianity was profoundly:

**historical, visible, communal, and sacramental.**

Bishops, sacraments, apostolic tradition, and ecclesial communion were not merely institutional additions surrounding the “real faith.”

They belonged to the Church's very nature.

Augustine famously wrote:

> I would not believe the Gospel unless the authority of the Catholic Church moved me.

This statement should not simply be interpreted as meaning that **the Church is above Scripture.**

It arose in a particular anti-Manichaean context and should not be used as a simplistic proof text for later theories of papal authority.

Nevertheless, it clearly shows one thing:

**Augustine did not imagine an isolated individual separating himself from the historical Church and reconstructing Christianity from the Bible alone.**

For Augustine, Scripture itself had been preserved, recognized, transmitted, and interpreted within a historical community.

If Augustine had therefore been transported into the sixteenth century and had seen:

Luther interpreting one way,

Zwingli another,

Calvin constructing another system,

and the Anabaptists reaching still different conclusions,

he would probably have asked a characteristically Augustinian question:

> Who, then, judges?

This does not necessarily mean that Augustine would have approved every later Catholic development concerning papal authority.

But it is very difficult to imagine him embracing a radically individualistic Christianity in which:

**the Bible is here, and I myself am the final interpreter.**

## 六、多纳图派争论可能是理解奥斯定态度的关键

要理解奥斯定为什么大概率不会选择宗教分裂，最重要的历史背景可能不是白拉奇争论，而是他与**多纳图派**几十年的斗争。

多纳图派认为，教会中的严重罪恶和不配神职人员破坏了教会的纯洁性。

奥斯定则不断坚持：

教会在现世本来就是一个混合体。

其中有圣人，也有罪人；

有忠信的主教，也可能有不配的主教；

有麦子，也有莠子。

教会最终的完全净化属于末世，而不是通过一群自认为更纯洁的人离开共同体来实现。

所以如果路德告诉奥斯定：

> 罗马已经腐败，所以真正忠于福音的人必须离开。

奥斯定大概不会首先回答：

> 罗马没有腐败。

他完全可能同意：

> 确实存在严重腐败，而且必须改革。

但是他很可能紧接着追问：

> 腐败为什么能够证明教会已经不再是教会？

这是完全不同的问题。

从奥斯定的教会论看来：

**教会需要改革，并不自动推出教会必须被抛弃。**

更重要的是，在奥斯定那里，**schism——分裂——本身就是严重的神学问题。**

现代人谈宗教改革时，很容易首先问：

> 谁在每一项教义上更正确？

奥斯定却可能同时问：

> 你是否仍然保持基督身体的合一？

对他而言，即使一个团体相信自己更纯洁、更正确，如果实现这种“纯洁”的代价是撕裂教会共融，那么这个代价本身就需要极其严肃地面对。

---

**6. The Donatist Controversy May Be the Key to Augustine's Likely Position**

To understand why Augustine would probably have resisted ecclesial separation, the most important historical background may be not the Pelagian controversy but his decades-long struggle with the **Donatists**.

The Donatists argued, in various ways, that serious corruption and unworthy clergy compromised the purity of the Church.

Augustine repeatedly insisted that the earthly Church is necessarily a mixed body.

It contains saints and sinners;

faithful bishops and potentially unworthy bishops;

wheat and weeds.

The Church's final purification belongs to the eschaton. It is not achieved simply by a group that considers itself purer separating from the wider communion.

If Luther had therefore said to Augustine:

> Rome is corrupt, and those who are faithful to the Gospel must therefore leave,

Augustine probably would not have begun by denying all corruption.

He might well have agreed:

> There is serious corruption, and reform is necessary.

But he would probably have followed immediately with another question:

> Why does corruption prove that the Church has ceased to be the Church?

That is a very different question.

From Augustine's ecclesiological perspective:

**The Church's need for reform does not automatically entail the necessity of abandoning the Church.**

More importantly, for Augustine **schism itself was a grave theological reality**.

Modern readers of the Reformation often begin with the question:

> Which side was more correct on each doctrinal issue?

Augustine might simultaneously have asked:

> Are you still preserving the unity of the Body of Christ?

For him, even if a community considered itself purer or more doctrinally correct, the price of achieving that “purity” through ecclesial rupture would itself require the most serious theological scrutiny.

## 七、圣事：奥斯定很难成为“低教会论”的新教徒

圣事是另一个非常明显的分界点。

奥斯定的基督宗教具有高度圣事性。

尤其是洗礼。

在他反对白拉奇主义时，婴儿洗礼甚至成为原罪教义的重要证据之一：

为什么教会一直给连个人罪行都尚未犯下的婴儿施洗？

因为他们同样需要基督的救恩。

这一点实际上使奥斯定与路德之间保持了相当程度的连续性，因为路德宗同样保留婴儿洗礼，而且坚持洗礼真正赐予恩宠。

但是奥斯定几乎肯定会强烈反对后来某些否认婴儿洗礼的宗教改革传统。

圣体也是如此。

奥斯定确实大量使用“记号”“奥迹”“基督身体”等丰富而复杂的语言，因此后来的不同传统都能够引用他。

但是，把他简单理解为：

> 圣体只是一种心理上的纪念活动。

显然过度简化。

在奥斯定那里，圣体、基督的身体、教会以及信友的共融之间存在极其深刻的圣事联系。

因此，他不太可能接受一种完全去圣事化的基督宗教。

这也是为什么，将奥斯定简单称为“生错时代的新教徒”并不成立。

他的恩宠论可以被宗教改革大量继承；

但是他的基督宗教整体结构依然属于古代大公教会的圣事世界。

---

**7. The Sacraments: Augustine Would Be Difficult to Fit into a Low-Church Protestant Framework**

The sacraments provide another obvious dividing line.

Augustine's Christianity was deeply sacramental.

Baptism is the clearest example.

In his anti-Pelagian writings, infant baptism becomes important evidence for the doctrine of original sin:

Why has the Church always baptized infants who have not yet committed personal sins?

Because they too need the salvation of Christ.

This actually preserves significant continuity between Augustine and Luther, since Lutheranism also retained infant baptism and continued to affirm that baptism truly conveys grace.

But Augustine would almost certainly have rejected later Reformation traditions that denied infant baptism.

The Eucharist presents a similar picture.

Augustine frequently used rich and complex language concerning “sign,” “mystery,” “the Body of Christ,” and ecclesial communion, allowing later theological traditions to appeal to different aspects of his thought.

But reducing Augustine's Eucharistic theology to:

> The Eucharist is merely a psychological act of remembrance

would clearly be inadequate.

For Augustine, the Eucharist, the Body of Christ, the Church, and the communion of believers are sacramentally intertwined.

It is therefore difficult to imagine Augustine embracing a thoroughly de-sacramentalized Christianity.

This is another reason why it is misleading simply to describe Augustine as “a Protestant born in the wrong century.”

His doctrine of grace could be appropriated extensively by the Reformation.

But the overall structure of his Christianity remained firmly rooted in the sacramental world of the ancient catholic Church.

## 八、奥斯定会怎么看教宗？答案可能比双方想象的都复杂

还有一个必须避免时代错置的问题：

**奥斯定时代的罗马主教制度，并不等同于十六世纪乃至十九世纪以后的教宗制度。**

五世纪的西方教会仍然具有非常强的主教集体性和地方教会结构。

因此，我们不能简单地说：

> 奥斯定是天主教圣人，所以他一定会赞成十六世纪罗马教廷的所有具体权力主张。

这并不严谨。

如果奥斯定认为某些教宗权力的运用已经越过合理边界，他完全可能提出批评。

他从来不是一个害怕与主教、神学家甚至整个社会风气争论的人。

因此，一个“活着的奥斯定”未必会成为教宗权力的无条件辩护者。

但是必须区分两件事：

**第一，教宗制度是否需要改革；**

与

**第二，因为教宗制度有问题，所以与整个罗马教会断绝共融是否合理。**

这两个命题之间存在巨大的距离。

奥斯定大概能够认真考虑第一个问题。

第二个问题却会直接触碰到他关于**教会合一、普世性与分裂**最深的神学直觉。

---

**8. What Would Augustine Have Thought of the Papacy? The Answer Is Probably More Complicated Than Either Side Would Like**

Another issue must be handled carefully to avoid anachronism:

**The role of the Bishop of Rome in Augustine's time was not identical to the papal system of the sixteenth century, still less to later nineteenth-century formulations.**

The Western Church of the fifth century retained a strong sense of episcopal collegiality and local ecclesial structures.

We therefore cannot simply argue:

> Augustine is a Catholic saint; therefore he would automatically have approved every concrete sixteenth-century claim made on behalf of papal authority.

That would be historically careless.

If Augustine believed that certain exercises of papal authority exceeded their proper bounds, he might very well have criticized them.

He was never a theologian afraid to argue with bishops, theologians, or the prevailing culture.

A living Augustine would not necessarily have been an unconditional defender of every papal claim.

But two questions must be distinguished:

**First, did the papal institution require reform?**

and

**Second, did problems in the papal institution justify breaking communion with the Roman Church altogether?**

There is an enormous distance between these propositions.

Augustine might have seriously entertained the first.

The second would have touched directly upon some of his deepest theological convictions concerning **ecclesial unity, catholicity, and schism**.

---

## 九、最可能出现的奥斯定：不是反改革者，而是激进的公教改革者

因此，如果我们真的把奥斯定放进十六世纪，一个很有可能出现的场景是：

他会同时攻击两个方向。

他可能会严厉批评罗马教会内部的：

腐败，

金钱化，

神职失职，

错误的功德观，

以及任何让普通信徒误以为“救恩可以购买、积累或计算”的宗教实践。

同时，他也会严厉批评宗教改革一方对于：

教会共融，

圣事，

传统，

主教职，

以及分裂后果

处理得过于轻率。

甚至可以想象一个很戏剧性的场面。

路德拿着奥斯定的著作对罗马说：

> 看，奥斯定也说救恩来自恩宠，而不是人的功劳。

然后“活着的奥斯定”站起来回答：

> 这一点你引用我，没有错。

> 但是如果你用我的恩宠论作为撕裂教会的理由，那么你没有理解我的教会论。

如果把它再浓缩一点：

> 你引用我的恩宠论，我同意；但你用它来拆散教会，我不同意。

这也许是整个思想实验中最接近历史上真实奥斯定的位置。

---

**9. The Most Likely Augustine: Not an Anti-Reformer, but a Radical Catholic Reformer**

If Augustine were genuinely transported into the sixteenth century, one plausible scenario is that he would attack in two directions at once.

He might denounce within the Roman Church:

corruption,

commercialization,

clerical failure,

distorted doctrines of merit,

and any religious practice that encouraged ordinary Christians to imagine that salvation could be bought, accumulated, or calculated.

At the same time, he might criticize the Reformers for treating too lightly questions of:

ecclesial communion,

the sacraments,

tradition,

the episcopate,

and the consequences of schism.

One can even imagine a dramatic encounter.

Luther raises Augustine's writings against Rome and says:

> Look: Augustine also teaches that salvation comes from grace, not human merit.

The “living Augustine” replies:

> On that point, you have quoted me correctly.

> But if you use my doctrine of grace as a reason to tear the Church apart, then you have failed to understand my doctrine of the Church.

In its shortest form:

> You quote my theology of grace, and I agree with you; but you use it to divide the Church, and there I do not.

This may come closer than almost anything else in the thought experiment to the historical Augustine.

---

## 十、为什么天主教和新教都如此喜欢奥斯定？

这也解释了一个非常有意思的历史现象：

**为什么宗教改革以后，天主教和新教都不断引用奥斯定？**

路德宗和改革宗从奥斯定那里看到：

原罪，

恩宠的优先性，

人的无力，

反白拉奇主义，

预定，

以及天主在救恩中的主动权。

天主教则从同一个奥斯定那里看到：

公教会，

教会可见的合一，

主教制度，

宗徒传统，

洗礼重生，

圣事，

圣体，

以及对分裂的强烈拒绝。

于是我们会发现：

> 奥斯定并不是“半个天主教徒、半个新教徒”。

更准确地说：

> 后来的天主教与新教，都从共同的古代基督宗教遗产中继承了奥斯定，但分别特别强调了他思想中的不同部分。

新教尤其放大了他的恩宠论。

天主教则将他的恩宠论与他的教会论、圣事论和爱德神学一起保留下来。

所以有时候读奥斯定关于恩宠的文字，会产生一种非常强烈的感觉：

> 这听起来怎么这么像宗教改革？

但继续读他的教会论，很快又会产生另一种感觉：

> 这又完全不像一个现代意义上的新教神学家。

真正的奥斯定恰恰存在于这两者同时成立的张力中。

---

**10. Why Do Both Catholics and Protestants Claim Augustine?**

This also explains a fascinating historical phenomenon:

**Why have both Catholics and Protestants appealed so persistently to Augustine since the Reformation?**

Lutherans and the Reformed find in Augustine:

original sin,

the priority of grace,

human inability,

anti-Pelagianism,

predestination,

and the initiative of God in salvation.

Catholics find in the same Augustine:

the Catholic Church,

visible ecclesial unity,

the episcopate,

apostolic tradition,

baptismal regeneration,

the sacraments,

the Eucharist,

and a profound rejection of schism.

The conclusion is not:

> Augustine was half Catholic and half Protestant.

A better conclusion is:

> Both Catholicism and Protestantism inherited Augustine from the common ancient Christian tradition, while emphasizing different dimensions of his theology.

Protestantism, especially, magnified his theology of grace.

Catholicism preserved that same theology of grace together with his ecclesiology, sacramental theology, and theology of charity.

This explains why reading Augustine on grace can sometimes produce the strong impression:

> This sounds remarkably like the Reformation.

Yet continued reading of his ecclesiology quickly produces another reaction:

> This does not sound at all like a modern Protestant theologian.

The historical Augustine exists precisely within the tension created by both of those observations being true.

---

## 十一、那么，他到底会不会加入新教？

如果一定要把这个思想实验量化，我大概会这样估计：

* **加入路德宗：约15%**
* **留在天主教内部、成为极其强烈的改革派：约70%**
* **形成一种处在改革双方之间的奥斯定主义公教改革路线：约15%**

当然，这些数字绝不是历史研究可以证明的统计结果，只是为了表达不同可能性的相对强弱。

真正重要的结论是：

> 圣奥斯定很可能会认为宗教改革指出了真实的问题，但不会因此认为离开公教会是解决这些问题最符合福音的办法。

他的恩宠论会让他非常理解路德为何愤怒。

他的反白拉奇主义会让他反对任何把救恩变成人类功劳工程的倾向。

但是他的教会论、圣事论和对分裂的警惕，又会阻止他轻易走向宗教分裂。

因此，如果一定用一句话来概括：

> 如果圣奥斯定活在宗教改革时期，他很可能会支持路德提出的许多问题，却拒绝路德最终给出的“以教会分裂来解决问题”的答案。

或者更尖锐一点：

> **路德也许会把奥斯定视为自己最重要的证人之一；但如果奥斯定本人真的坐在宗教改革的会场里，他未必会坐在路德那一边。**

---

**11. So, Would He Have Become Protestant?**

If this thought experiment had to be expressed in rough probabilities, I might estimate:

* **Becoming Lutheran: about 15%**
* **Remaining Catholic while becoming an exceptionally forceful reformer: about 70%**
* **Developing some intermediate form of Augustinian Catholic reform: about 15%**

These numbers are obviously not historical statistics. They merely express the relative plausibility of the different possibilities.

The deeper conclusion is this:

> Augustine would probably have believed that the Reformation identified genuine problems, but he would not necessarily have concluded that leaving the Catholic Church was the most evangelical solution to those problems.

His theology of grace would have enabled him to understand Luther's outrage.

His anti-Pelagianism would have made him deeply suspicious of any tendency to turn salvation into a human project of accumulated merit.

But his ecclesiology, sacramental theology, and fear of schism would have made him extremely reluctant to embrace ecclesial separation.

Therefore, if the entire thought experiment must be summarized in one sentence:

> If Saint Augustine had lived during the Reformation, he might have supported many of Luther's questions while rejecting Luther's final answer of solving them through the division of the Church.

Or, stated more sharply:

> **Luther might have regarded Augustine as one of his greatest witnesses; but if Augustine himself had been sitting in the room during the Reformation, he might not have been sitting on Luther's side.** 🟥
